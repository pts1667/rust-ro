# Ganbantein

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** High Wizard
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 1
- **Prerequisites:** ([Wizard](Wizard.md)) [Sense](Sense.md) Lv.1, [Ice Wall](Ice_Wall.md) Lv.1
- **SP cost:** 40
- **Cast time:** 1 second
- **Delay:** 5 seconds
- **Target:** Ground
- **Range:** Magic
- **Area:** 3x3
- **Catalyst:** 1 Blue and 1 Yellow Gemstone


## Description

Cast a spell that has an 80% chance to cancel ground targeting magic around the spot targeted by the caster. This skill can cancel the [Sage](Sage.md) skill, [Magnetic Earth](Magnetic_Earth.md), after it has enchanted the ground. Ganbantein is unaffected by items or skills that remove or reduce Gemstone item requirements.

The high-level NPC version of this skill affects a 9x9 area.
