# Castle Treasure Drops


## Overview

Castles captured by guilds participating in the [War of Emperium](War_of_Emperium.md) and [War of Emperium 2](War_of_Emperium_2.md) spawn treasure boxes each day at exactly 12:00 AM Pacific Time.  The boxes spawn in a special room only accessible by the Guild Leader through the castle [NPC](NPC.md).  Castles in each original WoE realm have one Common Treasure Box and their own individual box. On Ymir an exchange NPC Dr. Jonesy in [Prontera](Prontera.md) (xx, yy) will trade 150 Witched Starsands for 1 Treasure Box.
