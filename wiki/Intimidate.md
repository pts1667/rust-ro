# Intimidate

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Rogue
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** 10
- **Prerequisites:** ([Rogue](Rogue.md)) [Snatch](Snatch.md) Lv. 5


## Description

Enable the use of the last skill that was inflicted by an enemy. Intimidate`s skill level affects the level of the remembered skill that the Rogue can use. For example, if a Rogue is hit by [Bash](Bash.md) Lv.10, but the Rogue has only learned Intimidate Lv.5, the Rogue can only use Bash Lv.5 through the Intimidate skill.

## Notes

* Can only copy skills that damage the [Rogue](Rogue.md)/[Stalker](Stalker.md) directly.
* Copyable skills are those of 1-1, 2-1, 2-2 [Classes](Classes.md).
* Can not be used with skills exclusive to monsters, ex: [Property Attacks](Monster_Exclusive_Skills.md).
* Can not be used with [Transcendent](Classes.md) skills, ex: [Soul Destroyer](Soul_Destroyer.md)
* Can copy certain skills that are performed at a higher level by monsters than their player variant would allow. Ex.: [Water Ball](Water_Ball.md) Lv.10, [Claymore Trap](Claymore_Trap.md) Lv.10
* Can not copy [Blessing](Blessing.md) and [Increase AGI](Increase_AGI.md), even though they do 1 damage to the player due to the monster only skill [Change Undead](NPC_CHANGEUNDEAD.md).
* Copying [Jupitel Thunder](Jupitel_Thunder.md) Lv.10 does not work with Wind Ghost Card as described on the card.
* Copying [Heal](Heal.md) Lv.10 does not work with Enchanted Peachtree Card as described on the card.
* [Tomahawk Throwing](Tomahawk_Throwing.md) is currently the only non-naturally learned skill that is copyable by Intimidate.
