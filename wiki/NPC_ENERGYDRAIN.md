# NPC ENERGYDRAIN


## Description

*  Offensive skill
*  Levels: 1
*  Element: [Shadow](Shadow.md)

Deals physical damage to the target, which in turn heals the caster's HP for the equivalent amount. Damage is based on the monster's ATK.
