# Thanksgiving Event (2007/11/20) ~ (2007/11/27)

> **Note:** Not implemented on iRO Classic.

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Item requirement:** 1 Aloe Leaflet 1 Hinalle Leaflet 10 White Potion 10 Green Herb 10 Green Potion 10 Red Herb
- **Base EXP reward:** 420,000
- **Job EXP reward:** 150,000

1. Talk to Mr.Brando in front of the Rachel Airship Station to start the quest. He asks you to bring 1 Aloe Leaflet.
1. Give him 1 Aloe Leaflet.
1. Talk to him again and he will ask for 1 Hinalle Leaflet.
1. Go to the [Rachel](Rachel.md) Inn (close to Cool Event Corp Employee), Mrs. Brando is on the 2nd floor, in the last room. Talk to her. Give her 10 White Potions.
1. Talk to Grandma Vira and give her 10 Green Herbs. She's on the 2nd floor too, in the first room.
1. Go to the garden left of Rachel Inn. Talk to Kipp. Give him 10 Green Potions.
1. Talk to Cherice. Give her 10 Red Herbs.
1. Go back to Mr. Brando again to finish the quest
