# Spell Breaker

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Sage
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** ([Sage](Sage.md)) [Magic Rod](Magic_Rod.md) Lv. 1
- **SP cost:** 10
- **Cast time:** 0.7s
- **Delay:** None
- **Target:** Enemy


## Description

Cancel a magic spell cast by an enemy and receive the SP consumed to cast the enemy's spell.  At skill level 5, it will also inflict an amount of damage equal to 2% of the enemy's [Max HP](Max_HP.md), with the caster recovering HP equal to 1% of the enemy's [Max HP](Max_HP.md).

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SP Consumption (%) | 0 | 25 | 50 | 75 | 100 |
| Damage | No Damage | No Damage | No Damage | No Damage | 2% |
| HP Gain | None | None | None | None | 1% |


## Notes

* Spell Breaker can only be used on other players in [Town](categories/Towns.md)s and [PvP](PvP~d29d2.md) environment.
* When used against other players Spell Breaker ignores the Phen Card's effect which usually makes spell casting uninterruptible.
* Spell Breaker can interrupt skills from [Boss](Boss_Protocol.md) and Guardian monsters, although the chance of success is reduced to just 10%.
* When successful, Spell Breaker level 5 can damage any target, including Guardians and [Boss](Boss_Protocol.md) type monsters.
