## Overview

*[image not archived: HIGHWIZ.PNG‎]*

The [Mage](Mage.md) and the [Wizard](Wizard.md) have been able to tap into mystical and elemental magic but the real power is now fully realized in the spells mastered by the High Wizard. Only a fool would dare challenge the awesome might of a High Wizard's power head on!

A High Wizard has much more power than a regular [Wizard](Wizard.md), and at a much earlier level as well. Unlike the Wizard, it has [Mystical Amplification](Mystical_Amplification.md), which will give 50% more [MATK](Stats.md) on the next offensive magic skill to be cast. This grants a High Wizard a lot more power early on, and has about equal the strength of a level 99 Wizard in their early 80's, maybe even a little earlier. With that skill, they can level in Magma Dungeon immediately with [Priest](Priest.md) without worry of having too little MATK.

[Ganbantein](Ganbantein.md), and [Gravitational Field](Gravitational_Field.md) prove to be very useful in [WoE](War_of_Emperium.md) situations, whereas if there's too many of a certain element going on, or the precast breakers seem to be getting in, you can use [Gravitational Field](Gravitational_Field.md) to get much more damage than casting any of your other spells, since the damage cannot be negated, and always does the same amount.

[Ganbantein](Ganbantein.md) also has other uses in [WoE](War_of_Emperium.md). It can cancel many different spells that are cast on the ground. Such spells include [Pneuma](Pneuma.md), [Safety Wall](Safety_Wall.md), [Battle Chant](Battle_Chant.md), and [Warp Portal](Warp_Portal.md). It rips holes in the Sage class' ground spells as well, so it's good for stopping a [Sage](Sage.md) or [Scholar](Scholar.md) from getting their [Magnetic Earth](Magnetic_Earth.md) to work as they desired it to.

[Napalm Vulcan](Napalm_Vulcan.md) has a 25% chance of cursing at max level, and has 5 levels. Very good for cursing opponents in precasts, or just to buy time in general to help some killers get to the tanks in time to stop them, since it has splash damage.

[Soul Drain](Soul_Drain.md) allows the player to increase current [Max SP](Max_SP.md) by 20%, and every time a target is killed (monster) with a single target spell, player will restore SP equal to a certain percentage of that monster's SP. This basically allows the class to have infinite SP stacked on to the High Wizard's already really fast SP recovery rate.

[Stave Crasher](Stave_Crasher.md) is a very promising skill. This skill does a physical attack based on the High Wizard's [MATK](Stats.md). The higher the [MATK](Stats.md), the more damage. Since it counts as a physical attack, card effects and any other effects that come from doing physical attacks will work with this skill. You can pass on status effects, divest people in the near future, break their equipment, etc. Definitely a nice skill to have.

## Pure PvM/Semi WoE

* [STR](Stats.md): 1
* [AGI](Stats.md): 1
* [VIT](Stats.md): 25
* [INT](Stats.md): 99
* [DEX](Stats.md): 99
* [LUK](Stats.md): 1

This Build unleashes the High Wizards max potential by pulling out all the [MATK](Stats.md) it can. With [Mystical Amplification](Mystical_Amplification.md) they become deadly MvPers with their [Storm Gust](Storm_Gust.md). Very powerful during [WoE](War_of_Emperium.md) also, this build lacks however in the High Wizards [Max HP](Max_HP.md).


## PvM/WoE

* [STR](Stats.md): 1
* [AGI](Stats.md): 1
* [VIT](Stats.md): 40
* [INT](Stats.md): 93
* [DEX](Stats.md): 99
* [LUK](Stats.md): 1

With more [VIT](Stats.md) than the PvM Build, this build is more WoE oriented. It still has enough [MATK](Stats.md) with [Mystical Amplification](Mystical_Amplification.md) to deliver killing blows to opponents in [WoE](War_of_Emperium.md). This build gives you much more survivability than the PvM Build. It also lets you use all stat points without leaving any points unused.


## WoE

* [STR](Stats.md): 1
* [AGI](Stats.md): 25
* [VIT](Stats.md): 1
* [INT](Stats.md): 126-147
* [DEX](Stats.md): 130-150
* [LUK](Stats.md): 1

This is the glass cannon build for the current WoE meta.  Status Immunity is achieved through equipment.  Maximizing [INT](Stats.md) or [DEX](Stats.md) via gear depends on if you need to specialize in [Storm Gust](Storm_Gust.md) - which requires [INT](Stats.md) - or [Meteor Storm](Meteor_Storm.md) - which requires [DEX](Stats.md).  [Jupitel Thunder](Jupitel_Thunder.md) is good for focusing down low [VIT](Stats.md) characters, such as other High Wizards or [Biochemist](Biochemist.md)s.  A typical skill build will include:

* 10 [Storm Gust](Storm_Gust.md)
* 1~10 [Lord of Vermilion](Lord_of_Vermilion.md)
* 10 [Meteor Storm](Meteor_Storm.md)
* 10 [Jupitel Thunder](Jupitel_Thunder.md)
* 10 [Mystical Amplification](Mystical_Amplification.md)
* 3~5 [Heaven's Drive](Heaven's_Drive.md)
* 1 [Quagmire](Quagmire.md)
* 1 [Ganbantein](Ganbantein.md)
* 0 or 5 [Gravitational Field](Gravitational_Field.md)

Headgears:
Parade Hat with a Stalactic Golem Card for stun resistance.
Sunglasses \[1\] with a Stalactic Golem Card for stun resistance.
Well-Chewed Pencil for [DEX](Stats.md).

Armors:
Warlock's Battle Robe with either +3 [DEX](Stats.md) or +3 [INT](Stats.md), for [Storm Gust](Storm_Gust.md) or [Meteor Storm](Meteor_Storm.md), respectively.  Carded with a  or Evil Druid Card, but note that [Mind Breaker](Mind_Breaker.md) cannot be cast on Undead targets.
Valkyrja's Shield with a Flame Skull Card for Stun Resistance.
Commander's Manteau \[1\] with a Salamander Card for Meteor Storm damage or a Noxious Card or Raydric Card for damage reduction.
Combat Boots with a Zombie Slaughter Card for magic damage.

Accessory:
Glove \[1\] for [DEX](Stats.md).
Orlean's Glove for [DEX](Stats.md) and [MATK](Stats.md).
Earring \[1\] for INT, but only if it will give you a max [MATK](Stats.md) bonus.

Accessory Cards:
Zerom Card for [DEX](Stats.md).
Smokie Card for [Hiding](Hiding.md).

Weapon:
Warlock's Battle Wand for damage, [INT](Stats.md), [DEX](Stats.md) and to add Stun to all of your spells.
