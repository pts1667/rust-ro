# Haze Slasher

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Ninja
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 10
- **Prerequisites:** ([Ninja](Ninja.md)) [Shadow Leap](Shadow_Leap.md) Lv. 1
- **Target:** Foe
- **Range:** Melee


## Description

Strike an enemy and then immediately enter Hiding state. Can be followed up by [Shadow Leap](Shadow_Leap.md) or [Shadow Slash](Shadow_Slash.md).
