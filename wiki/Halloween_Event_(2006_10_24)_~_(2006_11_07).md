# Halloween Event (2006/10/24) ~ (2006/11/07)

> **Note:** Not implemented on iRO Classic.


## Fantastic Pumpkin Hat Quest

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** N/A (None?)
- **Item requirement:** 1 Pumpkin Mojo, (1 Pumpkin Hat or 1 Cap and 1 Pumpkin)
- **Item reward:** Fantastic Pumpkin Hat

1. Talk to the Cool Devi NPC in [Prontera](Prontera.md) (prontera 165,68).

2. Bring him 1 Pumpkin Mojo (Dropped by Delightful Ludes in virtually every field and dungeon) and 1 Pumpkin Hat or 1 Cap (Prontera Armor Dealer) and 1 Pumpkin (Prontera Veggie Merchant).

3. Receive the Fantastic Pumpkin Hat.


## Pumpkin Pie Quest

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** N/A (None?)
- **Item requirement:** 1 Pumpkin Mojo, 2 Pumpkins, 2 Eggs
- **Item reward:** 1 Pumpkin Pie (Repeatable)

1. Talk to Horin in the middle of [Geffen](Geffen.md). (144,132) She will ask you to help gather ingredients for a pumpkin pie. When you talk to her again, one of the question options Hoirin gives is "How can I get eggs?" If you choose that answer, she will offer to warp you to Chicken Masta to collect the eggs (she will only warp you there once, and you must talk to her before talking to the Chicken Masta about the eggs).

2. Collect the following items, and bring them to her: 1 Pumpkin Mojo, 2 Pumpkins, and 2 Eggs. She will then give you a Pumpkin Pie.

Eggs: You must find one of the Chicken Masta's many missing chickens and return them to him. There are a number of locations on the map where they spawn (and they only spawn in those places). Click on one of the chickens (Example: nif field01 269,144) and type "Return" when it asks you for the magic word. Return to the Chicken Masta after each chicken you return. You will be able to purchase 3 eggs at 1000z each after returning each chicken.
Pumpkins: Buy them from the [NPC](NPC.md) in [Prontera](Prontera.md) (prontera 49,58) for 15[z](Zeny.md) each, or gather from Delightful Ludes.
Pumpkin Mojo: Gather from Delightful Ludes (fields and dungeon maps).


## Familiar Warper

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** N/A (None?)
- **Item requirement:** 1 Pumpkin Pie
- **Item reward:** 3 Nifflheim Expresss Tickets -OR- Cookie Bag (Repeatable)'''

1. Talk to the Deviruchi (nif_in 20,32) in the Inn in [Nifflheim](Nifflheim.md) (niflheim 189,207). The Deviruchi will tell you that Pumpkin Pie is Loli Ruri's favorite dish and ask you for it. Give him the pumpkin pie and he'll give you 3 Nifflheim Express Tickets or a Cookie Bag.

2. If you want to use the tickets, go to the Familiar in [Prontera](Prontera.md) next to the statue on the path near the top of the map, (186,28) or in [Geffen](Geffen.md) (119,188) and [Alberta](Alberta.md) (81,171). In exchange for one ticket, it'll warp you to Niffheim.
