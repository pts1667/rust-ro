# Two Handed Sword


## Definition

Swords used by [Swordman](Swordman.md), [Knight](Knight.md), and [Crusader](Crusader.md) class characters. These are the same as One-Handed Swords, but obviously take up both hands instead of one. As such, you cannot wear a [Shield](Shield.md) while weilding a Two-Handed Sword. [Knight](Knight.md)s generally benefit more from wielding this weapon due to their skill set.

| Size | Damage |
| --- | --- |
| Small | 75% |
| Medium | 75% |
| Large | 100% |
