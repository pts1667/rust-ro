# Lex Aeterna

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Priest
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 1
- **Prerequisites:** ([Priest](Priest.md)) [Lex Divina](Lex_Divina.md) Lv. 5
- **SP cost:** 10
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** 3 seconds
- **Duration:** Until target receives damage or relogs
- **Target:** Monsters & *Players ([PvP](PvP~d29d2.md)/[WoE](War_of_Emperium.md) Only)*
- **Range:** 9 cells


## Description

The target will take double damage from the next real damage source of any kind. Healing, misses, status effects and [Shield Reflect](Shield_Reflect.md) do not trigger the effect.


## Information

Lex Aeterna is removed if the proceeding attack is blocked by [Guard](Guard.md), [Parry](Parry.md), or [Kaupe](Kaupe.md), even if no damage was received.
Cannot be cast on a [Frozen](Status_Effects.md) or [Stoned](Status_Effects.md) target. It can however be cast during the first stage of [Stone Curse](Stone_Curse.md), before the target becomes [Stoned](Status_Effects.md).


## Cards

Owl Baron Card (Accessory) - Automatically when attacking.
