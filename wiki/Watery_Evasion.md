# Watery Evasion

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Ninja
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 10
- **Prerequisites:** ([Ninja](Ninja.md)) [Freezing Spear](Freezing_Spear.md) Lv. 5
- **Cast time:** 3s
- **Delay:** 3s
- **Target:** Ground
- **Area:** Varies
- **Element:** Water
- **Catalyst:** 1 Ice Stone


## Description

Watery Evasion generates a huge area of swirling water to slow and trap those who attempt to pass through it. This skill drops the movement speed and agi of all enemies who move through it. In PVP and WoE settings, this skill affects everyone, allies or enemies, but does not affect any Ninja class characters. While the speed and agi of characters is reduced, Watery Evasion does not remove speed increasing skills, such as [Increase AGI](Increase_AGI.md), [Two Hand Quicken](Twohand_Quicken.md), and [Adrenaline Rush](Adrenaline_Rush.md). The reduction in AGI and Speed last for a short duration after they leave the affected area.

Watery Evasion cannot be cast on cells with [Magnetic Earth](Magnetic_Earth.md). If you cast [Blaze Shield](Blaze_Shield.md) it will cancel Watery Evasion. [Water Ball](Water_Ball.md) and [Aqua Benedicta](Aqua_Benedicta.md) can be cast on Watery Evasion cells, though any cells used for these skills will be removed after casting.

## Notes

Watery Evasion formerly caused permanent [AGI](Stats.md) reduction to all types of homunculus. Once AGI was reduced, it could be restored without the help of a Game Master. If a homunculus ran into this more than once, it was possible for their AGI and [FLEE](FLEE.md) to be reduced below 0.
