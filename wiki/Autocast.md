# Autocast

An **autocast** is the ability for a skill to be used without explicit activation. While some skills such as [Double Attack](Double_Attack.md), [Hindsight](Hindsight.md), and the [Taekwon Kid](TaeKwon_Kid.md) stances trigger skills randomly on attack, the term is generally used for cards or equipment that give a certain chance to cast skills when attacking or taking damage. Autocasting does not have any cast time, nor does it require any SP or catalyst.
