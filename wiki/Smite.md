# Smite

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Crusader
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** ([Crusader](Crusader.md)) [Guard](Guard.md) Lv. 5
- **SP cost:** 10
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** [ASPD](ASPD.md) Based
- **Target:** Enemy
- **Range:** Melee
- **Knockback:** (4+Skill Level) cells
- **Status:** [Stun](Status_Effects.md)
- **Equip:** Shield


## Description

Allows the caster to hurl their shield at an enemy, dealing damage based on their [ATK](Stats.md). The enemy is knocked back and has a chance to be inflicted with the [Stun](Status_Effects.md) status.

| Level | [ATK](Stats.md) | Knock Back | [Stun](Status_Effects.md) Chance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 120% | 5 | 20% |
| 2 | 140% | 6 | 25% |
| 3 | 160% | 7 | 30% |
| 4 | 180% | 8 | 35% |
| 5 | 200% | 9 | 40% |


## Information

* Requires user to have a shield equipped.
* The knock back is disabled in [WoE](War_of_Emperium.md).
* Smite carries status effects. Items or cards that have a chance to Stun enemies while attacking them stack with Smite's chance to [Stun](Status_Effects.md).
* Smite is not effective if its caster is mounted on a Peco Peco but does not have [Cavalier Mastery](Cavalier_Mastery.md) maxed, since its delay is affected by [ASPD](ASPD.md).


## Strategies

* A great skill to use to knock people out of [Pneuma](Pneuma.md), also because it gives players awful position lag, making it difficult for them to recast Pneuma. It can also be used to knock players out of [Sacrifice](Sacrifice.md)'s range, but may be difficult if the caster does not have a Peco Peco. Otherwise, it's obviously useful for stunning opponents.
