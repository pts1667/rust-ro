# Grand Cross

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Crusader
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 10 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Crusader](Crusader.md)) [Faith](Faith.md) Lv. 10, [Holy Cross](Holy_Cross.md) Lv. 6
- **SP cost:** (30 + (Skill level*7))
- **Cast time:** 3 seconds (Uninterruptable)
- **Delay:** 1 second
- **Duration:** .9 seconds
- **Target:** Self
- **Area:** Cross-Shape
- **Element:** Holy


## Description

Summons a Grand Cross around the caster, damaging the caster and enemies with [Holy](Holy.md) property waves. The skill lasts for 0.9 seconds, during which time the caster is immobilized.

## Damage


## Vs. Monsters

* Damage formula is: ([ATK](Stats.md)+[MATK](Stats.md))* (100% + 40*skill lvl%)
* The damage Grand Cross deals against enemies is not affected by:
  * [Weapon Masteries](Weapon_Mastery.md).
  * [Demon Bane](Demon_Bane.md).
  * Cards that increase damage by a percentage.
* Things that affect the damage are:
  * Weapon [ATK](Stats.md)
  * Cards, items, or skills that increase ATK by a set amount (Andre cards, [Impositio Manus](Impositio_Manus.md)).
  * [ATK](Stats.md) ([STR](Stats.md), [LUK](Stats.md), [DEX](Stats.md))
  * [MATK](Stats.md) ([INT](Stats.md))
  * The Sign


## Vs. Caster

* Part 1:
  * This damage dealt to caster is based on damage done to targets. It is of element: [Holy](Holy.md) and race: [Demi Human](Demi_Human.md). Therefore damage can be reduced with Holy (i.e. Angeling carded armor will negate it) reduction, Demi Human reduction (i.e. Thara frog card), [DEF](DEF.md), or [MDEF](Stats.md). In the same manner, wearing [Shadow](Shadow.md) or [Undead](Undead.md) armor increases the damage done to the caster.
* Part 2:
  * 20% HP is lost every cast regardless of reductions. It is part of the casting cost of Grand Cross.


## Information

* Grand Cross hits 1-5 times, depending highly on position and movement of enemy/enemies.
* When one or more monsters are on a single cell of GC, the number of hits are reduced by 1 per monster (to a minimum of one hit to one monster).
* Grand Cross does not work when wearing a Golden Thief Bug Card.
