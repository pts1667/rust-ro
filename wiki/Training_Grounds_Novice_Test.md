# Training Grounds Novice Test

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Class requirement:** Novice
- **Item reward:** Varies
- **Quest reward:** Transport to [Town](categories/Towns.md) of Choice


## Overview

The final task in completing the Training Grounds for a [Novice](Novice.md) is to take a Personality Quiz which will determine the character's future [class](Classes.md).  The following are the answers needed to reach each class.  Note that these answers are not the only set which will result in the desired class.

If taking the test and you do not like your [class](Classes.md), choosing "choose my own" will result in you receiving:

| Column 1 | Column 2 |
| --- | --- |
| Items | 1 Novice Adventurer's Suit 5 Free Ticket for Kafra Storage 1 Free Ticket for Kafra Transportation |
