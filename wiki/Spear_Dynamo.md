# Spear Dynamo

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Lord Knight
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 5 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Swordman](Swordman.md)) [Increase HP Recovery](Increase_HP_Recovery.md) Lv. 5, ([Knight](Knight.md)) [Peco Peco Ride](Peco_Peco_Ride.md) Lv. 1, [Spear Mastery](Spear_Mastery.md) Lv. 5
- **SP cost:** (10 + (Skill level*4))
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** [ASPD](ASPD.md) Based
- **Duration:** (20 + (Skill level*5)) seconds
- **Target:** Self


## Description

Grants the "Endure Effect", which stops flinching from being attacked (but not for the entire duration of Spear Dynamo, only until the hit charges are all used), while increasing [ATK](Stats.md) and [Accuracy](Stats.md). Also lowers the defense of the caster.

| Level | ATK Increase | SP Cost | Accuracy Increase | -[DEF](DEF.md) | Duration (sec) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | +5% | 14 | +10 | 5% | 25 |
| 2 | +10% | 18 | +20 | 10% | 30 |
| 3 | +15% | 22 | +30 | 15% | 35 |
| 4 | +20% | 26 | +40 | 20% | 40 |
| 5 | +25% | 30 | +50 | 25% | 45 |


## Information

* Despite the name of this skill, Spear Dynamo actually works with any weapon.
* The [ATK](Stats.md) gained does not add damage to [Clashing Spiral](Clashing_Spiral.md).
* The [ATK](Stats.md) gained does not add damage to [Sword Mastery](Sword_Mastery.md), [Two-Handed Sword Mastery](Two-Handed_Sword_Mastery.md) and [Aura Blade](Aura_Blade.md).
* The [Endure](Endure.md) Effect does not work in [WoE](War_of_Emperium.md).
* The 1 [MDEF](Stats.md) that would be added by level 1 Endure IS added, even in WoE.
* Defense reduced is both armor defense and [VIT](Stats.md) defense.
* Level 1 Spear Dynamo is good for taking advantage of the Endure effect for mobbing (not killing), mainly because not much defense is lost.
* The defense reduction can help reduce damage from [Occult Impaction](Occult_Impaction.md) and Ice Pick wielders.
* Cancels upon switching any gear or when [Divest](Status_Effects.md)ed (i.e. Divest Weapon is inflicted), but not when armor is broken.
* All [Lord Knight](Lord_Knight.md)s should have this skilled maxed out.
