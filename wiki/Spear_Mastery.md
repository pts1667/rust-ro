# Spear Mastery

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Knight
- **Secondary class:** Crusader
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** 10
- **Prerequisites:** None
- **Weapon:** Spear


## Description

Adds +4 ATK per skill level to [Spear](Spear.md) weapons. If the player is riding a PecoPeco, an additional +1 ATK per skill level is added.

| Skill Level | +[ATK](Stats.md) | +[ATK](Stats.md) on Peco Peco |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 4 | 5 |
| 2 | 8 | 10 |
| 3 | 12 | 15 |
| 4 | 16 | 20 |
| 5 | 20 | 25 |
| 6 | 24 | 30 |
| 7 | 28 | 35 |
| 8 | 32 | 40 |
| 9 | 36 | 45 |
| 10 | 40 | 50 |

* This is a [Weapon Mastery](Weapon_Mastery.md).
