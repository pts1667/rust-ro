# Huuma Shuriken

[Ninja](Ninja.md)-only weapons, used for the skill [Throw Huuma Shuriken](Throw_Huuma_Shuriken.md). Typically acquired through item quests, and are all two-handed. Huuma Shurikens have a low base [ASPD](ASPD.md), reducing their usefulness for melee attacking. This weapon class includes the highest base-attack weapon the Ninja can equip, the Huuma Blaze Shuriken, useful for raising the damage of [Throw Shuriken](Throw_Shuriken.md) and [Throw Kunai](Throw_Kunai.md).

See [Ninja Weapon Quests](Ninja_Weapon_Quests.md) for information on how to acquire Huuma Shurikens.

## List, Battleground Huuma Shurikens

These are specialized Huuma Shurikens for fighting [Demi Human](Demi_Human.md) targets, acquired by participating in the [Battlegrounds](Battlegrounds.md). Battleground weapons have no slots but raise damage substantially, are unbreakable, and have other bonuses as well.
