# Meteor Assault

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Assassin Cross
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 10
- **Prerequisites:** ([Assassin Cross](Assassin_Cross.md)) [Katar Mastery](Katar_Mastery.md) Lv. 5, [Righthand Mastery](Righthand_Mastery.md) Lv. 3, [Sonic Blow](Sonic_Blow.md) Lv. 5, [Soul Destroyer](Soul_Destroyer.md) Lv. 1
- **SP cost:** (8 + (Skill Level*2))
- **Cast time:** 1 second
- **Target:** Self
- **Area:** 5 x 5
- **Range:** 2
- **Status:** [Stun](Status_Effects.md), [Blind](Status_Effects.md) & [Bleeding](Status_Effects.md)

Attack all enemies within 2 cells in any direction around the caster with the probability of causing [Stun](Status_Effects.md), [Blind](Status_Effects.md) or [Bleeding](Status_Effects.md). There is a cast time and after-cast delay which can both be reduced. Levels of this skill increase the damage, chance of status effects and SP consumption.

## Notes

*  While this is an [Area of Effect](Area_of_Effect.md) skill, it only has a range of 2 cells outward from the caster.
*  The cast can be canceled. Thus when mobbing for MA, it is advised to bring along a Phen or Bloody Butterfly carded Accessory.
*  This attack takes on the weapon's element but not certain Weapon Cards. Neither +% Damage nor Status Cards function with MA. The only cards/properties of cards or equipment that work are ones that give +[ATK](Stats.md).
*  MA is unaffected by [Enchant Deadly Poison](Enchant_Deadly_Poison.md).
*  MA works in conjunction with the weapon [Ice Pick](Ice_Pick.md).
*  MA does not add the effects of Swordbreaker or Mailbreaker items to any targets hit.
