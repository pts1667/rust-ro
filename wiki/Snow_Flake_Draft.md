# Snow Flake Draft

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Ninja
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 5 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Ninja](Ninja.md)) [Ninja Mastery](Ninja_Mastery.md) Lv. 10, [Watery Evasion](Watery_Evasion.md) Lv. 7
- **SP cost:** 35+(Skill Level * 5)
- **Cast time:** 1.5+(Skill Level * 0.5)
- **Target:** Self
- **Area:** 7x7 cells
- **Element:** Water
- **Status:** Frozen
- **Catalyst:** 1 Ice Stone


## Description

Calls a huge frozen meteor to crash down on top of the Ninja, damaging those in range and freezing them to the ground. Snow Flake Draft strikes all enemies in a 7x7 area around the caster. This skill is considered a ground skill, and cannot be used on [Magnetic Earth](Magnetic_Earth.md). The chance to [Freeze](Status_Effects.md) is reduced by [Magic Defense](Stats.md) and [LUK](Stats.md). Targets already frozen will not be hit or unfrozen by repeated casts. One Ice Stone is consumed when casting this skill.
