# Stop Metto's Research

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Item reward:** 10 Yellow Gemstones -OR- 5 Blue Gemstones

1. Proceed to the item shop in [Juno](Juno.md) (yuno 196, 138) and talk to Wagan (yuno_in01 17, 30). He will ask for help in solving a problem with the Mad Scientist Metto.
1. Proceed to the Monster Museum in [Juno](Juno.md) (yuno 278, 293) and talk to Metto (yuno_in03 26, 39). Stop him from making his experiments.
1. Find Stangckle (yuno 58, 151 -> yuno_in01 17, 95) and stop his experiment.
1. Return to Metto.
1. Find Kato (yuno 48, 105 -> yuno_in01 19, 183) and stop his experiment, too.
1. Return to Metto.
1. Find CiCi in the [Sage](Sage.md) Test building (yuno 323, 284 -> yuno_in03 178, 43).
1. Return to Metto, and he will claim that he won't do any research again.
1. Return to Wagan and he will give 10 Yellow Gemstones or 5 Blue Gemstones as a reward.
