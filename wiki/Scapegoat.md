# Scapegoat

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Mercenary
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 1
- **Prerequisites:** None
- **SP cost:** 5
- **Cast time:** 3 Seconds
- **Target:** Player/[Mercenary](Mercenary_System.md)


## Description

The mercenary gives all of its remaining [HP](Max_HP.md) to the summoner and dies. Any excess HP is discarded.
