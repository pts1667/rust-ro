# NPC ACIDBREATH

> **Npc skill** — name: Acid Breath; type: Area-of-Effect Physical Attack; levels: 10; range: 9x14 cells; target: Single Opponet

Acid Breath is a physical poison elemental attack that deals damage to all targets in a path towards in an area of 9x14 cells. Has a 70% chance to cause poison to all targets struck.

| Skill Level | Damage |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 100% |
| 2 | 200% |
| 3 | 300% |
| 4 | 400% |
| 5 | 500% |
| 6 | 600% |
| 7 | 700% |
| 8 | 800% |
| 9 | 900% |
| 10 | 1000% |


## Notes

*  Does not affect Poison element foes
*  Can be blocked by [Safety Wall](Safety_Wall.md), if at most 4 cells away from the monster at the time of attack.
*  Can be blocked by [Pneuma](Pneuma.md), if at least 4 cells away from the monster at the time of attack.
*  Can be evaded
