# Venom Knife

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Assassin
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 1
- **Quest:** Assassin Skill Quest#Venom Knife Quest
- **SP cost:** 15
- **Delay:** [ASPD](ASPD.md)
- **Target:** Foe
- **Range:** 10 cells
- **Status:** [Poison](Poison.md)
- **Catalyst:** 1 [Venom Knife](Venom_Knife.md)


## Description

Throw a Venom Knife at mid range to deal damage and poison the enemy.


## Information

* Venom Knives are an arrow type equipment and can be purchased from the Assassin Guild for 50z each.
* Venom Knife damage is derived from base [ATK](Stats.md) and does not count [Race](Race.md)/[Size](Size.md)/[Element](Element.md) cards or [Enchant Deadly Poison](Enchant_Deadly_Poison.md).
Cards and items adding to [ATK](Stats.md) are counted in damage calculation.
* Chance to [Poison](Poison.md) is based on the player's level of [Envenom](Envenom.md).

The introduction of the quest skill Venom Knife allows an Assassin to equip arrows. Arrows only work with long range attacks so the only skill Assassins have that will benefit from this is [Grimtooth](Grimtooth.md) (level 3 or above). Additionally, only status arrows work with Grimtooth, not elemental arrows.


## Notes

* The projectile takes on the element of the right hand.
* Uses Ice Pick damage calculation when icepick is equipped, including  cards and items adding to [ATK](Stats.md) but excluding percent modifiers as seen above.
