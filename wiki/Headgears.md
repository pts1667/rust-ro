# Headgears

Equippable items worn on the head.  Some can be made through [NPC](NPC.md)s, while others are dropped by [Monsters](Monster.md).  Some headgears are currently unobtainable in the game, as no [Monster](Monster.md) drops it, no NPC creates it, and cannot be forged.


## Unobtainable Headgears

* Sunglasses \[1\]
* Fruit of Love
* Glasses \[1\]
* Lord Kaho's Horn


## Event Headgears

These are all items which were made for a specific event.  Since that event has now ended, there is no way to create them any longer.

* Fantastic Pumpkin-Head
* Festival Pumpkin-Head

* Pirate Dagger
* Pirate Eye Bandage
