# Dragonology

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Sage
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** ([Sage](Sage.md)) [Study](Study.md) Lv. 9


## Description

Dragonology increases resistance and damage against [Dragon](Dragon.md) class monsters, and also gives an [INT](Stats.md) bonus depending on skill level.

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Added Dragon Resistance (%) | 4 | 8 | 12 | 16 | 20 |
| +ATK (%) | 4 | 8 | 12 | 16 | 20 |
| +MATK (%) | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 10 |
| INT Bonus | 1 | 1 | 2 | 2 | 3 |


## Notes

* There was a long-term bug where level 4 gave +3 INT instead of +2 INT, however this has now been fixed.
