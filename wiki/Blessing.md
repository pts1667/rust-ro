# Blessing

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Acolyte
- **Secondary class:** Super Novice
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 10 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Acolyte](Acolyte.md) & [Super Novice](Super_Novice.md)) [Divine Protection](Divine_Protection.md) Lv. 5
- **SP cost:** (24 +(Skill level*4))
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** None
- **Duration:** (40+(Skill level*20)
- **Target:** Self, Foe, and Other Players


## Description

Increases the target's [STR](Stats.md), [DEX](Stats.md), and [INT](Stats.md).
Bless can also be used to cure a target from [Curse](Status_Effects.md) and [Stone](Status_Effects.md) (2nd stage only) status effects.

## Note

* When used on [Undead](Undead.md) element and [Demon](Demon.md) monsters, Bless reduces the target's DEX and INT by 50% for the skill duration. Offensive Blessing does not work on players.

* When used on a target to remove [Curse](Status_Effects.md) they will not receive the bonus stats.

* This skill is often even more integral to an [Acolyte](Acolyte.md) than other players, as the extra INT speeds up SP recovery considerably
