# Battle Command

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Guild
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 1
- **Prerequisites:** [Official Guild Approval](Official_Guild_Approval.md) Lv. 1, [Guild Extension](Guild_Extension.md) Lv. 2
- **SP cost:** None
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** 5 minutes
- **Duration:** 180 seconds


## Description

All guild members and allies visible on the Guild Leader's screen will receive a +5 bonus to [STR](Stats.md), [DEX](Stats.md) and [INT](Stats.md) for 180 seconds.


## Facts

* Can only be used inside [WoE](War_of_Emperium.md) maps. All members of the guild and its allies on screen of the guild leader recieve a +5 to all stats buff that lasts for 3 minutes.
* The delay associated with this skill is the same delay that is associated with the skills [Restoration](Restoration.md), [Regeneration](Regeneration.md), and [Urgent Call](Urgent_Call.md). If a guild leader preforms one of these, they cannot preform Battle Command until the 5 minute delay has enlapsed, and vice versa.
* This skill also affects a guild's allies, not just members of the leader's own guild as the in-game description suggests.
* Duration is 3 minutes, not just 1 as the in-game description suggests.
