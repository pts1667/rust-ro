# Mental Strength

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Monk
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 5 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Monk](Monk.md)) [Raging Thrust](Raging_Thrust.md) Lv. 3
- **SP cost:** 200
- **Cast time:** 5 seconds
- **Delay:** (Skills Not Allowed)
- **Duration:** (Skill level*30) seconds
- **Target:** Self


## Description

Use 5 [Spirit Sphere](Spirit_Sphere.md)s to temporarily set the user's armor (hard) [DEF](DEF.md) and [MDEF](Stats.md) to 90, at the expense of moving speed, attack speed (reduced by 25%), and is unable to use active skills.

| Level | Duration |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 30 seconds |
| 2 | 60 seconds |
| 3 | 90 seconds |
| 4 | 120 seconds |
| 5 | 150 seconds |


## Notes

*  The cast time is not interruptible.
*  Only active skills (such as [Heal](Heal.md) or [Summon Spirit Sphere](Summon_Spirit_Sphere.md)) will be disabled during Mental Strength status. Passive skills such as [Raging Trifecta Blow](Raging_Trifecta_Blow.md) or [Spiritual Cadence](Spiritual_Cadence.md) will function as normal.
*  Items are usable.
*  This skill *sets* the user's [DEF](DEF.md) and [MDEF](Stats.md). As a result, your equipment will not have any effect on your DEF like it usually does. [VIT](Stats.md) DEF (*soft* DEF) will still influence damage.
*  Mental Strength stacks with [Assumptio](Assumptio.md).
