# Glacier Fist

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Champion
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 5 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Monk](Monk.md)) [Raging Thrust](Raging_Thrust.md) Lv.3
- **SP cost:** (2 + 2*Skill Level)
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** 1.3 - [([AGI](Stats.md)*0.004) - ([DEX](Stats.md)*0.002)] seconds
- **Target:** Enemy ([via Combo](Monk.md))
- **Range:** Melee
- **Catalyst:** 1 [Spirit Sphere](Spirit_Sphere.md)


## Description

A combo skill that can be used after using [Raging Thrust](Raging_Thrust.md) or [Chain Crush Combo](Chain_Crush_Combo.md). Initiate Glacier Fist after Raging Thrust to inflict additional damage. Afterwards, the enemy will be stopped from any movement for a short period of time. Consumes 1 [Spirit Sphere](Spirit_Sphere.md).

| Level | +[ATK](Stats.md) (%) | Chance to Immobilize |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 140% | 20% |
| 2 | 240% | 30% |
| 3 | 340% | 40% |
| 4 | 440% | 50% |
| 5 | 540% | 60% |
