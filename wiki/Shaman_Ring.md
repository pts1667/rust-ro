# Shaman Ring

The [Shaman Ring](Shaman_Ring.md) is an item you can obtain by buying the Ragnarok Online game for the Nintendo DS.

If you are wearing the accessory Shaman Ring and enter Glast Heim there are hidden [NPC](NPC.md)s called Sapphire that move you to 5 different locations within Glast Heim. An effect at each NPC location is visible if you are wearing the Shaman Ring and walk by one of the Sapphire locations.

* Near the entrance to Glast Heim (glast_01 365,309).
You can move to the Castle Entrance, Abbey Entrance, Chivalry Entrance or Glast Heim Culvert.

* Near the Castle Entrance (glast_01 218,293).
You can move to Glast Heim Entrance, Abbey Entrance, Chivalry Entrance or Glast Heim Culvert.

* Near the Abbey (glast_01 200,98).
You can move to the Castle Entrance, Glast Heim Entrance, Chivalry Entrance or Glast Heim Culvert.

* Near the Chilvalry entrance (glast_01 78,189).
You can move to the Castle Entrance, Abbey Entrance, Glast Heim Entrance or Glast Heim Culvert.

* Near the Glast Heim Culvert (gl_sew02 287,298).
You can move to the Castle Entrance, Abbey Entrance, Chivalry Entrance or Glast Heim Entrance.
