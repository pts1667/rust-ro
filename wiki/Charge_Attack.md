# Charge Attack

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Knight
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 1
- **Quest:** Knight Skill Quest#Charge Attack Quest
- **SP cost:** 40
- **Cast time:** Range Dependent
- **Target:** Enemy
- **Range:** 14 cells
- **Knockback:** 1 cell


## Description

Charge Attack is a quest skill that causes you to rush the targeted monster/player, dealing more damage depending on how far away you are from it. The farther the target, the longer the cast time.

| Distance | < 3 Cells | < 6 Cells | < 9 Cells | < 12 Cells | < 14 Cells |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cast Time | .5 Sec. | 1 Sec. | 1.5 Sec. | 1.5 Sec. | 1.5 Sec. |
| Damage | 100% | 200% | 300% | 400% | 500% |


## Information

* [Safety Wall](Safety_Wall.md) does not block it since it is a ranged attack
* Interruptible cast time. The enemy can walk away from range, hide behind an obstacle, or use the skill hide.
* Enemy can reduced the damage by simply moving closer to you
* When you hit the enemy, they will be knocked back in a random direction.
* During [WoE](War_of_Emperium.md), your body will not “charge” or move when you use this skill. It becomes another range physical attack.
* If charge attack misses (like there is a [Pneuma](Pneuma.md)), your body will still relocate.
* Charge attack can get you “out” of any trap, spider web of [Scholar](Scholar.md)s, or the skill [Close Confine](Close_Confine.md) of [Rogue](Rogue.md)s/[Stalker](Stalker.md)s (provided another enemy is nearby to charge).
