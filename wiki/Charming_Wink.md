# Charming Wink

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Dancer
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 1
- **Quest:** Dancer Skill Quest
- **SP cost:** 40
- **Duration:** 10
- **Target:** Enemy
- **Status:** [Chaos](Status_Effects.md)


## Description

Wink at a specific target, charming them for 10 seconds. If used on a monster, they become non-aggressive for the duration, and has a success rate of 70%. The skill will fail on high level monsters and boss monsters. If used on a player, it will have a low chance to cause the [Chaos](Status_Effects.md) status ailment.

Charming Wink only works on [Demon](Demon.md), [Angel](Angel.md), and [Demi Human](Demi_Human.md) type monsters.
