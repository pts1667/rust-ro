# Basilica

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** High Priest
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 5 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Priest](Priest.md)) [Gloria](Gloria.md) Lv. 2, [Increase SP Recovery](Increase_SP_Recovery.md) Lv. 1
- **SP cost:** (70 + (Skill level*10))
- **Cast time:** (4 + Skill level) seconds
- **Delay:** (1 + Skill level) seconds
- **Duration:** (15 + (Skill level*5)) seconds
- **Target:** Ground
- **Area:** 5x5
- **Catalyst:** 1 Blue, Red and Yellow Gemstone, 1 Holy Water


## Description

Shields all characters within five cells of the caster against any attack, but prevents shielded characters from making attacks. The area needs to be empty 5 x 5 cells around the target tile. If a monster or impassable terrain blocks any one of the cells, the skill will fail. Cannot be used in [WoE](War_of_Emperium.md) castle maps.

| Level | Duration | Cast Time | Cast Delay | SP Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 20 sec | 5 sec | 2 sec | 80 SP |
| 2 | 25 sec | 6 sec | 3 sec | 90 SP |
| 3 | 30 sec | 7 sec | 4 sec | 100 SP |
| 4 | 35 sec | 8 sec | 5 sec | 110 SP |
| 5 | 40 sec | 9 sec | 6 sec | 120 SP |


## Notes

* Does not affect [Boss](Boss_Protocol.md) monsters.
* [Magnetic Earth](Magnetic_Earth.md) cancels affected areas if casted outside it.
