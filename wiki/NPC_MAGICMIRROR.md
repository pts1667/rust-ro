# NPC MAGICMIRROR

> **Npc skill** — name: Magic Mirror; type: Defensive Magical Skill; levels: 5; target: Self

Description: For 30 seconds, the monster will have a fixed chance to completely reflect magical damage (including area of effect magic).

| Skill Level | Chance to Reflect |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 20% |
| 2 | 40% |
| 3 | 60% |
| 4 | 80% |
| 5 | 100% |


## Obtainable via

* Platinum Shield - when being attacked with magic
