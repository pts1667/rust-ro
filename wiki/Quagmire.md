# Quagmire

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Wizard
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 5 (Fixed)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Wizard](Wizard.md)) [Heaven's Drive](Heaven's_Drive.md) Lv. 1
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** 1 second
- **Target:** Ground
- **Range:** Magic
- **Area:** 5 x 5


## Description

Change an area on the ground into marshland that will reduce the Movement Speed, AGI and DEX of monsters and players that walk through that area. [Movement Speed](Movement_Speed.md), [AGI](Stats.md) and [DEX](Stats.md) are reduced by 10 to 50, although there are limitations to these stat reductions. Quagmire cannot reduce said stats of monsters by more than 50%, and those of players by more than 25%. In [PvP](PvP~d29d2.md), Quagmire can cancel certain buff effects, skills such as [Increase AGI](Increase_AGI.md), [Twohand Quicken](Twohand_Quicken.md) and [Adrenaline Rush](Adrenaline_Rush.md). One Wizard can create a maximum of 3 Quagmires at a time.
