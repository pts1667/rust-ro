# Kaupe

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Soul Linker
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 3
- **Prerequisites:** ([Soul Linker](Soul_Linker.md)) [Rogue Spirit](Rogue_Spirit.md) Lv. 1
- **SP cost:** 10+10*Skill Level
- **Cast time:** 0.5 seconds
- **Delay:** None
- **Duration:** 10 minutes
- **Target:** Players


## Description

Kaupe status enables the chance of dodging any physical attack or magical attack from an enemy and lasts until the 10 minute duration elapses or until character successfully dodges an attack.

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SP Taken | 20 | 30 | 40 |
| Dodge Chance (%) | 33 | 66 | 100 |


## Skills not blocked

The following skills will ignore [Kaupe](Kaupe.md):
*  [Gloria Domini](Gloria_Domini.md)
*  [Gravitational Field](Gravitational_Field.md)
*  [Tarot Card of Fate](Tarot_Card_of_Fate.md) <small>(chariot, devil, tower)</small>


## Notes

* This skill can only be used on yourself, your spouse or your child, or other Soul Linkers. Unless getting Linked with [Soul Linker Spirit](Soul_Linker_Spirit.md), using the skill on anyone else causes the skill to fail and the caster to be stunned.

* Only the first hit from multiple-hit skills (e.g. [Bowling Bash](Bowling_Bash.md), [Storm Gust](Storm_Gust.md)) will be dodged. Kaupe must be reapplied in order to dodge successive hits.

* Kaupe is consumed before [Tumbling](Tumbling.md) gets its chance to activate.
