# PvM

PvM is an acronym and refers to Player versus [Monster](Monster.md). It is the gameplay mode to attain higher levels on a character, hunt for certain [Items](Items.md) or just whack pink blobs for the sheer fun of it. Not to be confused with [MVP](MVP.md).

PvM is generally considered to be one half of the game experience, the other being Player versus Player content: [PVP](PvP~d29d2.md), [WoE](War_of_Emperium.md)/[WoE 2](War_of_Emperium_2.md), and [Battlegrounds](Battlegrounds.md).
