# Laphine Craftsman

> **Repeatable** — cooldown: 24 Hour

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** 70
- **Item requirement:** 15 Crystallized Teardrop, 15 Florescent Liquid
- **Quests required:** Started [Two Tribes](Two_Tribes.md) (access to Yggdrasil Dungeon)
- **Base EXP reward:** 30,000
- **Job EXP reward:** 30,000
- **Item reward:** 3 Splendide Coin
- **Window:** yes

1.  In Splendide, go inside the building at (splendide 198, 238), and speak to the Laphine (spl_in 97, 313). She asks you to bring her 15 Crystallized Teardrop and 15 Florescent Liquid.
  * **Note**: Crystallized Teardrops are character bound.
1.  Return for 30,000 [Base EXP](Base_EXP_Chart.md), 30,000 [Job EXP](Job_EXP_Chart.md), and 3 Splendide Coin.
