# Knuckle

Knuckles, also known as Fists or Claws, are used by [Monk](Monk.md) and [Priest](Priest.md) classes. Knuckles usually have low to medium damage, but one of the best base attack speeds available. However, claws have a undesireable size modifier, and are often skipped over in favor of [Maces](Mace.md).

| Size | Damage |
| --- | --- |
| Small | 100% |
| Medium | 75% |
| Large | 50% |
