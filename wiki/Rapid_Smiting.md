# Rapid Smiting

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Paladin
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 5 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Crusader](Crusader.md)) [Shield Boomerang](Shield_Boomerang.md) Lv. 5
- **SP cost:** (25 + (Skill level*3))
- **Cast time:** 1 second
- **Delay:** 1 second
- **Target:** Enemy
- **Range:** 4 cells
- **Element:** Neutral
- **Equip:** Shield


## Description

Using a Shield the caster will deal 5 attacks in a strike against a single foe. There is a constant +20 [Hit](Stats.md) bonus at all levels when using this skill. Final damage will include subtraction from enemy [DEF](DEF.md) as well. SP taken increases with higher level use of this skill.

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Damage (%) | 130 | 160 | 190 | 220 | 250 |
| SP Cost | 28 | 31 | 34 | 37 | 40 |


## Notes

Compiled by Wind!crap on the RagnaInfo forums.

* Requires a Shield to be used.
* If you miss, you will dealing the shield refine rate damage to the enemy(similar to Lord Knight [Clashing Spiral](Clashing_Spiral.md)).
* At level 5 the damage is 5 * 250%.
* The attack is a ranged, ALWAYS neutral attack. GR and Deviling reduces the damage considerably.
* It can miss depending on the caster's [Hit](Stats.md).
* Ignores [Size](Size.md) Penalty.
* Does not ignore [DEF](DEF.md). Using [Provoke](Provoke.md) against enemies with strong defense can significantly boost damage.
* Cast Time is interruptable.
* Shield's weight and refine rate affect the damage, so Cross Shield and Strong Shield is the best choice for attack.
* ONLY stat-derived ATK contributes to damage. [STR](Stats.md) greatly affects damage; [DEX](Stats.md) and [LUK](Stats.md) have a minor effect.
* Any other ATK - weapon [ATK](Stats.md), weapon refinement, ATK-increasing cards or items - have NO effect on the damage.
* The attack bonus gained from [Provoke](Provoke.md) or [Berserk](Berserk.md) does not affect the damage.
* Weapon % cards affect the damage. For the maximum effectiveness, it is advised to use 4 slotted one hand weapons.
* Ice Pick does not work in conjunction with this skill.
* The damage is blocked by [Pneuma](Pneuma.md), [Basilica](Basilica.md), [Flip Tatami](Flip_Tatami.md) entirely.
* The damage is reduced by [Assumptio](Assumptio.md), [Energy Coat](Energy_Coat.md), [Kyrie Eleison](Kyrie_Eleison.md) and [Defending Aura](Defending_Aura.md).
* Reduction gears like Cranial, Poo and Immune are not ignored, but Immune can be bypassed by elemental endows.
* Being Cursed does not lower the caster's damage with this skill.
* Status weapons work in conjunction with this skill. But despite it hitting five times, the effects proc only once.
* [Lex Aeterna](Lex_Aeterna.md) doubles the damage of all five hits.


## Strategies

In [PvP](PvP~d29d2.md) situations you can use [Shield Boomerang](Shield_Boomerang.md) with Marina or Stormy Knight carded weapons to freeze your enemies and then use [Shield Chain](Rapid_Smiting.md). It can be used on [GR](GR.md) users, but does not work with Deviling card users. [Shrink](Shrink.md) can also be very useful when using Rapid Smiting.
