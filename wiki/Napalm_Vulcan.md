# Napalm Vulcan

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** High Wizard
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 5 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Mage](Mage.md)) [Napalm Beat](Napalm_Beat.md) Lv. 5
- **SP cost:** 10 ~ 70
- **Cast time:** .7 seconds
- **Delay:** None
- **Target:** Enemy
- **Range:** Magic
- **Area:** 3 x 3
- **Element:** Ghost
- **Status:** [Curse](Status_Effects.md)


## Description

Hits every Enemy in a 3x3 area around the target for Skill Lv times. The damage per hit is spread equally among the affected targets. The damage for one target is 1*MATK per hit. Has a chance to Curse based on skill level.

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hits | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| SP Cost | 10 | 25 | 40 | 55 | 70 |
| Curse Chance | 5% | 10% | 15% | 20% | 25% |
