# Assassin Spirit

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Soul Linker
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 5 (Fixed)
- **Prerequisites:** None
- **SP cost:** (560 - (Skill level*100))
- **Cast time:** 1 second
- **Delay:** N/A
- **Duration:** (100 + (Skill level*50)) seconds
- **Target:** [Assassin](Assassin.md)s


## Description

Imbues [Assassin](Assassin.md)s with the Spirit of the Assassin, giving them special powerful buffs that last until the Spirit buff runs out.


## Information

* [Sonic Blow](Sonic_Blow.md)'s attack power is increased by 100%, and its cast delay is reduced by half. However, this skill only gives 25% bonus damage and offers no cast delay reduction during [WoE](War_of_Emperium.md).
* Contrary to normal belief, this skill does not allow you to chain Sonic Blow faster. Even though the skill's cast delay is halved, its 2 second animation delay still remains. (Similar to Arrow Vulcan). So while you can use other skills after 1 second reduced delay (example: back sliding) you can't chain sonic blow again until its animation delay is over or your animation delay is interrupted.
