# Ore Discovery

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Blacksmith
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** 1
- **Prerequisites:** ([Blacksmith](Blacksmith.md)) [Hilt Binding](Hilt_Binding.md) Lv. 1, [Steel Tempering](Steel_Tempering.md) Lv. 1
- **Cast time:** None


## Description

Either:

* Allows an ore to be dropped at a small chance if the Blacksmith deals the final hit on a monster.
OR
* This skill's effect applies to items dropped from monsters if the Blacksmith earns the highest priority for the item.

* Ores that can be dropped
  * Iron Ore
  * Iron
  * Steel
  * Coal
  * Gold
  * Rough Oridecon
  * Oridecon
  * Rough Elunium
  * Elunium
  * Phracon
  * Emveretarcon
  * Emperium
  * Red Blood
  * Green Live
  * Crystal Blue
  * Wind of Vendure
  * Flame Heart
  * Great Nature
  * Mystic Frozen
  * Rough Wind
