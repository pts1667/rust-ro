# Traumatic Blow

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Lord Knight
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** ([Knight](Knight.md)) [Peco Peco Ride](Peco_Peco_Ride.md) Lv. 1, [Spear Mastery](Spear_Mastery.md) Lv. 9
- **SP cost:** 23
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** .5 seconds
- **Target:** Enemy
- **Range:** 5 cells
- **Status:** [Bleeding](Status_Effects.md)


## Description

An attack that increases damage and has a chance to inflict the [Bleeding](Status_Effects.md) status on the opponent.

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ATK | 140% | 180% | 220% | 260% | 300% |


## Information

* Works with any or no weapon.
* The [Bleeding](Status_Effects.md) status will not be inflicted upon [Race](Race.md)s that are immune to it (i.e. [Undead](Undead.md)).
