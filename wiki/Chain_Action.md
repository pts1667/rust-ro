# Chain Action

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Gunslinger
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** 10
- **Prerequisites:** ([Gunslinger](Gunslinger.md)) [Single Action](Single_Action.md) Lv. 1
- **Weapon:** Pistol


## Description

Enables the chance of shooting 2 bullets at an enemy in one attack using a [Pistol](Gun.md) class weapon. The activation chance is depentant on the level of the skill. When the skill activates, only a single bullet is consumed. It is in many ways similar to the thief skill [Double Attack](Double_Attack.md). It cannot be used with other classes of weapon through the use of a Sidewinder Card or Chick Hat.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chance | 5% | 10% | 15% | 20% | 25% | 30% | 35% | 40% | 45% | 50% |
