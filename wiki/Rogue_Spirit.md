# Rogue Spirit

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Soul Linker
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 5 (Fixed)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Soul Linker](Soul_Linker.md)) [Assassin Spirit](Assassin_Spirit.md) Lv. 1
- **SP cost:** (560 - (Skill level*100))
- **Cast time:** 1 second
- **Delay:** N/A
- **Duration:** (100 + (Skill level*50)) seconds
- **Target:** [Rogue](Rogue.md)s


## Description

Imbues [Rogue](Rogue.md)s and [Stalker](Stalker.md)s with the Spirit of the Rogue, giving them special powerful buffs that last until the Spirit buff runs out.
*  Immunity to [Dispell](Dispell.md) is gained when [Preserve](Preserve.md) is also active.
*  Immunity to [TaeKwon Kid](TaeKwon_Kid.md)'s [Flying Kick](Flying_Kick.md) link-buff dispelling attribute is gained if the [Stalker](Stalker.md) has [Preserve](Preserve.md).
*  Healing rate from Potions made by [Alchemist](Alchemist.md) rankers is vastly increased (Doubled in addition to the "Ranked" bonus).
*  [STR](Stats.md) bonus from [Stealth](Stealth.md) lasts 300 seconds.
*  [Movement Speed](Movement_Speed.md) while in [Stealth](Stealth.md) mode is vastly increased
