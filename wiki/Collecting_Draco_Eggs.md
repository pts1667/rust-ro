# Collecting Draco Eggs

> **Repeatable** — cooldown: 24 Hour

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** 70
- **Item requirement:** 10 Draco's Egg
- **Quests required:** Started [Two Tribes](Two_Tribes.md) (Access to Yggdrasil Dungeon)
- **Base EXP reward:** 40,000
- **Job EXP reward:** 40,000
- **Item reward:** 1 Splendide Coin & 1 Manuk Coin
- **Window:** yes

1.  Talk to Pinedel (mid_camp 146, 306), she requests you to collect 10 Draco's Egg.
1.  Find Draco's Egg at various locations on the 1st and 2nd map of the Yggdrasil Dungeon
1.  Return to Pinedel when you have 10 Draco's Egg.
  * Receive 40,000 [Base Experience](Base_EXP_Chart.md), 40,000 [Job Experience](Job_EXP_Chart.md), 1 Splendide Coin and 1 Manuk Coin.
