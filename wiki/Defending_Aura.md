# Defending Aura

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Crusader
- **Secondary class:** Mercenary
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** ([Crusader](Crusader.md)) [Guard](Guard.md) Lv. 5, [Smite](Smite.md) Lv. 3, [Shield Boomerang](Shield_Boomerang.md) Lv. 1
- **SP cost:** 30 to activate, 30 to cancel
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** 1 second
- **Duration:** 180 seconds, manually cancellable
- **Target:** Self (transfers to [Party](Party.md) members through [Sacrifice](Sacrifice.md))


## Description

Decreases the ATK of ranged attacks against the caster by 5%+15*SkillLV%, but also lowers [ASPD](ASPD.md) by 25%-5*SkillLV% and walking speed by 1/3 while active. The higher the level the lower the ASPD reduction, up to SkillLV 5 which has no ASPD penalty; however, the walking speed reduction applies for all levels. Can be shared with [Party](Party.md) members when [Sacrifice](Sacrifice.md) is cast on them, but the movement reduction will affect them too. Caster must have a shield equipped.

| Level | Long Range Reduction | Speed Reduction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 20% | -20% ASPD |
| 2 | 35% | -15% ASPD |
| 3 | 50% | -10% ASPD |
| 4 | 65% | -5% ASPD |
| 5 | 80% | No ASPD reduction |
