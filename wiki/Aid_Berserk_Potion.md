# Aid Berserk Potion

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Alchemist
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 1
- **Prerequisites:** None
- **SP cost:** 10
- **Cast time:** 0.5 Seconds
- **Delay:** Unknown
- **Duration:** 15 Minutes
- **Target:** Level 85+ Player
- **Range:** Unknown
- **Catalyst:** 2 Berserk Potions


## Description

With the power of the [Alchemist Spirit](Alchemist_Spirit.md), the Alchemist is now capable of endowing the power of Berserk Potion onto other players including him/herself. The skill will consume 2 Berserk Potions and last 15 minutes (half the normal Berserk Potion duration), but can be used on any character, even ones that aren't originally capable of using Berserk Potion (i.e. [Hunter](Hunter.md), [Assassin](Assassin.md), [Super Novice](Super_Novice.md), etc.)

Possible bug: This skill can currently be used on anyone regardless of level and will consume Berserk Potion, however the [ASPD](ASPD.md) bonus will not be granted for characters below level 85.
