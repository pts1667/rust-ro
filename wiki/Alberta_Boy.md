# Alberta Boy

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** 70
- **Item requirement:** 1 Banana, 1 Red Potion, 1 Monster's Feed
- **Quests required:** [Friendship Quest](Friendship_Quest.md) and [Shadow Quest (Rekenber Job Quest)](Shadow_Quest_(Rekenber_Job_Quest).md)
- **Base EXP reward:** 285,500
- **Window:** yes

1.  In [Alberta](Alberta.md), go to the large house in the top middle of the map ([alberta](Alberta.md) 93, 201).
1.  Upstairs, go into the first room and talk to Iromo and his mother. She suggests asking Iromo's friend what some of Iromo's favorite foods are to cheer him up.
1.  Back outside, talk to the Little Boy ([alberta](Alberta.md) 45, 106). He asks you to buy him a Banana.
1.  Return to the Boy with a Banana. He eats it, then tells you Iromo liked Monster's Feed with Red Potion.
1.  With 1 Red Potion and 1 Monster's Feed in hand, go back to Iromo, who eats them. He then says he's afraid of the cat outside that walks on two legs (Wild Rose).
1.  Defeat a Wild Rose, and make sure the one in [Alberta](Alberta.md) is dead.
1.  Go back and talk to Iromo and his mother until the 24 hour waiting period starts (see quest window for the time).
1.  After a day has passed, talk to Iromu.
1.  **Quest appears to be uncompletable on iRO at the moment.**
