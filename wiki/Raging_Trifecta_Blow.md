# Raging Trifecta Blow

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Monk
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** 10 (Fixed)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Monk](Monk.md)) [Flee (Skill)](Flee_(Skill).md) Lv. 5
- **Delay:** 1.3 - [(AGI*0.004) - (DEX*0.002)] seconds
- **Target:** Enemy (None Needed)
- **Range:** Melee


## Description

In the same manner as the Thief's [Double Attack](Double_Attack.md) skill, Raging Trifecta Blow gives the user the chance to perform Raging Trifecta Blow when attacking normally (without the use of skills).

This is the first skill of the infamous "combo skills" group. The higher the level of Raging Trifecta Blow, the lower the chance of performing it. However, since higher levels of Raging Trifecta Blow deal more damage, level 10 Raging Trifecta Blow will deal more damage over time than level 1.

| Level | Perform Chance | +ATK |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 29% | 120% |
| 2 | 28% | 140% |
| 3 | 27% | 160% |
| 4 | 26% | 180% |
| 5 | 25% | 200% |
| 6 | 24% | 220% |
| 7 | 23% | 240% |
| 8 | 22% | 260% |
| 9 | 21% | 280% |
| 10 | 20% | 300% |
