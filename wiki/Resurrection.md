# Resurrection

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Priest
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 4 (Fixed)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Priest](Priest.md)) [Increase SP Recovery](Increase_SP_Recovery.md) Lv. 4, [Status Recovery](Status_Recovery.md) Lv. 1
- **SP cost:** 60
- **Cast time:** (8 - (Skill Level*2)) seconds
- **Delay:** (-1 + (Skill Level)) seconds
- **Target:** Monsters & Players (Except Self)
- **Catalyst:** 1 Blue Gemstone


## Description

Revive a dead character. When used on [Undead](Undead.md) property monsters (excluding ones that are also [Boss](Boss_Protocol.md) flagged), it has the same effect (and chance and cast delay) as [Turn Undead](Turn_Undead.md).

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cast Time | 6 sec | 4 sec | 2 sec | None |
| Skill Delay | None | 1 sec | 2 sec | 3 sec |
| Amount of HP upon Revival | 10% Max HP | 30% Max HP | 50% Max HP | 80% Max HP |
