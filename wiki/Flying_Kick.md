# Flying Kick

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** TaeKwon Kid
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 7
- **Prerequisites:** None
- **SP cost:** (80 - (Skill level*10))
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** Atkspd
- **Target:** Foe
- **Range:** 10
- **Formula name:** Stance
- **Formula value:** None or [Tumbling](Tumbling.md)


## Definition

This is the most unique of the [TaeKwon Kid's](TaeKwon_Kid.md) kicks, as the user doesn't necessarily have to be in a [stance](TaeKwon_Kid.md). Flying Kick allows the TaeKwon to [Snap](Snap.md) to the enemy when used. The damage depicted in the table below is the damage that occurs when the player selects the target.

| Level | SP Cost | Attack |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 70 | 40% |
| 2 | 60 | 50% |
| 3 | 50 | 60% |
| 4 | 40 | 70% |
| 5 | 30 | 80% |
| 6 | 20 | 90% |
| 7 | 10 | 100% |


## Information

*  Flying Kick can also be used while in the [Tumbling](Tumbling.md) status.  If used then, it will automatically attack the enemy who caused the [Tumbling](Tumbling.md) status, and damage will increase based on the users [Base Level](Levels.md).
*  Flying Kick's damage is greatly increased when performed in [Sprint](Sprint.md)
*  It can dispell positive statuses bestowed by [Soul Linker's](Soul_Linker.md) [Ka Skills](Soul_Linker.md) and [Spirit](Soul_Linker.md) statuses bestowed by [Soul Linker](Soul_Linker.md)s or the benefits of skills granted by the aforementioned [Spirit](Soul_Linker.md) statuses.
  * The dispelling effect works even if the attack misses the target.
*  [Stalker](Stalker.md)s can become immune to the dispelling effects of the kick by casting [Preserve](Preserve.md).
*  [Soul Linker](Soul_Linker.md)s are also innately immune to the dispelling effects.
*  [Soul Linker](Soul_Linker.md)s cannot use this skill.
*  Berserk Potions are dispelled by Flying Kick (but not concentration/awakening potions)
