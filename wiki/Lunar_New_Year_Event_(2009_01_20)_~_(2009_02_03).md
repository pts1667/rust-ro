# Lunar New Year Event (2009/01/20) ~ (2009/02/03)

> **Note:** Not implemented on iRO Classic.

<big>**This quest is repeatable.**</big>

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Item requirement:** 5 Token of Ox
- **Item reward:** Red Envelope ([EXP](Experience.md) or [Zeny](Zeny.md) version)

1.  Speak with Celebrant east of the [Prontera](Prontera.md) Fountain (prontera 187, 209).
1.  She asks you to collect as many Token of Ox as you can.
1.  You find "Avatar of the Ox" Monsters on all the maps (dungeons and fields), they drop the tokens. Tokens can also be [Stolen](Steal.md) from the Avatars. These monsters also give 500 base EXP when killed.
1.  You can trade in 5 Tokens for a "New Years Gift", the gift can be:
  * Red Envelope ([EXP](Experience.md) version)
1.   *  gives Base Level*100 base exp when used.
  * Red Envelope ([Zeny](Zeny.md) version)
1.   *  gives a random amount of zeny less than 10,000

    Note: Sometimes, the NPC gives 2 Red Envelopes instead of 1.
