# Detoxify

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Thief
- **Secondary class:** Super Novice
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 1
- **Prerequisites:** ([Thief](Thief.md) & [Super Novice](Super_Novice.md)) [Envenom](Envenom.md) Lv. 3
- **SP cost:** 10
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** [ASPD](ASPD.md) Based
- **Target:** Self, Players, Monsters


## Description

Cures the target of the [poisoned](Status_Effects.md) status ailment.

* Granted by Poporing Card
