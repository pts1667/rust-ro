# Dropped Knife Quest

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Item reward:** 2 Blue Potion

1. Talk to Han Ran Jiao (237,226) in [Kunlun](Kunlun.md) a.k.a Gonryun; who has lost his knife left to him by his ancestors.

2. Go to the bushes (245,257) in Kunlun/Gonryun field.
* Receive Knife \[3\]

3. You can either:
* Return the Knife
  * Receive 2 Blue Potion

* Refuse
  * No Reward
