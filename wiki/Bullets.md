# Bullets

Bullets are similar to [Arrows](Arrows.md) in that they're not an actual weapon, but can be equipped and are necessary to attack with any [Gun](Gun.md) weapons. Unlike [Arrows](Arrows.md), there is only a small variety of bullets, and they come in two types: Bullets and Spheres.


## Bullets

The basic bullets are usable with any gun with the exception of [Grenade Launchers](Gun.md) and can be purchased or exchanged from [NPCs](NPC.md) in the [Gunslinger](Gunslinger.md) guild located in Einbroch ([Einbroch](Einbroch.md) 136, 199). 500 Bullets can be stored in a Bullet Case similar to an Arrow Quiver.
* **Note**: You must be on a [Gunslinger](Gunslinger.md) for the [NPC](NPC.md) to make Bullet Cases for you.

| Name | [ATK](Stats.md) | [Weight](Weight_Limit.md) | Price | [Element](Element.md) | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bullet | 10 | 0.2 | 1 | [Neutral](Neutral.md) | - |
| Silver Bullet | 15 | 0.2 | 15 | [Holy](Holy.md) | - |
| Bloody Shell | 30 | 0.2 | 30 | [Neutral](Neutral.md) | [Bleeding](Status_Effects.md) by chance |


## Spheres

Spheres are a special type of bullet that may only be fired with [Grenade Launchers](Gun.md). They must be crafted by an [NPC](NPC.md) at the [Gunslinger](Gunslinger.md) guild, rather then bought, and come in five different [elements](Element.md). Spheres may also be put into Bullet Cases.

| Name | [ATK](Stats.md) | [Weight](Weight_Limit.md) | [Element](Element.md) | Effects | Items Required |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fire Sphere | 50 | 0.5 | [Fire](Fire.md) | - | 1 Phracon + 1 Emveretarcon + 2 Burning Hearts = 30 Fire Spheres |
| Wind Sphere | 50 | 0.5 | [Wind](Wind.md) | - | 1 Phracon + 1 Emveretarcon + 3 Cyfar = 30 Wind Spheres |
| Ice Sphere | 50 | 0.5 | [Water](Water.md) | - | 1 Phracon + 1 Emveretarcon + 2 Brigan = 30 Ice Spheres |
| Poison Sphere | 50 | 0.5 | [Poison](Poison.md) | [Poison](Poison.md) by chance | 1 Phracon + 1 Emveretarcon + 10 Venom Canine = 30 Poison Spheres |
| Blinding Sphere | 50 | 0.5 | [Shadow](Shadow.md) | [Blind](Status_Effects.md) by chance | 1 Phracon + 1 Emveretarcon + 5 Squid Ink = 30 Blinding Spheres |
