# NPC SLOWCAST

> **Npc skill** — name: Slow Cast; type: Multi-Target Status Attack; levels: 5; target: Self

Description: A wide area of effect skill that causes the status effect Slow Casting to all players within. The Slow Casting status effect lasts 30 seconds, and increases the time it takes to cast skills based on the skill level used.

| Skill Level | Area of Effect | Increase in Cast Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 5x5 | +20% |
| 2 | 11x11 | +40% |
| 3 | 17x17 | +60% |
| 4 | 23x23 | +80% |
| 5 | 29x29 | +100% |


## Obtainable via

* Tooth Blade Lv. 1 when attacking (Lv. 2 if the weapon upgrade is over +9)
