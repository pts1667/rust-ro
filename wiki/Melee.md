# Melee

Melee is a term used to describe characters who attack physically more often than magically. Classes which are generally termed "Melee" include:

*  [Novice](Novice.md)
*  [Swordman](Swordman.md)
*  [Thief](Thief.md)
*  [Merchant](Merchant.md)
*  [Archer](Archer.md)
*  [TaeKwon Kid](TaeKwon_Kid.md)

*  [Knight](Knight.md) and [Lord Knight](Lord_Knight.md)
*  [Crusader](Crusader.md) and [Paladin](Paladin.md)
*  [Assassin](Assassin.md) and [Assassin Cross](Assassin_Cross.md)
*  [Rogue](Rogue.md) and [Stalker](Stalker.md)
*  [Blacksmith](Blacksmith.md) and [Mastersmith](Mastersmith.md)
*  [Hunter](Hunter.md) and [Sniper](Sniper.md)
