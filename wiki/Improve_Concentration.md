# Improve Concentration

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Archer
- **Secondary class:** Super Novice
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 10 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Archer](Archer.md) & [Super Novice](Super_Novice.md)) [Vulture's Eye](Vulture's_Eye.md) Lv. 1
- **SP cost:** (20 + (Skill level*5))
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** None
- **Duration:** (40 + (Skill level*20)) seconds
- **Target:** Self


## Description

Improve Concentration increases the [DEX](Stats.md) and [AGI](Stats.md) of the caster, and will not be affected by DEX received from cards and buffs (bonus DEX received from Job bonuses, armor and [Owl's Eye](Owl's_Eye.md) are factored in, though). Improve Concentration also detects any hidden enemies within a 3x3 tile range of the caster.
