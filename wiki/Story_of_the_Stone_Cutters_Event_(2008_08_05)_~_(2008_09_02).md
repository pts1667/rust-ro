# Story of the Stone Cutters Event (2008/08/05) ~ (2008/09/02)

> **Note:** Not implemented on iRO Classic.

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** 20
- **Item requirement:** Varies (See Table)
- **Base EXP reward:** 1000*base level per tribute (20~59) 220,000 per tribute (60~85) 330,000 per tribute (85~98)
- **Job EXP reward:** 700*base level per tribute (20~59) 110,000 per tribute (60~85) 175,000 per tribute (85~98)
- **Item reward:** 5 Yggdrasil Seed per tribute (Level 99)
- **Quest reward:** +3 to all [Stats](Stats.md) for 10 minutes

1.  Talk to Stonecutter Olisa in [Prontera](Prontera.md) (prontera, 157, 195). She requests that tribute be paid at several statues.
1.  Speak to the following [NPC](NPC.md)s in order to pay tribute to the statue in their respective towns listed below. They will each request a set of items to make a Krathong, then direct the player to the following NPC.
1.  After creating the Krathong, click on the plaque next to the Stonecutter NPC and give the tribute. Talk to the Stonecutter NPC again in order to be allowed speak with the next Stonecutter NPC. Players must complete the tributes in the given order.
  * **Stonecutter NPC locations**
  * Stonecutter Andreas in [Prontera](Prontera.md) (149, 205).
  * Stonecutter Utama in [Louyang](Louyang.md) (218, 121).
  * Stonecutter Nicolette in the [Rachel](Rachel.md) Temple (119, 154).
  * Stonecutter Cain in [Veins](Veins.md) (197, 257), inside the building.
  * Stonecutter Yoshiro in Archer Village (141, 30).
  * Stonecutter Bridget in [Kunlun](Kunlun.md) (158, 125).
  * Stonecutter Samantha in [Ayothaya](Ayothaya.md) (150, 173).
1.  After speaking to all the Stonecutter NPCs mentioned above, Return to Olisa in Prontera.
1.  Players can now make Krathongs at any NPC and pay tribute for additional +3 stat buffs. (no extra EXP).
1.  After enough characters have paid tribute, the statues will start giving stat buffs to any characters nearby them anytime tribute is paid.
  * **Observed Buffs**
  * +10 [Str](Stats.md), +5 [Dex](Stats.md), Increased [Movement Speed](Movement_Speed.md) all lasting 30 min
  * +10 [Int](Stats.md), +6 [Vit](Stats.md), +4 [Luk](Stats.md), Increased [Movement Speed](Movement_Speed.md) all lasting 30 min
  * +7 [Str](Stats.md), +7 [Dex](Stats.md), Increased [Movement Speed](Movement_Speed.md) all lasting 30 min
  * +6 to all stats lasting 30 min
