# Mage Skill Quest


## Energy Coat Quest

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Job level requirement:** 35
- **Class requirement:** Mage
- **Item requirement:** 3 Glass Beads, 1 1 Carat Diamond, 5 Shells, 1 Solid Shell
- **Quest reward:** Ability to use [Energy Coat](Energy_Coat.md)

1. Talk to the Great Wizard in the [Geffen](Geffen.md) Mage Guild. He'll require the following items in order to teach you Energy Coat: 3 Glass Beads, 1 1 Carat Diamond, 5 Shells, 1 Solid Shell.

* Glass Beads can be purchased from the [Prontera](Prontera.md) Gift Merchant (prontera 105, 87), [Lighthalzen](Lighthalzen.md) Gift Merchant, or Lutie Gift Merchant for 1,400 [Zeny](Zeny.md) each.
* 1 Carat Diamonds can be purchased from the [Morroc Ruins](Morroc.md) Jeweler (moc_ruins 52, 85) or the [Lighthalzen](Lighthalzen.md) Jeweler for 10,000 Zeny each.
