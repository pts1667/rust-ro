# Upgrade Weapon

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Mastersmith
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 10
- **Prerequisites:** ([Blacksmith](Blacksmith.md)) [Weaponry Research](Weaponry_Research.md) Lv. 10
- **SP cost:** 30
- **Target:** Self
- **Catalyst:** Upgrade Ore


## Description

Success rates are based on the player's [Job Level](Levels.md). Use of this skill with a Job Level above Job Level 50 gives the player an increased chance of upgrade success; up to a maximum added chance of 10% at Job Level 70 over the rate of upgrading weapons versus the [NPC](NPC.md). The desired weapon does **not** have to be equipped by the Mastersmith in order to be upgraded via this skill, and must *not* be in order for it to appear on the selection menu.
