# NPC SUMMONMONSTER


## Description

*  Supportive skill
*  Levels: 1

Creates monsters. These monsters act independently of their summoner, however; they will not die if the summoner does, nor do they respond to [Call Slave](NPC_CALLSLAVE.md) or Teleport when their summoner does. They essentially behave as regular monsters.

However, the monsters' movement speed is equivalent to their summoner's movement speed at the time of summoning. The exception to this is Amon Ra, who does not normally move at all.
