# Tumbling

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** TaeKwon Kid
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 1
- **Prerequisites:** ([TaeKwon Kid](TaeKwon_Kid.md)) [Flying Kick](Flying_Kick.md) Lv. 7
- **Cast time:** Instant
- **Delay:** ?
- **SP cost:** 1
- **Target:** Self


## Description

Enable a 20% chance of dodging long ranged physical and magic (both [AoE](Area_of_Effect.md) and target spells) attacks. When caster is in Spurt status from performing Lv. 7 or higher [Sprint](Sprint.md), **Tumbling** will then dodge melee attacks.  [Flying Kick](Flying_Kick.md) can be used to automatically counter the attacking enemy after the dodge. The damage of the counter [Flying Kick](Flying_Kick.md) is increased by base level and is naturally stronger than a manually casted [Flying Kick](Flying_Kick.md).


## Notes

*  This is the only stance that can be used by [Soul Linker](Soul_Linker.md)s.
*  The 20% chance to dodge an attack is checked after the attack actually "hits", but before it has any effect. Even otherwise unavoidable attacks like crits and spells can be dodged.
*  Bolt-type spells and other attacks that deal all their damage at the same time ([Thunderstorm](Thunderstorm.md), [Sonic Blow](Sonic_Blow.md) and the like), if tumbled from, deal no damage whatsoever. Attacks that spread their damage over several separate hits ([Storm Gust](Storm_Gust.md), [Meteor Storm](Meteor_Storm.md)) are dodged one hit at a time.
*  There is a slight delay before you can do anything or even move when dodging an attack. This may be a disadvantage if you're getting mobbed.
