# Scribble

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Rogue
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 1
- **Prerequisites:** ([Rogue](Rogue.md)) [Piece](Piece.md) Lv. 5
- **SP cost:** 15
- **Target:** Ground
- **Range:** Melee
- **Area:** 5x5
- **Catalyst:** Red Gemstone


## Description

Paint a graffiti message with a 20 character limit in a 5*5 cell targeted area.
