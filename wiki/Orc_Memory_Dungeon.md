# Orc Memory Dungeon

> **Repeatable** — cooldown: two (2) hour

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** 30 ~ 80
- **Party requirement:** 2+ members
- **Quest reward:** Private Orc Hero Battle
- **Window:** Yes
- **Cat:** no

1.  With 2 or more people in your [Party](Party.md), the party leader needs speak with *Dimensional Gorge Piece* (gef_fild10 242, 202) to "Reserve the Orc's Memory".
1.  After the dungeon is created, speak to the *Dimensional Gorge Piece* to "Enter the Dungeon".
  * **Note:** A window will appear showing pertinent time limits for the dungeon. If it is closed, it can be opened again with with the shortcut Alt+B.
  * **Note 2**: **There is a 2 hour time limit for completing the quest before the instance is destroyed.**
1.  Once inside, the party leader must speak to Kruger (1@orcs 180,29) to start the instance.

1.   ![Enchanted Orcs shown in blue](images/1%40orcs.png)

1.  To pass each zone on the first map, you must kill the *Enchanted Orc* in that zone (Enchanted Orcs are identical to Orc Warriors). Kruger explains that each non-enchanted orc you kill will cause a High Orc to spawn near the exit.
  * Depending on the challenge you wish to face, you may kill only the Enchanted Orc, or instead kill everything. If enough Orcs are killed, a large mob of Stalatic Golem will spawn at the end of the 4th zone.
1.  The first *Enchanted Orc* spawns at (142, 87). Killing it opens a portal at (168, 125).
1.  The second *Enchanted Orc* spawns near (106, 108). Killing it opens a portal at (89, 94).
1.  The third *Enchanted Orc* spawns at (32, 44). Killing it opens a portal at (38, 105).
1.  The fourth *Enchanted Orc* spawns near (24, 178). Killing it opens a portal at (21, 189). All monsters on 1@orcs will de-spawn as soon as the last *Enchanted Orc* dies.
1.  On the second map, speak to Kruger again (2@orcs 35, 169). He explains that you will need to extinguish the Braziers to proceed to the next zone.

1.   ![braziers in yellow, "bosses" in blue](images/2%40orcs.png)

  * As before, depending on the challenge you wish to face, you may kill only the specific guards, or instead kill everything. If enough Orcs are killed, a large mob of Wraiths will spawn at the end of the 4th zone.
  * Each Orc Zombie killed will respawn as an Orc Archer.
  * Each Orc Skeleton killed will respawn as a High Orc.
1.  The Braziers in the first zone are at (27, 164), (55, 154), (108, 145), and (99, 179). After being activated in order, *The Chief Orc of Safeguards* will spawn at (110, 156). Killing him will open a portal at (48, 100).
1.  The Braziers in the second zone are at (35, 92), (33, 70), (71, 30), and (84, 50). After being activated in order, *Orc Sniper* will spawn at (67, 64). Killing him will open a portal at (101, 55).
1.  The Braziers in the third zone are at (143, 144), (162, 134), (145, 116), and (137, 98). After being activated in order, *Depraved Vengeful Orc Spirit* will spawn at (156, 147). Killing him will open a portal at (167, 104).
1.  After the *Depraved Vengeful Orc Spirit* dies, a *Depraved Orc Hero* and *Shaman Cargalache* will spawn in the 4th zone.
1.  When *Shaman Cargalache* dies, all monsters on 2@orcs will de-spawn (except the Anophles and Orc Hero).
  * **Note**: If *Shaman Cargalache* dies at the same moment as another Orc, a bug may prevent the de-spawn.
