# Crowd Control Shot

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Gunslinger
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 10
- **Prerequisites:** ([Gunslinger](Gunslinger.md)) [Single Action](Single_Action.md) Lv. 5
- **Target:** Foe
- **Range:** Melee
- **Knockback:** 5 cells
- **Weapon:** Shotgun


## Description

Fire a close range shot that will push an enemy 5 cells backward. Cannot be blocked by [Pneuma](Pneuma.md). Skill range is 3 cells, not affected by [Snake Eyes](Snake_Eyes.md) skill.
