# Solar Blessings

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** TaeKwon Master
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** ([TaeKwon Master](TaeKwon_Master.md)) [Solar, Lunar and Stellar Perception](Solar,_Lunar_and_Stellar_Perception.md) Lv. 1, [Solar, Lunar and Stellar Opposition](Solar,_Lunar_and_Stellar_Opposition.md) Lv. 1


## Description

When killing a *Target of the Sun* on a *Solar Day*, the player gains additional experience.
A *Solar Day* is a day where the date is an even number.

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bonus Exp (%) | 10 | 20 | 30 | 40 | 50 |
