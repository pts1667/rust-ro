# Archer Job Change Guide

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Job level requirement:** 10
- **Item requirement:** Varies
- **Item reward:** [Bow \[4\]](Bow.md) ,[Arrows](Arrows.md)
- **Quest reward:** Jobchange to [Archer](Archer.md)
- **Cat:** no

1. Head to the [Archer](Archer.md) Guild (pay arche 145, 164) in the Archer Village north of [Payon](Payon.md).

2. Talk to the Archer Guildsman (payon_in02 64,71). You will be told to collect different type of Trunks for a minimum of 25 points. There are 4 types of Trunks:

| Trunk Type | Point Value |
| --- | --- |
| Trunk | 1 Point |
| Barren Trunk | 2 Points |
| Solid Trunk | 3 Points |
| Fine-grained Trunk | 5 Points |

* **Note**: Trunks can be obtained by the monster Willow, found near Payon on some maps (a good start is one east of Payon). Beware of Elder Willows, as they look similar but are strong at this point. Time can be saved by leveling off of Willow from 10/8 to 12/10 or so because the Trunks are needed anyway.

3. After enough Trunks are brought back, the player will have their job changed to an [Archer](Archer.md).
