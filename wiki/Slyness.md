# Slyness

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Rogue
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** 1
- **Prerequisites:** ([Rogue](Rogue.md)) [Divest Shield](Divest_Shield.md) Lv. 3


## Description

When two or more Rogues sit beside each other, if at least one of them has Slyness, monsters will not attack them. However, Slyness is ineffective against [Boss](Boss_Protocol.md) monsters, and the characters will still be hit by any [AoE](Area_of_Effect.md) skills.
