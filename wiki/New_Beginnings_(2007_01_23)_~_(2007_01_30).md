# New Beginnings (2007/01/23) ~ (2007/01/30)

> **Note:** Not implemented on iRO Classic.

**Item Requirements: 40 Milk, 20 Honey, 3 Empty Bottle, 1 Alcohol, 10 Brown Root, 20 Clover, 3 Mastela Fruit, 3 Grape Juice, 10 Meat, 30 Mushroom Spore, 10 Piece of Cake**

**Quest Rewards: Savage Bebe Egg + A) 1 of each +6 Stat Food B) Level weighed EXP: Lv.81-90: 650k; Lv.91-99 1.5m + Job EXP**

1. Go to [Alberta](Alberta.md), and talk to Marilyn (106,79). Agree to help her with Donovan.

2. Visit Donovan in [Payon](Payon.md), inside a building to the bottom right.

3. Going back to Marilyn in Alberta and she will ask the player to bring her **30 Milk, 15 Honey and 3 Empty Bottle to brew 3 Sweet Milk**.

4. Acquire a Savage Bebe Egg.

5. Back at Donovan, he wants to lynch the player for the false Bebe.

6. Marilyn will suggest to cook a dish for Donovan in order to calm him down. She will require **1 Alcohol, 10 Brown Root, 20 Clover, 3 Mastela Fruit, 3 Grape Juice, 10 Meat, 10 Milk, 30 Mushroom Spore, 10 Piece of Cake, 5 Honey**.

7. Giving her the items she will cook one of each +6 stat food.

8. **Ending A)** Keep the stat foods.

9. **Ending B)** Give the stat foods to Donovan and then return to Marilyn for weighed exp.
