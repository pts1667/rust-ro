# Summon Flora

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Alchemist
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** ([Alchemist](Alchemist.md)) [Prepare Potion](Prepare_Potion.md) Lv. 6
- **SP cost:** 20
- **Target:** Ground


## Description

Summon a plant monster to a targeted cell. The plant monster will disappear after the skill's duration. Skill level affects the type of plant monster that is summoned, as well as the monster's [Max HP](Max_HP.md). The caster can only summon monsters of the same type at once. At lower skill levels more monsters can be summoned at one time than at higher skill levels. Skill level 5 can only summon one monster.

| Skill Level | Monster Type | Maximum Number of Summonable Monsters | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Mandragora | 5 | 300 seconds |
| 2 | Hydra | 4 | 240 seconds |
| 3 | Flora | 3 | 180 seconds |
| 4 | Parasite | 2 | 120 seconds |
| 5 | Geographer | 1 | 60 seconds |


## Notes

* Monsters will not attack these plants.
* These plants will not attack players in PvM.
* These plants can be attacked by players, so it is generally not a good idea to use them in highly bot-infested maps.
