# Back Slide

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Thief
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 1
- **Quest:** Thief Skill Quest#Back Slide Quest
- **SP cost:** 7
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** [ASPD](ASPD.md) Based
- **Target:** Self
- **Knockback:** 5 cells


## Description

Back Slide will instantly move the character 5 cells opposite of the direction that the character is facing. It cannot be used during [WoE](War_of_Emperium.md) due to its knock-back nature.
