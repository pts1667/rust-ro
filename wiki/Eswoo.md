# Eswoo

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Soul Linker
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 7
- **Prerequisites:** ([Soul Linker](Soul_Linker.md)) [Priest Spirit](Priest_Spirit.md) Lv. 1
- **SP cost:** 85 - (Skill Level * 10)
- **Cast time:** 0.1 second
- **Delay:** 0.5 second
- **Duration:** (Skill Level) seconds
- **Target:** Monster


## Description

Temporarily shrink a monster to reduce its [Movement Speed](Movement_Speed.md). [Boss](Boss_Protocol.md) monsters will only be shrunk for 1/5th of the normal skill duration. If this skill is cast on a monster that is already shrunken, the caster will be inflicted by the [Stun](Status_Effects.md) status and the targeted monster will recover from Shrunken status more quickly.
