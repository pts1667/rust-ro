# Spear Quicken

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Crusader
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 10
- **Prerequisites:** ([Crusader](Crusader.md)) [Spear Mastery](Spear_Mastery.md) Lv. 10
- **SP cost:** (20 + (Skill Level*4))
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** N/A
- **Duration:** (Skill Level*30) seconds
- **Target:** Self
- **Weapon:** Two-Handed Spear


## Description

Temporarily increase [Attack Speed](ASPD.md) when equipped with a Two-Handed Spear for the skill's duration.

| Skill Level | Duration | % Increment | SP Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 30 sec | 21% | 24 |
| 2 | 60 sec | 22% | 28 |
| 3 | 90 sec | 23% | 32 |
| 4 | 120 sec | 24% | 36 |
| 5 | 150 sec | 25% | 40 |
| 6 | 180 sec | 26% | 44 |
| 7 | 210 sec | 27% | 48 |
| 8 | 240 sec | 28% | 52 |
| 9 | 270 sec | 29% | 56 |
| 10 | 300 sec | 30% | 60 |
