# GR

**GR** is an acronym and can stand for:

*  Ghostring, a [Boss](Boss_Protocol.md) type monster (but not an [MVP](MVP.md)).
*  The card of said monster, which grants [Ghost](Ghost.md) property to the wearer's armor.
*  Guild Recruiting, usually used in instances such as GR>lvl90+/WoE, which means guild recruiting level 90+ characters for guild siege.
