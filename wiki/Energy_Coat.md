# Energy Coat

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Mage
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 1
- **Quest:** Mage Skill Quest#Energy Coat Quest
- **SP cost:** 30
- **Cast time:** 5 seconds
- **Delay:** None
- **Duration:** 300 seconds
- **Target:** Self


## Description

Create a magical shield around the caster to reduce damage inflicted by physical attacks. This skill will last for 5 minutes or until player's SP reaches 0.

| SP Remaining | 81% ~ 100% | 61% ~ 80% | 41% ~ 60% | 21% ~ 40% | 1% ~ 20% |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Damage Reduction | 30% | 24% | 18% | 12% | 6% |
| SP Used | 3% | 2.5% | 2% | 1.5% | 1% |
