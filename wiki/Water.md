# Water

Elemental property. Opposite of the [Wind](Wind.md) property. Water does the most damage to [Fire](Fire.md) property monsters/armors, and is weakest to the Wind property. Weapons can be forged with the Water property using a Mystic Frozen.

## Cards

Cards which concern the Water property include:


## Head Gear

* Leaf Cat Card: Add the chance of gaining 'Crystal Blue' item each time a Fish or Shellfish monster is killed. Add 10% more tolerance against Water Property Attack.


## Armor

* Marc Card: Gain protection from the [Frozen](Status_Effects.md) status and increase resistance to Water property by 5%.
* Swordfish Card: Enchants Armor with the Water Property, [DEF](DEF.md) +1.


## Weaponry

* Crab Card: Inflict 30% more damage on Aster. ATK +5. If items compounded to the Crab, Shell Fish and Aster cards are all equipped, this card combination will increase damage on Water Property monsters by 30% and will add a 30% chance of gaining the Raw Fish item each time a Fish monster is killed.
* Drainliar Card: Increase damage on Water property monsters by 20%.


## Garment

* Marse Card: Increases Water resistance by 30%, [Flee](Stats.md) +5
* Roween Card: Increases physical damage versus Water property monsters by 10%. [Flee](Stats.md) + 5. [Perfect Dodge](Perfect_Dodge.md) + 3. Adds CRIT + 15 when fighting [Fish](Fish.md) race monsters.


## Accessory

* Galion Card: Increase damage on Water property monsters by 5%. [Hit](Stats.md) +5.
