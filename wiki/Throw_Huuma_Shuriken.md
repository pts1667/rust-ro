# Throw Huuma Shuriken

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Ninja
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** ([Ninja](Ninja.md)) [Dagger Throwing Practice](Dagger_Throwing_Practice.md) Lv. 5, [Throw Kunai](Throw_Kunai.md) Lv. 5
- **SP cost:** 15 + 5 * Skill Level
- **Cast time:** 3 seconds
- **Delay:** 3 seconds
- **Target:** Foe
- **Range:** 9 cells
- **Area:** 3x3
- **Weapon:** Huuma Shuriken


## Description

Throw a Huuma Shuriken that will damage its target and any nearby enemies. The total amount of damage is divided among the enemies damaged by this skill. Raising this skill's level increases its number of strikes. User must also equip Huuma Class Weapon.

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Damage | 300% | 450% | 600% | 750% | 900% |
| SP Cost | 20 | 25 | 30 | 35 | 40 |


## Notes

*  This skill's damage **IS** affected by % damage cards.
*  You can switch weapons during the cast time of this skill.
