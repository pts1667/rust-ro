# Shuriken


## Shuriken

Used for the skill [Throw Shuriken](Throw_Shuriken.md). The bonus damage added by the skills [Dagger Throwing Practice](Dagger_Throwing_Practice.md) and [Throw Shuriken](Throw_Shuriken.md) act like mastery bonuses and therefore bypass normal defense.  It will also hit, even if the Ninja would normally miss its target.
