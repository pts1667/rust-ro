# Swordman Skill Quest


## HP Recovery While Moving Quest

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Job level requirement:** 35
- **Class requirement:** Swordman
- **Item requirement:** 200 Empty Bottles, 1 Moth Wing
- **Items required:** 1 Armor
- **Quest reward:** Ability to use [HP Recovery While Moving](HP_Recovery_While_Moving.md)

1. Talk to Knight De Thomas inside the house in [Izlude](Izlude.md) (214,129). He'll require the following items to teach you [HP Recovery While Moving](HP_Recovery_While_Moving.md):

| Column 1 | Column 2 |
| --- | --- |
| Items | 200 Empty Bottles An Equippable Armor 1 Moth Wing |


## Fatal Blow Quest

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Job level requirement:** 30
- **Class requirement:** Swordman
- **Item requirement:** Banana Juice, 10 Fire Arrows, 10 Silver Arrows, 30 Tentacles, 5 Royal Jelly
- **Quest reward:** Ability to use [Fatal Blow](Fatal_Blow.md)

1. Talk to Leon Von Frich (prt_in 75,88) inside the Prontera Chivalry (top left building in [Prontera](Prontera.md)), and give him the following items to learn [Fatal Blow](Fatal_Blow.md):

| Column 1 | Column 2 |
| --- | --- |
| Items | 1 Banana Juice 10 Fire Arrows 10 Silver Arrows 30 Tentacles 5 Royal Jelly |


## Berserk Quest

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Job level requirement:** 30
- **Class requirement:** Swordman
- **Item requirement:** 10 Decayed Nails, 10 Honey, 10 Horrendous Mouths, 35 Powder of Butterfly
- **Quest reward:** Ability to use [Berserk](Berserk.md)

1. Talk to Juan inside the [Prontera](Prontera.md) Upgrade Shop (178, 185) and give him the following items to learn [Berserk](Berserk.md):

| Column 1 | Column 2 |
| --- | --- |
| Items | 10 Decayed Nails 10 Honey 10 Horrendous Mouths 35 Powder of Butterfly |
