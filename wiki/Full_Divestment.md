# Full Divestment

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Stalker
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** ([Rogue](Rogue.md)) [Divest Weapon](Divest_Weapon.md) Lv. 5
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** 1 second
- **Duration:** 135 seconds +/-
- **Target:** Foe
- **Range:** Melee
- **Status:** [Divest](Status_Effects.md)


## Description

Simultaneously casts [Divest Helm](Divest_Helm.md), [Divest Armor](Divest_Armor.md), [Divest Shield](Divest_Shield.md) and [Divest Weapon](Divest_Weapon.md). This skill is cast instantly with a 1 second Skill Delay.

Skill fails when used on a player with [Full Chemical Protection](Full_Chemical_Protection.md).

Can be used on [Boss](Boss_Protocol.md) type monsters.

Only shows animation on success.

[DEX](Stats.md) difference between the caster and the target affects on the success chance.  If the success rate drops below your base success chance, the base success chance will be used instead.  The success rate is believed to be:

Success Rate = 5 + 2 * Skill Level + (Your DEX - Enemy DEX) / 5

Duration = 135 seconds +/- 1 second per 2 difference in DEX between caster and target

| Skill level | Base success chance (%) | SP consumption |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 7 | 22 |
| 2 | 9 | 24 |
| 3 | 11 | 26 |
| 4 | 13 | 28 |
| 5 | 15 | 30 |
