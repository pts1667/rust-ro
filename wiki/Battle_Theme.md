# Battle Theme

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Bard
- **Secondary class:** Dancer
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** ([Dancer](Dancer.md)) [Gypsy's Kiss](Gypsy's_Kiss.md) Lv. 10; ([Bard](Bard.md)) [Song of Lutie](Song_of_Lutie.md) Lv. 10
- **SP cost:** Cast: (35 + (Skill level*5)) Use: 1 SP / 5 seconds
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** None
- **Duration:** 60 seconds
- **Target:** Self
- **Area:** 9x9
- **Weapon:** Instrument
- **Weapon2:** Whip


## Description

Party members within this skill's area will have increased [ATK](Stats.md) and [DEF](DEF.md) for the skill's duration. In addition to the initial SP cost, Battle Theme will consume 1 SP every 5 seconds while it is active.

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ATK Increment | +50 | +75 | +100 | +125 | +150 |
| DEF Increment | +4 | +6 | +8 | +10 | +12 |
| Initial SP Cost | 40 | 45 | 50 | 55 | 60 |
