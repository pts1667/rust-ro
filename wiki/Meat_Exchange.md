# Meat Exchange

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Item requirement:** 1 Meat
- **Quests required:** [Language Quest](Language_Quest.md)
- **Item reward:** 2 Jellopy, 2 Fluff, 2 Clover

1. Go to [Umbala](Umbala.md) and talk to the Utan Kid (umbala 59, 243).

2. The kid will ask for meat.  Give him 1 Meat and you will be rewarded with 2 Jellopy, 2 Fluff, and 2 Clover in exchange.
