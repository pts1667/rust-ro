# Kaizel

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Soul Linker
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 7 (Fixed)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Soul Linker](Soul_Linker.md)) [Priest Spirit](Priest_Spirit.md) Lv. 1
- **SP cost:** 120 - (Skill Level*10)
- **Cast time:** 5 - (Skill Level*.5) seconds
- **Duration:** 30 minutes
- **Target:** [Soul Linker](Soul_Linker.md)s, Spouse, Child


## Description

Instantly revive a fallen character that will be in [Kyrie Eleison](Kyrie_Eleison.md) status for 3 Seconds upon revival. This revival status lasts for 30 minutes and is canceled after the target gets resurrected. This skill is disabled during WoE (Guild War). Cast Time is **not affected** by [DEX](Stats.md).
