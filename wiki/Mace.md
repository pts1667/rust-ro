# Mace

**Maces** are a blunt weapon type usable by [Acolyte](Acolyte.md), [Merchant](Merchant.md) and [Swordman](Swordman.md) classes. All maces are naturally unbreakable except in upgrade attempts.

| Size | Damage |
| --- | --- |
| Small | 75% |
| Medium | 100% |
| Large | 100% |
