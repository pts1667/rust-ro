# Kaina

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Soul Linker
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** 7
- **Prerequisites:** ([TaeKwon Kid](TaeKwon_Kid.md)) [Happy Break](Happy_Break.md) Lv. 1


## Description

Increase Max SP and enhance the efficiency of the [Happy Break](Happy_Break.md) skill. At Base Level 70, this skill reduces the SP consumption of the [Estin](Estin.md), [Estun](Estun.md) and [Esma](Esma.md) skills by 3% in proportion to the skill level of Kaina. At Base Level 80, this SP consumption is reduced by 5% and at Level 90, it is reduced by 7% (for a total of 49% reduction).

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Happy Break Effect Increase (%) | 40 | 50 | 60 | 70 | 80 | 90 | 100 |
| +Max SP | 30 | 60 | 90 | 120 | 150 | 180 | 210 |


## Bugs

*  You can't cast skill with reduced SP cost when you have less than **full SP cost for skill** SP available. *(euRo)*
