# Song of Lutie

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Bard
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 10 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Bard](Bard.md)) [Unchained Serenade](Unchained_Serenade.md) Lv.3
- **SP cost:** Cast: (35+Skill Level*5) Use: 1 SP / 6 seconds
- **Cast time:** None
- **Duration:** 180 seconds
- **Target:** Self
- **Area:** 9x9
- **Weapon:** Instrument
- **Weapon2:** Whip


## Description

Cast a 9*9 cell area around the caster that will increase the Max HP of characters within its range. When cast, Song of Lutie immediately restores HP to characters within range and continues to restore HP every 6 seconds, giving a total of 30 heals to characters during its 180 second duration. The [Max HP](Max_HP.md) enhancement and the amount of HP restored by this skill is affected by the caster`s [VIT](Stats.md) and [Music Lessons](Music_Lessons.md) skill level. While active, Song of Lutie consumes 1 SP every 6 seconds.

## Soul Link

The [Bard and Dancer Spirits](Bard_and_Dancer_Spirits.md) enables use of Song of Lutie 10 when used on a [Dancer](Dancer.md) who has learned level 10 [Gypsy's Kiss](Gypsy's_Kiss.md).
