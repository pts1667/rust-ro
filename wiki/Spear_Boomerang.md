# Spear Boomerang

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Knight
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 5
- **SP cost:** 10
- **Prerequisites:** ([Knight](Knight.md)) [Pierce](Pierce.md) Lv. 3
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** 1 second
- **Target:** Enemy
- **Range:** (3 + 2 * Skill Level) cells
- **Weapon:** Spear


## Description

Must be wielding a Spear weapon. Throws the equipped spear at a single target (the spear does not require retrieval).

* Has an [ATK](Stats.md) bonus of 50% per skill level.
