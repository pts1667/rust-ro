# Weaponry Research

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Blacksmith
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** 10
- **Prerequisites:** ([Blacksmith](Blacksmith.md)) [Hilt Binding](Hilt_Binding.md) Lv. 1


## Description

Enhance [HIT](Stats.md)/[ATK](Stats.md) of the [Blacksmith](Blacksmith.md) and increase the chance of successfully [Forging](Forging.md) a weapon. The benefits of Weaponry Research are applied to all weapons that are equipped and all weapon creation attempts.

* The ATK bonus works like all other [Weapon Masteries](Weapon_Mastery.md), except that it is never-miss damage.

* Despite popular belief, this skill does **not** increase the rate of success on upgrades performed past the safe limit at upgrading NPCs.

| Level | +[HIT](Stats.md)/[ATK](Stats.md) | + Forging Success Rate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 2 | 1% |
| 2 | 4 | 2% |
| 3 | 6 | 3% |
| 4 | 8 | 4% |
| 5 | 10 | 5% |
| 6 | 12 | 6% |
| 7 | 14 | 7% |
| 8 | 16 | 8% |
| 9 | 18 | 9% |
| 10 | 20 | 10% |
