# Sandman

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Hunter
- **Secondary class:** Mercenary
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** ([Hunter](Hunter.md)) [Flasher](Flasher.md) Lv. 1
- **SP cost:** 12
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** [ASPD](ASPD.md) Based
- **Duration:** (180-Skill Level*30) seconds
- **Target:** Ground
- **Range:** Melee
- **Area:** 5x5
- **Status:** [Sleep](Status_Effects.md)
- **Catalyst:** 1 Trap


## Description

Set a trap that may inflict the [Sleep](Status_Effects.md) status on enemies within a 5*5 area once triggered. Sleeping enemies will not awaken unless they are hit by an attack. Sandman has no effect on [Boss](Boss_Protocol.md) monsters.

| Skill Level | Trap Duration | Sleep Chance (%) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 150 sec | 50 |
| 2 | 120 sec | 60 |
| 3 | 90 sec | 70 |
| 4 | 60 sec | 80 |
| 5 | 30 sec | 90 |
