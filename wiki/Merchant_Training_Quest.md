# Merchant Training Quest

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Job level requirement:** None (at least 22 to finish)
- **Class requirement:** Merchant
- **Skill requirement:** None ([Enlarge Weight Limit](Enlarge_Weight_Limit.md) 4, [Discount](Discount.md) 4, [Overcharge](Overcharge.md) 4, [Pushcart](Pushcart.md) 4, [Vending](Vending.md) 4 to finish)
- **Zeny requirement:** 3,900
- **Base EXP reward:** 6,690
- **Job EXP reward:** 1,732
- **Item reward:** Battle Axe \[3\], 10 Red Potions

1. After you job change to [Merchant](Merchant.md), talk to the man named Guarnien close to the Merchant job change [NPC](NPC.md).

2. If you accept his offer to train you, he'll require you to learn [Enlarge Weight Limit](Enlarge_Weight_Limit.md) Level 4 before he speaks with you again.

3. Return once you've gotten the skill, and Guarnien will talk to you about [Item Appraisal](Item_Appraisal.md), [Mammonite](Mammonite.md), and [Discount](Discount.md) after giving you one Battle Axe \[3\].

4. Guarnien then asks you to learn [Discount](Discount.md) Level 4.

5. After learning the skill, come back to Guarnien, and he'll give you 830 [Base EXP](Base_EXP_Chart.md) and 116 [Job EXP](Job_EXP_Chart.md).

6. Talk to him again, and he'll ask for the cheapest 10 Red Potions from a vendor in [Prontera](Prontera.md), and he'll proceed to warp you there.

*  Vendors
  * Jayon at 246,131
  * Aigie at 168, 13 (Inside the pub)
  * Sagle at 64,111
  * Maos at 251, 129 (Inside left tower by Inn Keeper)
  * Kellion at 90, 330

Talk to them, and to get the best price you'll have to "cancel" in the dialogue box before getting the best price. (390 from Aigie or Sagle works; if Aigie offers potions at 340 [Zeny](Zeny.md), try the others. 340 [Zeny](Zeny.md) is Novice Potions). Be careful not to mistake Novice Potions for Red Potions as they may give you those instead!

7. Bring him the 10 Red Potions, and he'll give you 1,120 [Base EXP](Base_EXP_Chart.md) and 220 [Job EXP](Job_EXP_Chart.md).

8. Talk to Guarnien again, and he'll talk about [Overcharge](Overcharge.md) before asking you to learn Level 4.

9. After learning the skill, come back and talk to him, and he'll give you 1,260 [Base EXP](Base_EXP_Chart.md) and 272 [Job EXP](Job_EXP_Chart.md).

10. Talk to him again, and he'll talk about [Pushcart](Pushcart.md) and ask you to learn Level 4.

11. Return after learning the skill, and he'll give you 1,620 [Base EXP](Base_EXP_Chart.md) and 520 [Job EXP](Job_EXP_Chart.md).

12. Talk to him again, and he'll talk about [Vending](Vending.md) and ask you to learn level 4.

13. After learning the skill, talk to him again and he'll give 1,860 [Base EXP](Base_EXP_Chart.md) and 604 [Job EXP](Job_EXP_Chart.md).
