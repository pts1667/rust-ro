# Guild Extension

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Guild
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** 10
- **Prerequisites:** None


## Description

Increase the maximum capacity of guildsmen that can be accepted by the guild at once. Adds capacity by 4 every level and holds 56 guildsmen (including Guild Master) by level 10.

* Note: The "Capacity" column is not cumulative.  At level 10, the skill adds 40 members TOTAL, **not** 40 + 36 + 32, etc.

| Level | Guild Capacity |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | +4 |
| 2 | +8 |
| 3 | +12 |
| 4 | +16 |
| 5 | +20 |
| 6 | +24 |
| 7 | +28 |
| 8 | +32 |
| 9 | +36 |
| 10 | +40 |
