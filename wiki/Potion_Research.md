# Potion Research

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Alchemist
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** 10
- **Prerequisites:** None


## Description

* Increase effectiveness of consumed potions. (much like [Increase HP Recovery](Increase_HP_Recovery.md) for [Swordman](Swordman.md) class)
* Increase effectiveness of potions thrown using [Aid Potion](Aid_Potion.md) and [Aid Condensed Potion](Aid_Condensed_Potion.md).
* Increase potion creation success rate.

| Skill Level | Additional Restorative Value | Additional Potion Creation Success Rate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 5% | 1% |
| 2 | 10% | 2% |
| 3 | 15% | 3% |
| 4 | 20% | 4% |
| 5 | 25% | 5% |
| 6 | 30% | 6% |
| 7 | 35% | 7% |
| 8 | 40% | 8% |
| 9 | 45% | 9% |
| 10 | 50% | 10% |
