# Coin Fling

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Gunslinger
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 1
- **Prerequisites:** ([Gunslinger](Gunslinger.md)) [Coin Flip](Coin_Flip.md) Lv. 1
- **Target:** Foe
- **Range:** Ranged
- **Catalyst:** 1-5 [Coins](Coin_Flip.md)


## Description

Uses the coin you have to attack and reduce enemy's [DEF](DEF.md) at the same time. It uses up to 5 coins at once, and the more coins you use, the greater the reduction of enemy DEF. Also, it inflicts [Job Level](Levels.md) based amount of damage to skill's target.
