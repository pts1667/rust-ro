# Slow Poison

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Priest
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 4
- **Prerequisites:** ([Priest](Priest.md)) [Status Recovery](Status_Recovery.md) Lv. 1
- **Target:** Players (Except Self)


## Description

Temporarily halts HP reduction caused by the [Poison](Status_Effects.md) status on the targeted character. Slow Poison will not nullify the [Defense](DEF.md) reduction (-25%) caused by the [Poison](Status_Effects.md) status.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Level** | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| **Required SP** | 6 | 8 | 10 | 12 |
| **Duration** | 10 sec | 20 sec | 30 sec | 40 sec |
