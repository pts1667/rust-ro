# Moonlight

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Filir
- **Secondary class:** Alchemist
- **Tertiary class:** Homunculus
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 5 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** None
- **SP cost:** Skill Level*4
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** Animation
- **Target:** Foe
- **Range:** 15 cells (1 cell off of visible screen)


## Description

Command Filir to peck repeatedly at a nearby target.


## Information

*  It will slash at the enemy a number of times depending on the skill level used.
*  Moonlight does not bypass defense.

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| # of Slashes | 1 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 3 |
| Damage | 220% | 330% | 440% | 550% | 660% |
| SP Cost | 4 | 8 | 12 | 16 | 20 |
