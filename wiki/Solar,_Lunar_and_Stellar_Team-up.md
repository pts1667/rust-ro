# Solar, Lunar and Stellar Team-up

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** TaeKwon Master
- **Secondary class:** Monk
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** 3
- **Prerequisites:** None
- **Target:** Party


## Description

When using [Counter Kick](Counter_Kick.md), any [Monk](Monk.md)s in the party will have their chance of using [Raging Trifecta Blow](Raging_Trifecta_Blow.md) increased. If any Monks in the party use [Raging Thrust](Raging_Thrust.md), player's chance of using Counter Kick increases.

| Level | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Chance to activate increased by 20% |
| 2 | Chance to activate increased by 50% |
| 3 | Chance to activate increased by 100% |
