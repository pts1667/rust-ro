# Vital Strike

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Lord Knight
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 10 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Knight](Knight.md)) [Cavalier Mastery](Cavalier_Mastery.md) Lv. 3, ([Lord Knight](Lord_Knight.md)) [Traumatic Blow](Traumatic_Blow.md) Lv. 3
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** (1~5) .8 seconds (6~10) 1 second
- **Target:** Enemy
- **Weapon:** Spear


## Description

Ranged attack that adds insignificant damage and a chance to cause 1 of 6 abnormal status effects.

| Level | [ATK](Stats.md) | SP Cost | Delay (sec) | Chance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 60% | 12 | 0.8 | 10% |
| 2 | 70% | 12 | 0.8 | 15% |
| 3 | 80% | 14 | 0.8 | 20% |
| 4 | 90% | 14 | 0.8 | 25% |
| 5 | 100% | 16 | 0.8 | 30% |
| 6 | 110% | 16 | 1 | 35% |
| 7 | 120% | 18 | 1 | 40% |
| 8 | 130% | 18 | 1 | 45% |
| 9 | 140% | 20 | 1 | 50% |
| 10 | 150% | 20 | 1 | 55% |


## Status Effects

* Using Vital Strike has a chance to trigger one of these six negative effects on the opponent:
  * Ankle Break: 1/2 [Movement Speed](Movement_Speed.md).
  * Knee Break: Lose 10% [ASPD](ASPD.md) and 30% movement speed.
  * Neck Break: Weakens them so Vital Strike caused 2x attack, inflicts [Bleeding](Status_Effects.md) status.
  * Should Break: Lose 50% VIT defense.
  * Waist Break: Lose 25% [VIT](Stats.md) defense and 25% [ATK](Stats.md).
  * Wrist Break: Lose 25% [ASPD](ASPD.md).


## Information

* Requires a Spear weapon.
* Vital Strike only adds ATK at level 6 and higher. At levels 1-4 ATK is lost, while at level 5 nothing is lost or gained.
* There is no indication of success upon inflicting a status to the target besides general observation.
* The statuses all stack.
* The duration of the statuses are reset each time a status is inflicted.
* Infliction rate: Base Chance - (0.27*Target Strength) (204 [STR](Stats.md) = immune)
* Duration: 30 - [AGI](Stats.md)/10 - [LUK](Stats.md)/4
* Unconfirmed:
  * Neck break may lower "critical resistance". (?)
