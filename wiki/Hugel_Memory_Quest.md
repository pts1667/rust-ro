# Hugel Memory Quest

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** 50
- **Base EXP reward:** 50,000
- **Item reward:** 1 Old Blue Box
- **Window:** yes

1. Walk past the tree in [Hugel](Hugel.md) (196 169). A dialogue will pop up saying "For some reason you feel very warm, safe ...."

2. Talk to Manainne in [Hugel](Hugel.md) (191 172) and find that she is sad because El Schatt wishes to cut down the Tree Of Memory.

3. Talk to El Schatt in [Hugel](Hugel.md) (196 164) and learn that his father plans on developing the area.

4. Talk to Perfitz in Hugel (100 103) and he will explain that El Schatt has changed.

5. Return to El Schatt. He will say that he will not change his mind on cutting down the tree.

6. Talk to Manainne and find out that her dead away sister used to date El Schatt.

7. Go to [Nifflheim](Nifflheim.md) and find the spirit of Kanainne in town (90 106). She wishes to use her sister's body to influence El Schatt. After you've finished speaking with her she teleports you back to Hugel.

8. Talk to Manainne many times until her sister possesses her body.

9. Using Manainne's body, Kanainne talks to El Schatt.

10. El Schatt finally realizes that Kanainne is talking to him and gives up cutting the trees down.

11. Rewarded with 50,000 [Base EXP](Base_EXP_Chart.md) and an Old Blue Box.
