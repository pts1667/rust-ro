# Fist Master Spirit

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Soul Linker
- **Secondary class:** TaeKwon Master
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 5 (Fixed)
- **Prerequisites:** None
- **SP cost:** (560 - (Skill level*100))
- **Cast time:** 1 second
- **Delay:** [ASPD](ASPD.md)?
- **Duration:** (100 + (Skill level*50)) seconds
- **Target:** [TaeKwon Master](TaeKwon_Master.md)s
- **Range:** ?


## Description

Imbues [TaeKwon Master](TaeKwon_Master.md)s with the Spirit of the TaeKwon Master.


## Information

*  Allows [TaeKwon Master](TaeKwon_Master.md)s to use their skill [Solar, Lunar and Stellar Union](Solar,_Lunar_and_Stellar_Union.md).
*  Spirit of the TaeKwon Master is canceled after one use of [Solar, Lunar and Stellar Union](Solar,_Lunar_and_Stellar_Union.md).
