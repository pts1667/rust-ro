# Soul Siphon

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Scholar
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 5 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Sage](Sage.md)) [Magic Rod](Magic_Rod.md) Lv. 3, [Dispell](Dispell.md) Lv. 3, [Cast Cancel](Cast_Cancel.md) Lv. 5
- **SP cost:** 70 + 10*Skill level
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** None
- **Range:** 10 cells
- **Target:** Foe
- **Formula name:** Reuse Cooldown
- **Formula value:** 1~4 (10 seconds), 5 (15 seconds)


## Description

Grants a chance to fully drain the SP amount of a target. If this skill succeeds, the target's SP will be fully drained. When this skill is mastered at Level 5, Soul Siphon will also inflict twice as much damage as the target's SP amount; however, if it fails, the caster will receive an amount of damage equal to twice the caster's SP amount.

This skill cannot be used on monsters but can be used in [PvP](PvP~d29d2.md) and [War of Emperium](War_of_Emperium.md) areas.

| Level | Success Rate (%) | Damage | Cooldown (sec) | SP Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 40 | None | 10 | 80 |
| 2 | 50 | None | 10 | 90 |
| 3 | 60 | None | 10 | 100 |
| 4 | 70 | None | 10 | 110 |
| 5 | 70 | SP drained * 2 | 15 | 120 |


## Information

* The damage of Soul Siphon at lvl 5 is reduced by opponent MDEF.
* Recommended levels are 5, 4 or 0.


## Usage Notes

*  It should be noted that the cooldown (reuse delay) **only** affects Soul Siphon and the caster can use other skills in the meantime.
*  An effective method to overcome a failure when attempting Soul Siphon is to quickly consume an SP restoration item, and then using [Soul Exhale](Soul_Exhale.md) on the target. This will effectively drain their SP as well.
