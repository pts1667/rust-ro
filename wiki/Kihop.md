# Kihop

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** TaeKwon Kid
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** None


## Description

Increases the player's [ATK](Stats.md) by (2% * Skill level) per [Party](Party.md) member on the same map. The player who has this skill is not counted for this skill. For example, a [TaeKwon Kid](TaeKwon_Kid.md) with level 5 Kihop will have their [ATK](Stats.md) increased by 100% if there are 10 party members NOT including the Taekwon Kid him/herself. This increase is applied to the base [ATK](Stats.md) rate, not counting in any other buffs that may have increased [ATK](Stats.md).

The calculation for skills with an [ATK](Stats.md) modifier at and above 500% is fixed to (100 * Bonus%) + 500%.

| Level | Attack Bonus |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 2% |
| 2 | 4% |
| 3 | 6% |
| 4 | 8% |
| 5 | 10% |
