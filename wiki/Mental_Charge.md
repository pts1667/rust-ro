# Mental Charge

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Lif
- **Secondary class:** Alchemist
- **Tertiary class:** Homunculus
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 3
- **Prerequisites:** Evolved and Loyal [Lif](Lif.md)
- **SP cost:** 100
- **Target:** Self


## Description

Change Lif so that her attacks are based on [MATK](Stats.md) instead of [ATK](Stats.md) for the duration. This also increases [VIT](Stats.md) by 30 and [INT](Stats.md) by 20 per skill level. When using this skill, [Lif](Lif.md)'s [HP](Max_HP.md) and [SP](SP.md) will be fully healed.

Despite the official description, Mental Charge does not swap [Lif](Lif.md)'s Max [SP](SP.md) to Max [HP](Max_HP.md). When the duration ends, [Lif](Lif.md)'s [HP](Max_HP.md) and [SP](SP.md) will be reduced to 10. "Spawning" [Lif](Lif.md) again will cancel the duration/cast delay. (Teleporting, Vaporizing, relogging, etc.) It is best to re-"spawn" [Lif](Lif.md) before the duration ends, so that her [HP](Max_HP.md)/[SP](SP.md) are not reduced to 10.

*  100 [SP](SP.md) is taken after Mental Charge heals.
*  [Lif](Lif.md) cannot use [Healing Hands](Healing_Hands.md) or [Urgent Escape](Urgent_Escape.md) while in the duration or the cast delay of Mental Charge.
*  The [VIT](Stats.md) and [INT](Stats.md) bonus do not affect Max [HP](Max_HP.md)/[SP](SP.md) or [Aid Potion](Aid_Potion.md)'s healing value, hence, the added [VIT](Stats.md) affects [VIT](Stats.md) [DEF](DEF.md) only.
*  Optimally, a [Lif](Lif.md)-user can level without potions, as long as they "spawn" [Lif](Lif.md) before Mental Charge ends.

| Level | Duration (min) | Cast Delay (min) | +VIT | +INT |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 | 10 | +30 | +20 |
| 2 | 3 | 15 | +60 | +40 |
| 3 | 5 | 20 | +90 | +60 |
