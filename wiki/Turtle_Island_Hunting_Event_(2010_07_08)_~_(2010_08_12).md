# Turtle Island Hunting Event (2010/07/08) ~ (2010/08/12)

> **Note:** Not implemented on iRO Classic.


## Overview

The following [NPC](NPC.md)s can be temporarily found around the Turtle Island dungeon.

* **Note:** *Each quest **is repeatable** so long as the player remains within the level limits.*
* **Note:** *[Mercenary](Mercenary_System.md) kills will **not** increase your kill count!*
* **Note:** Wearing the Turtle Hat will increase the EXP reward amounts by 2x.
* **Note**: Users wearing the Turtle Hat are able to take the [Base EXP](Base_EXP_Chart.md) and turn it to [Job EXP](Job_EXP_Chart.md) or vice versa when finishing the quest (on either server).
* **Note**: The EXP rewarded without any modifiers is simply 100x or 200x each monster's Base/Job EXP (effectively making leveling on them 2x).
