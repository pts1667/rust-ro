# Steel Tempering

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Blacksmith
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** ([Blacksmith](Blacksmith.md)) [Iron Tempering](Iron_Tempering.md) Lv. 1
- **Cast time:** None
- **Target:** Self
- **Catalyst:** 5 Iron, 1 Coal, 1 Mini Furnace


## Description

Enables Blacksmiths to create Steel by consuming 5 Iron, one Coal, and 1 Mini Furnace.

* See: [Refining](Refining.md)

| Level | Success Rate % |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 35% |
| 2 | 40% |
| 3 | 45% |
| 4 | 50% |
| 5 | 55% |
