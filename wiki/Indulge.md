# Indulge

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Scholar
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 5 (Fixed)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Mage](Mage.md)) [Increase SP Recovery](Increase_SP_Recovery.md) Lv. 1, ([Sage](Sage.md)) [Magic Rod](Magic_Rod.md) Lv. 1
- **SP cost:** (Skill level * 1)
- **HP cost:** 10%
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** (0.8 + (Skill level*0.2)) seconds
- **Target:** Self


## Description

Consumes an amount of HP equal to 10 % of [Max HP](Max_HP.md) to restore a certain amount of SP (In % of the consumed HP).

The amount of sp gained can be calculated using this formula:

[Max HP] x 0.1 x (Skill Level x 0.1) = SP gained.

| Column 1 | Column 2 | Column 3 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Level** | **SP Gained (%)** | **Delay (seconds)** |
| 1 | 10 | 1 |
| 2 | 20 | 1.2 |
| 3 | 30 | 1.4 |
| 4 | 40 | 1.6 |
| 5 | 50 | 1.8 |


## Usage Notes

*  Indulge requires a small amount of SP to cast. Because of this, scholars are unable to generate SP with indulge when their SP is 0, and should bring SP restoration items to obtain the required SP before using indulge.
*  [VIT](Stats.md) builds generate SP fastest with indulge, due to their Max HP being higher.
*  With this skill and [Heal](Heal.md) from either another player or a Vitata Card, Scholars can efficiently recover SP without the help of [Increase SP Recovery](Increase_SP_Recovery.md) outside of as the prerequisite of this skill.
