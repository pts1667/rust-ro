# Guard

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Crusader
- **Secondary class:** Mercenary
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 10 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** None
- **SP cost:** (10 + (Skill level*2)) to activate, 0 to cancel
- **Cast time:** None
- **Duration:** 300 seconds, manually cancellable
- **Target:** Self


## Description

Blocks physical attacks by chance for 300 seconds. A shield must be equipped to use this skill. Each activation stops movement for a short duration. Maxed at 30% chance to block physical attacks.

| Level | Guard Chance | SP Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 5% | 12 |
| 2 | 10% | 14 |
| 3 | 14% | 16 |
| 4 | 18% | 18 |
| 5 | 21% | 20 |
| 6 | 24% | 22 |
| 7 | 26% | 24 |
| 8 | 28% | 26 |
| 9 | 29% | 28 |
| 10 | 30% | 30 |


## Cards

Clock Card (Armor) - Automatically level 3 when taking damage; level 10 if mastered.


## Usage Notes

*  Consider not to use Guard for running, use [Endure](Endure.md) instead. The movement delay when guarding an attack will slow you down considerably.
*  If the [Crusader](Crusader.md) using Guard is also [Sacrificing](Sacrifice.md) for another player, the Guard protection will transfer as well to the targeted players.
*  If your shield is [Stripped](Divest_Shield.md), Guard will cease to function.
*  [Magnum Break](Magnum_Break.md) does not work well with this skill - it will do no damage/knockback if Guard blocks an attack at the same time when [Magnum Break](Magnum_Break.md) activates its effect.
