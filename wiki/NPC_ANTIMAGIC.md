# NPC ANTIMAGIC

> **Npc skill** — name: Anti-Magic; type: Defensive Magical Support; levels: 5; target: Self

When Anti-Magic is casted, the caster gains much more [magic defence](Stats.md), in exchange for [physical defence](DEF.md).

| Skill Level | Area of Effect | Physical Defence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 20% | -20% |
| 2 | 40% | -40% |
| 3 | 60% | -60% |
| 4 | 80% | -80% |
| 5 | 100% | -100% |


## Obtainable via

* Mithril Magic Cape - when being attacked with magic
