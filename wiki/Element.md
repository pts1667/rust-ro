# Element

Every person and monster in Ragnarok Online has its own element. The element something is defines its weaknesses and strengths against skills/other elements. The term is often used interchangeably with Property or Attribute.

There are ten different elements:

* [Neutral](Neutral.md)
* [Water](Water.md)
* [Earth](Earth.md)
* [Fire](Fire.md)
* [Wind](Wind.md)
* [Poison](Poison.md)
* [Holy](Holy.md)
* [Shadow](Shadow.md)
* [Ghost](Ghost.md)
* [Undead](Undead.md)
