# Spiritual Bestowment

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Monk
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 1
- **Quest:** Monk Skill Quest#Spiritual Bestowment Quest
- **SP cost:** 40
- **Cast time:** 2 Second
- **Delay:** 1 Second
- **Target:** Party
- **Range:** 9
- **Catalyst:** 1 [Spirit Sphere](Spirit_Sphere.md)


## Description

Transfers one of your existing [Spirit Sphere](Spirit_Sphere.md)s to a party member. The party member cannot have more than 5 spheres at a time, and if you do not have any spheres yourself, the skill will fail.

* [Gunslinger](Gunslinger.md)s cannot be bestowed upon.
