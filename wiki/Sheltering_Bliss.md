# Sheltering Bliss

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Gypsy
- **Secondary class:** Minstrel
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 5 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Archer](Archer.md)'s) [Improve Concentration](Improve_Concentration.md) Lv. 5, ([Dancer](Dancer.md)'s) [Dance Lessons](Dance_Lessons.md) Lv. 7, ([Bard](Bard.md)'s) [Music Lessons](Music_Lessons.md) Lv. 7
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** None
- **Target:** All Players
- **Area:** 9x9
- **Weapon:** Instrument
- **Weapon2:** Whip


## Skill Description

*  Ensemble Skill
*  Not usable in a WoE environment

Only a Minstrel and a Gypsy can perform this ensemble. Sheltering Bliss creates a cell area centered around the caster where monsters or characters can't enter. If a character or a monster is inside the skill's range when it is casted, they will be pushed out.

| Skill Level | Duration (Seconds) | SP Cost | SP Drained Every 10 Seconds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 20 | 30 | 4 |
| 2 | 25 | 40 | 8 |
| 3 | 30 | 50 | 12 |
| 4 | 35 | 60 | 16 |
| 5 | 40 | 70 | 20 |
