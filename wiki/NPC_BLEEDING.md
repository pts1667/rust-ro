# NPC BLEEDING

> **Npc skill** — name: Bleeding; type: Single-Target Physical Attack; levels: 5; target: Player; range: 7 cells

A physical melee attack that, if it connects with the target has a chance to cause the bleeding status ailment. There is a small bonus to the hit rate of the monster using the skill.

| 2 | 100% | 40% | +20 |

| 4 | 100% | 80% | +20 |
| 5 | 100% | 100% | +20 |
