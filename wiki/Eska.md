# Eska

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Soul Linker
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 3
- **Prerequisites:** ([Soul Linker](Soul_Linker.md)) [Monk Spirit](Monk_Spirit.md) Lv.1
- **SP cost:** 120 - (Skill Level * 20)
- **Cast time:** 4 - (Skill Level) seconds
- **Delay:** .5 seconds
- **Duration:** (10 * Skill Level) seconds
- **Target:** Monster
- **Range:** Magic


## Description

Force a monster into a status similar to Monk's [Mental Strength](Mental_Strength.md), in which Movement and Attack Speed is reduced, but Defense and Magic Defense will be temporarily enhanced.


## Effect

In fact it sets the SoftMDEF of the monster to 90 and increases the SoftDEF by an unknown factor. Also works on boss protocol monsters.

Like all Es Skills, this can be only casted on monsters. Using this on other players causes the skill to fail and the caster to be [stunned](Status_Effects.md).

## Tips

*  Use on high [INT](Stats.md) monsters (i.e. Kasa, Margaretha Sorin) to multiply [Esma](Esma.md) damage on them.
*  As supporter, use it on high [DEF](DEF.md) monsters to increase effectiveness of [Occult Impaction](Occult_Impaction.md) or Ice Pick attacks.
