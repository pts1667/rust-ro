# Area of Effect

*For a list of AoE Skills, check the :Category:AoE Skills page.*

**Area of Effect** (short: **AoE**) skills can affect more than just one monster/person, and have a larger range than Single Target skills. Some AoE skills are ground-targeted, and during casting a circle will appear over the area on which the skill is targeted.
