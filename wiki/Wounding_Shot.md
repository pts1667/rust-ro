# Wounding Shot

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Gunslinger
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** ([Gunslinger](Gunslinger.md)) [Tracking](Tracking.md) Lv. 5
- **SP cost:** (10 + (Skill level))
- **Target:** Foe
- **Range:** Ranged
- **Status:** [Bleeding](Status_Effects.md)
- **Weapon:** Pistol
- **Weapon2:** Rifle


## Description

Inflict defense piercing damage with a [Pistol](Gun.md) or [Rifle](Gun.md) that has the chance of causing [Bleeding](Status_Effects.md) effect upon its target.

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Damage (%) | 120 | 140 | 160 | 180 | 200 |
| Bleed Chance (%) | 3 | 6 | 9 | 12 | 15 |
| SP Cost | 11 | 12 | 13 | 14 | 15 |
