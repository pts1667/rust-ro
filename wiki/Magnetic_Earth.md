# Magnetic Earth

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Sage
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 5 (Fixed)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Sage](Sage.md)) [Deluge](Deluge.md) Lv. 3, [Volcano](Volcano.md) Lv. 3, [Whirlwind](Whirlwind.md) Lv. 3
- **SP cost:** (70 - (Skill level*4))
- **Cast time:** 5 seconds
- **Duration:** (75 + (Skill level*45)) seconds
- **Target:** Ground
- **Range:** 3 cells
- **Area:** 1~2 (7x7), 3~4 (9x9), 5 (11x11)
- **Catalyst:** 1 Blue Gemstone, 1 Yellow Gemstone


## Description

Cast an area on the ground that will negate previous ground targeting spells and block casts targeting the ground affected by Magnetic Earth. This skill cannot be double cast, except in situations in which the caster has cast [Deluge](Deluge.md), [Volcano](Volcano.md) or [Whirlwind](Whirlwind.md) on the targeted area. Each cast consumes 1 Blue Gemstone and 1 Yellow Gemstone.

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Area | 7x7 | 7x7 | 9x9 | 9x9 | 11x11 |
| Duration (sec) | 120 | 165 | 210 | 255 | 300 |
| SP Cost | 66 | 62 | 58 | 54 | 50 |


## Notes

*  Sages can cancel each others' Magnetic Earths.
*  The cells on the effect's rim do not block AoE spells due to their splashing nature.


## Blocked & Unaffected Skills

*  Examples of skills that Magnetic Earth blocks (will be removed & cannot recast on top of it):
  * [Battle Chant](Battle_Chant.md)
  * [Grand Cross](Grand_Cross.md)
  * [Blinding Mist](Blinding_Mist.md)
  * [Bomb](Bomb.md)
  * [Deluge](Deluge.md)
  * Ensembles of [Bard](Bard.md) & [Dancer](Dancer.md) (can be recast on top of ME but effects are nullified)
  * [Gravitational Field](Gravitational_Field.md)
  * Magic [AoE](Area_of_Effect.md) spells
  * [Pneuma](Pneuma.md)
  * [Safety Wall](Safety_Wall.md)
  * [Teleport](Teleport.md) (except when used by [Boss](Boss_Protocol.md) Monsters)
  * [Warp Portal](Warp_Portal.md)
  * [Whirlwind](Whirlwind.md)
  * [Volcano](Volcano.md)
  * [B.S Sacramenti](B.S_Sacramenti.md) (cannot be cast on top of ME, enchanted armor remains)

*  Examples of skills that work on Magnetic Earth (that seemingly target the ground)
  * [Fiber Lock](Fiber_Lock.md), [Ankle Snare](Ankle_Snare.md), [Land Mine](Land_Mine.md), [Shockwave Trap](Shockwave_Trap.md), etc
  * All [Dancer](Dancer.md)/[Bard](Bard.md) non-ensemble dances/songs
