# Harmonic Lick

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Dancer
- **Secondary class:** Bard
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 5
- **SP cost:** Cast: (35+(3*skill level)); Use: 1 SP / 3 seconds
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** None
- **Duration:** 60 seconds
- **Target:** Self
- **Area:** 9 x 9
- **Prerequisites:** ([Bard](Bard.md) & [Dancer](Dancer.md)) [Battle Theme](Battle_Theme.md) Lv. 3
- **Weapon:** Instrument
- **Weapon2:** Whip


## Description

Provides a bonus of armor piercing damage (ignores [DEF](DEF.md)) to all players within the area of effect, as long as they're wielding level 4 weapons. This skill is an [Ensemble](Ensemble_Skill.md).

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Armor Piercing Damage | 75 | 100 | 125 | 150 | 175 |
