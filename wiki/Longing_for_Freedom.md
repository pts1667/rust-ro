# Longing for Freedom

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Gypsy
- **Secondary class:** Minstrel
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 5 (Fixed)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Dancer](Dancer.md)'s) [Hip Shaker](Hip_Shaker.md) Lv. 3, [Dance Lessons](Dance_Lessons.md) Lv. 10, ([Bard](Bard.md)'s) [Unchained Serenade](Unchained_Serenade.md) Lv. 3, [Music Lessons](Music_Lessons.md) Lv. 10, ([Gypsy](Gypsy.md)'s/[Minstrel](Minstrel.md)'s) [Marionette Control](Marionette_Control.md) Lv. 1
- **SP cost:** 15
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** None
- **Target:** Self
- **Weapon:** Instrument
- **Weapon2:** Whip


## Skill Description

Enables movement during an ensemble skill. It also allows the use of normal attacks and skills besides ensemble skills. Lastly, [ASPD](ASPD.md) and [Movement Speed](Movement_Speed.md), when Longing for Freedom is activated, is affected by the skill's level.

| Level | Attack Speed (%) | Movement Speed (%) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 60 | 60 |
| 2 | 70 | 70 |
| 3 | 80 | 80 |
| 4 | 90 | 90 |
| 5 | 100 | 100 |


## Notes

*  Longing for Freedom is canceled when the caster moves out of the ensemble's skill range or uses the [Amp](Amp.md) skill. It is worth noting that if cells of the duet are destroyed by skills like [Ganbantein](Ganbantein.md), a character under the influence of Longing for Freedom will terminate the duet if the cell they are standing on is affected, while a partner not using Longing will not.
*  3 [SP](SP.md) with be drained from the caster every (x) seconds throughout the ensemble's duration.
