# Sonic Blow

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Assassin
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 10 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Assassin](Assassin.md)) [Katar Mastery](Katar_Mastery.md) Lv. 4
- **SP cost:** (14 + (Skill level * 2))
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** 2 seconds
- **Target:** Enemy
- **Range:** Melee
- **Status:** [Stun](Status_Effects.md)
- **Weapon:** Katar


## Description

A series of 8 fast hits with a chance to [Stun](Status_Effects.md) the target. The damage is applied instantly and counted as one hit despite the animation. The skill can miss. Stacks with [Enchant Deadly Poison](Enchant_Deadly_Poison.md).

| 9 | 32 | 760% | 28% |
| 10 | 34 | 800% | 30% |


## Information

* Requires user to be wielding a Katar class weapon.
* Can be elemental.
* Sonic Blow has an animation that lasts for 2 seconds, which cannot be reduced or shortened. Because of this, it cannot be spammed but it can be chained.
* [ASPD](ASPD.md) affects the invisible cast time (the amount of time from clicking the skill to the start of the animation) but does not affect the animation speed.
* [Sonic Acceleration](Sonic_Acceleration.md) buffs this skill.
* Injustice Card permits autocasting a Level 1 Sonic Blow on a melee attack with any weapon.
