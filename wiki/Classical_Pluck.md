# Classical Pluck

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Bard
- **Secondary class:** Dancer
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 1
- **Prerequisites:** ([Dancer](Dancer.md)) [Slow Grace](Slow_Grace.md) Lv. 10, ([Bard](Bard.md)) [Impressive Riff](Impressive_Riff.md) Lv. 10
- **SP cost:** Cast: 15 Use: 1 SP / 4 seconds
- **Cast time:** None
- **Duration:** 60 seconds
- **Target:** Self
- **Area:** 9x9
- **Weapon:** Instrument
- **Weapon2:** Whip


## Description

Casts an area around the caster where all skills are disabled, with the exception of [Longing for Freedom](Longing_for_Freedom.md).

* [Ensemble Skill](Ensemble_Skill.md)
* Boss monsters are unaffected by this skill
