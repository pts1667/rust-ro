# Mug

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Rogue
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 10 (Fixed)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Rogue](Rogue.md)) [Gank](Gank.md) Lv. 4
- **SP cost:** 15
- **Target:** Monsters


## Description

Extort [Zeny](Zeny.md) from monsters. Mug can only steal Zeny one time from each monster, although it is possible to use both Mug and [Steal](Steal.md) on a monster and receive zeny and a stolen item. The caster`s [DEX](Stats.md), [LUK](Stats.md), and level difference with the target affect Mug`s success rate. Casting it on a monster turns them aggressive towards the caster. Does not work on Players or [Boss](Boss_Protocol.md) type monsters.
