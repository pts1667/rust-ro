# NPC DARKTHUNDER


## Description

*[image not archived: Npc darkthunder.gif]*

*  Offensive skill
*  Levels: 10
*  Element: [Shadow](Shadow.md)

A variant of the Wizard class skill [Jupitel Thunder](Jupitel_Thunder.md). It fires a ball of dark lightning at the target, pushing it back and inflicting multiple hits.

| 5 | 7 times |

| 9 | 11 times |
