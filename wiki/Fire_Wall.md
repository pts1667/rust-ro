# Fire Wall

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Mage
- **Secondary class:** Super Novice
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 10 Non-Selectable
- **Prerequisites:** ([Mage](Mage.md) & [Super Novice](Super_Novice.md)) [Fire Bolt](Fire_Bolt.md) Lv. 4, [Fire Ball](Fire_Ball.md) Lv. 5, [Sight](Sight.md) Lv. 1,
- **SP cost:** 40
- **Cast time:** 2.15 - 0.15 seconds per level
- **Delay:** Negligible
- **Duration:** 4 seconds + 1.0 second per level; 2 hits + 1 per level
- **Target:** Ground
- **Range:** Magic
- **Area:** 1x3
- **Knockback:** Yes
- **Element:** Fire


## Description

Create a Fire Wall on the ground that will block monsters from approaching the caster. All monsters except [Undead](Undead.md) take 50% damage of the Magic attack strength of the caster and are knocked back when they attempt to walk across the Fire Wall. Skill level affects the Fire Wall`s strength. This fire property skill can be cast to create a maximum of concurrently existing Fire Walls. Consumes 40 SP regardless of skill level.


## Tips

* Each hit from a Firewall carries an [MATK](Stats.md) modifier of 50%.
* Firewall hits at a very high rate of attack: approximately 40 to 50 times per second.  Be aware that if there is any significant lag, fast-moving enemies can pass through Firewalls without being hit.  Also, if the mage casting the Firewall is incapacitated ([Stun](Status_Effects.md)ned, [Frozen](Status_Effects.md), [Stone Curse](Stone_Curse.md)d), the Firewall will not process attacks.
* Characters of the [Undead](Undead.md) property are neither blocked nor pushed back by Firewall.  They may pass through any Firewall cell before it has delivered all its hits.  [Quagmire](Quagmire.md) may be used to slow down fast-moving [Undead](Undead.md) property enemies so that more hits are inflicted.
* Boss-flagged monsters, including MVP's and Owl Barons/Dukes, are not pushed back by firewall.  However, they must use all hits in a Firewall cell before they can proceed.
* Firewalls are aligned such that they are generally perpendicular to a line formed by the caster and the cast point, with the center cell being on the cast point.
* Firewalls with the caster's position as the cast point are aligned north-south on the map.
* Stationary/immobile enemies, when hit with a Firewall, will generally be pushed south by the Firewall.
* See [Mystical Amplification](Mystical_Amplification.md) for special notes on Firewall usage with that skill.
