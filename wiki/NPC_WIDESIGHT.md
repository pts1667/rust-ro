# NPC WIDESIGHT

> **Npc skill** — name: Wide Sight; type: Area Detecting Magic; levels: 1; target: Self

Description: A powerful version of the mage skill 'Sight', Wide Sight unhides all hidden and cloaked players within an area 11x11 around the caster. It looks similar to the original Sight skill and lasts 10 seconds.
