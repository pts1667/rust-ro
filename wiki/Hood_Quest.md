# Hood Quest

**This quest must be started <u>before</u> you change jobs to [Swordman](Swordman.md), while you are still a [Novice](Novice.md)!**

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Class requirement:** Novice
- **Item reward:** 1 Hood \[0\]

1.  In [Prontera](Prontera.md), talk to Strife, (prontera 216, 70) who wants to become a Swordman.  Tell him that you also wish to become a Swordman.
1.  Successfully complete the [Swordman Job Change Guide](Swordman_Job_Change_Guide.md).
1.  Go back to Strife, and after talking, he will give you a Hood \[0\].
