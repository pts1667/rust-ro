# NPC MAGICALATTACK


## Description

*[image not archived: Npc magicalattack.gif]*

*  Offensive skill
*  Levels: 1

Deals physical damage to the target based on the caster's MATK.  Magical attack lasts 10 seconds, and during this time the monster's attacks cannot be evaded.
