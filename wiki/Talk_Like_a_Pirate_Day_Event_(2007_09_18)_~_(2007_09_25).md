# Talk Like a Pirate Day Event (2007/09/18) ~ (2007/09/25)

> **Note:** Not implemented on iRO Classic.

**Level Requirements:** *None/Unknown*

**Item Requirements: 10 Squid Ink, 5 Feather of Birds, 6 Worn Out Page, 1 Key of Clock Tower OR 1 Key of Underground**

**Gear Requirements: Eye Patch (Purchasable at the Payon armor dealer) OR Pirate Dagger (Available only through previous quests.)**

**Quest Rewards: Base/Job Experience Varies by Pirate equipment worn.**

## EXP Rewards

Heimdallr has stated that the exp given increases with the amount of pirate equipment worn. Wearing all three pieces gives 50% more exp than wearing just once piece. **Experience gained is *not* scaled by level, it is only affected by the amount of pirate equipment worn.**

*Experience gained (regardless of level):
* 250,000 base Exp and 70,000 job exp per reward wearing one piece of equipment.
* 312,500 base exp and 87,500 per reward wearing two pieces of equipment.
* 375,000 base exp and 105,000 job exp per reward when wearing all three.*Valid equipment seems to be:
* Top Headgear: Corsair - Pirate bandana did not count.
* Mid Headgear: NPC bought eyepatches, and quest reward pirate eyepatches
* Lower Headgear: Pirate Dagger.
