# Elemental Change

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Sage
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 1
- **Quest:** Sage_Skill_Quest#Elemental_Change_Quest
- **SP cost:** 30
- **Cast time:** 2 seconds
- **Delay:** 1 second
- **Duration:** Permanent
- **Target:** Monsters
- **Catalyst:** 1 Elemental Converter


## Description

Permanently changes the element of the target monster. Of the 4 elements [Fire](Fire.md), [Water](Water.md), [Earth](Earth.md), and [Wind](Wind.md), your [Sage](Sage.md) may only learn to change to one of of them. When the skill is cast, it will use one Elemental Converter of the element you've chosen. The chance of success is based on your level of that elemental endow, as well as your [DEX](Stats.md), [INT](Stats.md), and Job level. With medium to high [INT](Stats.md)/[DEX](Stats.md) and high level Endows, you should have close to 100% success rates.

* **Note**: This skill does not affect [Boss](Boss_Protocol.md) monsters or players.

| Endow Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Base Success Rate | 5% | 15% | 25% | 35% | 45% |
