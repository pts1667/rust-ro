# Counter Instinct

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Stalker
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** None
- **SP cost:** 5 + (Skill Level*5)
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** None
- **Duration:** ?
- **Target:** Self


## Description

Only works against enemies using Sword and Dagger weapons. Parry 3 attacks from an enemy and receive only half of the damage. Skill level affects the chance of blocking attacks. When activated and fighting monsters, this skill will reflect the damage parried by the Stalker back to the attacking monsters.

| Skill level | Parry chance (%) | SP Consumption |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 15 | 10 |
| 2 | 30 | 15 |
| 3 | 45 | 20 |
| 4 | 60 | 25 |
| 5 | 75 | 30 |


## Note

* A very useful skill when dealing with [Killing Stroke](Killing_Stroke.md)r [Ninja](Ninja.md)s that wear a Sword/Dagger type weapons. It will halve the damage of [Killing Stroke](Killing_Stroke.md) and will reflect the other half to the attacker with only 1 remaining [HP](Max_HP.md) left because of the [Killing Stroke](Killing_Stroke.md) backslash effect making it an instant kill to the [Ninja](Ninja.md).
