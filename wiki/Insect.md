# Insect

Insect are a race of monster. Notably they are one of the two races to ignore all [Hiding](Hiding.md) related skills, which include [Cloaking](Cloaking.md), [Stalk](Stalk.md), [Stealth](Stealth.md), and [Hiding](Hiding.md) itself. Cards which concern the insect race include:

*  **Anopheles Card**: Adds a 0.5% chance of the 'Tentacle Cheese Gratin' item dropping when killing an Insect race monster.
*  **Bigfoot Card**: Reduce damage from Insect monsters by 30%.
*  **Bloody Murderer Card**: Inflict 10% more damage with Critical Attack. Critical +7 on Insect monsters.
*  **Caramel Card**: Increase damage on Insect monsters by 20%.
*  **Deviace Card**: Increase damage on [Demi Human](Demi_Human.md), [Brute](Brute.md), [Plant](Plant.md) and Insect monsters by 7%.
*  **Galapago Card**: Add the chance of gaining 'Apple Juice'/'Banana Juice'/'Carrot Juice' each time an Insect monster is killed. Juice item restores 50% more [HP](Max_HP.md).
*  **Gargoyle Card**: Add the chance of gaining 'Box of Thunder' item each time an Insect monster is killed.
*  **Giant Hornet Card**: Add the chance of gaining 'Wind of Verdure' item each time an Insect monster is killed. Add 10% more tolerance against [Wind](Wind.md) Property Attack.
*  **Gig Card**: Gain 5 [SP](SP.md) each time an Insect monster is killed by Physical Melee Attack. Drain 5 [SP](SP.md) as the weapon is unequipped.
*  **Skeleton General Card**: Receive 10% more Experience Points from Insect monsters. Receive 20% more damage from Insect monsters.

Equipment concerning the Insect race are:
*  **Cigarette**: Reduces damage from Insect monster by 3%.
*  **Pipe**: Reduces damage from Insect monster by 3%.
*  **Sunflower**: Reduces damage from Insect monster by 10%.

Skills concerning the Insect race include:
*  **[Beast Bane](Beast_Bane.md)**: [Hunter](Hunter.md) skill that increases [ATK](Stats.md) against the [Brute](Brute.md) and Insect races.
*  **[Beast Charge](Beast_Charge.md)**: [Hunter](Hunter.md) skill that is an advanced form of [Double Strafe](Double_Strafe.md) modified to do additional damage against [Brute](Brute.md) and Insect type monsters.
