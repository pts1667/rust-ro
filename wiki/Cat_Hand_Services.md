# Cat Hand Services


## Overview

Similar to the functions of [Kafra](Kafra.md) Corporation and [Cool Event](Cool_Event.md) Corporation on the Midgard Continent, Cat Hand Services provides services in the New World such as:
* Save Point service (Free)
* Storage service (60 Zeny)
* Heal service
* Warp service
  * [Prontera](Prontera.md) (See below for Warp costs)
  * [Alberta](Alberta.md)
  * [Izlude](Izlude.md)
  * [Geffen](Geffen.md)
  * [Payon](Payon.md)
  * [Morroc](Morroc.md)
  * [Al De Baran](Al_De_Baran.md)
  * [Juno](Juno.md)
  * [Einbroch](Einbroch.md)
  * [Lighthalzen](Lighthalzen.md)

Unlike the other two companies', Cat Hand's services are unlocked with "Credits". In order to gain access to these services, each character must complete a short quest to gain credits.


## Warps

The cost of Warps to other cities is based on how many Credits a player currently has:

* 41-60 // 5500z
* 61-80 // 5025z
* 81-100 // 4765z
* 101-200 // 4590z
* 201-250 // 4170z
* 251-300 // 4025z
* 301 // 3970z

## Guide

1.  Talk to Cat Hand Agent (mid camp 61, 125).
1.  Talk to Fluffy Gyaruk (mid camp 71, 94).
1.  Return to Cat Hand Agent to sign a contract.
1.  Talk to Fluffy Gyaruk again, at this point he will tell you that you have earned 5 credits.
1.  Talk to Ferocious Gorurug, he will explain Fishing.
1.  Click the School of Fish (mid camp 74, 135) to attempt fishing.
1.  Once you have collected 10 Piece of Fish, give them to Gorurug. Your credit with Cat Hand will now be 10.  Subsequent sets of Piece of Fish will earn you 1 credit and 15,000 [Base EXP](Base_EXP_Chart.md).
1.  Marlins caught while fishing can be handed in to Henry Clifford (mid camp 66, 122) for 2 black market credits each.
  * Note: do not turn in any Marlins until after you have handed in your first set of fish parts.
1.  Talk to Hand Cat Mining Agent (mid camp 88, 100), he will explain mining. When you have 10 credit points, he will allow you to hand in Unidentified Minerals (3 at a time). Each set of minerals will earn you 1 credit and 30,000 Base EXP.


## Fishing

*  You can only fish 20 Pieces of Fish at a time
*  You can turn in 1 set of 10 pieces of fish every 24 hours
*  To get the most points possible in the least amount of time, it is best to fish up 20 pieces of fish, then store them. This will allow you to continue fishing. The Marlins you get will be your point income.

A random item (or nothing) is given each time you fish.
