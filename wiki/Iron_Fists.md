# Iron Fists

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Monk
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** 10 (Fixed)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Acolyte](Acolyte.md)) [Demon Bane](Demon_Bane.md) Lv. 10, [Divine Protection](Divine_Protection.md) Lv. 10
- **Weapon:** Knuckle
- **Weapon2:** Bare Handed


## Description

Increases the damage dealt with [Knuckle](Knuckle.md) type weapons or bare hands based on skill level.

| Skill Level | Damage Increase |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | +3 |
| 2 | +6 |
| 3 | +9 |
| 4 | +12 |
| 5 | +15 |
| 6 | +18 |
| 7 | +21 |
| 8 | +24 |
| 9 | +27 |
| 10 | +30 |


## Information

This is a Mastery Skill. The damage gained from it is applied after nearly all damage calculations, making it largely ineffective in Monk skills.
