# Urgent Escape

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Lif
- **Secondary class:** Alchemist
- **Tertiary class:** Homunculus
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 5 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Lif](Lif.md)) [Healing Hands](Healing_Hands.md) Lv.3
- **SP cost:** 15 + (Skill Level*5)
- **Delay:** 35 seconds
- **Duration:** 45 - (Skill Level*5) Seconds
- **Target:** [Lif](Lif.md) and [Alchemist](Alchemist.md)


## Description

Helps avoiding dangerous situations by temporarily increasing player's and Homunculus' movement speed.

| Level | Movement Bonus | Duration | SP Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | +10% | 40s | 20 |
| 2 | +20% | 35s | 25 |
| 3 | +30% | 30s | 30 |
| 4 | +40% | 25s | 35 |
| 5 | +50% | 20s | 40 |


## Notes

* This skill will still work (and player receiving movement speed bonus) even if the Lif is out of the owner's screen range.
