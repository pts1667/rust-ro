# S.B.R.44

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Filir
- **Secondary class:** Alchemist
- **Tertiary class:** Homunculus
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 3 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** Evolved and Loyal [Filir](Filir.md)
- **SP cost:** 1
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** Animation
- **Target:** Foe
- **Range:** ?


## Description

Lower an evolved Filir's intimacy level in exchange for high damage to a single target.

| Level | Damage Done |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 100*Intimacy Damage |
| 2 | 200*Intimacy Damage |
| 3 | 300*Intimacy Damage |
