# Improve Dodge

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Thief
- **Secondary class:** Super Novice
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** 10
- **Prerequisites:** None


## Description

Permanently increases [FLEE](FLEE.md), enabling the character to successfully dodge more attacks. Also increases the movement speed of [Assassin](Assassin.md) classes.

| Level | +[Flee](Stats.md) Rate (Normal) | +[Flee](Stats.md) Rate ([Assassin](Assassin.md) and [Rogue](Rogue.md)) | [Assassin](Assassin.md) Movement Speed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 3 | 4 | 1% |
| 2 | 6 | 8 | 1% |
| 3 | 9 | 12 | 2% |
| 4 | 12 | 16 | 2% |
| 5 | 15 | 20 | 3% |
| 6 | 18 | 24 | 3% |
| 7 | 21 | 28 | 4% |
| 8 | 24 | 32 | 4% |
| 9 | 27 | 36 | 5% |
| 10 | 30 | 40 | 5% |
