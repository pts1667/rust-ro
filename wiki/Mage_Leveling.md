# Mage Leveling

*This article is a guide on where and how to level [Mage](Mage.md)s and [High Mage](High_First_Class.md)s*


## Early Leveling


## Rocker

*1-15*

* Prontera Field 7


## Mandragora

*15-25*

* Prontera Field 2
* Mt. Mjolnir 11


## Poporings

*15-25*

* Payon Field 4


## Floras

*25-35*

* Mt. Mjolnir 11


## Stapos and Droseras

*25-*

* ra fild08 - You can begin leveling here as soon as you can kill Stapos with a single cast of a spell. Be careful to walk carefully here as Drosera have an attack range of 7. When engaging Droseras, cast spells from the horizontal or vertical oriention, not diagonally.


## Late Leveling

[Fire Wall](Fire_Wall.md) is highy recommended for the following areas:


## Mi Gaos

*30-65+*

Louyang Field (Take a boat from Alberta)

Tactics: Mantis here are annoying, always be prepared for one to come out of nowhere and you will do fine.  Vertical Fire Walling here is suggested, its great exp.


## Geographers

*35-65*

* Einbroch Field (ein fild04) Geographers


## Clocks

*35-65*

* Clock Tower 2F Clocks


## Stings

*35-65*

* Glast Heim Culvert Stings


## Stapos and Droseras

*20-*

* The Plain of Ida (ra fild08) - does not require any item or equipment
* Audhumbla Grassland (ra fild09) - requires Fly Wings or Creamy Card accessory, not recommended if map is overcrowded with high level bots.
