# Spring Trap

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Hunter
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** ([Hunter](Hunter.md)) [Falconry Mastery](Falconry_Mastery.md) Lv. 1, [Remove Trap](Remove_Trap.md) Lv. 1
- **SP cost:** 10
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** [ASPD](ASPD.md) Based
- **Target:** Trap
- **Range:** (3+Skill Level) cells


## Description

Commands a Falcon to remove a set trap from a distance. Unlike [Remove Trap](Remove_Trap.md), Spring trap will remove enemy traps.

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Falcon Range | 4 cells | 5 cells | 6 cells | 7 cells | 8 cells |


## Information

* The trap selected for this skill will be destroyed. It is not like [Remove Trap](Remove_Trap.md) in which the Trap can be collected and re-used.
* Requires a Falcon.
* Does not destroy traps set by monsters. Only works on player-set traps.
