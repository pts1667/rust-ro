# Sage Leveling

[Sage](Sage.md) leveling is somewhat linear, meaning that in many cases, you will level the same place from your job change through 99. However, as you get higher up in level, a few options do open up, provided that you have the correct skill prerequisites to level there effectively.

## Bio 3 - 95+

---

  {|


## Important Skills

  [Fire Bolt](Fire_Bolt.md)
  [Cold Bolt](Cold_Bolt.md)
  [Lightning Bolt](Lightening_Bolt.md)


## Important Gear

  Unfrozen Wind Armor highly recommended


## Locations

Biolabs 2 and 3


## Pros

This will be great [Exp](Experience.md) with a decent [Monk](Monk.md)/[Champion](Champion.md) and [High Priest](High_Priest.md) who knows what they're doing


## Cons

[Wizard](Wizard.md)s will be somewhat difficult to deal with.
[Party](Party.md) absolutely needed


## Monsters


## Leeching


## Courtesies


## Techniques

  A root tank works best here and switch elements depending which monster you're killing. A 95 [Soul Linker](Soul_Linker.md) is best if available, other than that, hang around the entrance and relink when needed on floor 2. If you come across a [Wizard](Wizard.md) and you're in a [Party](Party.md) of three (yourself, a [High Priest](High_Priest.md), and [Monk](Monk.md)/[Champion](Champion.md)), the only real way of dealing with the [Wizard](Wizard.md) is by having the monk TSS them to death. Since you can't easily deal with them like that, it's important to have an unfrozen wind or decent support at the least.
|}
