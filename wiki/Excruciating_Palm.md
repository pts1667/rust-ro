# Excruciating Palm

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Monk
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 1
- **Prerequisites:** ([Monk](Monk.md)) [Spiritual Bestowment](Spiritual_Bestowment.md) Lv. 1 and a *[Quest](Monk_Skill_Quest.md)*
- **SP cost:** 20
- **HP cost:** 10
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** 2 seconds
- **Target:** Enemy
- **Range:** Melee
- **Area:** 3x3
- **Knockback:** 5
- **Status:** [Stun](Status_Effects.md)


## Description

Hits an opponent with a focused blast of energy, which stuns nearby foes and causes them to be knocked back. The target of this attack takes 300% damage. Any enemies adjacent to the target get knocked back 5 cells and have a 70% chance to stun for 2 seconds. The target itself does not get knocked back or stunned. This skill is usable in [PvP](PvP~d29d2.md) and [WoE](War_of_Emperium.md), but targets will not be knocked back on maps which knock back is not permitted.


## Notes

The skill takes into account the player's [ATK](Stats.md) derrived from [STR](Stats.md) and the weapon's attack, upgrades etc., but will not take into account the effects of cards that increase damage by a percentile (i.e., Hydra card). The elemental attribute of your weapon is taken into account.
The skill's delay is actually unknown, but it is in fact much shorter than 2 seconds. It is believed the delay might be related to [ASPD](ASPD.md), and in practice has a delay no longer than say, [Slinging Arrow](Slinging_Arrow.md).
