# Redemptio

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Priest
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 1
- **Quest:** Priest Skill Quest
- **SP cost:** 400
- **Cast time:** 4 Seconds (Uninterruptible and Unreducible by [DEX](Stats.md))
- **Target:** Self
- **Area:** 15 x 15


## Description

Sacrifice yourself in order to revive all [Party](Party.md) members near you. The skill will affect any [Party](Party.md) member within a 15 * 15 area around you, and they will all be revived with 50% [HP](Max_HP.md). When successful, this skill reduces your [HP](Max_HP.md) and [SP](SP.md) to 1, and you lose 1% of your Base [EXP](Experience.md). For each person you revive with the skill however, the penalty is reduced by 0.2% exp. With 5 or more people, you do not suffer any [EXP](Experience.md) penalty. If you do not have enough [EXP](Experience.md) to cast this skill, it will fail.


## For Level 99 Characters

Characters at level 99 still receive experience which can be seen on the character select screen. This [EXP](Experience.md) never decreases due to death, so it should continue to rise as the character obtains [EXP](Experience.md) as usual. This [EXP](Experience.md) is used when casting Redemptio; resurrecting less than five people will reduce the [EXP](Experience.md) shown in the character select screen. The [EXP](Experience.md) required to resurrect is 1% of the 99,999,999 maximum, or equal to 1 million, as a whole; each person resurrected reduces the cost by 200,000 EXP.
