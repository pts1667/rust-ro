# Muff's Loan Quest

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** 60
- **Item requirement:** 20 Rusty Screw, 10 Iron Ore, 5 Steel, 2 Ruby, 5 Red Gemstone
- **Item reward:** 1 Old Blue Box, 4 Yggdrasil Seed

1. In [Comodo](Comodo.md), talk to the man "Muff" northeast of the Kafra at (224, 187).  He explains how he borrowed money from someone named Belder in [Alberta](Alberta.md), and has lost the money to repay it with.  He thinks he may have dropped it on a field near town.

2. Go to Comodo Field 1, (cmd_fild01 55 160), and investigate the "Heap of Earth".  You pull something out, but unfortunately drop it again.

3. Talk to the Heap again, and you find it's a soggy wallet.

4. Bring the wallet back to Muff, but the wallet needs to be dried.  Muff suggests taking it to a man named Dorian in [Izlude](Izlude.md), who's invented a "Magic Dryer".

5. Go to the Izlude Tool Shop (146, 145), and you'll find Dorian next to the Tool Dealer.  He explains how his glorious creation works, but unfortunately you fall asleep.  Dorian then asks what the theory behind the Dryer's workings is, and you must type in:

* 3 Centrifuge Wavelength Theory

6. Satisfied with your answer, Dorian explain that, (naturally) the Dryer is in need of repairs, and you must bring some items to fix it.

* 20 Rusty Screw
* 10 Iron Ore
* 5 Steel
* 2 Ruby
* 5 Red Gemstone (The [NPC](NPC.md) asks for 10 but only takes 5)

7. You have to talk to Dorian again **without** all of the items, then talk to him again with them.  Dorian then explains how to fix the machine.

8. First, Replace the Engine.

9. Then, Tighten the Screw until it *Click*s.

10. Then, Press a Switch:

| Column 1 | Column 2 |
| --- | --- |
| **Machine is** | **Switch to Press** |
| Vibrating Weakly | Red |
| Vibrating a Bit More Strongly | Blue |
| Vibrations are a Bit More Stable | Yellow |
| Vibrating Fairly Strongly | Green |

11. Once you have had to push all 4 Switches, the machine will be fixed.

12. Talk to Dorian again, who says you can now use the machine.

13. Use the machine, and obtain a Debt Note.

14. Bring the Note back to Muff in Comodo. He is thankful, and rewards you with 1 Old Blue Box, and 4 Yggdrasil Seed.
