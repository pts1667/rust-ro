# NPC POWERUP


## Description

*[image not archived: Npc powerup.gif]*

*  Supportive skill
*  Levels: 5
*  Duration: 5 + (Skill Level * 5) Seconds

Increases [ATK](Stats.md) Power and [Hit](Stats.md) Rate by three times for a period of time.  The boost in Hit is based on the caster's dexterity.

| Skill level | Duration |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 10 Seconds |
| 2 | 15 Seconds |
| 3 | 20 Seconds |
| 4 | 25 Seconds |
| 5 | 30 Seconds |
