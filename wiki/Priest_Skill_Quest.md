# Priest Skill Quest


## Redemptio Quest

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Job level requirement:** 40
- **Class requirement:** Priest
- **Skill requirement:** [Resurrection](Resurrection.md) Level 1
- **Item requirement:** 30 Holy Water, 20 Blue Gemstones
- **Quest reward:** Ability to use [Redemptio](Redemptio.md)

1. Talk to the [NPC](NPC.md) in the [Prontera](Prontera.md) Church "Sister Linus". Bring 30 Holy Water and 20 Blue Gemstones to her, although it's recommended that you bring 10 extra Blue Gemstones.

2. After bringing her the required items, she will attempt to teach you [Redemptio](Redemptio.md). The chance of this succeeding is random, and each attempt uses 1 Holy Water and 1 Blue Gemstone. Once you succeed, you'll have learned the skill.
