# Wizard Skill Quest


## Sight Blaster Quest

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Job level requirement:** 30
- **Class requirement:** Wizard
- **Item requirement:** 10 Crystal Blue, 10 Green Live, 10 Red Blood, 10 Wind of Verdure
- **Quest reward:** Ability to use [Sight Blaster](Sight_Blaster.md)

1. Go to the very top floor of the [Geffen](Geffen.md) Tower, there will be a Cat you need to talk to. He will ask you to go to the [Prontera](Prontera.md) Knight Guild.

2. In the Knight Guild, located in northwest Prontera, you'll need to introduce yourself to Simon Meyas.

3. Return to the highest floor of the Geffen Tower again, with specific items:

*  10 Crystal Blue
*  10 Green Live
*  10 Red Blood
*  10 Wind of Verdure

4. Now you've learned Sight Blaster!
