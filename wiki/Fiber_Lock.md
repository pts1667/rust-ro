# Fiber Lock

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Scholar
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 1
- **Prerequisites:** ([Sage](Sage.md)) [Dragonology](Dragonology.md) Lv. 4
- **SP cost:** 30
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** None
- **Duration:** 8 seconds (4 seconds in [PvP](PvP~d29d2.md)/[WoE](War_of_Emperium.md))
- **Target:** Foe
- **Range:** 10
- **Catalyst:** 1 Cobweb


## Description

Shoot a spider web to immobililze a target for 8 seconds (or 4 seconds if cast on a player). In addition, the target's [flee](Stats.md) rate is reduced by -50.


## Notes

* Targets trapped in Fiber Lock  will receive double damage from [Fire](Fire.md) elemental attacks. However, [Fire](Fire.md) attacks will also release characters trapped in Fiber Lock.
* A Scholar can only have up to 3 active webs at any given time.
* A single Fiber Lock can hold multiple monsters on the same cell, similar to [Ankle Snare](Ankle_Snare.md).
