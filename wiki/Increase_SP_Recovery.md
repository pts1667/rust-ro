# Increase SP Recovery

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Mage
- **Secondary class:** Priest
- **Tertiary class:** Super Novice
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** 10
- **Prerequisites:** None


## Description

Restores SP every 10 seconds. Higher Max SP gives higher recovery. Player must not be walking (sitting, standing, or teleporting) for the full 10 seconds for this skill to work.

| Level | Base SP Gain | Bonus Recovery (% of Max SP) | Additional Potency of SP Items |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 3 | 0.2 | +10% |
| 2 | 6 | 0.4 | +20% |
| 3 | 9 | 0.6 | +30% |
| 4 | 12 | 0.8 | +40% |
| 5 | 15 | 1 | +50% |
| 6 | 18 | 1.2 | +60% |
| 7 | 21 | 1.4 | +70% |
| 8 | 24 | 1.6 | +80% |
| 9 | 27 | 1.8 | +90% |
| 10 | 30 | 2 | +100% |
