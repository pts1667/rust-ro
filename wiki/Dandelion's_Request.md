# Dandelion's Request

<big>**Note: If your character still has a Letter of Recommendation in their inventory, talk to Valdes (que_job01) at the Morroc Bar (morocc 45, 107), and he will remove it.**</big>

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** 60
- **Party requirement:** 5 Players (Thanatos Portion)
- **Item requirement:** 1 Flame Heart, 1 Great Nature, 1 Rough Wind, 1 Mystic Frozen
- **Base EXP reward:** 1,280,000
- **Item reward:** A) Wrapped Mask B) Jewelry Box
- **Quest reward:** Access to the Dimensional Gorge maps.

1. Find the respective [NPC](NPC.md), according to job class, to receive the Letter of Recommendation.

*  **[Swordsman](Swordman.md) / [Novice](Novice.md):** [Izlude](Izlude.md) Guild Official, at the 8 o'clock area (izlude 103 106)
*  **[Acolyte](Acolyte.md):** Nun, at the entrance of the [Prontera](Prontera.md) Church (prontera 237 313)
*  **[Merchant](Merchant.md) / [Soul Linker](Soul_Linker.md):** [Alberta](Alberta.md) Guild Official, at the 6 o'clock area (alberta 129 59)
*  **[Magician](Mage.md):** [Geffen](Geffen.md) Academy Official, at the entrance of Geffen Tower (geffen 123 109)
*  **[Thief](Thief.md) / [TaeKwon Master](TaeKwon_Master.md):** [Morroc](Morroc.md) Girl, at the 6 o'clock area (morocc 155 56)
  * **[Assassin](Assassin.md) class doesn't receive a physical letter
***[Archer](Archer.md) / [TaeKwon Kid](TaeKwon_Kid.md):**[Payon](Payon.md) Guild Official, at the entrance of Archer Village (payon 231 323)
***[Gunslinger](Gunslinger.md) / [Ninja](Ninja.md):**Messenger, northeast corner of the top deck of the domestic airship (airship 251 64)

2. Bring the Letter of Recommendation to a building at [Morroc](Morroc.md) 8 o'clock and be greeted by the Sharp-Looking Kid (morocc 43 108).

3. Find the Bar Master (que_job01 82 95) to ask about the mission.

4. Proceed to the inner room and enter the room to the left of Tao. (*Players are given 4 minutes to remain in the room)

5. Talk to Valdes (que_job01 16 21) and select the desired mission:

A. Tracking and Prevent, or B. Protect the Target

*  Note: You can only do**either**A or B once per character. You cannot do both.**Mission A - Tracking and Prevent**1A. Receive Written Request (A) and find Kidd (que_job01 91 83) at the bar.

2A. Talk to Dandelion Member until you are asked to track down Raiyan Moore.

3A. Proceed to Juno 3 o'clock to find the Reading Girl, Yunia (yuno_in04 180 106) in the Juno Library to ask about Raiyan Moore.

4A. Go upstairs and find the Workbook (yuno_in04 175 129), Kidd will appear to investigate.

5A. Return to Morocc, find Kidd and Dandelion Member. Players will be asked to search for traces of Raiyan at the Sphinx.

6A. Investigate the 7 o'clock area of the Sphinx (moc fild19 21 33) and find the Upturned Spot.

7A. Return to Kidd and you will be requested to destroy the elements at the 4 directions of Morroc:

a. West of Morroc (moc fild19 21 33), 7 o'clock direction, 1 Great Nature required.

b. South of Morroc (moc fild12 280 36), 5 o'clock direction, 1 Flame Heart required.

c. East of Morroc (moc fild10281 148), center location, 1 Rough Wind required.

d. North of Morroc (moc fild07 192 166), center location, 1 Mystic Frozen required.

Report back to Kidd after completing the assignment.

8A. Proceed to the Inn (morocc 199 64) and find the Assistant, Sephit, to gather information.

9A. Return to Kidd and will be assigned to investigate the Tower of Thanatos. Players will need to proceed until they reach the second mechanism (tha_t04, upper right side of the map), obtain the yellow key, and read the text of the mechanism.

*  Note: If you have already started the Thanatos Tower Quest and still have the red and yellow keys, you will need to sell them to an NPC and get them again.
*  Note: You only have to obtain the yellow key to go on with the quest. Characters that are unable to use lvl 4 weapons (such as TK-Kids) are able solve the quest.
*  Important: Make sure you accept a work contract on the first floor reception desk, she will tell you to talk to the NPC on the 2nd floor if you've done it correctly.
*  Speak to the NPC that grants access to the third floor and**ask about the development**(if you don't do this part you may climb up the tower without any progress to the quest). Continue to do this until he offers no further information.
*  You will need 5 people total with work permits to be near the NPC on the 2nd floor to go to the 3rd floor.
*  The machine on the 4th floor is in the NE corner.
*  After you guess the security code, you MUST read the screen text to get the details on Satan Morroc's history, then choose to open the drawer (will all be done in one conversation set)  If you open the drawer to get the key you will have to sell the key and resolve the machine.
*  You can do this part completely out of order prior to even beginning the Dandelion quest.

10A. Return to Kidd and then Valdes. After the conversation, proceed to the empty room and you will hear some creepy sounds.

11A. Talk to Kidd and Dandelion Member and you will be asked to search around Morroc.

12A. Proceed to the Morroc Castle and find the Man (moc_castle 40 20). (Players will be sent to the next location after the conversation with the NPC) NOTE: It seems that only one player can finish this part of the quest at a time. If someone is in the passageway, the NPC will not warp you.

13A. Explore the passage and defeat all the monsters at the end of the path.

14A. Talk to Dandelion Member (que_job02 66 85) and proceed to look for Kidd and Lin. (Hint: Don't backtrack!)

15A. Talk to Kidd and Lin, and Mr. R will summon monsters.

16A. After defeating the monsters, return to Morroc with Kidd.

17A. Find Valdes and you will be rewarded with a Wrapped Mask and 1,280,000 base experience.

18A. Quest completed!**Mission B - Protect the Target**1B. Proceed to the room on the right of Tao to find NPC Lin (que_job01 84 17) and Mr R (que_job01 79 15).

2B. Proceed to Juno 3 o'clock to find the Reading Girl, Yunia (yuno_in04 180 106) in the Juno Library to gather information.

3B. Return to Lin and Mr R will request to restore the elements at the 4 directions of Morroc.

4B. Upon exiting, Lin (que_job01 6 79) will appear to show her doubt about Mr R.

5B. Proceed to the designated spots to restore the Elements:

a. West of Morroc (moc fild19 21 33), 7 o'clock direction, 1 Rough Wind required.

b. South of Morroc (moc fild12 280 36), 5 o'clock direction, 1 Great Nature required.

c. East of Morroc (moc fild10 281 148), center location, 1 Mystic Frozen required.

d. North of Morroc (moc fild07192 166), center location, 1 Flame Heart required.

Report back to Lin after completing the assignment.

6B. Proceed to the Inn (morocc 199 64) and find the Assistant, Sephit to gather information about the Tower of Thanatos.

7B. Inform Lin and you will be requested not to inform Mr R regarding this.

8B. Talk to Mr R and you will be assigned to investigate the Tower of Thanatos.

*  Important: Make sure you accept a work contract on the first floor reception desk, she will tell you to talk to the NPC on the 2nd floor if you've done it correctly.
*  Speak to the NPC that grants access to the third floor and**ask about the development''' (if you don't do this part you may climb up the tower without any progress to the quest). Continue to do this until he offers no further information.

9B. Return to Morroc. Meet Lin at the entrance. Proceed to Mr R's room but will find it to be empty, but overhear the conversation from Lin.

10B. Find Lin and Valdes and will hear the sound of ritual chanting.

11B. Proceed to the Morroc Castle and find the Man (moc_castle 40 20). (Players will be sent to the next location after the conversation with the NPC) NOTE: It seems that only one player can finish this part of the quest at a time. If someone is in the passageway, the NPC will not warp you.

12B. Explore the passage and defeat all of the monsters.

13B. Talk to Dandelion Member (que_job02 66 85) and proceed to look for Lin. You will have to walk past the platform into the darkness. Just move your cursor around to see where you can walk. You will eventually see R attempting to resurrect Satan Morroc.

14B. Talk to Kidd, Lin and Dandelion Member, and Mr R will summon monsters.

15B. After defeating the monsters, return to Morroc to find Lin, Kidd and Valdes to receive a Jewelry Box and 1,280,000 base experience as reward.

16B. Quest completed!
