# Mental Sensing

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Dancer
- **Secondary class:** Bard
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** ([Bard](Bard.md) & [Dancer](Dancer.md)) [Acoustic Rhythm](Acoustic_Rhythm.md) Lv. 3
- **SP cost:** Cast: 20 Use: 1 SP / 3 seconds
- **Cast time:** None
- **Duration:** 60 seconds
- **Target:** Self
- **Area:** 9x9
- **Weapon:** Instrument
- **Weapon2:** Whip


## Description

Increases the EXP earned from monsters killed in the affected area. The experience earned is dependent on the skill's level.

*  Does not increase EXP earned from [Boss](Boss_Protocol.md) monsters.
* [Ensemble Skill](Ensemble_Skill.md)
* Drains 3 SP for every 3 seconds that the skill is active from the caster and the partner.
*  The exp increase multiplies with Battle Manuals and [Experience Shoes](Card_Reference.md)

| Skill Level | Experience Increase |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 36% |
| 2 | 47% |
| 3 | 58% |
| 4 | 69% |
| 5 | 80% |
