# Enchant Deadly Poison

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Assassin Cross
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 5 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Assassin Cross](Assassin_Cross.md)) [Create Deadly Poison](Create_Deadly_Poison.md) Lv. 1
- **SP cost:** 50 + (Skill Level*10)
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** 2 seconds
- **Duration:** 35 + (Skill Level*5)
- **Target:** Self
- **Catalyst:** Poison Bottle

This skill consumes one Poison Bottle (created with the [Create Deadly Poison](Create_Deadly_Poison.md) skill), and enchants a weapon with deadly poison to increase its damage potential. This skill does not change the weapon's attack property. Skill level affects damage increase, duration and [SP](SP.md) consumption. There is a chance to cause a more severe version of the poison [Status effect](Status_Effects.md) on enemies as you deal damage with all levels of this skill. This poisoning aspect of the skill does not work against [Boss Protocol](Boss_Protocol.md) monsters, as they are immune to status effects.

## Notes

*  EDP does not change the wielder's Weapon Element to [Poison](Poison.md).
*  EDP is the signature skill of the Melee [Assassin Cross](Assassin_Cross.md). All skills aside from [Soul Destroyer](Soul_Destroyer.md) and [Meteor Assault](Meteor_Assault.md) benefit from the high damage increase.
*  EDP cannot be dispelled by [Dispell](Dispell.md).
*  The poison effect depletes [HP](Max_HP.md) significantly faster than regular poison, but cannot drain a target's [HP](Max_HP.md) below 1/4th health (in the red). This poison effect also cannot be cured with [Detoxify](Detoxify.md), Green Herbs, Green Potions, or Panacea.
