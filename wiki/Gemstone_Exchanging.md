# Gemstone Exchanging

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** N/A (None?)
- **Item requirement:** 2+ of one type of Gemstone
- **Item reward:** Gemstones (Varies)

1. Talk to Jade in [Payon](Payon.md) (173,238).

He exchanges:

* 2 Blue Gemstones for 1 Red Gemstone
* 2 Red Gemstones for 1 Yellow Gemstone
* 2 Yellow Gemstones for 1 Blue Gemstone
