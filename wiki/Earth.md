# Earth

Elemental Property. Earth is damaged most by the Fire property, and does the most damage to the Wind property. Weapons can be forged with an Earth property using a Great Nature.

## Cards

Cards which concern the Earth property include:


## Head Gear

* Dryad Card: Add the chance of gaining 'Green Live' item each time a [Plant](Plant.md) monster is killed. Add 10% more tolerance against Earth Property Attack.
* Giearth Card: Gain immunity to the [Chaos](Status_Effects.md) status. Increase resistance to Earth property attacks by 15%.


## Armor

* Sandman Card: Enchant an Armor with the Earth property. [DEF](DEF.md) +1.


## Weaponry

* Kaho Card: Increase damage on Earth property monsters by 20%.


## Shield

* Munak Card: Increase resistance to the [Stone](Status_Effects.md) status by 15% and Earth property attacks by 5%. [DEF](DEF.md) +1.


## Garment

* Hode Card: Increase resistance to Earth property attacks by 30%. [FLEE](FLEE.md) +5.
* Magmaring Card: [ATK](Stats.md) +5. Increases physical damage versus Earth property monsters by 10%. Adds [CRIT](Stats.md) + 15 when fighting [Brute](Brute.md) and [Plant](Plant.md) race monsters.
