# Acoustic Rhythm

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Dancer
- **Secondary class:** Bard
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** ([Bard](Bard.md)) [Magic Strings](Magic_Strings.md) Lv. 10; ([Dancer](Dancer.md)) [Lady Luck](Lady_Luck.md) Lv. 10
- **SP cost:** Cast: 20 Use: 1 SP / 3 seconds
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** None
- **Duration:** 60 seconds
- **Target:** Self
- **Range:** 9x9
- **Weapon:** Instrument
- **Weapon2:** Whip


## Description

Increases resistance against [Fire](Fire.md), [Water](Water.md), and [Status Effects](Status_Effects.md) of party members within the area of effect. [Elemental](Element.md) and Status Effect resistance bonus are increased with skill level. This skill is an [Ensemble](Ensemble_Skill.md).

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Elemental<br> Resistance Bonus | 40% | 50% | 60% | 70% | 80% |

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Status Effect<br> Resistance Bonus | 10% | 20% | 30% | 40% | 50% |
