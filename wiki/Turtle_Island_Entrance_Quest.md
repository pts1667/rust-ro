# Turtle Island Entrance Quest

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Quest reward:** Access to Turtle Island

1. Enter the inn in [Alberta](Alberta.md) near (61, 233), then go into the room on the right.

2. Talk with ‘Turtle Grampa’ and choose “How can I get there?”.

You can now travel to Turtle Island by talking to the sailor in Alberta located at (247, 122). Each trip costs 10,000 [Zeny](Zeny.md).
