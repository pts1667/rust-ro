# Hallucination Walk (Unimplemented)

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Assassin Cross
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 10
- **Prerequisites:** ([Thief](Thief.md)) [Improve Dodge](Improve_Dodge.md) Lv. 5 ([Assassin](Assassin.md)) [Cloaking](Cloaking.md) Lv. 5
- **SP cost:** 40 + (5*Skill Level)


## Description

Create mirror images while enabling the player to [Cloak](Cloaking.md) freely without walls. Hallucinations have twice your [Max HP](Max_HP.md) and last for the set duration or until their HP is gone. When all the images disappear, the player will be uncloaked. The player can be detected via Detection skills as well.
