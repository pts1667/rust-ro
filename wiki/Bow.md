# Bow

Bows are the main weapons of the [Archer](Archer.md) and [Hunter](Hunter.md) classes, but can also be used by the [Thief](Thief.md), [Rogue](Rogue.md), [Bard](Bard.md) and [Dancer](Dancer.md) classes. Bows are always two-handed, so you cannot wear a shield if you are using a bow.

| Size | Damage |
| --- | --- |
| Small | 100% |
| Medium | 100% |
| Large | 75% |
