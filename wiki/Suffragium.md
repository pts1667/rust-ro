# Suffragium

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Priest
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 3 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Priest](Priest.md)) [Impositio Manus](Impositio_Manus.md) Lv. 2
- **SP cost:** 8
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** 3 seconds
- **Duration:** (40-Skill Level*10) seconds
- **Target:** Monsters & Players


## Description

Reduces the cast time of the target's next skill. Cannot be cast on yourself.

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Duration (secs) | 30 | 20 | 10 |
| Cast Time Reduction | 15% | 30% | 45% |
