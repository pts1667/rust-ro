# Rebellion Quest

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** N/A
- **Item requirement:** 1 Tropical Sograt, 1 Vermilion on the Beach, 1 Marine Sphere Bottle
- **Items required:** 1 Geek Glasses, 1 Grampa Beard
- **Zeny requirement:** 3,000
- **Quests required:** [Biolabs Entrance Quest](Biolabs_Entrance_Quest.md), [President Quest](President_Quest.md), [Cursed Spirit Quest](Cursed_Spirit_Quest.md), [Odin's Temple Excavation Quest](Odin's_Temple_Excavation_Quest.md)
- **Base EXP reward:** 2,000,000
- **Item reward:** 1 Old Purple Box
- **Quest reward:** Exchanging 100 Handcuffs for a Level 4 Food Item (Repeatable)

1. Talk to Shede in [Hugel](Hugel.md) courtyard (70,137). She will ask you for a random set of items.
*  5 Clam Fleshes: You'll find these clams to the right of the airship in Hugel ([Hugel](Hugel.md) 216,158).
*  5 Mok Mushrooms: You'll find the mushrooms located just outside of the city of Hugel (hu fild06 169,359).
*  5 Mok Shells: Find the Thief Bugs hiding in the bushes just outside of the city of Hugel (hu fild06 218,373).

2. Shede tells you to find Mr. Herico in the Hugel Inn ([Hugel](Hugel.md) 104,77).

3. Find Herico upstairs in the room next to the stairs (hu_in01 255, 40). Talk to him, and he talks about Hugel before asking you to find information about the Airship and Odin's Temple for him.

4. If You've completed the four listed quests, you can continue.

5. Talk to Herico again, and he tells you his story. He then asks you to find a Reporter in Lighthalzen.

6. From exiting the airport, head west to the left side of the map. Enter the house at (52, 87), and talk to Enquro Carson. He says he'll tell you the location of the reporter if you bring him the two drinks from the Private Pub in [Morroc](Morroc.md).

7. Bring him a Tropical Sograt and Vermilion on the Beach, and he'll tell you to find Wintzil Trony in Hugel.
* Each drink is 1,000z a piece; unless you're an assassin, then they are 800z a piece. (You can buy it at morroc_in(165,76). (Inside the building at Morroc (55,259)).

8. Go to the house in Hugel near the top (hugel 150, 216) and talk to the little girl. She tells you her name is Wintzil Trony, and tells you you can find a picture of her father in a bookshelf.

9. Go upstairs and click on the Book in the bookshelf, and go through the journal.

10. Head back to Herico and explain what happened. He asks you to join him in bringing down Rekenber. Accept, and he asks you to go to Lighthalzen to steal a Master Copy of their research.

11. Head to Rekenber HQ in Lighthalzen, and go down to the research levels used by Regenschirm. (Don't forget to wear the Geek Glasses and Grampa Beard!).

12. Go to the far room across from the portal, find Stack of Files on one of the tables next to (lhz_in01 213, 130). Pick out a File Folder of research and an intruder alert will sound; two Remover will spawn. Kill them, and head back to Herico in [Hugel](Hugel.md).

13. Herico tells you to find a Marine Sphere Bottle to help destroy level 2 of Biolabs. Go to Einbech (einbech 208, 123) and buy a Marine Sphere Bottle from him. If you already have a Marine Sphere Bottle, go to step 14.

14. Go Biolabs Level 2 (lhz dun02 247, 226) and destroy the Unethical Machine.

15. Head back to Herico in Hugel, and he asks who could help with his plans. Tell him "President" and he gives you a File Folder to give to him.

*  You can now exchange 100 Handcuffs for any Level 4 Food Item from Herico.

16. Go to Juno and visit the president's office (yuno 157, 317) and give him the File. He reaffirms his commitment to ridding the world of Rekenber, and gives you an Old Purple Box along with some base experience.

*  Receive 2,000,000 [Base Experience](Base_EXP_Chart.md)
