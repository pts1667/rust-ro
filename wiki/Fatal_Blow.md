# Fatal Blow

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Swordman
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** 1
- **Prerequisites:** ([Swordman](Swordman.md)) [Bash](Bash.md) Lv. 6 and a *[Quest](Swordman_Skill_Quest.md)*


## Description

Fatal Blow enables the [Bash](Bash.md) skill, at skill level 5 and above, to inflict the [Stun](Status_Effects.md) status on targets. The chance to cause Stun is affected by the skill level of Bash that is used.

| [Bash](Bash.md) Level | [Stun](Status_Effects.md) Chance |
| --- | --- |
| 6 | 5% |
| 7 | 10% |
| 8 | 15% |
| 9 | 20% |
| 10 | 25% |

Stun chance increses as base level increases.
