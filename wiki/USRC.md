# USRC


## Overview

The United States Ragnarok Campionship or **USRC** is an annual [PVP](PvP~d29d2.md) tournament held on iRO in the summer as part of the Ragnarok World Championship. All players are welcome to register teams of (currently) seven members to participate. The tournament is held on a weekend, with teams battling each other in elimination matches. The winner of USRC is sent to RWC to compete there as the iRO representatives. The top four winners have usually received God Items or other valuable items and intangibles such as subscription time as prizes. The rules have been modified over the years, with new skill, [class](Classes.md) and item restrictions being added or removed.
