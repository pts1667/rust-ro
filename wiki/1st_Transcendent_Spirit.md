# 1st Transcendent Spirit

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Soul Linker
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 5 (Fixed)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Soul Linker](Soul_Linker.md)) [Super Novice Spirit](Super_Novice_Spirit.md) Lv. 5
- **SP cost:** (560 - (Skill level*100))
- **Cast time:** 1 second
- **Delay:** [ASPD](ASPD.md)?
- **Duration:** (100 + (Skill level*50)) seconds
- **Target:** First Transcendent Classes
- **Range:** ?


## Description

Imbues [High First Classes](High_First_Class.md) with the Spirit of the 1st Transcendent, giving them special powerfull buffs that last until the Spirit buff runs out.


## Information

*  This can be used only on High First classes. Therefore, the applicable classes are: [High Swordman](High_First_Class.md), [High Acolyte](High_First_Class.md), [High Archer](High_First_Class.md), [High Thief](High_First_Class.md), [High Mage](High_First_Class.md), [High Merchant](High_First_Class.md). It is also only usable up to base level 69 on said classes.
*  The target characters, if they have stats that are lower than number equal to (Base Lv. of target character - 10), then all those stats will be brought to equal to that number. However, the bonus stats will not have more than +50 stats total. This spirit skill will never boost a stat above 50 total.

**Note:** This skill is currently bugged on iRO, increasing the stats of a linked character with a buff such as [Blessing](Blessing.md) or [Increase AGI](Increase_AGI.md) will cancel the link.
