# Neutral

Basic Elemental Property. A large portion of skills/monsters are Neutral property. Neutral has no elemental weakness, but the [Ghost](Ghost.md) property is not as effective against it as the rest. All weapons, unless specified, are of the Neutral property.

## Cards

Cards which concern the Neutral property include:


## Shield

* Parasite Card: DEF +1. Add 5% more tolerance against Neutral Attack.


## Garment

* Deviling Card: Add 50% more tolerance against Neutral Property Attack. Receive 50% more damage from other Property Attack.
* Harpy Card: Add 15% more tolerance against Neutral Attack. Inflict 5% more damage with Napalm Beat skill.
* Noxious Card: -10% damage from Neutral element and -10% damage from long-ranged attacks.
* Raydric Card: Increase resistance to Neutral property attacks by 20%.
