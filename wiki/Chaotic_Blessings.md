# Chaotic Blessings

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Vanilmirth
- **Secondary class:** Alchemist
- **Tertiary class:** Homunculus
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 5 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Vanilmirth](Vanilmirth.md)) [Caprice](Caprice.md) Lv. 3
- **SP cost:** 40
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** None
- **Target:** Self ([Alchemist](Alchemist.md) or [Vanilmirth](Vanilmirth.md)) or Foe
- **Range:** [Alchemist](Alchemist.md)'s Screen


## Description

Restore HP to a target randomly selected among enemies, the Master, or the Homunculus itself.


## Information

The Vanilmirth will randomly choose who it will heal so no targeting is done. It also randomly chooses the level of Heal for levels above 1. This skill will even restore the HP of Undead property monsters (or self).

This skill has unlimited range, such that a Vanilmirth can heal the owner even if they're on the opposite corners of the map.

This skill has high SP requirement as it is fixed at 40 which is better used for Caprice. It doesn't heal a good amount until the higher levels and has a chance of being more of a disadvantage. It is these reasons why most players choose to get this skill last, after Instruction Change.

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Random Heal Level | 1 | 1-2 | 1-3 | 1-4 | 1-5 |
| Chance of Healing Self | 20% | 50% | 25% | 60% | 34% |
| Chance of Healing Owner | 30% | 10% | 50% | 4% | 33% |
| Chance of Healing Enemy | 50% | 40% | 25% | 36% | 33% |


## Bugs

Despite the fact that Chaotic Blessing does target the vanilmirth, the vanilmirth's HP is not restored by this skill.
