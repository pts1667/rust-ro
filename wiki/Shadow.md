# Shadow

Elemental Property, not to be confused with the Demon and Undead [Race](Race.md)s. Shadow is the opposite of [Holy](Holy.md), and does the most damage against it. Cursed Water temporarily enchants weapons with the Shadow Property.

## Cards

Cards which concern the Shadow Property are:


## Head Gear

* Permeter Card: Add 15% more tolerance against Shadow Property Attack. Add 15% more tolerance against Undead Property Attack.


## Armor

* Bathory Card: Enchant an Armor with the Shadow property.


## Weaponry

* Santa Poring Card: Increase damage of attacks inflicted on Shadow property by 20%.


## Garment

* Isis Card: Increase resistance to the Shadow property by 30%. Flee Rate +5.
