# Soul Exhale

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Scholar
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 1
- **Prerequisites:** ([Sage](Sage.md)) [Magic Rod](Magic_Rod.md) Lv. 3, [Spell Breaker](Spell_Breaker.md) Lv. 2
- **SP cost:** 5 SP
- **Cast time:** 3 seconds
- **Delay:** 5 seconds
- **Target:** [PvM](PvM~a1fb2.md) - Monsters, Party members, Guild Members; [PvP](PvP~d29d2.md) - Other Players
- **Range:** 10 cells


## Description

Exchange caster's remaining [SP](SP.md) amount with the [SP](SP.md) amount of a target. Cannot gain more than the maximum [SP](SP.md) amount of the target or caster. This skill takes 3 seconds to be cast and consumes 5 [SP](SP.md) each time it is used. Soul Exhale also has a 5 second skill delay.

In normal fields, this skill can only be activated on other characters in your party or guild, but in [PvP](PvP~d29d2.md) and [WoE](War_of_Emperium.md) areas Soul Exhale can be used on anyone.


## Tips and Tricks

Caster may technically attain full or close to full [SP](SP.md) while giving another player very little [SP](SP.md). Very helpful in [WoE](War_of_Emperium.md) settings.

*  If caster has [SP](SP.md), Soul Exhale to a party member that has none. Then, regenerate some [SP](SP.md) or use an [SP](SP.md) item that gives very little [SP](SP.md) (Grape, Strawberry, etc.) Finally, Soul Exhale the Enemy.
*  Most [SP](SP.md) dependent people bring close to empty [SP](SP.md) then heal themselves back with blue pots. Soul Exhale the enemy (must be immobilized), then [Soul Siphon](Soul_Siphon.md) the opponent with a LV 5 (ex: [Scholar](Scholar.md) with full [SP](SP.md) of 2000 and [High Wizard](High_Wizard.md) with a [SP](SP.md) count of 120. Soul Exhale the opponent then pound the opponent with a LV 5 [Soul Siphon](Soul_Siphon.md). Approximately 4000 damage will be taken depending on opponent's [MDEF](Stats.md).)
