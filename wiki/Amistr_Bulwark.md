# Amistr Bulwark

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Amistr
- **Secondary class:** Alchemist
- **Tertiary class:** Homunculus
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 5 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Amistr](Amistr.md)) [Castling](Castling.md) Lv. 5
- **SP cost:** 15 + (5*Skill Level)
- **Duration:** 45 - (5*Skill Level) seconds
- **Target:** Homunculus and Alchemist


## Description

Temporarily increases the [VIT](Stats.md) of the [Amistr](Amistr.md) and owner.

| Level | SP Cost | Duration (s) | VIT Bonus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 20 | 40 | +2 |
| 2 | 25 | 35 | +4 |
| 3 | 30 | 30 | +6 |
| 4 | 35 | 25 | +8 |
| 5 | 40 | 20 | +10 |
