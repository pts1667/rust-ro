# Impressive Riff

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Bard
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 10
- **Prerequisites:** ([Bard](Bard.md)) [Unchained Serenade](Unchained_Serenade.md) Lv. 3
- **SP cost:** Cast: (35 + (Skill Level*3)) Use: 1 SP / 3 seconds
- **Cast time:** None
- **Duration:** 120 seconds
- **Target:** Self
- **Area:** 7x7
- **Weapon:** Instrument
- **Weapon2:** Whip


## Description

Increases attack rate ([ASPD](ASPD.md)) of all players in the area of effect (7x7 cells) by (10+SkillLV)% for a duration of 120 seconds. Attack speed increase is affected by the caster's AGI and level of Music Lessons. The skill stacks with Speed Potions but not with [Adrenaline Rush](Adrenaline_Rush.md) and it will not affect players who are using Bows.

## Soul Link

The [Bard and Dancer Spirits](Bard_and_Dancer_Spirits.md) enables use of Impressive Riff 10 when used on a [Dancer](Dancer.md) who has learned level 10 [Slow Grace](Slow_Grace.md).
