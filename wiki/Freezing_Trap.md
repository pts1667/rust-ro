# Freezing Trap

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Hunter
- **Secondary class:** Mercenary
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 5 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Hunter](Hunter.md)) [Flasher](Flasher.md) Lv. 1
- **SP cost:** 10
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** [ASPD](ASPD.md) Based
- **Duration:** (180-(Skill Level*30)) seconds
- **Target:** Ground
- **Range:** Melee
- **Status:** [Frozen](Status_Effects.md)
- **Catalyst:** 1 Trap


## Description

Damage and possibly [freeze](Status_Effects.md) an enemy when triggered. The chance of freezing an enemy is affected by its [MDEF](Stats.md). [Boss](Boss_Protocol.md) monsters cannot be frozen by this skill.

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Trap Duration (seconds) | 150 | 120 | 90 | 60 | 30 |
| Freeze Duration (seconds) | 3 | 6 | 9 | 12 | 15 |
