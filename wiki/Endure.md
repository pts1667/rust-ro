# Endure

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Swordman
- **Secondary class:** Super Novice
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 10
- **Prerequisites:** ([Swordman](Swordman.md) & [Super Novice](Super_Novice.md)) [Provoke](Provoke.md) Lv. 5
- **SP cost:** 10
- **Cast time:** N/A
- **Delay:** N/A
- **Duration:** (7+Skill Level*3) seconds
- **Target:** Self


## Description

The Endure skill removes the attack and movement delay that usually occurs when a character receives damage. This enables the character to retaliate against enemy attack, or escape, without being slowed down by damage.
