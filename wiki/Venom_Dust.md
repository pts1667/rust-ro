# Venom Dust

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Assassin
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 10 (Fixed)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Assassin](Assassin.md)) [Enchant Poison](Enchant_Poison.md) Lv. 5
- **SP cost:** 20
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** None
- **Duration:** (Skill level*5) seconds
- **Target:** Ground
- **Range:** Melee
- **Area:** 2x2
- **Status:** [Poison](Poison.md)
- **Catalyst:** 1 Red Gemstone


## Description

Creates a poison cloud in a 2x2 area. Anything entering or standing in the cloud receives the poison effect. The skill will not reset the [Poison](Poison.md) duration to already poisoned opponents. Does not work against [Boss](Boss_Protocol.md) monsters.

## Uses

* [Poison](Poison.md)s targets with 100% chance.
* Use to weaken the [DEF](DEF.md) and [HP](Max_HP.md) of target/s and to halt regeneration.
