# Weapons


## Overview

Each class has its own specialty concerning weapons (aside from TaeKwon), and some weapons are more versatile, usable by multiple classes. There are 14 types of weapons:

*  [Bows](Bow.md), [Archer](Archer.md) class and [Rogue](Rogue.md)s only.
*  [Instruments](Instrument.md), [Bard](Bard.md) class only.
*  [Whips](Whip.md), [Dancer](Dancer.md) class only.
*  [One Handed Swords](One_Handed_Sword.md)
*  [Two Handed Swords](Two_Handed_Sword.md), [Swordman](Swordman.md) class only.
*  [Rods](Rod.md)
*  [Maces](Mace.md)
*  [Knuckles](Knuckle.md), [Acolyte](Acolyte.md) class only.
*  [Books](Book.md), [Sage](Sage.md), [Priest](Priest.md) and [Taekwon Master](TaeKwon_Master.md) classes only.
*  [Daggers](Dagger.md)
*  [Spears](Spear.md), [Swordman](Swordman.md) class only.
*  [Katars](Katar.md), [Assassin](Assassin.md) class only.
*  [Axes](Axe.md)
*  [Guns](Gun.md) , [Gunslinger](Gunslinger.md) class only.
*  [Huuma Shuriken](Huuma_Shuriken.md) , [Ninja](Ninja.md) class only.

There are also "Support" weapons, which are used in conjunction with the main weapons. In the equipment window, it will show up in the center of your character's sprite. Usually these items are consumed when you execute an attack.

*  [Arrows](Arrows.md)
*  Venom Knives ([Assassin](Assassin.md)s)
*  [Bullets](Bullets.md) ([Gunslinger](Gunslinger.md)s)
*  [Shuriken](Shuriken.md) and [Kunai](Kunai.md) ([Ninja](Ninja.md)s)


## Weapon Level

There are 4 levels of weapon, which directly affect how that weapon is [upgraded](Refinement_System.md) and the effect upgrading has on that weapon.

| Weapon Level | Upgrade Catalyst | Catalyst Cost | Upgrade Cost | Bonus [ATK](Stats.md) per Upgrade | Safety Level<br> (Premium Servers Only) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Level 1 | Phracon | 200 [Zeny](Zeny.md) from [NPC](NPC.md) Shop | 50 Zeny | +2 | +7 |
| Level 2 | Emveretarcon | 1,000 Zeny from NPC Shop | 200 Zeny | +3 | +6 |
| Level 3 | Oridecon | Monster Drop | 5,000 Zeny | +5 | +5 |
| Level 4 | Oridecon | Monster Drop | 20,000 Zeny | +7 | +4 |
