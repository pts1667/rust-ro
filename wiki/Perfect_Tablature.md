# Perfect Tablature

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Bard
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 10 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Bard](Bard.md)) [Unchained Serenade](Unchained_Serenade.md) Lv. 3
- **SP cost:** Cast: (20+Skill Level*4) Use: 1 SP / 5 seconds
- **Cast time:** None
- **Duration:** 60 seconds
- **Target:** Self
- **Area:** 7x7
- **Weapon:** Instrument
- **Weapon2:** Whip


## Description

Raises [Flee](Stats.md) Rate by Skill Level and [Perfect Dodge](Perfect_Dodge.md) by Skill Level/2 rounded down of all players in the area of effect. The Flee Rate is affected by [AGI](Stats.md), and [Music Lessons](Music_Lessons.md) skill of the caster. The [Perfect Dodge](Perfect_Dodge.md) rate is affected by [LUK](Stats.md), and [Music Lessons](Music_Lessons.md) of the caster.

| Level | [Flee](Stats.md) Rate | Perfect Dodge | SP Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | +1% | +1% | 24 |
| 2 | +2% | +1% | 28 |
| 3 | +3% | +2% | 32 |
| 4 | +4% | +2% | 36 |
| 5 | +5% | +3% | 40 |
| 6 | +6% | +3% | 44 |
| 7 | +7% | +4% | 48 |
| 8 | +8% | +4% | 52 |
| 9 | +9% | +5% | 56 |
| 10 | +10% | +5% | 60 |


## Soul Link

The [Bard and Dancer Spirits](Bard_and_Dancer_Spirits.md) enables use of Perfect Tablature 10 when used on a [Dancer](Dancer.md) who has learned level 10 [Focus Ballet](Focus_Ballet.md).
