# Guild Surplus Box

**<big>Note: [Guild Surplus Box](Guild_Surplus_Box.md) is a specificity of iRO Classic</big>**


## General

Guild Surplus Boxes haves been introduced on Octobre 2013 on Classic Loki to help with the supplying or [War of Emperium](War_of_Emperium.md). They are obtainable from every [Monster](Monster.md) in WoE 1 Guild dungeons (excluding every [Boss](Boss_Protocol.md) Monsters).


## Content

Guild Surplus Box gives one of the following :

| Items | Quantity |
| --- | --- |
| Crystal Fragment | 100 |
| Heart of Mermaid | 400 |
| Immortal Heart | 200~400 |
| Authoritative Badge | 200 |
| Karvodailnirol | 7 |
| Poison spore | 100~700 |
| Speed Potion | 50~70 |
| Witch Starsand | 300~700 |
| Stem | 100~700 |
| Zenorc's Fang | 150 |
| Fabric | 400 |


## Hunting Areas


## Guild Dungeons

Guild Dungeons are the main source for Guild Surplus Box farming. Here's the list of monster that have a chance of dropping a box:

Note: The drop is not specific to Guild Dungeon, it has a chance to drop wherever the monster was killed (example: Mini Demon naturally found in Geffenia dungeon can drop them).

**Luina**
*  Giant Spider
*  Giant Hornet
*  Killer Mantis
*  Ancient Worm

**Valkyrie**
*  Leib Olmai
*  Creamy Fear
*  Gullinbursti
*  Caterpillar

**Baldur**
*  Am Mut
*  Gajomart
*  Skeleton General

**Britoniah**
*  Zombie Master
*  Mini Demon
*  Wraith Dead


## Other Areas

Other dungeon can be considered reliable sources to hunt Guild Surplus Boxes :

**Somatology Laboratory 3**

The drop chance is notably higher than for Guild Dungeon monsters.
*  Cecil Damon
*  Howard Alt-Eisen
*  Eremes Guile
*  Margaretha Sorin
*  Seyren Windsor
*  Kathryne Keyron

**Sealed Shrine**

The instance contains the following guild dungeon monsters:
*  Zombie Master
*  Wraith Dead
*  Skeleton General
*  Mini Demon

**Other**

Satan Morroc drops multiple Guild Surplus Boxes.
