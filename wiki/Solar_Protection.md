# Solar Protection

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** TaeKwon Master
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 4
- **Prerequisites:** ([TaeKwon Master](TaeKwon_Master.md)) [Solar, Lunar and Stellar Perception](Solar,_Lunar_and_Stellar_Perception.md) Lv. 1
- **Target:** Self


## Description

This skill can be used on a *Solar Day* when the player is in a map designated as *Place of the Sun*. It increases [VIT](Stats.md) [DEF](DEF.md) by the formula: ([Base Level](Levels.md)+[DEX](Stats.md)+[LUK](Stats.md)) / 2

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Duration (Sec.) | 80 | 160 | 240 | 320 |
| SP Cost | 70 | 60 | 50 | 40 |
