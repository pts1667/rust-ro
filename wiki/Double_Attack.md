# Double Attack

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Thief
- **Secondary class:** Super Novice
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** 10
- **Prerequisites:** None


## Description

Gives the chance for a single attack with a knife to do double the damage. Increasing the level of the skill increases the chance that a double attack will happen.

* The chance of a double attack happening is equal to (SkillLvl*5)%.
* Adds [Hit](Stats.md) equal to the Skill's Level. This Hit is only added in the instance of a Double Attack happening.
* Also boosts the off-hand (left hand) damage of a Katar weapon ([Assassin](Assassin.md)) by (1+2*SkillLV)% [ATK](Stats.md).
* Has higher priority over [Critical](Stats.md) attacks.

## Notes

* When attacking Plants or Mushrooms with Double Attack, skill procs appear as "Miss Miss," but still do 1 damage.
* Granted by:
Sidewinder Card - Lv. 1
Chick Hat - Lv. 2
Nagan - Lv. 5
* All items that give double attack will work with all weapons, and will use a higher level, if that level is learned.
