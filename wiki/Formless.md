# Formless

Formless are a race of monster. In game it is sometimes referred to as *Neutral Monster*, without adding the word *Property*. So it is NOT to be confused with *[Neutral Elemental Property](Neutral.md)*. Cards which concern the formless race include:

*  **Goblin Steamrider Card**: Inflict 10% more damage with Critical Attack. Critical +7 on Formless monsters.
*  **Grove Card**: Adds a 0.5% chance of the 'Fruit Mix' item dropping when killing a Formless race monster.
*  **Iron Fist Card**: Receive 10% more Experience Points from Formless monsters. Receive 20% more damage from Formless monsters.
*  **Knocker Card**: Increases physical damage on formless property monsters by 5%. Adds a 0.1% chance of obtaining 'Rough Elunium' and 'Rough Oridecon' when killing a monster.
*  **Pecopeco Egg Card**: Increase damage of attacks inflicted on Formless monsters by 20%.
*  **Penomena Card**: Receive 30% less damage from Formless monsters.
*  **Tri Joint Card**: Gain 5 [SP](SP.md) each time a Neutral monster is killed by Physical Melee Attack. Drain 5 [SP](SP.md) as the weapon is unequipped.
