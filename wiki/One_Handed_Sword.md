# One Handed Sword

Used mostly by [Swordman](Swordman.md) class characters, but [Assassin Cross](Assassin_Cross.md) (specifically for [Soul Destroyer](Soul_Destroyer.md)) and [STR](Stats.md) based [Rogue](Rogue.md)/[Stalker](Stalker.md) characters may also find them useful. Many come in slotted varieties, and some of the same type have more slots than others.

| Size | Damage |
| --- | --- |
| Small | 75% |
| Medium | 100% |
| Large | 75% |
