# Katar

Katars are weapons can only be equipped by the [Assassin](Assassin.md) class. Katars are always two-handed so you cannot wear a [Shield](Shield.md) if you are using one. [Sonic Blow](Sonic_Blow.md) and [Grimtooth](Grimtooth.md) can only be used with Katars. The [Critical rate](Stats.md) is doubled with katar-type weapons. In spite of the fact that a katar wielding assassin has a blade in each hand, it counts as a single two-handed weapon rather than tow dual-wielded weapons, so [Righthand Mastery](Righthand_Mastery.md) and [Lefthand Mastery](Lefthand_Mastery.md) have no effect on them.
