# Smith Dagger

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Blacksmith
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** 3
- **Prerequisites:** ([Blacksmith](Blacksmith.md)) [Iron Tempering](Iron_Tempering.md) Lv. 1
- **Cast time:** None
- **Target:** Self
- **Catalyst:** Hammer, Weapon Materials


## Description

Enables Blacksmiths to forge various Dagger class weapons. This skill's level affects the level of weapons that can be created. Higher level weapons have lower creation success rates than lower level weapons.

See: [Forging](Forging.md)


## Materials

| 1 | 25 Iron | Cutter |

| 2 | 30 Steel | Dagger |
