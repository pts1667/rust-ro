# Dazzler

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Dancer
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 5 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Dancer](Dancer.md)) [Encore](Encore.md) Lv. 1
- **SP cost:** (10 + (Skill level*2))
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** 3 seconds
- **Target:** Self
- **Area:** User's Viewable Area
- **Status:** [Stun](Status_Effects.md)


## Description

Inflict a [stun](Status_Effects.md) status to all enemies with a probrability rate that is affected by the skill's level. There is a low chance of inflicting stun on party members. It also says a quote, which is pulled from the game's resource file, no matter if it fails or works.

| Level | Stun Chance | SP Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 30% | 12 |
| 2 | 35% | 14 |
| 3 | 40% | 16 |
| 4 | 45% | 18 |
| 5 | 50% | 20 |
