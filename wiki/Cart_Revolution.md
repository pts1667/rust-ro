# Cart Revolution

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Merchant
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 1
- **Quest:** Merchant Skill Quest#Cart Revolution Quest
- **SP cost:** 12
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** [ASPD](ASPD.md) Based
- **Target:** Enemy
- **Range:** Melee
- **Area:** 3x3
- **Knockback:** 2 cells


## Description

Inflict splash damage to all enemies in a 9 cell area around a target. You must have a [Pushcart](Pushcart.md) equipped to use this skill. Cart Revolution inflicts an amount of damage equal to 150% of caster`s normal attack with an added bonus from the weight of the cart itself. The target hit by this skill is forced 2 cells backward.


## Notes

* [Biochemist](Biochemist.md) players can use Cart Revolution for pushing players out of [Pneuma](Pneuma.md), exposing them to [Acid Bomb](Acid_Bomb.md).
