# Hunter Spirit

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Soul Linker
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 5 (Fixed)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Soul Linker](Soul_Linker.md)) [Bard and Dancer Spirits](Bard_and_Dancer_Spirits.md) Lv. 1
- **SP cost:** (560 - (Skill level*100))
- **Cast time:** 1 second
- **Delay:** N/A
- **Duration:** (100 + (Skill level*50)) seconds
- **Target:** [Hunter](Hunter.md)s


## Description

Imbues Hunters with the Spirit of the [Hunter](Hunter.md), giving them special powerfull buffs that last until the Spirit buff runs out.


## Information

* Effectiveness of Beast Bane is increased by the Hunter's [STR](Stats.md).
* [Beast Charge](Beast_Charge.md), a combo skill usable after [Double Strafe](Double_Strafe.md) for use on [Brute](Brute.md) and [Insect](Insect.md) race targets, becomes available given the Hunter has Double Strafe Lv. 10.
