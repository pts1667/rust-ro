# Plant

**Plants** are a race of [Monster](Monster.md). Cards which concern the plant race include:

*  **Caterpillar Card**: Gain 5 SP each time a Plant monster is killed by Physical Melee Attack. Drain 5 [SP](SP.md) as the weapon is unequipped.
*  **Cloud Hermit Card**: Add the chance of gaining 'Box of Storms' item each time a Plant monster is killed.
*  **Deviace Card**: Increase damage on [Demi Human](Demi_Human.md), [Brute](Brute.md), Plant and Insect monsters by 7%.
*  **Dryad Card**: Add the chance of gaining 'Green Live' item each time a Plant monster is killed. Add 10% more tolerance against [Earth](Earth.md) Property Attack.
*  **Gajomart Card**: Receive 10% more Experience Points from Plant monsters. Receive 20% more damage from Plant monsters.
*  **Hermit Plant Card**: Add the chance of gaining 'Red Herb' /'Yellow Herb' /'White Herb' item each time a Plant monster is killed. Herb items restore 50% more [HP](Max_HP.md).
*  **Kobold Archer Card**: Inflict 10% more damage with Critical Attack. Critical +7 on Plant monsters.
*  **Magmaring Card**: [ATK](Stats.md) +5. Increases physical damage versus [Earth](Earth.md) property monsters by 10%. Adds [CRIT](Stats.md) + 15 when fighting [Brute](Brute.md) and Plant race monsters.
*  **Scorpion Card**: Increase damage on Plant monsters by 20%.
*  **Soldier Andre Card**: Receive 30% less damage from plant race monsters. (Not obtainable yet.)

Equipment which concerns the plant race include:
*  **Caesar's Sword**: One Handed Sword. Do 25% more damage to Plant race targets. Physical attacks ignore the defense of Plant race targets.
*  **Fancy Flower**: Headgear. Reduces damage received from Plant race by 10%.
*  **Lunakaligo**:Mace. If the user's base Str is 77 or higher: Aspd +4%, 15% chance of inflicting [Stun](Status_Effects.md) on the target when doing a melee attack, 5% chance of the 'Bomber Steak' item dropping when killing a [Brute](Brute.md) race monster,5% chance of the 'Green Salad' item dropping when killing a Plant race monster, 5% chance of the 'Steamed Ancient Lips' item dropping when killing a [Fish](Fish.md) race monster.
*  **Leather of Tendrilion**: Garment. Reduces damage received from [Earth](Earth.md) and [Water](Water.md) element attacks by 5%. Reduces damage received from [Brute](Brute.md) and Plant race by 5%.
*  **Mystic Rose**: Headgear. Reduces damage received from Plant race by 2%.
*  **Romantic Flower**: Headgear. Reduces damage received from Plant race by 3%.
*  **Romantic Leaf**: Headgear. Reduces damage received from Plant race by 3%.
*  **Weeder Knife**: Dagger. Physical attacks ignore the defense of Plant race targets. Increases physical damage against Plant race targets by 15%. Reduces damage received from Plant race by 15%.
