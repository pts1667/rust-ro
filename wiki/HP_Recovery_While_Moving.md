# HP Recovery While Moving

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Swordman
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** 1
- **Quest:** Swordman_Skill_Quest#HP Recovery While Moving Quest


## Description

Gain the ability to naturally recover HP while you are moving, but at half the rate of if you were not moving or 1/4 of if you were sitting.

This skill does not affect the skill [Increase HP Recovery](Increase_HP_Recovery.md); You will not get the extra recovery until you stop moving and/or sit down.
