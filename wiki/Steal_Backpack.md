# Steal Backpack

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Stalker
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 10
- **Prerequisites:** ([Thief](Thief.md)) [Steal](Steal.md) Lv. 10, ([Rogue](Rogue.md)) [Mug](Mug.md) Lv. 3, [Slyness](Slyness.md) Lv. 1

## Description

Unknown. Possibly a more profitable version of [Mug](Mug.md).

* May be able to temporarily take a player's item.
* May just give items such as ores from players.
