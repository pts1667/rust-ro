# Rune-Midgarts Kingdom

The Rune-Midgarts Kingdom is the main country in the world of Ragnarok.  With over 50 separate map areas, it is also by far the largest.  With regions ranging from dense jungles to arid deserts to temperate forests, it has a highly varied landscape.


## Location

The Republic lies to the south of [Republic of Schwartzvald](Republic_of_Schwartzvald.md). The closest city to the Rune-Midgarts being [Juno](Juno.md).  It is also to the west of many different foreign lands.  The other two countries in Ragnarok, the [Arunafeltz States](Arunafeltz_States.md) and aforementioned Republic of Schwartzvald can be accessed via the International [Airship](Airship.md) in [Izlude](Izlude.md).


## Climate

The kingdom has many varied and different types of climate, with jungles to the southwest, desert in the south, and forest in the remainder.  There are numerous rivers and mountains, as well as lakes and grasslands.


## Government

The Head of State of Rune-Midgarts, is the king, who resides in a castle in the capital city of [Prontera](Prontera.md).  The king commands a legion of [Knight](Knight.md)s and [Crusader](Crusader.md)s who protect the kingdom from outside forces, though the kingdom does have a peace treaty with its closest neighbor, the [Republic of Schwartzvald](Republic_of_Schwartzvald.md).


## Cities and Towns

* **[Prontera](Prontera.md)** is the capital of Rune-Midgarts. It is the economic center of the country, where [Merchant](Merchant.md)s gather to vend their findings, and citizens chat and gather into [parties](Party.md).  Prontera is home to the [Acolyte](Acolyte.md), [Knight](Knight.md), and [Crusader](Crusader.md) guilds.
* **[Izlude](Izlude.md)** is a small satellite town of Prontera, home to the [Swordman](Swordman.md) guild.  The town is known for its battle arena, as well as the airship link to the other two countries in Ragnarok.
* **[Jawaii](Jawaii.md)** can be found off the shores of the mainland.  It is a tropical paradise used by couples for rest and relaxation.  The warm beaches there are sublime, though the hotel accomodations may leave something to be desired.
* **[Geffen](Geffen.md)** is known as the magic city, which is apt considering it is the base for the [Mage](Mage.md) and [Wizard](Wizard.md) guilds.  Geffen is located to the west of Prontera on and island.  The center of town is dominated by the famous Geffen Tower.
* **[Payon](Payon.md)** is a quiet Korean-style city located to the southeast of Prontera.  It is home to the [TaeKwon Kid](TaeKwon_Kid.md) and [TaeKwon Master](TaeKwon_Master.md) classes, and its neighbor Archer Village is home to the [Archer](Archer.md) class.  The town has a large palace at the northern end of town and is located in the midst of a dense forest.
* **[Morroc](Morroc.md)** resides in the middle of a sprawling desert.  It is home to the [Soul Linker](Soul_Linker.md) class, and the nearby pyramids house the [Thief](Thief.md) guild.  Further to the southeast is the [Assassin](Assassin.md)'s guild.  The town has a large castle in the center.
* **[Alberta](Alberta.md)** is a seaside town famous for its light and airy atmosphere.  It is home to the [Merchant](Merchant.md) guild, and is the gateway to many exciting foreign lands and dungeons.
* **[Comodo](Comodo.md)** is the true party city of the country, with gambling, festivities, and many ways to have fun.  The town is home to the [Bard](Bard.md) and [Dancer](Dancer.md) guilds, and has a large volcano in the center of it.  Comodo is unique in that it is located inside a cave.
* **[Umbala](Umbala.md)** is a city built up in the high treetops of a jungle.  Gateway to [Nifflheim](Nifflheim.md), Umbala is famous for its isolated tribe of people, who speak an entirely different language from that of the rest of the country.  It is governed by a chief.
* **[Al De Baran](Al_De_Baran.md)** is the northernmost city in the country, just to the south of the [Republic of Schwartzvald](Republic_of_Schwartzvald.md).  Home to the [Alchemist](Alchemist.md) guild, it is also the location of the Clock Tower dungeon, and is the gateway to [Lutie](Lutie.md), the Christmas town.
* **[Lutie](Lutie.md)** can be described as "Christmas every day" in that it has a giganic Christmas tree in the center, along with a Toy Factory dungeon.  Santa Claus and Mrs. Claus can also be found, working away until December 25th.
