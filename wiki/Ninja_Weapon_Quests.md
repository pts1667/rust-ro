## Asura [3] Quest

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Job level requirement:** None
- **Class requirement:** Ninja
- **Item requirement:** 25 Steel, 5 Worn-Out Magic Scroll
- **Zeny requirement:** 32,000 Zeny
- **Item reward:** [Asura \[3\]](Guillotine_Fist.md)

1. Talk to Artisan Kaibara (que_ng 72,34) in the Ninja Guild in [Amatsu](Amatsu.md) (147,136). Select "Slotted Asura" and give him the required items.

## Huuma Giant Wheel Shuriken [4] Quest

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Job level requirement:** None
- **Class requirement:** Ninja
- **Item requirement:** 1 Huuma Giant Wheel Shuriken \[3\], 20 Cracked Diamond, 3 Oridecon
- **Quests required:** 40,000 Zeny
- **Item reward:** Huuma Giant Wheel Shuriken \[4\]

1. Talk to Tetsu (que_ng 22,62) in the Ninja Guild in [Amatsu](Amatsu.md) (147,136). Select "Huuma Giant Wheel Shuriken [1]" and give him the required items.

* Note: The NPC will incorrectly refer to the Huuma Giant Wheel Shuriken [3] as being an "unslotted Huuma Giant Wheel". There is no unslotted version of the weapon, so simply hand him the 3 slotted Huuma.
