# NPC METAMORPHOSIS


## Description

*  Supportive skill
*  Levels: 1

The caster transforms into another monster, possibly more than one. The monster(s) resulting from this skill have full HP, stats, experience, and drops as their regular versions. No experience or drops are rewarded for damage done to the caster prior to using this skill.
