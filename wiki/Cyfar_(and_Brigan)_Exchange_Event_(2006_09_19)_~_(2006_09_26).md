# Cyfar (and Brigan) Exchange Event (2006/09/19) ~ (2006/09/26)

> **Note:** Not implemented on iRO Classic.

Exchange your Cyfars and Brigans today, for Cool Event Points!
Talk to Melissa Ma the representative in Prontera, just north of the Fountain!  Give her either Brigan, Cyfar, or [Zeny](Zeny.md) for points.  You can then spend points on certain items.

**Points:**

| Item | 1 Cyfar | 1 Brigan | 200 Zeny |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Points | 8 | 10 | 1 |

**Prizes Available:**

| Item | Authoritative Badge | Honey | Royal Jelly | Green Ale | 3 Carat Diamond | Yggdrasilberry |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Points | 20 | 25 | 50 | 500 | 550 | 600 |
