# Ninja Aura

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Ninja
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** ([Ninja](Ninja.md)) Lv. 5 [Ninja Mastery](Ninja_Mastery.md)
- **SP cost:** 10 + 10 * Skill Level
- **HP cost:** 5%
- **Cast time:** 6 - Skill Level
- **Duration:** 15 + 15 * Skill Level
- **Target:** Self


## Description

Focus spiritual energy to enter Ninja Aura status, which endows the caster with [INT](Stats.md) and [STR](Stats.md) bonuses. Ninja Aura status allows the use of [Mirror Image](Mirror_Image.md) and [Killing Stroke](Killing_Stroke.md) skills, although using those skills will cancel Ninja Aura.

| Level | +STR/INT | SP Cost | HP Cost | Cast Time | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 | 20 | 5% | 5 Secs. | 30 Secs. |
| 2 | 2 | 30 | 5% | 4 Secs. | 45 Secs. |
| 3 | 3 | 40 | 5% | 3 Secs. | 60 Secs. |
| 4 | 4 | 50 | 5% | 2 Secs. | 75 Secs. |
| 5 | 5 | 60 | 5% | 1 Sec. | 90 Secs. |
