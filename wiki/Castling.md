# Castling

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Amistr
- **Secondary class:** Alchemist
- **Tertiary class:** Homunculus
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** None
- **SP cost:** 10
- **Range:** Unlimited


## Description

Instantaneously switch the location of the player and the Homunculus. The monsters that were attacking the player will now attack the Homunculus instead.

| Level | Chance (%) |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 20 |
| 2 | 40 |
| 3 | 60 |
| 4 | 80 |
| 5 | 100 |


## Notes

* Does not work in [War of Emperium](War_of_Emperium.md).
