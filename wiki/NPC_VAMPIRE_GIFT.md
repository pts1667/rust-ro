# NPC VAMPIRE GIFT

> **Npc skill** — name: Vampire's Gift; type: Area-of-Effect Physical Attack; levels: 10; target: Self

Description: Deals a strong physical blow to all players within range, absorbing the resulting damage dealt as HP. This damage is neutral element and cannot be blocked by Safety Wall or Pneuma.

| Skill Level | Damage | Area of Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 100% | 11x11 |
| 2 | 200% | 15x15 |
| 3 | 300% | 19x19 |
| 4 | 400% | 23x23 |
| 5 | 500% | 27x27 |
| 6 | 100% | 27x27 |
| 7 | 200% | 27x27 |
| 8 | 300% | 27x27 |
| 9 | 400% | 27x27 |
| 10 | 500% | 27x27 |


## Obtainable via

* Death Guidance Lv. 1 when attacking (Lv. 2 is used if the weapon upgrade is over +9)
