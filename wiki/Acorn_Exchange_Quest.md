# Acorn Exchange Quest

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** ?
- **Item requirement:** 20 Acorn
- **Items required:** GUSLI (After Step 12 of the Finding The Moving Island Quest)
- **Quests required:** [Finding The Moving Island Quest](Finding_The_Moving_Island_Quest.md)
- **Item reward:** 1 Cracked Diamond -OR- 1 Cursed Ruby -OR- 1 Random Gemstone

1. Travel to Whale Island.

2. With 20 Acorns in your inventory, speak with Huge Squirrel (mosk_fild01 113, 108). There is an NPC that sells Acorns in [Moscovia](Moscovia.md).

3. Huge Squirrel will trade a Cracked Diamond, a Cursed Ruby, or at a much lower chance, 1 random gemstone, for your acorns.

Gemstones that players have received include:
  * Biotite
  * Phlogopite
  * Agate
  * Muscovite
  * Rose Quartz
  * Citrine
  * Amethyst
  * Emerald
  * Pearl
  * Ruby
  * Sardonyx
  * Opal
  * Topaz
  * 2-carat Diamond
  * 3-carat Diamond
  * Aquamarine
