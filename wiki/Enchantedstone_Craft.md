# Enchantedstone Craft

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Blacksmith
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** ([Blacksmith](Blacksmith.md)) [Iron Tempering](Iron_Tempering.md) Lv. 1
- **Catalyst:** 1 Mini Furnace, 10 Elemental Ores


## Description

Enables Blacksmiths to create elemental stones by consuming 10 elemental ores and 1 Mini-Furnace.

| Skill Level | Success Rate |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 15% |
| 2 | 20% |
| 3 | 25% |
| 4 | 30% |
| 5 | 35% |


## Materials

| Ingredients | Result |
| --- | --- |
| 10 Green Live, 1 Mini Furnace | 1 Great Nature |
| 10 Crystal Blue, 1 Mini Furnace | 1 Mystic Frozen |
| 10 Wind of Verdure, 1 Mini Furnace | 1 Rough Wind |
| 10 Red Blood, 1 Mini Furnace | 1 Flame Heart |
| 10 Star Dust, 1 Mini Furnace | 1 Star Crumb |
