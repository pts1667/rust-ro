# Wizard Job Change Guide

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Job level requirement:** 40
- **Class requirement:** Mage
- **Item requirement:** 10 Red Gemstone, 10 Blue Gemstone, 10 Yellow Gemstone -OR- 5 Crystal Blue, 5 Green Live, 5 Red Blood, 5 Wind of Verdure; Worn Out Scroll (Optional)
- **Quest reward:** Jobchange to [Wizard](Wizard.md)

1. Head to the Geffen tower in the middle of [Geffen](Geffen.md), and proceed to the top floor to talk to the Wizard Guildsman.

2. [Mage](Mage.md)s that are between Job Levels 40 and 49 will have to collect items from one of the following sets:

| Set | Items |
| --- | --- |
| Set 1 | 10 Red Gemstone, 10 Blue Gemstone, 10 Yellow Gemstone |
| Set 2 | 5 Crystal Blue, 5 Green Live, 5 Red Blood, 5 Wind of Verdure |

3. The second test involves answering questions. Currently there are 3 different sets.

Set 1

The skill not neccessary to learn [Fire Wall](Fire_Wall.md) is [Napalm Beat] level 4.

Casting [Frost Diver](Frost_Diver.md) on a monster changes its attribute to [Water](Water.md).

Mastering [Napalm Beat](Napalm_Beat.md) makes the skill yield 1.7 times [MATK](Stats.md).

A Red Gemstone is required for casting [Stone Curse](Stone_Curse.md).

The skill not needed to learn [Safety Wall](Safety_Wall.md) is [Increase SP Recovery](Increase_SP_Recovery.md) level 6.

Without the [INT](Stats.md) bonus, 21 SP will be recovered every 10 seconds with [Increase SP Recovery](Increase_SP_Recovery.md) level 7.

Using [Energy Coat](Energy_Coat.md) with 50% remaining SP, 2% SP is used per hit and the damage is reduced by 18%.

[Safety Wall](Safety_Wall.md) level 6 consumes 35 SP and blocks 7 attacks.

[Thunderstorm](Thunderstorm.md) level 10 consumes 74 SP.

The most useful skill for training in Byalan dungeon is [Lightning Bolt](Lightening_Bolt.md).

Set 2

Slotted Guards can be obtained from Pupas.

The easiest monster for a low level to hunt is Flora.

Evil Druid is not affected by [Stone Curse](Stone_Curse.md).

A [Wind](Wind.md) attribute weapon deals 200% damage to a level 3 [Water](Water.md) attribute monster.

If a Baby Desert Wolf and Familiar fought, the Baby Desert Wolf would win.

Roda Frogs cannot be Cute Pets.

Goblin (Mace) is weak against a [Fire](Fire.md) attribute attack.

Caramel has the highest defence.

Ghostring is of different species when compared to others.

Deviace is not an [Undead](Undead.md) monster.

Set 3

The most important stat for a [Mage](Mage.md) is [INT](Stats.md).

[Earth](Earth.md) does not have a "bolt" type attack.

"Good at selling stuff" is not something that would relate to a [Mage](Mage.md).

[Geffen](Geffen.md) is the town of the [Mages](Mage.md).

Soldier Andre card has nothing to do with [INT](Stats.md).

Compared to other job classes, the [Mage](Mage.md) is good at magic skills.

[INT](Stats.md) bonus at job level 40 is +5 for a [Mage](Mage.md).

A Cap cannot be equipped by [Mage](Mage.md)s.

The catalyst when making the [Mage](Mage.md) test solution 3 is a Blue Gemstone.

Magnolia card is irrelevant to magic.

4. To proceed, talk to Gloomy Wizard in the corner of the room who will teleport you to a waiting area. When ready, enter the chatroom to wait your turn to take the test. Only one player can be in this part of the test at the same time.

5. The next segment requires fighting through 3 rooms of monsters. Participants have 3 minutes to clear each room. If you die or run out of times, you will fail and need to retake the test.

**Note**: If you are unable to pass this portion of the test, you can instead skip the test by making it to the water room's boss and failing five times. Once this is done, hand Gloomy Wizard a Worn Out Scroll to automatically pass.

| Room | Aggressive Monster(s) | Cast Sensor Monster(s) | Other Monster(s) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room 1<br>([Water](Water.md) Room) | Obeaune, 4 Hydra | Cornutus, Phen, Shellfish, Vadon, Magnolia | Marin, Marina, Marine Sphere (kill the Marine Spheres first) |
| Room 2<br>([Earth](Earth.md) Room) | Mantis, Orc Warrior, 8 Mandragora, Flora | Bigfoot, Caramel, Giearth, Savage, Vitata | Deniro, Hode |
| Room 3<br>([Fire](Fire.md) Room) | Desert Wolf, Goblin, Zerom, Elder Willow, Frilldora, Scorpion, Greatest General, 4-6 Horong | None | Pecopeco, Metaller |

*  Recommended Skills for the Test:
  * [Fire Bolt](Fire_Bolt.md)
  * [Fire Wall](Fire_Wall.md)
  * [Lightning Bolt](Lightening_Bolt.md)
  * [Cold Bolt](Cold_Bolt.md)

6a. If you pass the battle test, skip to step 7.

6b. If you fail the test, or give up queuing for the test, you will be required to answer 5 multiple choice questions:

1.  What monster is different in attribute? Cornutus
1.  Which monster does not covet items? Zerom
1.  What monster is not cast sensitive? Marina.
1.  What magic is effective to marine sphere? Lightning bolt
1.  What is not a stationary monster? Frilldora

7. After passing the test, talk to the Wizard Guildsman again. He will change your job to Wizard. Congratulations!
