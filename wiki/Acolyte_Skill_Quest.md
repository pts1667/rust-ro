# Acolyte Skill Quest


## Holy Light Quest

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Job level requirement:** 30
- **Class requirement:** Acolyte
- **Item requirement:** 1 Rosary, 1 Opal, 1 Crystal Blue
- **Quest reward:** Ability to use [Holy Light](Holy_Light.md)

1. Talk to the Cleric in the [Prontera](Prontera.md) Sanctuary, and give him the following items to learn Holy Light: 1 Rosary, 1 Opal, 1 Crystal Blue.
