# Resistance Potions


## Potion Types


## Resist Cold

* Strong against: [Water](Water.md)
* Weak against: [Wind](Wind.md)
* Items: 3 Heart of Mermaids + 1 Blue Gemstone + 1 Empty Potion Bottle


## Resist Thunder

* Strong against: [Wind](Wind.md)
* Weak against: [Earth](Earth.md)
* Items: 3 Moth Dusts + 1 Blue Gemstone + 1 Empty Potion Bottle


## Resist Earth

* Strong against: [Earth](Earth.md)
* Weak against: [Fire](Fire.md)
* Items: 2 Large Jellopies + 1 Yellow Gemstone + 1 Empty Potion Bottle


## Resist Fire

* Strong against: [Fire](Fire.md)
* Weak against: [Water](Water.md)
* Items: 2 Frills + 1 Red Gemstone + 1 Empty Potion Bottle
