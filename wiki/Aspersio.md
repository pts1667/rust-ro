# Aspersio

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Priest
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 5 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Acolyte](Acolyte.md)) [Aqua Benedicta](Aqua_Benedicta.md) Lv. 1, ([Priest](Priest.md)) [Impositio Manus](Impositio_Manus.md) Lv. 3
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** 2 seconds
- **Duration:** (30 + (Skill Level*30)) seconds
- **Target:** Monsters & Players
- **Catalyst:** 1 Holy Water


## Description

Endow a weapon with the [Holy](Holy.md) property for the skill's duration. [Holy](Holy.md) property weapons can inflict more damage on [Undead](Undead.md) and [Shadow](Shadow.md) property monsters.

| Level | Duration (seconds) | SP Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 60 | 12 |
| 2 | 90 | 14 |
| 3 | 120 | 16 |
| 4 | 150 | 18 |
| 5 | 180 | 20 |


## Information

* Can be used to override the weapon element of players.
* Wears off when switching to a different weapon.
*  Casting it on [Undead](Undead.md) property or [Demon](Demon.md) race targets does minor [Holy](Holy.md) elemental damage. Wearing [Undead](Undead.md) armor also prevents Aspersio from overriding weapon elements, as damage is received rather than having the weapon endowed.
