# Beginner Skill Resetter

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** 40 or Below
- **Quest reward:** Skill Reset
- **Cat:** no

*This quest **is repeatable** as long as your character remains at [Base Level](Levels.md) 40 or below.* **[High First Class](High_First_Class.md) characters are also unable to use the resetter.**

1. In [Prontera](Prontera.md), talk to the Hypnotist to the right of the Tool Shop in the center of town (prontera 146, 232).  Make sure your character is at 0 [weight](Weight_Limit.md) before resetting.
1. When asked if you want to reset your character, say yes, and the Hypnotist will reset your skills for you.

* **Note**: The [Basic Skill](Basic_Skill.md) must be returned to level 9 before you can put any points back into your first class skills. *(On some servers Basic Skill remains when resetting with this quest.)*

* **Note 2**: Only non-Transcendent characters are eligible to reset. If you are a Transcendent First Class character, the [NPC](NPC.md) will **not** reset your skills.
