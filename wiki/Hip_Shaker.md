# Hip Shaker

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Dancer
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** ([Dancer](Dancer.md)) [Dance Lessons](Dance_Lessons.md) Lv.1, [Amp](Amp.md) Lv.1
- **SP cost:** Cast: 20+3*LVL, Use: 1 SP / 3 seconds
- **Duration:** 30 seconds
- **Area:** 7x7
- **Weapon:** Whip


## Description

Drains the [SP](SP.md) of all targets within the area of effect. Loss of SP begins upon entering the area and will be repeated every 3 seconds as long as the target remains inside, with a maximum of 11 ticks per use of the skill. The SP drain is affected by the caster`s Hip Shaker and [Dance Lessons](Dance_Lessons.md) skill levels. When cast over another Dance skill, Hip Shaker is always considered to be at skill level 1.

| Level | SP Loss per Tick |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 10 |
| 2 | 15 |
| 3 | 20 |
| 4 | 25 |
| 5 | 30 |
