# NPC EVILLAND

> **Npc skill** — name: Evil Land; type: Magical Ground Spell; levels: 10; target: Ground; element: Shadow

Description: What looks like an unholy version of the Priest's skill Sanctuary, Evil Land covers a 11 x 11 area with an effect that heals demons and undead and deals dark elemental damage to demi-human targets every second and blinds them by chance. Unlike Sanctuary, there is no knockback effect.
