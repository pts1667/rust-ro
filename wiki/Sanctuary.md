# Sanctuary

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Priest
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 10 (selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Acolyte](Acolyte.md)) [Heal](Heal.md) Lv. 1
- **Cast time:** 5 seconds
- **Target:** Ground
- **Range:** Magic
- **Area:** 5 × 5
- **Knockback:** 2
- **Element:** Holy
- **Catalyst:** 1 Blue Gemstone


## Description

Sanctuary creates an area on the ground that heals players and monsters inside it in waves, at a 1 second interval. The number of waves, number of targets that can be healed per wave, and the heal amount of each wave depends on the skill level used.

[Undead](Undead.md) element and [Demon](Demon.md) race monsters that move inside a Sanctuary will be damaged and pushed back by 2 cells. The damage dealt is half the healing power of Sanctuary and is [Holy](Holy.md) property.

## Notes

* Creating a Sanctuary over another will replace the existing one.
* Offensive damage can be increased by use of a Spiritual Ring set.
* Use of this skill is the only way to heal [emperiums](War_of_Emperium.md).
