# Cure

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Acolyte
- **Secondary class:** Crusader
- **Tertiary class:** Super Novice
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 1
- **Prerequisites:** ([Acolyte](Acolyte.md) & [Super Novice](Super_Novice.md)) [Heal](Heal.md) Lv. 2; ([Crusader](Crusader.md)) [Faith](Faith.md) Lv. 5
- **SP cost:** 15
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** None
- **Target:** Self, Foe, or Other Players


## Description

Cures the target from [Silence](Status_Effects.md), [Chaos](Status_Effects.md) and [Blind](Status_Effects.md) status effects.


## Obtainable via

* Obeaune Card - Lv. 1
* Rudra Bow - Lv. 1
