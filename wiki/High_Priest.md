## Overview

*[image not archived: FS.PNG‎]*

High Priest is the Transcendent variant of [Priest](Priest.md). The High Priest is the epitome of spiritual calm. A bulwark in the storm of chaos, he is more than capable of protecting his comrades when faced with even the most ferocious enemy.
Despite the many skills taught to them by the Priests of [Prontera](Prontera.md) Church, they still need to learn a few more from the Gods themselves. Now able to create areas where no violence may occur, they are able to Heal more, Bless more and Serve even more. Their skills are truly gifts from God.
High Priests get further improved tanking ability thanks to [Assumptio](Assumptio.md). The additional statpoints from being a Transcendent, coupled with [Meditatio](Meditatio.md), makes them able to spread their stats out further, which in turn makes new builds like the DEX/VIT/INT WoE possible.

## Builds


## [DEX](Stats.md)/[VIT](Stats.md) WoE

* STR: 1
* AGI: 1-40
* VIT: 100
* INT: 50-100
* DEX: 100-120
* LUK: 1

This build is for the WoE-oriented High Priest.  It isn't a bad PvM build, either, but requires a better player due to reduced max [Heal](Heal.md) output and reduced SP regeneration.

100 [VIT](Stats.md) is an absolute must.  This can be acquired using just base VIT and job bonuses, or +10 VIT Food.  High Priests don't die very often, so typically only 4 to 6 Foods will be used per WoE.

100 [DEX](Stats.md) or more is nice because of the cast time on [Increase AGI](Increase_AGI.md) and [Assumptio](Assumptio.md), for buffing.  A huge amount of [DEX](Stats.md) isn't necessary.

The [INT](Stats.md) is where most people vary.  Having no INT is bad because healing the Barricades in WoE 2.0 is necessary when under attack.  [Sanctuary](Sanctuary.md) will not cut it.  100 [INT](Stats.md) provides status immunity to Sleep, which will help you support.  If 100 [INT](Stats.md) cannot be attained, use a Nightmare Card to provide equipment based Sleep immunity.  This can be carded in either a slotted Middle Headgear such as Sunglasses \[1\], or in a Ulle's Cap \[1\].

The [AGI](Stats.md) will allow a High Priest to spam [Blessing](Blessing.md) and [Status Recovery](Status_Recovery.md) much more quickly.  Standing in [Assassin Cross of Sunset](Impressive_Riff.md) with a Concentration Potion or under the effects of [Aid Berserk Potion](Aid_Berserk_Potion.md) will greatly increase buffing speed.

## Accessory Cards

Alligator Card to reduce incoming [Acid Bomb](Acid_Bomb.md) damage.
Smokie Card for [Hiding](Hiding.md), which allows the player to dodge many forms of incoming damage, namely [Guillotine Fist](Guillotine_Fist.md).  It also makes it harder to focus fire the player with skills like [Acid Bomb](Acid_Bomb.md) and [Jupitel Thunder](Jupitel_Thunder.md).

Skills:
*  [Sanctuary](Sanctuary.md) and [Safety Wall](Safety_Wall.md) are both necessary for WoE.  Level 7 will be sufficient.  Advancing Sanctuary beyond Level 7 is ill-advised.
*  [Impositio Manus](Impositio_Manus.md) Level 5 is good when you are supporting [Champion](Champion.md)'s and [Sniper](Sniper.md)'s.
*  [Status Recovery](Status_Recovery.md) is a must when supporting [High Wizard](High_Wizard.md)'s.  Use it to clear Stone Curse.  It can also clear Sleep on low [INT](Stats.md) classes, granted they are not using an Evil Druid Carded armor.
*  [Benedictio Sanctissimi Sacramenti](B.S_Sacramenti.md) can be useful for a guild to carry on a single High Priest. The Holy Armor effect will reduce all elemental damage (Except for Dark) greatly.  It nullifies Evil Druid Card and Ghostring Card, but that can be fixed by swapping armors.
*  [Suffragium](Suffragium.md) is a very niche skill that applies very seldom.  It's generally best to skip it, unless you need it for PvM.
*  [Gloria](Gloria.md) is a very useful skill when supporting [Sniper](Sniper.md)'s, as [Focused Arrow Strike](Focused_Arrow_Strike.md) can critical hit.  Level 1 is sufficient.
*  [Kyrie Eleison](Kyrie_Eleison.md) is not a good defensive skill.  The protection it supplies is minimal, and it doesn't protect from the most dangerous sources of damage, i.e, [Acid Bomb](Acid_Bomb.md), [Guillotine Fist](Guillotine_Fist.md), [Meteor Storm](Meteor_Storm.md), [Storm Gust](Storm_Gust.md), and [First Wind](First_Wind.md).  However, [Kyrie Eleison](Kyrie_Eleison.md) cancels [Assumptio](Assumptio.md), and it also causes the target to flinch.  With enough [DEX](Stats.md), you could essentially immobilize a target.
*  [Turn Undead](Turn_Undead.md) is also a niche skill, but can deal massive damage.  Note that in WoE, however, it will not kill a target, just deal massive damage (about 85% of their HP).


## [INT](Stats.md)/[DEX](Stats.md) ME

* STR: 1
* AGI: 1
* VIT: 1-40
* INT: 105-125
* DEX: 110-120
* LUK: 1

The [INT](Stats.md)/[DEX](Stats.md) ME High Priest build is the staple [Magnus Exorcismus](Magnus_Exorcismus.md) build with a kick. They can solo well in certain places with a lot of [Demon](Demon.md) or [Undead](Undead.md) thanks to [Assumptio](Assumptio.md) boosting their fragile build. Also thanks to the added statpoints, they can optionally get by with less [INT](Stats.md) or [DEX](Stats.md) for some added [VIT](Stats.md).

Unless you pick up a lot of [VIT](Stats.md), steer clear from WoE, as this build will be stunlocked.


## [INT](Stats.md)/[VIT](Stats.md) Support

* STR: 1
* AGI: 1
* VIT: 100
* INT: 100+
* DEX: 40-100 (Non-WoE builds do not need Dex as much as WoE builds)
* LUK: 1

The [INT](Stats.md)/[VIT](Stats.md) support High Priest is a staple build for non-WoE players.  The high SP pool means SP is rarely if ever an issue, their [Heal](Heal.md) amount will easily top 3000, and with 100 VIT without foods, they will sit with a sturdy 12-14k HP.

[DEX](Stats.md) is the major varying factor in this build.  No [DEX](Stats.md) will mean your [Assumptio](Assumptio.md) cast time is dismal, and buffing becomes more of an ordeal that holds up the party.  Additionally, the ability to throw up a level 1 [Assumptio](Assumptio.md) in under 1 second if it drops is a life saver.

[Sanctuary](Sanctuary.md) 7 is a nice skill to have in order to speed up the buffing process.  By placing one down you can begin casting [Blessing](Blessing.md), [Increase AGI](Increase_AGI.md), [Assumptio](Assumptio.md) and any other skills that need to be up.

## [DEX](Stats.md)/[VIT](Stats.md)/[INT](Stats.md)

* STR: 1
* AGI: 1
* VIT: 80-100
* INT: 78-84+ (base)
* DEX: 80-100
* LUK: 1

This build tries to combine the best of both worlds: they get fairly high [VIT](Stats.md) like a support build, but also decent [DEX](Stats.md). While they'll have slower cast times than a ME High Priest, they will be more durable and status resistant because of their added [VIT](Stats.md). The build heavily relies on the added benefits of [Meditatio](Meditatio.md) due to their lack of [INT](Stats.md), although they still have much better [SP](SP.md) Regen and Heal-Power than regular Priests.

They tend to get skills that can be useful during [WoE](War_of_Emperium.md), like [Safety Wall](Safety_Wall.md) and [Sanctuary](Sanctuary.md). They also generally have lower levels of [Gloria](Gloria.md) (Lv. 1-2) and [Magnificat](Magnificat.md) (Lv. 3).


## [AGI](Stats.md)-based Battle High Priest

* STR: 70-100+
* AGI: 80-100+
* VIT: 20-50+
* INT: 1-20
* DEX: 40-75+
* LUK: 1-30+

This build is the staple of the old battle priests before the 2nd jobs. This build is very rare in WoE/PvP scenarios since they don't have skills to amplify attack, but are flexible characters in PvM scenarios due to their ability to both support and fight at the same time. Support-wise, battle builds usually have the same skills as support builds, although the lower SP pool and cast time reduces the efficiency. [Signum Crucis](Signum_Crusis.md) is optional. Due to their nature of using [Aspersio](Aspersio.md) and [Demon Bane](Demon_Bane.md), battle builds are commonly oriented towards fighting [Demon](Demon.md) race monsters. [Mace Mastery](Mace_Mastery.md) is an optional bonus.

Status builds may vary greatly depending on the preferred playing style. The only common similarity is high [AGI](Stats.md). [STR](Stats.md) is needed for attack strength, as there are no offensive melee skills. [DEX](Stats.md) is required for [Hit](Stats.md) and cast time. Some builds are [Crit](Stats.md) oriented, leveraging specialized weapons and cards, as well as [Gloria](Gloria.md). The amount of [VIT](Stats.md) and [INT](Stats.md) taken depends on hunting locations and intended playing style.
