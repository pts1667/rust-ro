# Dispell

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Sage
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** ([Sage](Sage.md)) [Spell Breaker](Spell_Breaker.md) Lv. 3
- **SP cost:** None
- **Cast time:** 2 seconds
- **Delay:** None ([ASPD](ASPD.md))
- **Target:** Enemy
- **Range:** Magic
- **Catalyst:** 1 Yellow Gemstone


## Description

Cancel all buff effects, including status ailments, on the target by a chance. Each successful cast consumes 1 Yellow Gemstone.

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chance of Success (%) | 60 | 70 | 80 | 90 | 100 |


## Applications

Dispell is not widely used in PVM environments, but is a heavily favored skill ini [PvP](PvP~d29d2.md). Most [Classes](Classes.md) have specific buffs and usable items that ensure their efficiency, and Dispell is capable of removing many of them. The opponent is therefore forced to continue with limited abilities or re-buff themselves. Certain classes have skills that *require* a specific buff to be active, such as [Monk](Monk.md)'s [Guillotine Fist](Guillotine_Fist.md) which requires [Fury](Fury.md) to be active, or [Paladin](Paladin.md)'s [Martyr's Reckoning](Martyr's_Reckoning.md) which is a self-buff until it is consumed. This allows dispell to temporarily disable such classes effectively.

Most Sages interested in [PVP](PvP~d29d2.md) will want a high level of dispell. It can, however, be simply recast if an attempt fails, making lower levels of dispel still viable.


## Notes

Dispell works on guild mates and allies in [PvP](PvP~d29d2.md) environment when forced with shift. Party members can also be targeted in non-PVP areas.

If skill is not mastered, target [MDEF](Stats.md) lowers the chance of skill works.

There are certain buffs which can not be dispelled:
* Chemical Protection skills of [Alchemist](Alchemist.md)s
* [Divest](Status_Effects.md) status of [Rogue](Rogue.md) skills
* [Enchant Deadly Poison](Enchant_Deadly_Poison.md)
* [Hindsight](Hindsight.md)
* [Ninja Aura](Ninja_Aura.md)
* Players using GTB cannot be dispelled
* [Power Up](NPC_POWERUP.md)
* [Stalker](Stalker.md)s buffed with the [Spirit of Rogue](Rogue_Spirit.md) are immune
* [Soul Linker](Soul_Linker.md)s are naturally immune
* [Shattering Strike](Shattering_Strike.md)
* [TaeKwon Kid](TaeKwon_Kid.md)'s [Mild Wind](Mild_Wind.md), [Tumbling](Tumbling.md), and kick stances
* [TaeKwon Master](TaeKwon_Master.md)'s Heat skills
* Walking Speed boost gained from items
* [Cart Boost](Cart_Boost.md)
* Food Items purchased from the [Kafra Shop](Kafra_Shop.md)
* [Gunslinger](Gunslinger.md)'s self buffs


## Etymology

Although the correct English spelling would be "Dispel"<http://www.thefreedictionary.com/dispel><http://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/dispel>, kRO's skillnametable.txt lists it as SA_DISPELL, where SA stands for [Sage](Sage.md). Subsequently it has been translated as Dispell for iRO. For consistency's sake the article uses iRO's spelling.
