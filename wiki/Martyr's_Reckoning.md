# Martyr's Reckoning

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Paladin
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** ([Swordman](Swordman.md)) [Endure](Endure.md) Lv. 1, ([Crusader](Crusader.md)) [Sacrifice](Sacrifice.md) Lv. 3
- **SP cost:** 100
- **HP cost:** 9%
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** [ASPD](ASPD.md)
- **Duration:** Until 5 melee hits are dealt or dispelled
- **Target:** Self/Foe (buff self then attack foe)
- **Range:** Melee
- **Element:** Neutral


## Information

Inflict high damage for the next few normal attacks, while sacrificing [HP](Max_HP.md). Once Martyr's Reckoning is cast, the character will lose an amount of health equal to 9% of the caster's [Max HP](Max_HP.md) upon executing the next five attacks. However, these 5 attacks will have enhanced damage. Martyr's Reckoning has no time duration.

Martyr's Reckoning is an instant cast self-buff like [Guard](Guard.md) or [Shield Reflect](Shield_Reflect.md). Upon casting 100 [SP](SP.md) is consumed and the buff will last forever until the 5 hits are consumed or the buff is removed. Re-logging, a sage's [Dispel](Dispell.md), dying, or casting [Battle Chant](Battle_Chant.md) will remove the buff.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| HP Consumption Per Hit (%) | 9 | 9 | 9 | 9 | 9 |
| Damage Multiplier | 1 | 1.1 | 1.2 | 1.3 | 1.4 |


## Damage Factors

*  Damage is increased according to [Max HP](Max_HP.md), skill level and  weapon +% damage cards, Battlegrounds Assaulter Spear  being the best weapon for [WoE](War_of_Emperium.md)/[PVP](PvP~d29d2.md). [ATK](Stats.md) does not affect the  damage.
*  Cards increasing the damage will not affect the amount of HP sacrificed.
*  It is always [Neutral](Neutral.md) element. Meaning Ghostring and [Neutral](Neutral.md) resistances reduces its damage. Using a weapon elemental endow does NOT allow it to bypass Neutral resistances.
*  Reduction gears like Cranial and Poo lower the damage.
*  [Magnum Break](Magnum_Break.md)'s Fire damage bonus does increase its damage.
*  The Sign accessory boosts MR's damage by 5%, and Battlegrounds Swordman's Medal of Honor only the boosts with +500 HP part(its attack 5% part does not).
*  [Shield Reflect](Shield_Reflect.md) reflects its damage.
*  It does suffer skill damage penalty during [WoE](War_of_Emperium.md).


## Notes

*  Other skills can be used after turning MR on. Those skills will not gain the damage multiplier.
*  The player loses 9% [HP](Max_HP.md) based on [Max HP](Max_HP.md) after each swing. Meaning 5 swings equal 45% HP lost.
*  It ignores [FLEE](FLEE.md) and [DEF](DEF.md) of the target.
*  It can be [Perfectly Dodged](Perfect_Dodge.md)
*  The buff allows usage within [Classical Pluck](Classical_Pluck.md) areas if casted outside it.
*  Upon hitting the Emperium with it, it does no damage, but still takes 9% [HP](Max_HP.md) each swing.
*  Foes inside [Safety Wall](Safety_Wall.md) take no damage from MR, but the caster loses 9% [HP](Max_HP.md) each swing regardless.
*  [Ice Pick](Ice_Pick.md) does not affect the damage.
*  Baphomet Card does not give splash damage for hits.
*  Can be used on Boss Monsters.
*  Turning MR on and then casting [Gloria Domini](Gloria_Domini.md), MR can still be used during GD's delay.
*  [Counter Instinct](Counter_Instinct.md) has a chance of halving MR's damage and reflecting it back upon the caster if it is being used with a Sword or Dagger type weapon.
