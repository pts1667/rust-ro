# Shield Reflect

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Crusader
- **Secondary class:** Mercenary
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 10
- **Prerequisites:** ([Crusader](Crusader.md)) [Guard](Guard.md) Lv. 5, [Smite](Smite.md) Lv. 3, [Shield Boomerang](Shield_Boomerang.md) Lv. 3
- **SP cost:** (30 + (Skill level*5))
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** 1 second
- **Duration:** 300 seconds
- **Target:** Self (transfers to party members through [Sacrifice](Sacrifice.md))
- **Formula name:** Equipment
- **Formula value:** Shield


## Description

Reflect a fraction of the damage received by the caster back at the enemy for the skill's duration. Each successful hit of the reflected damage is considered as an melee attack, and will trigger autocasts that are per attack based.

| Level | Reflected Damage | SP Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 13% | 35 |
| 2 | 16% | 40 |
| 3 | 19% | 45 |
| 4 | 22% | 50 |
| 5 | 25% | 55 |
| 6 | 28% | 60 |
| 7 | 31% | 65 |
| 8 | 34% | 70 |
| 9 | 37% | 75 |
| 10 | 40% | 80 |


## Information

* Requires the caster to be wielding a shield.
