# Archer Skill Quest


## Arrow Crafting Quest

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Job level requirement:** 30
- **Class requirement:** Archer
- **Item requirement:** 7 Mushroom Spore, 1 Red Potion, 20 Resin, 41 Tough Scalelike Stem, 13 Trunk
- **Quest reward:** Ability to use [Arrow Crafting](Arrow_Crafting.md)

1. Talk to Roberto at the Morroc Ruins (moc ruins 118, 99) (morocc 121, 109, euRO). Deliver the following items to him:

* 7 Mushroom Spore
* 1 Red Potion
* 20 Resin
* 41 Tough Scalelike Stem (Pointed Scales, euRO)
* 13 Trunk


## Arrow Repel Quest

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Job level requirement:** 35
- **Class requirement:** Archer
- **Item requirement:** 36 Banana Juice, 10 Bill of Birds, 2 Emerald, 10 Tentacle, 3 Yoyo Tail
- **Quest reward:** Ability to use [Arrow Repel](Arrow_Repel.md)

1. Talk to Jason (103, 63) in [Payon](Payon.md). Deliver him the following items to learn the skill:

* 36 Banana Juice
* 10 Bill of Birds
* 2 Emerald
* 10 Tentacle
* 3 Yoyo Tail
