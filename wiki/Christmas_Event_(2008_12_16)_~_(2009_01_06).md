# Christmas Event (2008/12/16) ~ (2009/01/06)

> **Note:** Not implemented on iRO Classic.


## Christmas Carols

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** Unknown
- **Item requirement:** 10 Wooden Block (Trunk), 1 Hammer of Blacksmith, 1 Jubilee, 10 Sticky Mucus, 1 3-carat Diamond (1st time); 6 Piece of Singing Crystal
- **Item reward:** Christmas Musicbox (1st time) and Buche De Noel

1.  Speak with Carolling ([Prontera](Prontera.md) 226,306) in front of Prontera Church. Select *Carols*.
1.  She asks you to bring her 6 Piece of Singing Crystal.
1.  After returning with the Fragments, Carolling then asks you to bring 10 Wooden Block (Trunk), 1 Hammer of Blacksmith, 1 Jubilee, 10 Sticky Mucus, and 1 3-carat Diamond.
1.  Talk to her again after collecting the items to receive a Christmas Musicbox and a Buche De Noel.

The Musicbox is an Accessory which enables use of the [Christmas Carol](Christmas_Carol.md) skill when equipped.  The skill plays the first part of the song "We Wish You a Merry Christmas".

You can repeat this part of quest, but you will receive only a Buche De Noel for exchange of 6 Piece of Singing Crystal.

## Gears

Two limited-time items are obtainable during the event. Both items were intended to be removed on **December 30, 2008**, but instead remained until the end of the event itself. Antonio's Santa Hat was still available from Xmas Gifts for a while, but didn't provide any effect. Remaining hats and drops were fixed later *(not all servers have that fix)*.


## Santa's Beard

A beard that seems to make you let out a guffaw.
Increases defense on [Brute](Brute.md) monsters by 5%. [DEF](DEF.md)+5


## Antonio's Santa Hat

A hat that Antonio used to wear.
Increases the resistance against [Demi-human](Demi_Human.md), [Devil](Demon.md), and [Angelic](Angel.md) mobs by 5%.
When you attack, there's a low chance that it autocasts Lv. 10 [Blessing](Blessing.md) and a chance of casting Lv. 3 [Heal](Heal.md) on its user. When you kill mobs with this hat on, there's a low chance that they will drop Xmas presents.


## Wedding in Lutie

1.  Speak to Wedding Staff at [Lutie](Lutie.md) (90,265).
1.  Apply for Wedding.
  * Grooms will need 600,000 [Zeny](Zeny.md), 1 Diamond Ring, and a Tuxedo.
  * Brides will need 400,000 [Zeny](Zeny.md), 1 Diamond Ring, and a Wedding Dress.
1.  Speak to Santa Pastor (96,270) to become [married](Marriage_System.md).
