# Gloria

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Priest
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 5 (Fixed)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Priest](Priest.md)) [Kyrie Eleison](Kyrie_Eleison.md) Lv. 4, [Magnificat](Magnificat.md) Lv. 3
- **SP cost:** 20
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** 2 seconds
- **Duration:** [(Skill Level*5)+5] seconds
- **Target:** Self


## Description

Temporarily endow the caster and their [Party](Party.md) members with an additional 30 [LUK](Stats.md) for this skill's duration.

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Skill Duration | 10 sec | 15 sec | 20 sec | 25 sec | 30 sec |
