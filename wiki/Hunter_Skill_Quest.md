# Hunter Skill Quest


## Phantasmic Arrow Quest

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Job level requirement:** 40
- **Class requirement:** Hunter
- **Item requirement:** 5 Cursed Rubies, 5 Harpy Feathers, 30 Pet Food
- **Quest reward:** Ability to use [Phantasmic Arrow](Phantasmic_Arrow.md)

1. In the north end of the [Archer Village](Archer_Village.md), you need to talk to two [NPC](NPC.md)s, Arpesto (pay arche, 109,169) and Reidin Corse. They will request the following items: 5 Cursed Rubies, 5 Harpy Feathers, 30 Pet Food.

2. Bring them these items, and they will teach you [Phantasmic Arrow](Phantasmic_Arrow.md).
