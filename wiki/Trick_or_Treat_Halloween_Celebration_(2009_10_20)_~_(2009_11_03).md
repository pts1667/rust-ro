# Trick or Treat Halloween Celebration (2009/10/20) ~ (2009/11/03)

> **Note:** Not implemented on iRO Classic.

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** 45
- **Item requirement:** 20 Jack o' Pumpkins
- **Item reward:** Weird Pumpkin Hat
- **External:** <http://iro.ragnarokonline.com/News/Event/eventContent.asp?mNum=3&cid=104&page=1>


## Pumpkin Hat

The Pumpkin Hat Researcher in [Prontera](Prontera.md) at (152, 192) will make a Weird Pumpkin Hat for you if you bring him 20 Jack o' Pumpkins.


## Trick-or-treater

1. Walk near any of the following locations in [Prontera](Prontera.md) until a voice asks you if you want a trick or treat:

* (270, 350) (in the graveyard)
* (234, 310) (in front of the church)
* (156, 321) (by the northern [Kafra](Kafra.md) employee)
* (156, 283) (by the handshake monument)
* (142, 214) (by the north-west side of the fountain)
* (151, 173)
* (134, 125)
* (220, 72)

**Note**: Walking near any of the above locations will reveal the trick-or-treater at (151, 173), which allows other people to talk with him. Waiting near this location is the easiest way to begin the conversation.

**Note**: This list is currently incomplete.

2. Ask for a treat, then give the trick-or-treater one of any of the following items:

* Candy
* Candy Cane
* Well-Baked Cookie

The trick-or-treater bestows you with a bonus of 5 points to all of your [Stats](Stats.md) and 15 points to your [Flee](Stats.md), which lasts for 30 minutes.


## Monster summoning

Talk with the Halloween Wizard, who can be found in the following locations:

* [Prontera](Prontera.md) (156, 195) (by the fountain)
* [Geffen](Geffen.md) (133, 122) (by the east side of Geffen Tower)
* [Payon](Payon.md) (156, 196) (north of the armory)
* [Alberta](Alberta.md) (96, 57) (west of the center Kafra employee)
* [Aldebaran](Al_De_Baran.md) (146, 122) (by the center Kafra employee)

He offers to play a trick on someone for you by summoning monsters in another town if you bring him any of the following items:

* Fabric
* Worn Fabric
* Jack o' Pumpkins
* Crushed Pumpkins

You can only summon one monster per item, up to a maximum of 100 monsters at a time.


## Notes

* Weird Pumpkin Hats add 5 [DEF](Stats.md), 5 [MDEF](Stats.md), and have a low chance to cause monsters to drop Pumpkin Pie.
* Jack o' Pumpkins can be purchased from the [Nifflheim](Nifflheim.md) tool shop.
* Halloween Whispers and Halloween Dark Lords are passive and drop trick Gift Boxes. Halloween Whispers also drop Worn Fabric.
* The trick Gift Boxes either contain low-value items or cause random effects when opened.
