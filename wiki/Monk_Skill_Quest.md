# Monk Skill Quest


## Spiritual Bestowment Quest

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Job level requirement:** N/A
- **Class requirement:** Monk
- **Quest reward:** Ability to use [Spiritual Bestowment](Spiritual_Bestowment.md)

1. Enter the building at the center of the St. Capitolina Abbey (prt_monk 192, 171), and greet the elderly man there (monk_test 316, 68). Next, a 3 o'clock from here, outside the building, you should see an NPC "Apprentice Monk" (prt_monk 270, 197). If you talk to him with 0 Weight, he will teach you [Spiritual Bestowment](Spiritual_Bestowment.md).


## Excruciating Palm Quest

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Job level requirement:** N/A
- **Class requirement:** Monk
- **Item requirement:** 20 Stems, 3 Shoots
- **Quests required:** Completed [Spiritual Bestowment Quest](Monk_Skill_Quest.md)
- **Quest reward:** Ability to use [Excruciating Palm](Excruciating_Palm.md)

1. Head to the old man (monk_test 316, 68) in the center building of the St. Capitolina Abbey (prt_monk 192, 171). He requires two things before he will teach you the skill.

2. First, you must learn [Spiritual Bestowment](Spiritual_Bestowment.md), and second, you must gather items dropped by Mandragoras. Return to the man with 20 Stems and 3 Shoots, and he will then teach you [Excruciating Palm](Excruciating_Palm.md).
