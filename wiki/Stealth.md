# Stealth

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Stalker
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** ([Thief](Thief.md)) [Hiding](Hiding.md) Lv. 5, ([Rogue](Rogue.md)) [Stalk](Stalk.md) Lv. 3
- **SP cost:** Cast: 10 Use: 10 + 2*SkillLV per 10 sec., ([WoE](War_of_Emperium.md)) 50 + 10*SkillLV per 10 sec.,
- **Cast time:** 1 second
- **Delay:** None
- **Duration:** Sustained (Limited by SP)
- **Target:** Self


## Description

Hides the Stalker and makes it undetectable by all detecting skills. Makes the Stalker unable to attack in this mode and and will become visible if SP runs out. Any [AoE](Area_of_Effect.md) attack or spell will reveal the Stalker. The Stalker leaves shoe prints behind as the skill is used. Walking speed is reduced during this mode and is increased with higher Skill Levels. During [WoE](War_of_Emperium.md), the SP cost is 5x as much. Also gives the user a [STR](Stats.md) bonus for 30 seconds

| Level | Walk Speed (%) | SP Cost<br> / 10 sec. | WoE SP Cost<br> / 10 sec. | +STR Bonus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 70 | 12 | 60 | 1 |
| 2 | 75 | 14 | 70 | 2 |
| 3 | 80 | 16 | 80 | 4 |
| 4 | 85 | 18 | 90 | 8 |
| 5 | 90 | 20 | 100 | 16 |


## Notes

[Rogue Spirit](Rogue_Spirit.md) imbues the Stalker with special Stealth buffs, such as the STR bonus from Stealth lasts 300 seconds, and increased walking speed.
