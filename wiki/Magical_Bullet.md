# Magical Bullet

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Gunslinger
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 1
- **Prerequisites:** ([Gunslinger](Gunslinger.md)) [Coin Flip](Coin_Flip.md) Lv. 1
- **Target:** Foe
- **Range:** Ranged
- **Catalyst:** 1 [Coin](Coin_Flip.md)


## Description

Consume 1 coin to fire a magic shot which does not actually use any bullets, inflicting an amount of [Ghost](Ghost.md) property damage equal to the caster's [ATK](Stats.md)+[MATK](Stats.md).
