# Blitz Beat

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Hunter
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 5 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Hunter](Hunter.md)) [Falconry Mastery](Falconry_Mastery.md) Lv. 1
- **SP cost:** (7 + (Skill level*3))
- **Cast time:** 1.5 seconds
- **Delay:** 1 second
- **Target:** Enemy
- **Range:** 5 + [Vulture's Eye](Vulture's_Eye.md)
- **Area:** 3x3
- **Element:** Neutral


## Description

Blitz Beat uses the [Falcon](Falconry_Mastery.md) to attack an enemy and all surrounding foes within a 3*3 cell area. When casted manually, enemies within a 3*3 cell area around the target will receive the same amount of damage as the targeted enemy. This skill will also go off passively.

| Number of Hits | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| Required SP | 10 | 13 | 16 | 19 | 22 |


## Information

* Requires a Falcon.
* Blitz Beat is always [Neutral](Neutral.md) [Element](Element.md) but will bypass Raydric cards in [PvP](PvP~d29d2.md) if elemental arrows are equipped.
* Damage is based on [INT](Stats.md), [DEX](Stats.md), and [Steel Crow](Steel_Crow.md).
* [Steel Crow](Steel_Crow.md) adds 6 points per level, up to 60.
* Each hit delivers: 80 + 6*(Steel Crow lvl) + 2*Floor(INT/2) + 2*Floor(DEX/10)
* Cards increasing [ATK](Stats.md) will not affect the damage of Blitz Beat and it will not inflict status ailments.
* Blitz Beat will bypass [Guard](Guard.md).
* Blitz Beat will bypass [Defending Aura](Defending_Aura.md) in [PvP](PvP~d29d2.md) and [WoE](War_of_Emperium.md).


## Auto-Blitz Beat

* Only set off from normal attacks with a bow. Attacking with skills, daggers, or fists will not trigger it.
* May be triggered even if the attack misses or is blocked by a skill. Basically, a chance for every arrow.
* For every 3 points of [LUK](Stats.md) there is an additional 1% chance Auto-Blitz Beat will be triggered.
* Does not consume SP, has no cast time, and has no cast delay.
* Auto-Blitz may occur during a cast delay.
* The number of times Auto-Blitz Beat will hit its enemy depends on the level of Blitz Beat and the job level of the character. Job levels 1-9 allow it to hit up to one time, while job levels 40+ allow the maximum of 5 hits if level 5 Blitz Beat is attained.
* The damage dealt is divided by the number of enemies Auto-Blitz Beat hits. For example, if it hits 3 enemies at once each enemy would receive 1/3 of the damage.
