# B.S Sacramenti

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Priest
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** ([Priest](Priest.md)) [Aspersio](Aspersio.md) Lv. 5, [Gloria](Gloria.md) Lv. 3
- **SP cost:** 20 + 10*2
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** N/A
- **Duration:** (Skill Level*40) seconds
- **Target:** Ground
- **Area:** 3x3
- **Element:** Holy


## Description

Endows the [Holy](Holy.md) property on the armor within a 3*3 cell area for this skill's duration. 2 [Acolyte](Acolyte.md) class type characters need to be next to the caster, on either side in a straight horizontal line (default camera view), in order for B.S. Sacramenti to be cast. Requires 20 [SP](SP.md) to cast, and drains 10 [SP](SP.md) from both of [Acolyte](Acolyte.md)-class characters. [Undead](Undead.md) and [Demon](Demon.md) monsters within the range of this skill will be damaged by the [Holy](Holy.md) property at half of your [Heal](Heal.md) amount.

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Duration | 40 sec | 80 sec | 120 sec | 160 sec | 200 sec |


## Information

*  Full name: Benedictio Sanctissimi Sacramenti. (Translation: Benediction of the most Sacred Sacrament.)
*  Even though B.S Sacramenti requires you to have 2 [Acolyte](Acolyte.md)-classes next to you, the skill will still work even if they are [hidden](Hiding.md), dead, not in the same guild and un[partied](Party.md). The only requirement is for them to be next to you in a horizontal line.
*  The skill will fail if any of the acolytes has under 10 SP
*  Cannot be cast on [Magnetic Earth](Magnetic_Earth.md)
*  Can be cast using [Game Master](Game_Master.md) instead of [Acolyte](Acolyte.md)-class character.
*  Unequipping armor doesn't cancel this skill, so elemental armor is rendered useless when under the skill's effects.
