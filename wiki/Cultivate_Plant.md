# Cultivate Plant

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Biochemist
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 2
- **Prerequisites:** None
- **SP cost:** 10
- **Target:** Ground
- **Catalyst:** 1 Mushroom Spore (Level 1) or 1 Stem (Level 2)


## Description

This skill creates a random type of mushroom or plant with a 50% success rate. Level 1 creates mushrooms and level 2 creates plants.


## Notes

* This skill can't be used on an occupied cell.
* This skill can't be used inside [War of Emperium](War_of_Emperium.md) castles.
* Plants and mushrooms created by this skill disappear after 5 minutes, even if they've been transformed into something else.
