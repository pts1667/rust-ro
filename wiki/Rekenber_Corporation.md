# Rekenber Corporation

Evil corporation bent on (probably!) world domination!! Has ties to the faction in Arunafeltz which is trying to mobilize against Rune-Midgarts.  Created the infamous Biolabs, and performed horrible experiments on unwilling participants.  Many [Quests](Quests.md) revolve around Rekenber. Their headquarters is located in the city of [Lighthalzen](Lighthalzen.md).
