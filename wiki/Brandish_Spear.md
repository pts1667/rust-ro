# Brandish Spear

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Knight
- **Secondary class:** Mercenary
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 10
- **SP cost:** 12
- **Prerequisites:** ([Knight](Knight.md)) [Spear Stab](Spear_Stab.md) Lv. 3, [Peco Peco Ride](Peco_Peco_Ride.md) Lv. 1
- **Cast time:** 1 second (Not Interruptible)
- **Delay:** 1 second (Reduced by [DEX](Stats.md))
- **Target:** Foe
- **Range:** Melee
- **Area:** Depends on skill level
- **Knockback:** 2 cells
- **Formula name:** Weapon
- **Formula value:** Spear


## Description

Attacks an area of ememies around the caster.
The area of effect increases in range at skill levels 4, 7, and 10.
The attack has a minimum [ATK](Stats.md) bonus of 20% per skill level, but damage for each enemy increases the closer they are to the caster.

**Area of effect by Skill Level (Attack facing North)**

| Column 1 | Column 2 | Column 3 | Column 4 | Column 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|   | 10 | 10 | 10 |   |
| 7+ | 7+ | 7+ | 7+ | 7+ |
| 4+ | 4+ | 4+ | 4+ | 4+ |
| 1+ | 1+ | 1+ | 1+ | 1+ |
| 1+ | 1+ | Player | 1+ | 1+ |


## Information

* Requires the player to be wielding a Spear.
* Requires the player to be mounted on a [Peco Peco](Peco_Peco_Ride.md).
* Requires a targeted enemy to determine direction, but the area of effect is based on the position of the player.
*  Knock-back does not occur during [War of Emperium](War_of_Emperium.md).
*  The player's weapon cannot be [Divested](Status_Effects.md) while casting.  The player cannot change weapons during this time, but attempts by enemies to divest or break the weapon will fail ([Full Divestment](Full_Divestment.md) can still succeed on other equipment).
