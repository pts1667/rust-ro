# Contract with Kafra

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Guild
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** 1
- **Prerequisites:** [Official Guild Approval](Official_Guild_Approval.md) Lv. 1


## Description

Sign a contract with the [Kafra](Kafra.md) Headquarters in [Al De Baran](Al_De_Baran.md). Through the contract, the guild can hire a Kafra employee for their territory. The Kafra Service is only available to the members of the guild that currently holds the castle.

* Allows you to teleport to the [Town](categories/Towns.md) that holds the area of that castle you are in and access your storage.
* Needs to activated by the guild master through the guild manager [NPC](NPC.md). Since it resets everytime ownership of the castle changes.
