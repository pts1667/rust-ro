# Earth Spike

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Wizard
- **Secondary class:** Sage
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 5 Selectable
- **Prerequisites:** ([Mage](Mage.md)) [Stone Curse](Stone_Curse.md) Lv. 1, ([Sage](Sage.md)) [Endow Quake](Endow_Quake.md) Lv. 1
- **SP cost:** 10 + 2*Skill Level
- **Cast time:** 0.7*Skill Level
- **Delay:** 0.8 seconds
- **Target:** Foe
- **Range:** Magic
- **Element:** Earth


## Description

Hits the targeted enemy with 1 Earth Element Spike per SkillLV for 100%*MATK damage each. Although it is similar to the other [Mage](Mage.md) skills, **it is not a bolt skill**.


## Notes

*  Earth Spike is the only single-target spell (cast by players) capable of hitting enemies in the Hidden status.  It must be cast while the enemy is visible.
*  Since Earth Spike is not a bolt skill (despite the homunculus's [Caprice](Caprice.md) skill triggering Earth Spike as if it was a bolt), it is completely unaffected by [Double Bolt](Double_Bolt.md).
*  In addition to level 1 [Stone Curse](Stone_Curse.md), [Sages](Sage.md) also have a prerequisite of level 1 [Endow Quake](Endow_Quake.md). [Wizards](Wizard.md) only require level 1 [Stone Curse](Stone_Curse.md).

This skill is obtained via the following items:

*  Level 3 Earth Spike - Level 3; one time usage
*  Level 5 Earth Spike - Level 5; one time usage
*  Elemental Sword - Level 3; autocast when attacking or using Level 3 [Lightening Bolt](Lightening_Bolt.md)

This skill is enhanced with the following items:

*  Pitman Card - Damage +5%
