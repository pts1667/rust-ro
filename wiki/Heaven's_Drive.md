# Heaven's Drive

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Wizard
- **Secondary class:** Sage
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 5 Selectable
- **Prerequisites:** ([Wizard](Wizard.md)) [Earth Spike](Earth_Spike.md) Lv. 3; ([Sage](Sage.md)) [Earth Spike](Earth_Spike.md) Lv. 1
- **SP cost:** (24 + (Skill Level*2))
- **Cast time:** 1 second*Skill Level
- **Delay:** 1 seconds
- **Target:** Ground
- **Range:** Magic
- **Area:** 5x5
- **Element:** Earth


## Description

Summon numerous [Earth](Earth.md) Spikes from the ground within a 25 cell area that will damage enemies. Each cast is followed by a 1 second Skill Delay.

This spell is basically a 5x5 [Area of Effect version](Area_of_Effect.md) of [Earth Spike](Earth_Spike.md).  At level 5 it will hit 5 times.


## Information

* Like [Earth Spike](Earth_Spike.md), this spell can hit Hidden enemies. However, since the enemy does not need to be targeted to cast this skill, Heaven's Drive is often easier to use than [Earth Spike](Earth_Spike.md).
