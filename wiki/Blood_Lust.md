# Blood Lust

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Amistr
- **Secondary class:** Alchemist
- **Tertiary class:** Homunculus
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 3 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** Evolved and Loyal [Amistr](Amistr.md)
- **Duration:** Varies by Skill Level


## Description

For the skill duration, Amistr's [ATK](Stats.md) is increased and every attack has a certain chance to leech 20% of the damage as [HP](Max_HP.md).

| Level | +ATK (%) | Leech Chance (%) | Cool Down (sec) | Duration (sec) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | +30 | 3 | 300 | 60 |
| 2 | +40 | 6 | 600 | 180 |
| 3 | +50 | 9 | 900 | 300 |
