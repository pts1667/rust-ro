# Wind

Elemental property. Wind is weak to [Earth](Earth.md), and does extra damage to the [Water](Water.md) property. Weapons can be forged with the Wind property using a Rough Wind.

## Cards

Cards concerning the Wind property include:


## Head Gear

* Giant Hornet Card: Add the chance of gaining 'Wind of Verdure' item each time an Insect monster is killed. Add 10% more tolerance against Wind Property Attack.


## Armor

* Dokebi Card: Enchant an Armor with the Wind property. DEF +1
* Steel ChonChon Card: Increase resistance to Wind property attacks by 10%. DEF +2


## Weaponry

* Mandragora Card: Increase damage inflicted upon Wind property monsters by 20%.


## Garment

* Dustiness Card: Increase resistance to the Wind property by 30%. Flee Rate +5
