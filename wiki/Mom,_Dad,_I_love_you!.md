# Mom, Dad, I love you!

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Baby
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 1
- **Prerequisites:** None
- **SP cost:** %10
- **Target:** Parents


## Description

Give's your parent's a 2 minute buff that removes the loss of exp upon dying. This skill must be cast within the line of sight of your parents.
