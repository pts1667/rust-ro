# Meditatio

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** High Priest
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** 10
- **Prerequisites:** ([Priest](Priest.md)) [Increase SP Recovery](Increase_SP_Recovery.md) Lv. 5, [Lex Divina](Lex_Divina.md) Lv. 5, [Aspersio](Aspersio.md) Lv. 3


## Description

Increases the player's [Maximum SP](Max_SP.md) and SP regeneration rate. Meditatio also increases the amount of HP that is restored using the [Heal](Heal.md) skill.

| Level | SP Regeneration (%) | Max SP (%) | Heal Boost (%) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | +3 | +1 | +2 |
| 2 | +6 | +2 | +4 |
| 3 | +9 | +3 | +6 |
| 4 | +12 | +4 | +8 |
| 5 | +15 | +5 | +10 |
| 6 | +18 | +6 | +12 |
| 7 | +21 | +7 | +14 |
| 8 | +24 | +8 | +16 |
| 9 | +27 | +9 | +18 |
| 10 | +30 | +10 | +20 |

The Heal Boost is applied after Base Level and INT, but before equipment.
