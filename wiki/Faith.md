# Faith

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Crusader
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** 10
- **Prerequisites:** None


## Description

Increases Max HP and resistance to [Holy](Holy.md)-property attacks.

| Level | [Max HP](Max_HP.md) Gained | [Holy](Holy.md) Resistance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 200 | +5% |
| 2 | 400 | +10% |
| 3 | 600 | +15% |
| 4 | 800 | +20% |
| 5 | 1000 | +25% |
| 6 | 1200 | +30% |
| 7 | 1400 | +35% |
| 8 | 1600 | +40% |
| 9 | 1800 | +45% |
| 10 | 2000 | +50% |


## Notes

* The holy resistance gained will decrease damage taken from anything of the [Holy](Holy.md) property, such as Holy Light, Silver Arrows, or any physical attacks from a user enchanted with [Aspersio](Aspersio.md).
* It will also reduce the recoil damage from [Grand Cross](Grand_Cross.md).
