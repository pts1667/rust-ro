# Cart Boost

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Mastersmith
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 1
- **Prerequisites:** ([Merchant](Merchant.md)) [Pushcart](Pushcart.md) Lv. 5, [Cart Revolution](Cart_Revolution.md) Lv. 1, [Change Cart](Change_Cart.md) Lv. 1, ([Blacksmith](Blacksmith.md)) [Hilt Binding](Hilt_Binding.md) Lv. 3
- **SP cost:** 20
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** [ASPD](ASPD.md)
- **Duration:** 60 seconds
- **Target:** Self


## Description

Increase the character's movement speed while carrying a Pushcart.


## Information

*  [Decrease AGI](Decrease_AGI.md) and Cart Boost mutually cancel each other out. If you have Cart Boost status and someone successfully decreases your agility, you must use Cart boost once to cancel [Decrease AGI](Decrease_AGI.md) and once more to receive the Cart Boost status again.
*  Movespeed does not stack with [Increase AGI](Increase_AGI.md).
*  [Slow Grace](Slow_Grace.md) does not cancel Cart Boost.
*  [Dispell](Dispell.md) does not cancel Cart Boost.
