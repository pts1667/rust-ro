# Frost Nova

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Wizard
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 10 (Fixed)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Mage](Mage.md)) [Frost Diver](Frost_Diver.md) Lv. 1, ([Wizard](Wizard.md)) [Ice Wall](Ice_Wall.md) Lv. 1
- **SP cost:** (47 - (Skill level*2))
- **Cast time:** 6~4 seconds
- **Delay:** 1 second
- **Target:** Self
- **Area:** 5x5
- **Element:** Water
- **Status:** [Frozen](Status_Effects.md)


## Description

Freeze all enemies around the caster. Frost Nova can freeze the enemies for a maximum of 15 seconds. Monsters that are already [Frozen](Status_Effects.md) cannot be damaged by Frost Nova.
