# Urgent Call

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Guild
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 1
- **Prerequisites:** [Guardian Research](Guardian_Research.md) Lv. 1, [Regeneration](Regeneration.md) Lv. 1
- **SP cost:** None
- **Cast time:** 5 seconds
- **Delay:** 1 second


## Description

All guild members will be teleported next to the Guild Master. Once used, the caster must wait 5 minutes before using this skill again.

* If a guild member is on a loading screen when the skill is cast, they will not be brought to the guild leader.
* Guild members who are dead (ex. If a party of guild members is dead in a dungeon for some reason) will also not be brought to the guild leader.
* The delay associated with this skill is the same delay that is associated with the skills [Restoration](Restoration.md), [Regeneration](Regeneration.md), and [Battle Command](Battle_Command.md). If a guild leader preforms one of these, they cannot preform Urgent Call until the 5 minute delay has enlapsed, and vice versa.
* If the guild leader is a [TaeKwon Kid](TaeKwon_Kid.md) or [TaeKwon Master](TaeKwon_Master.md) class, once they put skill points into [Leap](Leap.md), that leader's recall cast time is doubled (from 5 to 10 seconds).
