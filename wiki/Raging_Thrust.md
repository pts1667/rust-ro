# Raging Thrust

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Monk
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 5 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Monk](Monk.md)) [Raging Quadruple Blow](Raging_Quadruple_Blow.md) Lv. 3
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** 1.3 - [(AGI*0.004) - (DEX*0.002)] seconds
- **Target:** Enemy ([via Combo](Monk.md))
- **Range:** Melee


## Description

Similiar in nature to [Raging Quadruple Blow](Raging_Quadruple_Blow.md) and the third move in combo skill set, Raging Thrust attacks the target with massive force. Raging Thrust must be casted right after a successfully Raging Quadruple Blow, and also requires one [Spirit Sphere](Spirit_Sphere.md) to cast. If the user has 4 Spirit Spheres and is in the Fury state, then the caster can chain [Guillotine Fist](Guillotine_Fist.md) after Raging Thrust.

| 2 | 360 | 12 |
