# Ore Combining

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Item requirement:** 8 (or 12) of 1 type of Elemental Stone
- **Quest reward:** Elemental Stone

1. Talk the Alchemist on the second floor (ama_in02 61, 27) in the [Amatsu](Amatsu.md) Palace (amatsu 85, 235).

2. He offers you a trade of 8 of one type of Elemental Stone for another.

Example:

* 8 Mystic Frozens for 1 Flame Heart.
* 8 Great Nature for 1 Flame Heart.
* 8 Rough Wind for 1 Flame Heart.

**Note**: Turning elemental stones into Rough Wind is more expensive. It will cost 12 Elemental Stones.

* 12 Great Nature for 1 Rough Wind.
* 12 Flame Heart for 1 Rough Wind.
* 12 Mystic Frozen for 1 Rough Wind.
