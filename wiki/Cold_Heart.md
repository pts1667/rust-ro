# Cold Heart

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Guild
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** [Glorious Wounds](Glorious_Wounds.md) Lv. 1
- **Area:** 5x5


## Description

All guild members in a 5x5 area around the Guild Leader will receive a (1*SkillLV) [AGI](Stats.md) bonus.

* Usable outside [WoE](War_of_Emperium.md) period and outside WoE maps.
* The bonus doesn't apply to the Guildmaster.
