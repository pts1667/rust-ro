# Happy Break

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** TaeKwon Kid
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** 10
- **SP cost:** None
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** None
- **Target:** Self
- **Prerequisites:** None


## Description

Same in principle as [Peaceful Break](Peaceful_Break.md), however, this skill recovers SP.


## Information

*  This skill activates when the player is overweight.
*  Base recovery rate increases with [Max SP](Max_SP.md).
*  Each tick occurs every 10 seconds.
*  Doing the /doridori emote gives a bonus +3 [SP](SP.md). This will also place the player under the state of Enjoyable Rest for 30 minutes.
*  Using an "Earth Spike Scroll" while in the Enjoyable Rest state takes 10 [SP](SP.md) and has a small chance of using the scroll up. At level 1, there is a 10% chance the scroll will be consumed, and at level 10, the chance is reduced down to 1%.
*  [SP](SP.md) Recovered = Skill Lvl*3 + Floor(Max SP/50)

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
