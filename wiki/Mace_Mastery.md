# Mace Mastery

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Priest
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** 10 (Fixed)
- **Prerequisites:** None
- **Weapon:** Mace


## Description

Increase damage inflicted with [Mace](Mace.md) class weapons. The additional damage granted by this skill pierces the defense of monsters.
