# Frost Diver

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Mage
- **Secondary class:** Super Novice
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 10 (Fixed)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Mage](Mage.md) & [Super Novice](Super_Novice.md)) [Cold Bolt](Cold_Bolt.md) Lv. 5
- **SP cost:** (26 - Skill level)
- **Cast time:** 0.8 seconds
- **Delay:** 1.5 seconds
- **Target:** Enemy
- **Element:** Water
- **Status:** [Frozen](Status_Effects.md)


## Description

Attack a target with a Water spell that has the chance of freezing it.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| MATK% | 110 | 120 | 130 | 140 | 150 | 160 | 170 | 180 | 190 | 200 |
| SP Cost | 25 | 24 | 23 | 22 | 21 | 20 | 19 | 18 | 17 | 16 |
| Time Frozen | 3 Sec. | 6 Sec. | 9 Sec. | 12 Sec. | 15 Sec. | 18 Sec. | 21 Sec. | 24 Sec. | 27 Sec. | 30 Sec. |
| Freezing Chance | 38% | 41% | 44% | 47% | 50% | 53% | 56% | 59% | 62% | 65% |


## Applications

Frost Diver is a disabling skill, since [Frozen](Status_Effects.md) status stops any monsters immediately. From this point, it is possible to cast a high-damage skill without fearing retaliation from the monster (unless it breaks free). Since a monster turns into [Water](Water.md) element after being frozen, it is most liable to [Wind](Wind.md) attacks such as [Lightning Bolt](Lightening_Bolt.md).

[Undead](Undead.md) and [Boss](Boss_Protocol.md) monsters cannot be frozen but can still be damaged by Frost Diver. If a mage decides to become a [Wizard](Wizard.md), the skill [Storm Gust](Storm_Gust.md) can replace the need of Frost Diver for mobs. The other disabling skill, [Stone Curse](Stone_Curse.md), is also an option.


## Notes

* Target's [Magic Defense](Stats.md) affects the chance of being [Frozen](Status_Effects.md), as well as the duration of the frozen status.
* Enemies frozen by Frost Diver skill become [Water](Water.md) property (level 1) regardless of their original property.
* Frost Diver will not freeze [Boss](Boss_Protocol.md) or [Undead](Undead.md) monsters.
