# Enchant Poison

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Assassin
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 10 (Fixed)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Thief](Thief.md)) [Envenom](Envenom.md) Lv. 1
- **SP cost:** 20
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** 1 second
- **Duration:** (15 + (15*Skill level)) seconds
- **Target:** Self, Party Members
- **Element:** Poison
- **Status:** [Poison](Poison.md)


## Description

Imbues the character's weapon with [Poison](Poison.md) property. Switching or removing weapons removes the effect. Every hit has a chance of causing the Poison status ailment to the target.


## Uses

* The Poison property deals 125% damage to Lv 1 and Lv 2 [Earth](Earth.md), [Fire](Fire.md) and [Wind](Wind.md) property monsters.
* Bypass Raydric carded garments.
* Deals 100% damage to Ghostring carded armors.
