# Demon

Demons are a race of monster. Notably they are one of the two races to ignore all [Hiding](Hiding.md) related skills, which include [Cloaking](Cloaking.md), [Stalk](Stalk.md), [Stealth](Stealth.md), and [Hiding](Hiding.md) iteself. The skill [Blessing](Blessing.md) lowers their [Flee](Stats.md) and [Hit](Stats.md) when cast on them.

[Cards](Card_System.md) concerning the Demon race are:

*  **Ancient Worm Card**: Receive 10% more [Experience](Experience.md) Points from Demon monsters. Receive 20% more damage from Demon monsters.
*  **Diabolic Card**: Gain 5 [SP](SP.md) each time a Demon monster is killed by Physical [Melee](Melee.md) [Attack](Attacks.md). Drain 5 SP as the weapon is unequipped.
*  **Fur Seal Card** ([Acolyte](Acolyte.md) Types Only): Accuracy +10, [Flee](Stats.md) Rate +3. If worn by [Acolyte](Acolyte.md) Class characters, it adds [Critical](Stats.md) +9 on Demon and [Undead](Undead.md) monsters.
*  **Khalitzburg Card**: Reduce damage from Demon monsters by 30%.
*  **Medusa Card**: Gain immunity to the [Stone](Status_Effects.md) status. Reduce damage from Demon monsters by 15%.
*  **Panzer Goblin Card**: Inflict 10% more damage with [Critical](Stats.md) Attack. Critical +7 on Demon monsters.
*  **Raydric Archer Card**: Add the chance of gaining 'Box of Resentment' each time a Demon monster is killed.
*  **Strouf Card**: Increase damage on Demon monsters by 20%.

Equipment concerning the Demon race are:
*  **Angel Wing**: [Headgear](Headgears.md). Reduces damage from Demon monsters by 3%. [AGI](Stats.md) +1, [LUK](Stats.md) +1. [MDEF](Stats.md) +3
*  **Angelic Guard**: [Super Novice](Super_Novice.md) [Shield](Shield.md). Reduces damage from Demon monsters by 5%.
*  **Combat Knife**: Dagger. Increases damage inflicted on [Demi Human](Demi_Human.md) monsters by 10%, but increases damage received by Demon monsters by 10%. Pierces defense of [Demi Human](Demi_Human.md) monsters.
*  **Exorciser**: [Dagger](Dagger.md). Pierces [Defense](DEF.md) of Demon monsters. 5% additional tolerance against Demon monsters. Increases damage from [Demi Human](Demi_Human.md) monsters by 10%.
*  **Holy Robe**: [Armor](Armor.md). Increases resistance to Demon monsters by 15% and resistance against [Shadow](Shadow.md) property by 10%. [MDEF](Stats.md) + 5
*  **Light Epsilon**: Axe. Increases damage inflicted on Demon monsters by 3%. Enables use of Level 3 [Heal](Heal.md).
