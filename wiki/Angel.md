# Angel

Angel is a race of monster. They are fairly rare and most are [Boss Protocol](Boss_Protocol.md). There is no weapon card known to inflict additional physical damage against them.

Cards which concern the angel race are:


## Weapon

* False Angel Card: Gain 5 SP each time an Angel monster is killed by Physical Melee Attack. Drain 5 SP as the weapon is unequipped.


## Shield

* Anubis Card: Reduce damage from Angel monsters by 30%.


## Armor

* Fallen Bishop Hibram: [MATK](Stats.md) + 10%. Maximum SP - 50%. Increases magical damage inflicted on [Demi Human](Demi_Human.md) and Angel monster by 50%.
* Gloom Under Night: Increases physical damage against Angel and [Demon](Demon.md) [Race](Race.md) targets by 40%. Increases physical damage against [Shadow](Shadow.md) and [Holy](Holy.md) [Element](Element.md) targets by 40%.


## Foot Gear

* Christmas Cookie Card: Receive 10% more Experience Points from Angel monsters. Receive 20% more damage from Angel monsters.


## Accessory

* Jing Guai Card: Add the chance of gaining 'Box of Sunlight' item each time Angel monster is killed.

Equipment concerning the Angel race are:
*  **Evil Wing**: Headgear. Reduces damage from Angel monsters by 3%. STR +1. MDEF +2.
