# Double Bolt

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Scholar
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 5 (Fixed)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Sage](Sage.md)) [Hindsight](Hindsight.md) Lv. 1
- **SP cost:** (35 + (Skill Level*5))
- **Cast time:** 2 seconds (not reducible by [DEX](Stats.md))
- **Duration:** 90 seconds
- **Target:** Self


## Description

This skill gives the caster a Doublecasting buff which lasts 90 seconds (this buff is shown on the side of the screen as "Magic×Magic"). While active, all bolt spells have a chance of being cast instantly a second time immediately after the first bolt lands (skill level determines the chance). This skill affects [Fire Bolt](Fire_Bolt.md), [Cold Bolt](Cold_Bolt.md) and [Lightning Bolt](Lightening_Bolt.md), but not [Earth Spike](Earth_Spike.md). Bolts which are cast via [Hindsight](Hindsight.md), scrolls and [Autocast](Autocast.md) items are also affected.

Doublecasting works with the [Soul Linker](Soul_Linker.md) [Sage Spirit](Sage_Spirit.md) skill when used with [Hindsight](Hindsight.md), causing two level 10 bolts to fall when successful.

| Level | Chance | SP Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 40% | 40 |
| 2 | 50% | 45 |
| 3 | 60% | 50 |
| 4 | 70% | 55 |
| 5 | 80% | 60 |


## Notes

* Double Bolt's cast time cannot be reduced by [DEX](Stats.md), however, [Magic Strings](Magic_Strings.md) and [Foresight](Foresight.md) do.
* Despite being triggered by [Caprice](Caprice.md), [Earth Spike](Earth_Spike.md) is not a bolt skill and is unaffected by Double Bolt.
