# Bomb

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Alchemist
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** ([Alchemist](Alchemist.md)) [Prepare Potion](Prepare_Potion.md) Lv. 4
- **SP cost:** 10
- **Cast time:** 1 second
- **Delay:** 0.5 second
- **Duration:** (40 + Skill level) seconds
- **Target:** Ground
- **Range:** 9 cells
- **Area:** 3 x 3
- **Catalyst:** 1 Bottle Grenade


## Description

Target an area and set it on fire by throwing a bottle grenade. The flames will not push back enemies within their range, but they do have a chance of damaging the weapons of the enemies.
*  Each cast requires 1 Bottle Grenade.

| Skill Level | Attack | Weapon Damage Chance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 120% | 1% |
| 2 | 140% | 2% |
| 3 | 160% | 3% |
| 4 | 180% | 4% |
| 5 | 200% | 5% |


## Notes

*  The damage is increased via +ATK cards, and status and +% cards have no effect.
*  Bomb can not be placed next to other bombs within the radius or be stacked on top of another Bomb.
*  Can not be cast directly under an enemy or directly next to yourself.
