# Bash

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Swordman
- **Secondary class:** Super Novice
- **Tertiary class:** Mercenary
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 10 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** None
- **SP cost:** 8/15
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** [ASPD](Stats.md)
- **Target:** Enemy
- **Range:** Melee
- **Status:** [Stun](Status_Effects.md) ([Quest](Swordman_Skill_Quest.md))


## Description

Bash is a skill that increases [ATK](Stats.md) and [Accuracy](Stats.md). It also has a chance to stun opponents once the [quest skill](Quests.md) [Fatal Blow](Fatal_Blow.md) is obtained.

## Information

* Bongun Card accessories give a chance to cast level 1 Bash on an enemy when doing a physical attack. This Bash also pushes the enemy away 5 tiles.
* Cutlus enables the use of level 5 Bash.
* The [Accuracy](Stats.md) gained is a percent of your [hit rate](Stats.md) added to your [hit rate](Stats.md). Therefore, if you have a 67% chance of hitting a monster, level 10 Bash will give you a 100% chance of hitting them: ((50% of 67%) + 67%) or (1.5 x 67)%.
* [Stun](Status_Effects.md) chance inceases with base level.


## Strategies

PvM

* Levelling with Bash is a fast way to level up a [Swordman](Swordman.md), although quite SP-intensive. It adds a lot of damage even to 10-30 [STR](Stats.md) [Swordmen](Swordman.md) and helps compensate for the lack of [hit rate](Stats.md) due to being low level. One may want to get it early on if they have the resources to keep up with the high SP cost.
* There are better skills to [MvP](MVP.md) with rather than Bash.

PvP

* Bash is used in PvP just as it would be in PvM situations.
* Every aspect of Bash can be employed at once or separately: a [Knight](Knight.md) may use it to deal decent damage to a high-[flee](Stats.md) character while rendering its opponent useless by taking advantage of the [stun](Status_Effects.md) chance, whereas a [Grand Cross](Grand_Cross.md) [Crusader](Crusader.md) may wish to use Bash purely to [stun](Status_Effects.md) victims before using [Grand Cross](Grand_Cross.md), so the opponent cannot escape.
* Do not waste time attempting to [stun](Status_Effects.md) high [VIT](Stats.md) characters with Bash.
