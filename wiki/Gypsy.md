## Overview

*[image not archived: GYPSY.PNG‎]*

Everybody loves the Gypsy's dancing, especially her enemies. It's the last thing they see before they go to their graves with a smile on their lips and an arrow through their hearts. The Gypsy is so alluring that hundreds of acts of heroism (and foolishness) have been performed in her name.
With more lithe and agile bodies granted by the gods, Gypsies are hard to resist especially when one begins her dance. Not a single drop of sweat can be seen. Every dance and movement seemingly effortless. Graceful dance is her specialty, dancing gracefully to lure the monster to its death with the newly acquired [Arrow Vulcan](Arrow_Vulcan.md) and
[Tarot Card of Fate](Tarot_Card_of_Fate.md) skills.

## Builds


## PvM/WoE

*  [STR](Stats.md): 1
*  [AGI](Stats.md): 1
*  [VIT](Stats.md): 80-100 (final)
*  [INT](Stats.md): 2x-5x (final)
*  [DEX](Stats.md): 99 (base)
*  [LUK](Stats.md): 1

The staple Gypsy build for people who like to mix [PvM](PvM~a1fb2.md) and [WoE](War_of_Emperium.md). This build takes high [DEX](Stats.md) to capitalize on the skill [Arrow Vulcan](Arrow_Vulcan.md), and high [VIT](Stats.md) for survivability and status resistance. Remaining points go in [INT](Stats.md) to boost their [SP](SP.md) pool and regen. The high natural DEX of Gypsies gives this build easy access to instant cast.


## Full Support

*  [STR](Stats.md): 50+ (base)
*  [AGI](Stats.md): 1
*  [VIT](Stats.md): 90-100 (final)
*  [INT](Stats.md): 60-90 (final)
*  [DEX](Stats.md): 1-4 (base)
*  [LUK](Stats.md): 1

This build focuses on high survivability at the cost of being a pure leech or [Party](Party.md) character.
High VIT for (nearly) [Stun](Status_Effects.md) immunity, high INT for excellent [SP](SP.md), plus great SP Song effectiveness. Keep in mind that INT also increases your recovery rate from SP restoring items. STR for a higher weight limit to carry lots of potions during Sieges. You may want to increase DEX for a more effective [Slow Grace](Slow_Grace.md), but don't go for more than 4 base DEX, which adds a cheap Slow Grace bonus. Cast time of [Tarot Card of Fate](Tarot_Card_of_Fate.md) is poor and you might get interrupted, but you should be in a [Bard](Bard.md)'s [Magic Strings](Magic_Strings.md) if you plan to use it.

There are about 20 skill points left to play around with since you won't get [Arrow Vulcan](Arrow_Vulcan.md) due to your low DEX and [Slinging Arrow](Slinging_Arrow.md) is a less time-robbing skill to inflict statuses on your enemies. Instead you can get more ensembles or you might want more solo songs.


## Marionette Control Slave

*  [STR](Stats.md): 1
*  [AGI](Stats.md): 1
*  [VIT](Stats.md): 1x-3x
*  [INT](Stats.md): 1x-5x
*  [DEX](Stats.md): 8x-9x
*  [LUK](Stats.md): 8x-9x

This build's main purpose is to use [Marionette Control](Marionette_Control.md) on [Blacksmith](Blacksmith.md)s, [Alchemist](Alchemist.md)s, and [Assassin Cross](Assassin_Cross.md)es to maximize their success rates. It may also be set-up to serve as a [Gypsy's Kiss](Gypsy's_Kiss.md) and/or [Mental Sensing](Mental_Sensing.md) slave. Since the build has [DEX](Stats.md), it is possible for it to level itself, especially if it is partied with a [Priest](Priest.md) class. While it can be used in [WoE](War_of_Emperium.md), it lacks the higher [VIT](Stats.md) that is generally preferred.

Being a slave build, this build is not recommended as a main character.


## Pure WoE

*  [STR](Stats.md): 1
*  [AGI](Stats.md): 9x (base)
*  [VIT](Stats.md): 90-100 (final)
*  [INT](Stats.md): 1
*  [DEX](Stats.md): x (remaining points)
*  [LUK](Stats.md): 1

Similar to the Full Support build, this build is about maximum survivability and is also incapable of leveling itself. While the [VIT](Stats.md) is the same, the build drops [INT](Stats.md) since it won't be using [Gypsy's Kiss](Gypsy's_Kiss.md) much, and drops the [STR](Stats.md) due to the availability of Increase Weight Limit tickets in the [Kafra Shop](Kafra_Shop.md). Instead this build favors high [AGI](Stats.md) in order to reduce the [ASPD](ASPD.md) delay of [Dazzler](Dazzler.md) when in a strong [Magic Strings](Magic_Strings.md), reduce movement speed more via [Slow Grace](Slow_Grace.md), and potentially flee melee attackers. Leftover points go into [DEX](Stats.md) in order to somewhat relieve the build's poor cast times.


## AGI PvM

*  [STR](Stats.md): 1
*  [AGI](Stats.md): 7x-9x
*  [VIT](Stats.md): 1x-5x
*  [INT](Stats.md): 2x-5x
*  [DEX](Stats.md): 99 (base)
*  [LUK](Stats.md): 1

This build sacrifices the [VIT](Stats.md)-based survivability that the other builds use in favor of [AGI](Stats.md) and [Flee](Stats.md). [DEX](Stats.md) is maxed for [Arrow Vulcan](Arrow_Vulcan.md), while [INT](Stats.md) is used for [SP](SP.md). Depending on personal preference, builds can have higher [AGI](Stats.md) or put some points in [VIT](Stats.md). The investment in AGI, combined with Arrow Vulcan's burst damage, gives this build the ability to solo mid-level monsters with little trouble, but doesn't help much in high-level dungeons. In [Party](Party.md) situations the build is similar to others, but lacks their survivability. (With the possible exception of Nameless Island, the only endgame dungeon where Flee is viable.) In [WoE](War_of_Emperium.md), this build performs poorly due to its low [HP](Max_HP.md) and status resistance. Perhaps its only redeeming factor is to [Slow Grace](Slow_Grace.md) enemies entering a [Classical Pluck](Classical_Pluck.md).

In terms of skills, AGI Gypsies use skill sets similar to their PvM/WoE cousins. [Arrow Vulcan](Arrow_Vulcan.md) is the staple for leveling, [Dazzler](Dazzler.md), [Slow Grace](Slow_Grace.md), and [Tarot Card of Fate](Tarot_Card_of_Fate.md) for WoE. [Slinging Arrow](Slinging_Arrow.md) can be effective if used properly, due to the build's high DEX and AGI. [Classical Pluck](Classical_Pluck.md) can be used but is better left to the VIT builds. Remaining skill points can be distributed depending on personal preference and what the character will be used for.

This build is rarely used due to the [Dancer](Dancer.md) class' focus on being a WoE support character. While it could potentially be called a pure [Slow Grace](Slow_Grace.md) build, the difference isn't significant enough to justify the downsides in PvP/WoE.
