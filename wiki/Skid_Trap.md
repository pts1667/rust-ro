# Skid Trap

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Hunter
- **Secondary class:** Mercenary
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** None
- **SP cost:** 10
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** [ASPD](ASPD.md) Based
- **Duration:** (360 - (Skill Level*60)) seconds
- **Target:** Ground
- **Range:** Melee
- **Knockback:** (5 + (Skill Level*1))
- **Catalyst:** 1 Trap


## Description

Set a trap that will cause an enemy to slide in a certain direction once it is triggered. The distance that the target slides is determined by the skill's level.

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sliding Range (cells) | 6 | 7 | 8 | 9 | 10 |
| Duration (seconds) | 300 | 240 | 180 | 120 | 60 |
