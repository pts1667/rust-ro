# Monster Activity Researcher Event (2010/04/14) ~ (2010/04/29)

> **Note:** Not implemented on iRO Classic.


## Overview

The following [NPC](NPC.md)s can be temporarily found in Juperos Dungeon. Players can choose to kill 50, 100 or 150 of each set at a time.

* **Note:** *Each quest **is repeatable** so long as the player remains within the level limits.*
* **Note:** *[Mercenary](Mercenary_System.md) kills will **not** increase your kill count!*
