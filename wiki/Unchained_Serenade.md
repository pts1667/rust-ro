# Unchained Serenade

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Bard
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** ([Bard](Bard.md)) [Music Lessons](Music_Lessons.md) Lv.1, [Amp](Amp.md) Lv.1
- **SP cost:** Cast: (15+Skill Level*3) Use: 1 SP / 3 seconds
- **Cast time:** None
- **Duration:** 30 seconds
- **Target:** Self
- **Area:** 9x9
- **Weapon:** Instrument


## Description

Hits enemies in area with a slow sonic attack, piercing their defense. Deals 30+10*Skill Lv. damage every 3 seconds for 30 seconds (11 attacks). Also occurs where two Bard songs overlap.

| Level | Damage | SP Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 40 | 18 |
| 2 | 50 | 21 |
| 3 | 60 | 24 |
| 4 | 70 | 27 |
| 5 | 80 | 30 |
