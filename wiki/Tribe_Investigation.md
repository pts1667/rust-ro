# Tribe Investigation

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** 70
- **Item requirement:** 30 Horn of Hillslion
- **Quests required:** [Ring of the Wise King](Ring_of_the_Wise_King.md)
- **Item reward:** 1 Manuk Coin
- **Window:** yes

1. In the Midgard Expedition Camp, speak to Bazett Teablack (mid camp 283, 198); he requests your help investigating the local tribes.
1. Speak to him again, and he will ask you to go search for giants on the Manuk Fields.
1. Speak to the Worker in [Manuk](Manuk.md) ([Manuk](Manuk.md) 194, 135). He asks you to get him 30 Horn of Hillslion so that he can make a replacement screw for a building.
1. Return with 30 Horn of Hillslion.
1. Return to Bazett, he thanks you and tells you to go rest.
  * Receive 1 Manuk Coin.
