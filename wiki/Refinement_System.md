# Refinement System


## Overview

Equipment in Ragnarok Online can be 'upgraded' or 'refined' by upgrade [NPC](NPC.md)s in each major [Town](categories/Towns.md)s to make them more powerful. All equipment has a maximum upgrade level of +10, starting from +0. +0 items do not have any prefixes, but once they are upgraded, the number of upgrade levels will be displayed on the equipment - e.g. +5 Stunner.

Also, you can turn in Rough Oridecon and Rough Elunium to create single refined Oridecon and Elunium, which are needed for upgrading Lv. 3 / Lv. 4 weapons, items and armors, respectively. The [Kafra Shop](Kafra_Shop.md) offers Enriched versions of these two ores, which, when used by a special upgrade NPC, increases the chance of successfully upgrading an item.

## Upgrade Requirements

To upgrade equipment, make sure you have the following:

* Equipment
* Money
* Reagent

Not all item types can be upgraded. The following item types cannot be upgraded:

* Mid and Low [Headgears](Headgears.md)
* [Accessories](Accessories.md)
* Any items that are not equipment
* Any equipment that specifically says it cannot be upgraded


## Reagents and Cost

To upgrade, you need to provide a reagent in order to upgrade your equipment. Reagent required differs depending on weapon level of the equipment. As for armors, they all take same reagent regardless of type.

Money taken also differs depending on weapon level of the [Weapons](Weapons.md).

Following list shows reagent + money required.

* Weapon Lv. 1 - Phracon (200 Z from NPC Shop) + 50 Z Fee
* Weapon Lv. 2 - Emveretarcon (1,000 Z from NPC Shop) + 200 Z Fee
* Weapon Lv. 3 - Oridecon (Monster drop) + 5,000 Z Fee
* Weapon Lv. 4 - Oridecon + 20,000 Z Fee
* All Armor Types - Elunium (Monster drop) + 2,000 Z Fee


## Refining Rough Oridecons and Eluniums

When you collect 5 Rough Oridecons and Rough Eluniums, you can convert them into a single Oridecon or Elunium by talking to an [NPC](NPC.md) in the upgrade shop. Note that you can make only one pure Oridecon or Elunium each time you talk to the NPC. This service does not cost any Zeny.


## Effects Gained

When you successfully upgrade, you gain following effects:

* Weapons
  * Weapon Lv. 1 - Every +1 upgrade gives +2 ATK (+3 ATK for every overupgrade).
  * Weapon Lv. 2 - Every +1 upgrade gives +3 ATK (+5 ATK for every overupgrade).
  * Weapon Lv. 3 - Every +1 upgrade gives +5 ATK (+7 ATK for every overupgrade).
  * Weapon Lv. 4 - Every +1 upgrade gives +7 ATK (+13(?) ATK for every overupgrade).

* Armors
  * Every +1 upgrade gives +1 (it's actually only 0.66) Equipment [DEF](DEF.md) (not VIT [DEF](DEF.md)).

Upgrades are useful, as for weapon upgrade, it reduces damage range between min - max damage (allowing you to do better average damage), it also increases your damage range. For armors, it reduces damage you take by 0.66% per every upgrade level.


## Safety Level

The Safety Level is the maximum level of refinement that can be given to equipment with guaranteed success (0% chance of failure). Anything past the safety level has chance to fail, and failure means your equipment will be permanently destroyed, including any cards and other enchantments it may have had. You won't be refunded Zeny and the ore used to upgrade if it fails. Give careful thought before deciding if you want to upgrade a piece of equipment beyond its safety level.

Premium (subscription) servers offer the following safety levels when upgrading with regular ores:

* Weapons
  * Weapon Lv. 1 - Safety Level +7
  * Weapon Lv. 2 - Safety Level +6
  * Weapon Lv. 3 - Safety Level +5
  * Weapon Lv. 4 - Safety Level +4
* Armors
  * All armor types have Safety Level +4

There are no safety levels on almost all [free-to-play](F2P.md) servers worldwide when upgrading with regular ores. By using Enriched Elumium and Enriched Oridecon however, these safety levels are re-enabled.

## Socket Enchants*For more details on the system, see [Socket Enchant](Socket_Enchant.md).*Players may use this system to add an additional card slot into selected pieces of equipment. They are divided by "ranks", which will require different amount of zeny and items (more expensive stuff will be required the higher the rank of the item you plan to upgrade). Success rates will also depend on the rank; higher rank items will have lower success rates.

When successfully enchanted, items will gain one or more slots to insert a card into. However, there are some exceptions. Some items have different effects as a result of successful enchant.


## Hidden Enchants*For more details on the system, see [Hidden Enchant](Hidden_Enchant.md).''

This new system allows players to add stat bonuses to selected body armors.  The gear is randomly given +1 to +3 of one of the 6 stats.  Success rates vary according to the type of armor.  Each attempt costs 400,000 zeny, but no items are needed other than the armor itself.
