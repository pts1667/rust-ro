# Mystical Amplification

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** High Wizard
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 10 (Fixed)
- **Prerequisites:** None
- **SP cost:** (10 + 4*Skill Level)
- **Cast time:** 0.75 seconds
- **Delay:** Animation
- **Duration:** 30 seconds or next cast skill
- **Target:** Self


## Description

Ampifies the magic attack strength of a skill for 30 seconds. (5% * level of Amp)


## Tips

* The first skill cast after using Mystical Amplification uses the Amplification.  This includes non-attack skills such as Heal and Energy Coat.
* The Amplified magic attack strength applies to ALL active spells, from the beginning of the cast of the Amplified skill.
* The effect of Mystical Amplification ends immediately upon the start of the cast of a skill following the Amplified spell's cast.  When this happens, all active spells lose their Amplification.
* Because of the nature of this spell, when using Vertical [Fire Wall](Fire_Wall.md) to kill mobs of enemies, you should only cast Mysical Amplification before casting the spell you wish to use to kill the mob.  Using Mystical Amplification between casts of [Fire Wall](Fire_Wall.md) will only drain SP.  However, any active [Fire Wall](Fire_Wall.md) will receive the benefits of the Mystical Amplification skill, subject to the above rules.
* While under the effects of [Magic Strings](Magic_Strings.md), if casting a spell with a duration like [Storm Gust](Storm_Gust.md), [Lord of Vermilion](Lord_of_Vermilion.md), or [Meteor Storm](Meteor_Storm.md), using a pattern of SG-MA-SG, rather than MA-SG-MA-SG, will result in similar damage while using less SP.
