# Spear Stab

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Knight
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 10
- **SP cost:** 9
- **Prerequisites:** ([Knight](Knight.md)) [Pierce](Pierce.md) Lv. 5
- **Delay:** [ASPD](Stats.md) Based
- **Target:** Enemy
- **Range:** Melee
- **Knockback:** 6 cells
- **Weapon:** Spear


## Description

Strikes a target enemy and all enemies in a line between the player and the target.  The attack has an [ATK](Stats.md) bonus of 20% per skill level. All struck enemies are knocked back 6 cells.


## Information

*  Knockback does not occur during [War of Emperium](War_of_Emperium.md).
