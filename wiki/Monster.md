# Monster

<big>**For specific stats, skills, and item drops on the various monsters in Ragnarok,
 visit one of the many [Databases](Links.md).**</big>

Monsters are the creatures of the Ragnarok world that players can kill in order to gain [experience](Experience.md) to level up. Each monster has its own AI, and some will react differently to players than others. When monsters are killed, the player killing them receives experience, and one or more items may be dropped by the monster. Each monster has an [Element](Element.md), a [Size](Size.md), and a [Race](Race.md), which the player can take advantage of using appropriate gear, skills, and items. Some monsters are also considered [Boss](Boss_Protocol.md)es or [MVP](MVP.md)s.  Not all monsters fall under the no-[Kill Steal](Kill_Steal.md)ing rule.  Some are considered [Free for All](Free_for_All.md).
