# Merchant Skill Quest


## Change Cart Quest

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Job level requirement:** 30
- **Class requirement:** Merchant
- **Item requirement:** 20 Animal Skins, 10 Iron, 50 Trunks
- **Quest reward:** Ability to use [Change Cart](Change_Cart.md)

1. Talk to Charlron in [Alberta](Alberta.md) (119,221). Bring him the following items to learn [Change Cart](Change_Cart.md): 20 Animal Skins, 10 Iron, and 50 Trunks.


## Crazy Uproar Quest

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Job level requirement:** 15
- **Class requirement:** Merchant
- **Item requirement:** 7 Pearls, 50 Mushroom Spores, 1 Banana Juice
- **Quest reward:** Ability to use [Crazy Uproar](Crazy_Uproar.md)

1. Talk to Necko in [Alberta](Alberta.md) (83,96). Bring him the following items to learn [Crazy Uproar](Crazy_Uproar.md): 7 Pearls, 50 Mushroom Spores, and 1 Banana Juice.


## Cart Revolution Quest

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Job level requirement:** 35
- **Class requirement:** Merchant
- **Item requirement:** 1 Banana Juice, 20 Iron, 30 Sticky Mucus, 20 Fly Wings, 5 Tentacles, 2 Grape Juice
- **Quest reward:** Ability to use [Cart Revolution](Cart_Revolution.md)

1. Talk to Gershaun in [Alberta](Alberta.md) (232,106). Bring him **exactly** 1 Banana Juice, 20 Iron, 30 Sticky Mucus, 20 Fly Wings, 5 Tentacles, and 2 Grape Juice to learn [Cart Revolution](Cart_Revolution.md). **It will fail if you bring more items than requested.**
