# Increase Accuracy

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Gunslinger
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 1
- **Prerequisites:** ([Gunslinger](Gunslinger.md)) [Snake Eyes](Snake_Eyes.md) Lv. 10, [Coin Flip](Coin_Flip.md) Lv. 2
- **Duration:** 1 minute
- **Target:** Self
- **Catalyst:** 4 [Coins](Coin_Flip.md)


## Description

Consume 4 [Coins](Coin_Flip.md) to add +20 [Hit](Stats.md), +4 [DEX](Stats.md) and +4 [AGI](Stats.md) for a 1 minute duration.
