# Jumping Janeway

Jumping Janeway is an iRO only quest chain that allows players an accelerated leveling path. On Classic it can get a player from base level 1 to 70.

She first shows up in the Novice Training Grounds at 95, 35.

![](images/JanewayNTG.jpg)

She will give you 100 Novice Red Potions and ask you to then kill 10 porings. When completed return to her, she will award you experience until you hit base level 10 job level 10 and give you a Janeway Dagger named after your character. If you continue to speak to her she will keep giving you Novice Red Potions 100 at a time (be careful not to go over 90% weight).

After that you can find her in [Asgard Training Village](Asgard_Training_Village.md) at 90, 123.

![](images/JanewayAsgard.jpg)

Speaking to her will pick up the next quest in the chain, she will often award a handful of Novice Red Potions before warping a player to the location of the monsters she requires you to kill. When you have killed the required monsters return to her in [Asgard Training Village](Asgard_Training_Village.md) and she will grant you experience until you reach the per-determined level.

In total she awards roughly 400 Novice Red Potions (not including any extras gained from speaking to her after completing the Poring quest). She will also apply a short term elemental [Endow Blaze](Endow_Blaze.md) for the final Pitman quest.
