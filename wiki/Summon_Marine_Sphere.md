# Summon Marine Sphere

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Alchemist
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** ([Alchemist](Alchemist.md)) [Prepare Potion](Prepare_Potion.md) Lv. 2
- **SP cost:** 10
- **Cast time:** 2 seconds
- **Target:** Ground
- **Catalyst:** 1 Marine Sphere Bottle


## Description

Summon a Marine Sphere to a targeted cell. Marine Spheres will cast Self Destruct upon receiving damage. The explosion deals 100% damage in an area of 11x11.

*  Each cast requires 1 Marine Sphere Bottle.

| Skill Level | Marine Sphere [HP](Max_HP.md) |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 2,400 |
| 2 | 2,800 |
| 3 | 3,200 |
| 4 | 3,600 |
| 5 | 4,000 |


## Information

* In PVM environments, an exploding Marine Sphere will do [Fire](Fire.md) damage to all Monsters, [Mercenaries](Mercenary_System.md), and [Homunculuii](Homunculus_System.md) in range.
* In [PVP](PvP~d29d2.md) environments, an exploding Marine Sphere will damage allies, enemies, and yourself.
* Summoned Marine Spheres are still considered plant type monsters and thus will do extra damage to targets wearing full battleground sets.
* The Marine Sphere will travel in the direction you tap it and move approximately 7 squares before detonating.
* Self destruction damage is based on the remaining [HP](Max_HP.md) of the marine sphere. Healing it prior to explosion will maximize damage.
* A Marine Sphere's explosion does not cause an [EXP](Experience.md) tap on the effected monsters.
* Summoned Marine Spheres will drop typical Marine Sphere monster drops.
* Monsters killed via a marine sphere explosion count towards quest kill counts.
