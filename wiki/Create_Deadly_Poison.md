# Create Deadly Poison

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Assassin Cross
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 1
- **Prerequisites:** ([Thief](Thief.md)) [Envenom](Envenom.md) Lv. 10, [Detoxify](Detoxify.md) Lv. 1, [Enchant Poison](Enchant_Poison.md) Lv. 5
- **SP cost:** 50
- **Cast time:** None
- **Target:** Self
- **Catalyst:** Berserk Potion, Venom Canine, Cactus Needle, Bee Sting, Poison Spore, Empty Bottle, Karvonidailrol


## Description

Creates a bottle containing Deadly Poison. If the skill fails, the caster will receive an amount of damage equal to 20% of maximum [HP](Max_HP.md).

Poison Bottles can only be created through the skill Create Deadly Poison. Some ingredients are needed to create one. [DEX](Stats.md) and [LUK](Stats.md) affect the success rates of brewing Poison Bottles.


## Ingredients

*  Berserk Potion: The Tool Dealers in [Comodo](Comodo.md) and [Lighthalzen](Lighthalzen.md) sell these.
*  Venom Canine: Dropped by Boas, Anacondaqs and Side Winders. They spawn in large quantities at Sograt Desert 13.
*  Cactus Needle: Dropped by Mukas. The best places to hunt them are Sograt Desert 18 and Sograt Desert 06.
*  Bee Sting: Dropped by Hornets. The best places to hunt them are Prontera Field 00 and Mt. Mjolnir 04.
*  Poison Spore: Dropped by Poison Spores. The best places to hunt them are Payon Forest 05 and Mt. Mjolnir 07.
*  Empty Bottle: Dropped by different monsters. It is best to gather them from Mukas so as to harvest two ingredients at once. Goats drop them at the highest rate and are found on Einbroch Field 06 and Juno Field 06. Ant Eggs in Ant Hell 1 and Removers in Somatology Laboratory 1 are also viable options.
*  Karvonidailrol: Only dropped by four monsters and at very low rates. Poison Spores are the easiest to hunt. Removers are another option. A third option is the [Biochemist](Biochemist.md) skill [Cultivate Plant](Cultivate_Plant.md), which at level 1 summons a Red Mushroom or a Black Mushroom by chance using one Mushroom Spore. Red Mushrooms drop these at 0.5%.


## Formula

Success Rate = Base Rate + [ (Base Rate * DEX/50) + (Base Rate * LUK/100) ]

**Base Rate is assumed to be at 20%.*

Simplified: Success Rate = 20% + (0.4% DEX) + (0.2% LUK)


## Ways To Increase Success Chance

Decent base [DEX](Stats.md) and [LUK](Stats.md).
[Blessing](Blessing.md). (+10 [DEX](Stats.md))
[Gloria](Gloria.md). (+30 LUK)
[Marionette Control](Marionette_Control.md). (skill of [Minstrel](Minstrel.md)/[Gypsy](Gypsy.md))
[Battle Chant](Battle_Chant.md). (+20 stats)
Guild Skill [Sharp Gaze](Sharp_Gaze.md) (+5 [DEX](Stats.md) at level 5, not really recommended)
Equipments: Apple of Archer (+3 [DEX](Stats.md)), Lucky Mantle (+2 [LUK](Stats.md)), Quad Dexterous Knife x2 (+8 [DEX](Stats.md)), Crystal Pumps (+5 [LUK](Stats.md)), Nimble Gloves x2 (+8 [DEX](Stats.md))

## Tips

*  Wear the equipments above and get [Blessing](Blessing.md) only after [Marionette Control](Marionette_Control.md) has been casted on the player to get the full [DEX](Stats.md) bonus.
*  Have a [Priest](Priest.md) standby to [Heal](Heal.md) the player when Create Deadly Poison fails.
*  Make use the [Bard](Bard.md) skill [Magic Strings](Magic_Strings.md) to reduce Create Deadly Poison's cast delay thus speeding up brewing.
*  Only brew when either a [Priest](Priest.md) or a [Minstrel](Minstrel.md)/[Gypsy](Gypsy.md) can assist so Karvonidailrols won't be wasted.


## Uses

*  A requirement for [Enchant Deadly Poison](Enchant_Deadly_Poison.md) to activate.
*  When Poison Bottles are used by a non-[Assassin](Assassin.md) class character, it causes instant death.
*  If an [Assassin Cross](Assassin_Cross.md) drinks a Poison Bottle, they will become poisoned, and receive a boost in [ASPD](ASPD.md) for 30 seconds. This is not the same as using Poison Bottles by activating [Enchant Deadly Poison](Enchant_Deadly_Poison.md).
