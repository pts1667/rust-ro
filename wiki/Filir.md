# Filir

> **Homunculus info** — race: Brute; feedItem: Garlet; startHP: 90; startSP: 25; startStr: 29; startAgi: 35; startVit: 9; startInt: 8; startDex: 30; startLuk: 9; HP: 60; SP: 4.5; str: 0.71; agi: 0.92; vit: 0.1; int: 0.59; dex: 0.71; luk: 0.59


## What is a Filir?

A Filir is one of the creatures available through the [Homunculus System](Homunculus_System.md), summonable by Alchemist and Biochemist who have completed the [Bioethics Quest](Alchemist_Skill_Quest.md).

Primarily a physical attack type among the four Homunculi, Filir is potentially the fastest, sporting impressive [ASPD](ASPD.md) and [Flee](Stats.md) capabilities. His skill pool is specifically tailored to take advantage of these traits, either increasing physical damage or further boosting already intimidating speed values. Of noteworthy mention is [S.B.R.44](S.B.R.44.md), arguably the most powerful single hit attack of all the Homunculi: but at the cost of lost intimacy. Filir requires the consumption of Garlet for feedings.


## Filir's Appearances

Like all Homunculi, Filir is obtainable in two styles, in this case visually resembling avian based life: one vaguely that of an owl motif and the other employing a phoenix look. Outside an aesthetic sense, there is ultimately no difference between "Owl Filir" and "Phoenix Filir", as they possess the same pool of skills and general stat growth. Transforming Filir from his chick appearance into a mature form requires a Stone of Sage when he possesses loyal disposition towards his creator.


## Filir and Alchemist Builds

The combination of Filir's speed and attack strength makes him an excellent universal choice for Alchemists, regardless of build. While somewhat more suitable for player versus monster engagements, where AGI can prove more valuable than [VIT](Stats.md), the sheer might of a maximum strength [S.B.R.44](S.B.R.44.md) makes the Homunculi a lethal weapon in player versus player situations: like ridding oneself of an approaching one hit killing [Champion](Champion.md) or [Assassin Cross](Assassin_Cross.md).


## Pros

* Cheapest feeds
* Processes a ranged attack skill, [Moonlight](Moonlight.md).
* Highest flee among all Homunculus on average.


## Cons

* Lowest maximum HP among all Homunculus.
* Extremely low VIT, which means lowest defense and HP regeneration rate.
* The ultimate skill [S.B.R.44](S.B.R.44.md) sets intimacy to Hate with a Passion.
* The [Accelerated Flight](Accelerated_Flight.md) skill does not work at all.
