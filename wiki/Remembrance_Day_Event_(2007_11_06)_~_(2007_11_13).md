# Remembrance Day Event (2007/11/06) ~ (2007/11/13)

> **Note:** Not implemented on iRO Classic.

**Item Requirements: 1000 Flower, 100 Red Herb**

**Quest Rewards: 75,000 Base Experience and 30,000 Job Experience per spot**

1.  Talk to Elegant Woman near the [Prontera](Prontera.md) fountain (prontera 157, 196)
1.  Ask her to make Poppy Wreaths for you, continue to do so until you have 9 of them
1.  Talk to the sign next to her (prontera 155, 196), and take note of the locations mentioned.
1.  Visit the locations given.
  * Near the entrance to Prontera Castle (Prontera 153, 331)
  * Near the Prontera Sanctuary (Prontera 235, 319)
  * The entrance to Prontera Culverts (prt_fild05 271,215)
  * The Undersea Tunnel Entrance in Byalan Island (izlu2dun 115, 83)
  * The docked ship in [Alberta](Alberta.md) (alberta 185,170)
  * [Payon](Payon.md) Cave Entrance (pay_arche 34, 128)
  * Near [Morroc](Morroc.md) Pyramid Ruins (moc_ruins 54,149)
  * Near the [Geffen](Geffen.md) Tower (geffen 116,113)
  * In front of the [Al De Baran](Al_De_Baran.md) Clock Tower (aldebaran 145, 133)
1.  Return to the Elegant Woman in Prontera and she will offer to make another wreath for you. She will ask if you've been to "where the Orcs bury their fallen."
1.  Visit the entrance to Orc Dungeon (gef_fild10 61,335) to lay another wreath
