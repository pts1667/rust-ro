# Coin Craft

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Mastersmith
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 3
- **Prerequisites:** ([Blacksmith](Blacksmith.md)) [Enchantedstone Craft](Enchantedstone_Craft.md) Lv. 3, [Iron Tempering](Iron_Tempering.md) Lv. 5, [Oridecon Research](Oridecon_Research.md) Lv. 2, [Steel Tempering](Steel_Tempering.md) Lv. 5
- **SP cost:** N/A
- **Target:** Self


## Description

Create a coin that gives you a random amount of [Zeny](Zeny.md) when it is used. You can receive many different coins depending on the item you use to create. Sometimes you can even receive a Money Pouch!
