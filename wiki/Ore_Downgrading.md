# Ore Downgrading

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Item requirement:** 1 Mr. Smile, Various Elemental Stones
- **Items required:** 1 Blue Gemstone, 1 Red Gemstone
- **Quests required:** [Umbala Language Quest](Language_Quest.md)
- **Item reward:** Elemental Stone(s)

1. Prepare 1 Red Gemstone and 1 Blue Gemstone. You will use them, but they will be given back immediately.

2. In [Umbala](Umbala.md), near (216,190) click the skull and look at it carefully. The skull is on/by the door.

3. It will ask you what you want to do. First put in a Blue Gemstone then a Red Gemstone. These go into the Left and then Right eyes.

4. You will be teleported into the room and should talk to the shaman.

5. Go to the leader's house (umbala 68, 253), and talk to him. He'll ask you to bring him a Mr. Smile as a gift. Give him one.

6. Now you may have the shaman process ore for you. Every time you enter her room you will need to put the gems into the skull again. She can break a pure Elemental Stone into lesser stones, or make a lesser elemental stone out of certain monster drops. Examples:

*  Turn a Rough Wind into Wind of Verdures

*  Turn a Mystic Frozen into Crystal Blues

*  Turn 25 Rainbow Shells into a Wind of Verdure

*  Turn 20 Snail's Shells into a Crystal Blue

*  Turn 20 Scorpion Tails into a Red Blood

*  Turn 15 Horns into a Green Live

*  Note: *The amount of stones you get when breaking a pure stone will vary from 6 ~ 10.*
*  Note: *It is possible to fail and get nothing when, for example, turning 20 Snail's Shells into a Crystal Blue*
