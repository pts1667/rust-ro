# Dagger

Dagger-type weapons range from small Knives to not-so-small Daggers, which are mainly used by [Thieves](Thief.md), Dagger-[Assassin](Assassin.md)s and [Rogue](Rogue.md)s. Some daggers are specialized for non-Thief classes, but [Acolyte](Acolyte.md)-class based characters are unable to equip any Daggers.

Daggers are compatible with the passive skills [Double Attack](Double_Attack.md) and [Sword Mastery](Sword_Mastery.md).

| Size | Damage |
| --- | --- |
| [Small](Size.md) | 100% |
| [Medium](Size.md) | 75% |
| [Large](Size.md) | 50% |
