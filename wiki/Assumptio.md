# Assumptio

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** High Priest
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 5 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Acolyte](Acolyte.md)) [Angelus](Angelus.md) Lv. 1, ([Priest](Priest.md)) [Increase SP Recovery](Increase_SP_Recovery.md) Lv. 3, [Impositio Manus](Impositio_Manus.md) Lv. 3
- **SP cost:** (10 + (Skill level*10))
- **Cast time:** (.5 + (Skill level*.5)) seconds
- **Delay:** (1 + (Skill level*.1)) seconds
- **Duration:** (Skill level*20) seconds
- **Target:** Players
- **Range:** 9 cells


## Description

Reduce damage taken from enemies by half for the spell's duration. Damage is only reduced to 2/3 of the original damage by this skill in [PvP](PvP~d29d2.md) areas, including [Battlegrounds](Battlegrounds.md) and [WoE](War_of_Emperium.md) areas. Cannot be cast inside a guild castle, but retains its effect after entering one. Assumptio and [Kyrie Eleison](Kyrie_Eleison.md) cannot be used at the same time - they overwrite each other.

| Skill Level | Cast Time | Cast Delay | Duration | SP Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 sec | 1.1 sec | 20 sec | 20 SP |
| 2 | 1.5 sec | 1.2 sec | 40 sec | 30 SP |
| 3 | 2 sec | 1.3 sec | 60 sec | 40 SP |
| 4 | 2.5 sec | 1.4 sec | 80 sec | 50 SP |
| 5 | 3 sec | 1.5 sec | 100 sec | 60 SP |


## Notes

*  Does not reduce fixed damage from [Gloria Domini](Gloria_Domini.md), [Gravitational Field](Gravitational_Field.md) and [Tarot](Tarot_Card_of_Fate.md) cards (Chariot, Tower and The Devil)
*  Assumptio and [Kaite](Kaite.md) overlaps each other
*  Can be used on monsters as [Kyrie Eleison](Kyrie_Eleison.md) cannot
