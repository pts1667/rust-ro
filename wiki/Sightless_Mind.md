# Sightless Mind

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Rogue
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** ([Rogue](Rogue.md)) [Stalk](Stalk.md) Lv. 2, [Back Stab](Back_Stab.md) Lv. 2
- **SP cost:** 20
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** [ASPD](ASPD.md) Based
- **Target:** Enemy
- **Range:** Melee
- **Area:** 3x3
- **Status:** [Blind](Status_Effects.md), [Stun](Status_Effects.md)


## Description

This skill is only usable during the Hiding status. Inflict an attack that will cause damage within a 3*3 cell area around the caster, and immediately cancel the Hiding status. Sightless Mind also has the chance of causing the [Blind](Status_Effects.md) or [Stun](Status_Effects.md) statuses to enemies. Card Modifiers are considered in this skill (% cards and +[ATK](Stats.md) card).

| Level | Damage (%) | Blind/Stun Chance (%) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 140 | 13 |
| 2 | 180 | 16 |
| 3 | 220 | 19 |
| 4 | 260 | 22 |
| 5 | 300 | 25 |
