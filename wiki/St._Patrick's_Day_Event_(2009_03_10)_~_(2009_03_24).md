# St. Patrick's Day Event (2009/03/10) ~ (2009/03/24)

> **Note:** Not implemented on iRO Classic.

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None/Unknown
- **Item requirement:** 200 Firecracker
- **Base EXP reward:** 200,000
- **Job EXP reward:** 70,000
- **Item reward:** Green Ale for Coins


## The Lost Gold

1.  Speak to Anxious Leprechaun (O'Riley the Leprechaun) at (prt fild05 170, 286). He hid his gold in the same place of the last year. He needs you to go there and fetch 200 Firecracker to blast it open.
  * Note: Firecracker can be purchased in sets of 5 for 500[z](Zeny.md) inside the Party Supplies Shop in [Hugel](Hugel.md) (91, 104).
1.  Take the Firecrackers to (mjolnir 01 271, 363).  In order for the quest to proceed properly, the player must walk by this spot and follow the message prompts that pop up.  Doing this successfully consumes all 200 Firecrackers, causes the [Lord of Vermillion](Lord_of_Vermilion.md) skill animation, and you will gain the item Treasure Box. Return to O'Riley with the Treasure Box.
  * Note: If you are over 50% weight you will not be able to get the Treasure Box.
1.  After returning the Treasure Box to O'Riley, he asks you to rest, then speak to him again.
  * Gain 200,000 [Base EXP](Base_EXP_Chart.md) & 70,000 [Job EXP](Job_EXP_Chart.md)
1.  He asks you to retrieve his kinsmen's coins from the filthy snakes, he will gladly exchange his magical brew for coins.
  * 1 Green Ale for 1 Gold Coin or 5 Silver Coins or 10 Bronze Coins
  * Note: Players can only get a maximum of 20 Green Ale at a time from O'Riley when exchanging coins.
