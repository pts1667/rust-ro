# Lunar Heat

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** TaeKwon Master
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 3
- **Prerequisites:** ([TaeKwon Master](TaeKwon_Master.md)) [Solar, Lunar and Stellar Perception](Solar,_Lunar_and_Stellar_Perception.md) Lv. 2
- **Cast time:** None
- **SP cost:** 100
- **Target:** Foe
- **Area:** 3x3
- **Knockback:** 2 cells


## Description

This skill can be used on a map designated as *[Place of the Moon](Solar,_Lunar_and_Stellar_Perception.md)*. When this skill is used, it puts up an offensive barrier, dealing 100% damage to foes right next to the player and pushes them back by 2 cells. Every time the barrier hits, 2 [SP](SP.md) is consumed. Upon SP depletion, the barrier disappears.
