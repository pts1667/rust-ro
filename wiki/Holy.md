# Holy

Elemental property. The Holy element is weak against the [Shadow](Shadow.md) element, and does extra damage against the Shadow element. Weapons can be temporarily enchanted with the Holy property using the [Priest](Priest.md)'s [Aspersio](Aspersio.md) skill, while armors can be blessed with [B.S Sacramenti](B.S_Sacramenti.md).

## Cards

Cards which concern the Holy property include:


## Armor

* Angeling Card: Enchants armor with the Holy property.
* Archdamn Card: Enchants armor with the Holy property when used in combination with the Crusader set by Crusaders.


## Weaponry

* Orc Skeleton Card: Increases damage to Holy property monsters by 20%.
