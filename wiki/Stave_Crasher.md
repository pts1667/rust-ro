# Stave Crasher

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** High Wizard
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 1
- **Prerequisites:** ([Mage](Mage.md)) [Increase SP Recovery](Increase_SP_Recovery.md) Lv. 1
- **SP cost:** 8
- **Cast time:** 0.3 seconds
- **Delay:** 0.3 seconds
- **Target:** Enemy
- **Range:** N/A


## Description

Does a physical attack based on the element of your weapon on one target by consuming 8 SP. As a normal physical attack, the inflicted damage is affected by various conditions such as the accuracy of the caster, the defense of the target, monster [Size](Size.md), and monster cards in caster's equipped weapon. This attack can be blocked by [Pneuma](Pneuma.md).

* If the attack misses due to insufficient [Hit](Stats.md), it will still do damage based on the attack strength added from the weapon's upgrade level.
