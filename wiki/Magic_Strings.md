# Magic Strings

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Bard
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 10
- **Prerequisites:** ([Bard](Bard.md)) [Unchained Serenade](Unchained_Serenade.md) Lv. 3
- **SP cost:** Cast: (35 + (Skill level*5)) Use: 1 SP / 5 seconds
- **Duration:** 180 seconds
- **Target:** Self
- **Area:** 7x7
- **Weapon:** Instrument
- **Weapon2:** Whip


## Description

Reduces [Cast Time](Skills.md) and [Cast Delay](Skills.md) of skills used inside the effect.


## Information

[DEX](Stats.md) and [INT](Stats.md) reductions only work in multiples of 10 and 5 respectively. Having 9 INT will provide the same Cast Delay reduction as 5 INT.

## Soul Link

The [Bard and Dancer Spirits](Bard_and_Dancer_Spirits.md) enables use of Magic Strings 10 when used on a [Dancer](Dancer.md) who has learned level 10 [Lady Luck](Lady_Luck.md).
