# Frenzy

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Lord Knight
- **Secondary class:** Mercenary
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 1
- **Prerequisites:** Job Level 50
- **Cast time:** None
- **Duration:** Until HP < 100
- **Target:** Self


## Description

Frenzy, upon activation, has many effects. It makes its user very powerful in many regards at the cost of making it excessively vulnerable in others.  The skill consumes 200 [SP](SP.md), and will not end naturally until the caster's [HP](Max_HP.md) drops below 100.


## Effects

* [ASPD](ASPD.md): Adds +30% [ASPD](ASPD.md).
* [ATK](Stats.md): Attack is doubled (200%). (Note this only affects base [ATK](Stats.md), equipment bonuses and weapon [ATK](Stats.md). Things such as weapon mastery and [Aura Blade](Aura_Blade.md) are not affected by the bonus.
* [Defense](DEF.md): All defense ([DEF](DEF.md), [VIT](Stats.md), [MDEF](Stats.md), [INT](Stats.md)) is set to 0.
* [HP](Max_HP.md): Upon activation, [Max HP](Max_HP.md) is tripled (300%) and fully regained. HP is reduced by 5% of Max HP every 15 seconds.
* [Flee](Stats.md): Subtracts 50% flee, cutting it in half. (This does not affect perfect dodge)
* Movement: Increased walking speed. (Only stacks with peco bonus)
* Unlimited [Endure](Endure.md) effect.
* [SP](SP.md): Natural SP regeneration is disabled during Frenzy and for 5 minutes after Frenzy ends (After frenzy ends normal SP regen can be restored by relogging, like the similar effect after casting Asura). SP is set to 0 when Frenzy is cast.
* Does not allow:
  * Usage of skills - This includes most [Autocast](Autocast.md) effects.
  * Changing equipment.
  * Chatting.
  * Usage of items.
  * User to be healed, except by Geographers created using [Summon Flora](Summon_Flora.md). (Some cards and equipment that cause hp to be returned, but do not show up as a green number above the character will still heal the [Lord Knight](Lord_Knight.md) eg: Sky Deleter card works; Solar Sword or Hunter Fly cards do not work)


## Information

* When [Dispell](Dispell.md)ed in Frenzy, a [Lord Knight](Lord_Knight.md) will have full (normal, not tripled) [HP](Max_HP.md). Frenzy can be immediately recasted if they have at least 200 [SP](SP.md).
* The [ASPD](ASPD.md) +30% bonus allows [Lord Knight](Lord_Knight.md)s with even 1 base [AGI](Stats.md) to reach 190 attack speed, even without [Increase AGI](Increase_AGI.md), by combining it with [Two Hand Quicken](Twohand_Quicken.md) and a Berzerk Potion.
* While chatting in public or guild channels is not permitted, it is still possible to set up chat rooms to communicate with nearby players.
* Frenzy ends when 100 HP or less is calculated to be reached: the player will be left with exactly 100 HP, and their HP will never actually drop below 100.
* Relogging will cancel Frenzy and leave its user with 100 [HP](Max_HP.md).
* Remember to use all items (such as [ASPD](ASPD.md) potions) and equip all gear desired before using Frenzy.


## Strategies


## PvM

Leveling with Frenzy is possible, even with its massive restrictions. The only difficulty is having enough SP to continually recast Frenzy, as it uses 200 [SP](SP.md) to activate, cancels natural SP regeneration, and sets SP to 0. Other skills, such as [Aura Blade](Aura_Blade.md) and [Spear Dynamo](Spear_Dynamo.md) should also be cast every time Frenzy runs out, requiring even more SP.

* Some methods of quickly restoring SP:
  * [SP](SP.md) Items: These items will have to be used every time Frenzy runs out which can be regularly. HP may deplete very quickly in leveling areas with high level monsters (due to Frenzy's 0 defense penalty in combination with its natural 5% HP lost every 15 seconds), thus bringing the player to 100 HP often and canceling Frenzy.
  * SP Cards: There are race-specific cards that give its user SP when the respective race is killed, and are compounded to weapons. Earth Deleter cards are the most ideal as they can be compounded to armor instead of weapon, work on all races of monsters, and won't take the place of a card that adds damage. The goal with these cards is to kill enough monsters to collect enough SP to be able to recast Frenzy before the [Max HP](Max_HP.md) drains to 100.
  * [Scholar](Scholar.md): Not only can Scholars cancel out unneeded Frenzy with [Dispell](Dispell.md), they can use [Soul Exhale](Soul_Exhale.md) to recover Lord Knight's SP.


## PvP

* The [HP](Max_HP.md) bonus from Frenzy can be used to survive nearly anything. Frenzy can be cast before an opponent uses [Guillotine Fist](Guillotine_Fist.md), hopefully giving the player enough HP to survive. The long term usefulness of this is debatable, however, because it leaves the player in a very vulnerable state.
* While Frenzy is useful for breaking emperiums, the additional walk speed Frenzy provides is helpful for rushing through forts to reach the emperium long before opponents.
* Some players use Frenzy right as they step into a fort - while this provides fast walking speed, by the time the player reaches the emperium, their [Spear Dynamo](Spear_Dynamo.md) and possibly [Aura Blade](Aura_Blade.md) and [Twohand Quicken](Twohand_Quicken.md) may have run out, and they will be unable to use potions if there are other players defending the fort.
* Evil Druid armor is a wise choice when breaking emperiums in Frenzy. Otherwise, [Priest](Priest.md)s can cast [Aspersio](Aspersio.md) on players in Frenzy (rendering them unable to deal any damage to the emperium), and frenzy prevents them from switching weapons or using items to counteract it.
