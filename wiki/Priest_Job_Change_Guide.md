# Priest Job Change Guide

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Job level requirement:** 40
- **Class requirement:** Acolyte
- **Item reward:** Bible \[2\] (Job Level 50) -OR- [Book \[3\]](Book.md) (Job Level 40~49)
- **Quest reward:** Jobchange to [Priest](Priest.md)

1. Apply to become a [Priest](Priest.md) in the [Prontera](Prontera.md) Church (prt_church 16, 41) by speaking to Bishop Paul.

2. You must visit all three Ascetics from the [Acolyte Job Quest](Acolyte_Job_Change_Guide.md).  However, this step may be skipped by [Job Level](Levels.md) 50 [Acolyte](Acolyte.md)s.

3. After returning from the three Ascetics, Bishop Paul grants you access to a room where the next two tests will take place: Exorcism and Temptation.

4. In this room, click Peter S. Alberto multiple times to activate his dialogue window. The excessive clicking required is actually part of the test, not a bug. A [Priest](Priest.md) can come along to help for these two tests by following the same instructions and bringing a Rosary in their inventory (not equipped).

5. In the next few rooms, [Heal](Heal.md) "bomb" all the Zombie that appear. All must be killed in each room to clear the Exorcism stage and move onto Temptation.

6. Successfully walk past four [NPC](NPC.md)s (Deviruchi, Doppleganger, Dark Lord, and finally Baphomet) without accepting their deals. Some may offer bribes or insist Priest life is too difficult to be worthwhile, but choose options that reject their offers.

7. Following this is another series of rooms where Zombie spawn, with Mummies mixed in. It is not necessary to kill them all, simply run by.

8. After exiting the last room, you'll be warped back to Bishop Paul. He suggests speaking with Sister Cecelia on the other side of the room. Employ common sense to answer her questions with holy and righteous responses to pass it.

9. Return to Bishop Paul to become a [Priest](Priest.md) after a bit of dialogue.  He will reward [Job Level](Levels.md) 50 Acolytes with a Bible \[2\], while those below Job 50 will receive a [Book \[3\]](Book.md).
