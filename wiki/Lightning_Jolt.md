# Lightning Jolt

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Ninja
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 5 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Ninja](Ninja.md)) [Wind Blade](Wind_Blade.md) Lv. 5
- **Target:** Self
- **Cast time:** 4s
- **Delay:** None
- **Element:** Wind
- **Catalyst:** 1 Wind Stone


## Description

Huge bolts of lightning crash down, causing wind damage to all those in range. Lightning Jolt does one single hit (broken into several small hits for the visual effect only), and covers a wide 9x9 area at max level. The catalyst cost and low magic attack can be considered a downside, but there is only a short cast time and absolutely no aftercast delay.

Lightning Jolt cannot be cast on [Magnetic Earth](Magnetic_Earth.md). One Wind Stone is consumed when casting this skill.

| Number of hits | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
