# Forgotten Tales of Rune Midgard (2007/08/21) ~ (2007/09/11)

> **Note:** Not implemented on iRO Classic.

**Level Requirements: Base Level 20+**

**Item Requirements: Varies by Level**

**Quest Rewards: Base/Job Experience Varies by Level, Nile Rose \[1\] OR Baseball Cap \[1\] OR Pirate Dagger**

*Source: Storyteller Quest Info*

1.  Talk to the old man in Prontera above the fountain. (155,220) and agree to help him by visiting 9 storytellers around Midgard.
1.  For each storyteller, listen to their story, collect some items, return and get a 3 question quiz about the story you just heard. You get a little reward when you finish each.
1.  After completing all 9 storytellers, return to the old man in Prontera, and he will quiz you on the stories. You will be asked a set of four questions, the answers to which must be typed in. Capitalization matters. Upon successful completion of the quiz, you will be given a random item from one of the past anniversary events. This can include: Nile Rose [1], Baseball Cap [1] and Pirate Dagger.
  * note: The Nile Rose received is the same item as received from the Eye of Hellion Quest meaning it will have a different description and appear on a different hotkey than Nile Roses obtained during the 2nd anniversary quest.
