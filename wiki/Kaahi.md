# Kaahi

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Soul Linker
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 7 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Soul Linker](Soul_Linker.md)) [Priest Spirit](Priest_Spirit.md) Lv. 1, [Crusader Spirit](Crusader_Spirit.md) Lv. 1
- **SP cost:** 5 * (Skill Level)
- **Duration:** 350 seconds
- **Target:** [Soul Linker](Soul_Linker.md)s, Spouse, Child


## Description

Consume a certain amount of SP to restore HP each time an enemy uses a normal attack. Kaahi heals regardless of whether or not the attack hits.

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SP Taken | 5 | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 | 30 | 35 |
| HP Healed | 200 | 400 | 600 | 800 | 1000 | 1200 | 1400 |
| SP Cost | 30 | 30 | 30 | 30 | 30 | 30 | 30 |


## Notes

* Only triggers from non-skill physical attacks.
* Does not trigger if you're at 100% HP.
* Triggers before the attack deals damage.
* The healing effects of Kaahi are not visible to other players.
