# Pang Voice

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Bard
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 1
- **Quest:** Bard Skill Quest
- **SP cost:** 20
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** 1 second
- **Target:** Enemy
- **Status:** [Chaos](Status_Effects.md)


## Description

The [Bard](Bard.md) screams out at a single target, causing him to be inflicted with [Chaos](Status_Effects.md) status effect. The success rate is based on your own level, and the [VIT](Stats.md), level, and [LUK](Stats.md) of the target. This affects players and monsters, but does not affect [boss monsters](Boss_Protocol.md).
