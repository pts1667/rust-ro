# Full Blast

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Gunslinger
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 10
- **Prerequisites:** ([Gunslinger](Gunslinger.md)) [Crowd Control Shot](Crowd_Control_Shot.md) Lv. 3
- **SP cost:** (15 + (Skill level*5))
- **Target:** Foe
- **Range:** Ranged
- **Status:** [Blind](Status_Effects.md) (Self)
- **Catalyst:** 2-10 Bullets
- **Weapon:** Shotgun


## Description

Shoot all of the bullets in the gun's chambers to inflict devastating damage onto an enemy. This skill has a small chance of causing [Blind](Status_Effects.md) status to the caster. Consumes up to 10 bullets per use.

* Wearing Dark Blinder removes the chance of blinding yourself
