# Twohand Quicken

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Knight
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 10
- **Prerequisites:** ([Swordman](Swordman.md)) [Two-Handed Sword Mastery](Two-Handed_Sword_Mastery.md) Lv. 1
- **SP cost:** (10+Skill Level*4)
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** [ASPD](ASPD.md) Based
- **Duration:** (Skill level * 30) seconds
- **Target:** Self
- **Weapon:** Two Handed Sword


## Description

Temporarily increase Attack Speed when a Two Handed Sword weapon is equipped for the skill`s duration.

*  Can be canceled by [Decrease AGI](Decrease_AGI.md) skill.
*  Can also be canceled by entering an area on which [Quagmire](Quagmire.md) has been cast.
*  [ASPD](ASPD.md) Boost is canceled when you unequip a Two Handed Sword.
