# Brain Surgery

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Lif
- **Secondary class:** Alchemist
- **Tertiary class:** Homunculus
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** [Healing Hands](Healing_Hands.md) lvl. 5


## Description

Alter the Lif Homunculus's brain to increase its Max SP, SP Recovery Rate, and the effectiveness of the Healing Hands skill.

| Level | Max SP | Healing Hands Bonus | SP Recovery Rate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | +1% | +2% | +3% |
| 2 | +2% | +4% | +6% |
| 3 | +3% | +6% | +9% |
| 4 | +4% | +8% | +12% |
| 5 | +5% | +10% | +15% |
