# Haggle

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Rogue
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** ([Rogue](Rogue.md)) [Slyness](Slyness.md) Lv. 1


## Description

Purchase items from [NPC](NPC.md) shops at lower prices. When mastered, Haggle reduces NPC item prices 1% more than the [Merchant](Merchant.md)`s [Discount](Discount.md) skill.

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Haggle (%) | -9 | -13 | -17 | -21 | -25 |


## Trivia

The "C DC" in this skill's icon refers to its original name, which is Compulsion Discount.
