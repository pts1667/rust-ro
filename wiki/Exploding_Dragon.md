# Exploding Dragon

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Ninja
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 5 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Ninja](Ninja.md)) [Ninja Mastery](Ninja_Mastery.md) Lv. 10, [Blaze Shield](Blaze_Shield.md) Lv. 7
- **SP cost:** (3+Skill Level)*5
- **Cast time:** 3s
- **Delay:** 3s
- **Target:** Foe
- **Range:** 9 cells
- **Area:** 5x5 cells
- **Element:** Fire
- **Catalyst:** 1 Flame Stone


## Description

Summon a flaming dragon that will inflict 3 strikes on all enemies in a 5*5 cell area around the enemy targeted by this skill. Each cast consumes 1 Flame Stone. Mastered at Skill Level 5. This skill is considered an Area of Effect skill, but is targeted and therefore cannot be evaded by simply running away.

This skill is calculated as a single hit that is then divide into three separate hits. Each hit is then rounded down; as a result, dealing damage of 1 (such as to plants) will split up and rounded to zero.

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Damage | 300% | 450% | 600% | 750% | 900% |
| SP Cost | 20 | 25 | 30 | 35 | 40 |
