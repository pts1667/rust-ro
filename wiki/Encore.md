# Encore

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Bard
- **Secondary class:** Dancer
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 1
- **Prerequisites:** ([Bard](Bard.md) & [Dancer](Dancer.md)) [Amp](Amp.md) Lv. 1
- **SP cost:** 1
- **Target:** Self
- **Weapon:** Instrument
- **Weapon2:** Whip


## Description

Recast the last [Performance Skill](categories/Performance_Skills.md) that was cast at half of the [SP](SP.md) cost. All requisites for casting the song or dance (i.e. equipping the right weapon, standing next to Ensemble partner) still apply. If the requisites for recasting are not met, the casting of Encore consumes 1 [SP](SP.md).
