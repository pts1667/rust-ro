# Righthand Mastery

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Assassin
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** None


## Description

When an Assassin equips two weapons, one in each hand, the weapon in the right hand only inflicts 50% of its potential damage, and the weapon in the left hand only inflicts 30% of its potential damage. Righthand Mastery increases the damage potential of weapons equipped in the right hand, and mastering this skill nullifies the righthand damage reduction.

Has no effect on [Katar](Katar.md)s.

| Skill Level | Right-handed Weapon Damage |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 60% |
| 2 | 70% |
| 3 | 80% |
| 4 | 90% |
| 5 | 100% |
