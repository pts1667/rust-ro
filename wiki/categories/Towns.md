# Towns

These are all towns in Ragnarok Online.  [Juno](../Juno.md) is the capitol of the [Republic of Schwartzvald](../Republic_of_Schwartzvald.md), [Prontera](../Prontera.md) is the capitol of the [Rune-Midgarts Kingdom](../Rune-Midgarts_Kingdom.md), and [Rachel](../Rachel.md) is the capital of the [Arunafeltz States](../Arunafeltz_States.md).
