# Instances

Special dungeons in Ragnarok Online; they are different from "regular" dungeons in that players progress through them in order to accomplish certain goals, as well as having time restrictions and [Party](../Party.md) requirements. They are analogous to repeatable-dungeon quests. *See: [Instance](../Instance.md)*
