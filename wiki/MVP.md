# MVP

MVP stands for Most Valuable Player, a definition not exclusive to Ragnarok Online. This is a [Monster](Monster.md) with [Boss Protocol](Boss_Protocol.md) and MVP Protocol. MVP's are much stronger than any other normal monsters and appear on an array of maps throughout Ragnarok. MVP's only spawn at certain times, anywhere from once per hour to once every 8 hours since the last MVP of that kind was killed. While MVP's can be defeated by a single person (depending on the [Classes](Classes.md) of the players), they are most often taken down by a party of 2 or more players. Because of the difficulty with which MVP's are defeated, they give a high amount of experience and MVP rewards given directly to the player's inventory to the player who has the most damage interaction with it.  They are also [Free for All](Free_for_All.md) (i.e. *ANYONE* can attack them, no matter who found it first or summoned it).

MVP monsters will also summon slaves or mobs to increase the difficulty with which they are defeated. The mob will often times consist of 3-9 monsters, sometimes all of them the same, sometimes different, depending on the MVP.


## Normal MVP Monsters

Normal MVPs spawn a set amount of time after the previous one was defeated. For example, if Amon Ra is defeated at 11:00, it will spawn again at 12:00. If an MVP spawns in multiple places, the two times are independent of one-another and the respawn period may be different. Below is a list of all normal MVPs, where they are located, and the time that needs to elapse before it will respawn.

## Special Spawn Conditions MVP Monsters

The following MVPs have special spawn conditions that do not follow either the timed spawn model or the
* Baphomet (event) is an event boss and only spawns when GMs decide to spawn it.
* Poring the Conqueror (aka Bring It On) is an event boss and only spawns when GMs decide to spawn it. This MVP is classified as Very High Difficulty.
* Ktullanux is spawned by using 4 Freezing Snow Powder (obtained via the [Ice Necklace Quest](Ice_Necklace_Quest.md)) or 4 Ice Scale (obtained by defeating Ktullanux previously) to put out the four sacred fires on Ice Dungeon 3. Once defeated, Ktullanux cannot be resummoned for another 2 hours. This MVP is classified as Mid-High Difficulty.
* Memory of Thanatos is spawned by the completion of the [Thanatos Tower Quest](Thanatos_Tower_Quest.md). This MVP is classified as Medium Difficulty.
* Naght Sieger (Floor 101) and Entweihen Crothen (Floor 100) spawn in the [Endless Tower](Endless_Tower.md) instance.
* Nidhoggur's Shadow spawns in the [Nidhoggur's Nest](Nidhoggur's_Nest.md) instance.
