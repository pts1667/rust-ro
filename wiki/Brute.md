# Brute

**Brute** are a race of [Monster](Monster.md). Most of them are animals or have animalistic traits.

Cards which concern the brute race include:
*  **Cruiser Card**: Inflict 10% more damage with Critical Attack. Critical +7 on Brute monsters.
*  **Deviace Card**: Increase damage on [Demi Human](Demi_Human.md), Brute, [Plant](Plant.md) and [Insect](Insect.md) monsters by 7%.
*  **Goblin Card**: Increase damage on Brute monsters by 20%.
*  **Leib Olmai Card**: Add the chance of gaining 'Red Blood' item each time a Brute monster is killed. Add 10% more tolerance against [Fire](Fire.md) Property Attack.
*  **Magmaring Card**: [ATK](Stats.md) +5. Increases physical damage versus [Earth](Earth.md) property monsters by 10%. Adds [CRIT](Stats.md) + 15 when fighting Brute and [Plant](Plant.md) race monsters.
*  **Mini Demon Card**: Receive 10% more Experience Points from Brute Monsters. Receive 20% more damage from Brute Monsters.
*  **Nereid Card**: Gain 5 SP each time a Brute monster is killed by Physical Melee Attack. Drain 5 SP as the weapon is unequipped.
*  **Orc Warrior Card**: Reduce damage from Brute monsters by 30%.
*  **Spring Rabbit Card**: Add the chance of gaining 'Meat' or 'Monster's Feed' item each time a Brute monster is killed. Meat and Monster's Feed restore 50% more HP.
*  **Stem Worm Card**: Add the chance of gaining 'Box of Storms' item each time a Brute monster is killed.

Equipment which concerns the brute race include:
*  **Hunting Spear**: One Handed Spear. Adds a 10% chance of using [Vital Strike](Vital_Strike.md) Lv 3 on the target when doing a physical attack. Physical attacks ignore the defense of Brute race targets. Adds a 10% chance of the 'Meat' item dropping when killing a Brute race monster.
*  **Kitchen Knife**: Dagger. Adds a 50% chance of the 'Meat' item dropping when killing a Brute race monster
*  **Nekomimi**: Headgear. [LUK](Stats.md) +2. Reduces damage from Brute monsters by 5%.
*  **Leather of Tendrilion**: Garment. Reduces damage received from [Earth](Earth.md) and [Water](Water.md) element attacks by 5%. Reduces damage received from Brute and [Plant](Plant.md) race by 5%.
*  **Lunakaligo**: If the user's base Str is 77 or higher: [Aspd](ASPD.md) +4%, 15% chance of inflicting [Stun](Status_Effects.md) on the target when doing a melee attack, 5% chance of the 'Bomber Steak' item dropping when killing a Brute race monster,5% chance of the 'Green Salad' item dropping when killing a [Plant](Plant.md) race monster, 5% chance of the 'Steamed Ancient Lips' item dropping when killing a [Fish](Fish.md) race monster.
*  **Slaughter**: Axe. Pierces Defense of Brute monsters. Causes [Coma](Status_Effects.md) effect on Brute monsters by a low chance. [Earth](Earth.md) Elemental.

Skills concerning the Brute race include:
*  **[Beast Bane](Beast_Bane.md)**: [Hunter](Hunter.md) skill that increases [ATK](Stats.md) against the Brute and [Insect](Insect.md) races.
*  **[Beast Charge](Beast_Charge.md)**: [Hunter](Hunter.md) skill that is an advanced form of [Double Strafe](Double_Strafe.md) modified to do additional damage against Brute and [Insect](Insect.md) type monsters.
