# Lunar Wrath

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** TaeKwon Master
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** 3
- **Prerequisites:** ([TaeKwon Master](TaeKwon_Master.md)) [Solar, Lunar and Stellar Opposition](Solar,_Lunar_and_Stellar_Opposition.md) Lv. 2

Gain bonus [attack](Stats.md) power when fighting a *[Target of the Moon](Solar,_Lunar_and_Stellar_Opposition.md)*.

| Level | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Bonus damage is ( (BLv.+LUK+DEX) /9 )% |
| 2 | Bonus damage is ( (BLv.+LUK+DEX) /6 )% |
| 3 | Bonus damage is ( (BLv.+LUK+DEX) /3 )% |
