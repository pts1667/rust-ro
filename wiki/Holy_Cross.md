# Holy Cross

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Crusader
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 10 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Crusader](Crusader.md)) [Faith](Faith.md) Lv. 7
- **SP cost:** (10 + (Skill level))
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** [ASPD](ASPD.md) Based
- **Target:** Enemy
- **Range:** Melee
- **Element:** Holy
- **Status:** [Blind](Status_Effects.md)


## Description

Summon a holy cross that inflicts [Holy](Holy.md) damage twice on a target. It can [Blind](Status_Effects.md) the target by a low chance.

| Level | [ATK](Stats.md) | SP Cost | [Blind](Status_Effects.md) Chance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 135% | 11 | 3% |
| 2 | 170% | 12 | 6% |
| 3 | 205% | 13 | 9% |
| 4 | 240% | 14 | 12% |
| 5 | 275% | 15 | 15% |
| 6 | 310% | 16 | 18% |
| 7 | 345% | 17 | 21% |
| 8 | 380% | 18 | 24% |
| 9 | 415% | 19 | 27% |
| 10 | 450% | 20 | 30% |
