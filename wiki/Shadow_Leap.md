# Shadow Leap

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Ninja
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** ([Ninja](Ninja.md)) [Flip Tatami](Flip_Tatami.md) Lv. 1
- **SP cost:** 10
- **Target:** Walkable cell


## Description

This skill instantly moves the caster to a targeted cell, ignoring snipe-able obstacles in the caster's way. **Shadow Leap** can only be performed when the caster is in [Hiding](Hiding.md) status (attainable through the [Haze Slasher](Haze_Slasher.md) skill), and the caster will be unhidden upon reaching their target. Using the skill on WoE maps will simply unhide the caster without moving.

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Max Leap Distance | 6 | 7 | 8 | 9 | 10 |


## Notes

*  Non snipe-able cells (obstacles that usually do not allow skills to be cast through them) cannot be bypassed.
*  [Cloaking](Cloaking.md) cannot be used as an alternative to [Hiding](Hiding.md).
