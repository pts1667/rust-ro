# Party Relay Quest

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** 40
- **Party requirement:** 1 of Each 6 Base [Classes](Classes.md)
- **Item requirement:** Varies
- **Base EXP reward:** 850,000 / Person
- **Item reward:** 2x Yggdrasil Berry for Each Person

1. Talk to Ledrion in one of the following cities:

| Column 1 | Column 2 |
| --- | --- |
| Town | Location |
| [Payon](Payon.md) | 103, 113 |
| [Comodo](Comodo.md) | 71, 137 |
| [Veins](Veins.md) | 274, 118 |
| [Einbroch](Einbroch.md) | 107, 93 |
| [Hugel](Hugel.md) | 102, 130 |
| [Rachel](Rachel.md) | 215, 71 |

* Note: *You must have all 6 players in your [Party](Party.md) to begin, and only the party leader can start the quest.  Each player must be at least Base Level 40.  It doesn't matter if the players are 1st Class, 2nd Class, or Transcendent Class.*


## Swordman Class (11AM - 2PM PST or 12AM - 3AM PST)

First, give the Mission Ticket to a [Swordman](Swordman.md) class character and have them give it to Gatan between 11AM - 2PM PST or 12AM - 3AM PST.  Gatan can be found in the following places:

| Column 1 | Column 2 |
| --- | --- |
| Town | Location |
| [Payon](Payon.md) | 83, 327 |
| [Comodo](Comodo.md) | 236, 197 |
| [Veins](Veins.md) | 138, 207 |
| [Einbroch](Einbroch.md) | 260, 128 |
| [Hugel](Hugel.md) | 169, 139 |
| [Rachel](Rachel.md) | 45, 163 |

Once you speak to Gatan during the specified times, he will ask you to get stronger.. by gaining 2 more levels.

When you get the levels, go back and speak to him (again, between 11AM - 2PM PST or 12AM - 3AM PST). For doing this you will be rewarded with 2x Yggdrasil Berries and roughly 850k EXP (exact amount unconfirmed) and 1x Mission Ticket 2 to give to a Thief Class character, and instructs them to speak to Bafhail.

(Update: I talked to the NPC on a 98 Crusader, and he said "You are already strong enough" and I didn't have to get a level and still got the reward. So i'll keep a record of what levels need to do what)

Level 77 - Asked to Get 2 Levels.

Level 91 - Asked to Get 1 Level.

Level 98 - Asked to Get 0 Levels.

Once you return to him between his operating hours, he will give you 2x Yggdrasil Berry, EXP and a Mission Ticket 3.
The next ticket is to be given to an Archer class character. He instructs them to speak to Lospii.

EXP Reward differs between characters. Currently unsure of the level ranges.

## Archer Class (2PM - 5PM or 6PM - 9PM PST)

Take the Mission Ticket 3 to Lospii. He can be found at these locations.

| Column 1 | Column 2 |
| --- | --- |
| Town | Location |
| [Payon](Payon.md) | 168, 314 |
| [Comodo](Comodo.md) | 64, 219 |
| [Veins](Veins.md) | 97, 245 |
| [Einbroch](Einbroch.md) | 173, 72 |
| [Hugel](Hugel.md) | 144, 201 |
| [Rachel](Rachel.md) | 158, 215 |

His mission is to gather up 10 of your friends. They don't have to be on friends list or in party, just have to be standing near him. And in fact you yourself count as a friend, so really you only need 9 People Plus yourself. For completing this you get 859,200 EXP (on a level 68 Dancer), 2x Yggdrasil Berries and a Mission Ticket 4, which is to be given back to the leader who started the quest. The leader has to go back and speak to Ledrion.


## Leader

Ok the leader has to speak to Ledrion, and for this each of the party members has to be online. He congratulates you for getting half way, and rewards you with a little bit of exp (I forgot to record it... was about 1% at 94) and 3x Gift Boxes, and 1x Old Blue Box. He also gives you a Mission Ticket 5 to give to a Mage class character. They are to go back to Gatan.
