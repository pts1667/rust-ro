# Guillotine Fist

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Monk
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 5 (fixed)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Monk](Monk.md)) [Throw Spirit Sphere](Throw_Spirit_Sphere.md) Lv. 3, [Fury](Fury.md) Lv. 3
- **SP cost:** 1
- **Target:** Foe (multiple methods)
- **Range:** Melee
- **Element:** Neutral


## Description

Guillotine Fist is the Monk's ultimate attack that consumes all of their remaining [SP](SP.md) to deal massive damage to one target. Guillotine Fist is effected by [Race](Race.md)/[Size](Size.md)/[Elemental](Element.md) cards, but always remains a [Neutral](Neutral.md) property attack. It bypasses the target's [DEF](DEF.md), but is affected by cards (for example race reduction cards).

| Skill Level | Damage Formula | Cast time | Cast Delay |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | ATK*(8 + SP/10) + 400 | 4 Seconds | 3 Seconds |
| 2 | ATK*(8 + SP/10) + 550 | 3.5 Seconds | 2.5 Seconds |
| 3 | ATK*(8 + SP/10) + 700 | 3 Seconds | 2 Seconds |
| 4 | ATK*(8 + SP/10) + 850 | 2.5 Seconds | 1.5 Seconds |
| 5 | ATK*(8 + SP/10) + 1000 | 2 seconds | 1 second<br> |


## Damage Calculation

*  Damage is derived from this formula: ATK * (8 + Remaining SP/10) + Skill Level Bonus. As a result, the following characteristics determine what is needed to maximize damage:
  * As much Base [ATK](Stats.md) as possible; due to this, Monks oriented to Guillotine will have very high [STR](Stats.md).
  * As much [SP](SP.md) as possible; if a card slot can't add to [STR](Stats.md) (or reach the next bonus of it), it should instead be used for [SP](SP.md) adding cards.
  * Maintain [SP](SP.md) before using Guillotine Fist. The damage will be low if the user's [SP](SP.md) is nearly empty when casted.
  * Higher levels of Guillotine Fist will add a small amount of damage.
*  Guillotine Fist is always [Neutral](Neutral.md), and therefore will always be reduced by Raydric cards. Due to this as well, users do not equip Elemental weapons for Guillotine.
*  The preferred weapon cards for Guillotine Fist are that add damage by a percent, as they increase the overall damage.
*  The preferred weapons for Guillotine Fist are Chain and +9 or +10 Lich's Bone Wand; the [SP](SP.md) bonus of the +9 or higher Lich's Bone Wand results in higher damage in most cases compared to a chain, despite the loss of a card. See [Card Reference](Card_Reference.md) for more information. However, against [Demi Human](Demi_Human.md) targets the Battlefield Morning Star ([Battlegrounds](Battlegrounds.md) reward) provides better damage.


## Usage Notes

*  Casting *can* be interrupted.
*  [Dispelling](Dispell.md) a Monk while Guillotine Fist is being casted will not disrupt it's execution; it is necessary to do so before casting starts.
*  It is possible to use [Hiding](Hiding.md) or a skill like it to evade a directly casted Guillotine Fist. Other evasion skills, such as [Cicada Skin Shed](Cicada_Skin_Shed.md), also function. [Cloaking](Cloaking.md), however, does not.
*  A line of sight must be available when the skill activates, otherwise Guillotine will deal no damage but still drain the caster's [SP](SP.md).
*  It is possible to observe if Guillotine Fist successfully does damage to a target by the screen shaking effect. If it does not occur, then the target has evaded the hit and no damage has been done.
*  There are four ways Guillotine Fist can be cast:
  * If the caster has 5 [Spirit Sphere](Spirit_Sphere.md)s and is in the [Fury](Fury.md) state, then Guillotine Fist can be cast in the same manner as single-target skills; by selecting Guillotine by a hotkey and clicking on a target.
  * If the caster has 4 [Spirit Sphere](Spirit_Sphere.md)s, is in the [Fury](Fury.md) status, and is in the [Raging Thrust](Raging_Thrust.md) delay, then the caster can cast Guillotine Fist. This will *bypass* the cast time. This is done as the last part of a [Combo](Monk.md).
  * If the caster has 1 [Spirit Sphere](Spirit_Sphere.md), is in the [Fury](Fury.md) status, and is in the [Chain Crush Combo](Chain_Crush_Combo.md) delay, then the caster can cast Guillotine Fist. This is a method only [Champion](Champion.md)s can use, as the last part of their [Combo](Monk.md)s and will *bypass* the cast time.
  * If the caster has 4 [Spirit Sphere](Spirit_Sphere.md)s, is in the [Fury](Fury.md) status, and has [Root](Root.md)ed a target with level 5 [Root](Root.md) learned, then Guillotine Fist can be cast on the [Root](Root.md)ed target.
*  After Guillotine Fist is successfully casted, then the caster cannot regenerate [SP](SP.md) naturally for 5 minutes afterwards. The only exception is [Spiritual Cadence](Spiritual_Cadence.md), which will continue to regenerate [SP](SP.md) during this time, or under the influence of a [Monk Spirit](Monk_Spirit.md), which causes your [SP](SP.md) to still regenerate.
