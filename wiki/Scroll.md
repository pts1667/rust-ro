# Scroll

Scrolls are special consumable items that enable the user to cast a spell that may not normally be available to them, without spending [SP](SP.md).
When using a spell via a scroll, all calculations are handled as if you're actually casting the spell yourself: MATK, casting time and so on, and are influenced by your equipment.


## List

*  [Fire Ball](Fire_Ball.md) Level 1
*  [Fire Wall](Fire_Wall.md) Level 1
*  [Frost Diver](Frost_Diver.md) Level 1
*  [Teleport](Teleport.md) Level 1
*  [Cold Bolt](Cold_Bolt.md) Level 3, Level 5
*  [Earth Spike](Earth_Spike.md) Level 3, Level 5
*  [Bolt](Fire_Bolt.md) Level 3, Level 5
*  [Heal](Heal.md) Level 3, Level 5
*  [Lightening Bolt](Lightening_Bolt.md) Level 3, Level 5
*  [Soul Strike](Soul_Strike.md) Level 3, Level 5


## Special exception

Although usually scrolls are expended when used, there is an exception: a [Taekwon](TaeKwon_Kid.md)-class character in [Happiness](TaeKwon_Kid.md) status can lower the chance of an [Earth Spike](Earth_Spike.md) (Either level 3 or 5) scroll actually burning up to as low as 1% per use by raising his or her [Happy Break](Happy_Break.md) skill. However, using a scroll in such way expends 10 [SP](SP.md).
* Additional note: the scroll is consumed when you use it, not when you actually select a target and use the spell. Don't mash your activation hotkey.
