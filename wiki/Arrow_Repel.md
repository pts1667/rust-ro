# Arrow Repel

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Archer
- **Secondary class:** Mercenary
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 1
- **Quest:** Archer_Skill_Quest#Arrow_Repel_Quest
- **SP cost:** 15
- **Cast time:** 1.5 seconds (Not interruptible)
- **Delay:** None
- **Target:** Foe
- **Range:** 14
- **Knockback:** 6 cells
- **Catalyst:** 1 Arrow
- **Weapon:** Bow


## Description

Strike a target with an attack that will inflict 150% damage and knock the target back 6 cells.
