# Language Quest

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Item requirement:** 10 Oil Paper, 5 Slick Paper, 1 Feather of Birds, 1 Squid Ink
- **Items required:** 1 Mr. Smile (equipped)
- **Quest reward:** [Sign Quest](Sign_Quest.md), Ability to speak with [Umbala](Umbala.md) [NPCs](NPC.md)

1. Speak to the Village Leader (um in 39, 121) located inside a hut in north-west [Umbala](Umbala.md) ([Umbala](Umbala.md) 68, 253). You must be wearing a Mr. Smile.

2. He will ask you to bring him 10 Oil Paper, 5 Slick Paper, 1 Feather of Birds, and 1 Squid Ink. Once you bring the items back you will be able to talk to the rest of the [NPCs](NPC.md) in the village.
