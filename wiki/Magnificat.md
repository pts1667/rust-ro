# Magnificat

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Priest
- **Secondary class:** Mercenary
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 5 (Fixed)
- **Prerequisites:** None
- **SP cost:** 40 SP
- **Cast time:** 4 seconds
- **Delay:** 2 seconds
- **Duration:** (15 + (Skill Level*15)) seconds
- **Target:** Self
- **Range:** 14 x 14


## Description

Doubles the [SP recovery](SP_Recovery.md) rate for the caster and her/his [Party](Party.md) for the skill's duration.

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Skill Duration (sec) | 30 | 45 | 60 | 75 | 90 |


## Notes

* Magnificat ends if affected player logs out (as opposed to other buffs that will persist after logoff). Travelling between areas of different zone servers will also end Magnificat (as that consists of a logoff and a login)
