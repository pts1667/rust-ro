# Book

Books are used by [Priest](Priest.md), [Sage](Sage.md), and [TaeKwon Master](TaeKwon_Master.md) classes. Books usually have slower attack speeds than other weapons, but have medium ranged [ATK](Stats.md). Books don't have many varieties, though they do come in elemental form. Books are not sold from NPCs, making them fairly rare and hard to find. All books are also naturally unbreakable except in upgrade attempts.
