- **Window:** yes


## Helping Santa Deliver Christmas on time

1.  Talk to **Frantic Child** near the Christmas tree in [Lutie](Lutie.md) (142, 133) and ask about Santa needing help.
1.  Now go to **Santa Claus** in Lutie (198, 253) and agree to help.
1.  Talk to **Santa's Helper** directly to the right and have her warp you to Lutie Field.
1.  Talk to the **Holiday Helper** and hunt 1 Antonio.
1.  Return to the Holiday Helper and have him warp you back to Santa.
1.  Talk to Santa and he will tell you to go find his mother near the benches in Lutie.
1.  Speak with **Grandma Claus** and she ask you to gather 1 Milk, 5 Well-baked Cookie, 1 Needle Packet and 50 Piece of Red Fabric.
1.  After collecting all of the items return to Grandma and she will tell you to let Santa know the bag is complete.
1.  Talk to Santa Claus again and he thanks you for saving Christmas and Rewards you with 5 Well-baked Cookie.
1.  Now return to the Frantic Child and she thanks you and rewards you with 1 Named Rudolf Santa Hat.
