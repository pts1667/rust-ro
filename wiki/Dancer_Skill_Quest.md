# Dancer Skill Quest


## Charming Wink

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Job level requirement:** 40
- **Class requirement:** Dancer
- **Item requirement:** 1 Alcohol, 1 China, 1 Apple, 3 Banana, 5 Well Baked Cookies, 1 Crystal Mirror
- **Quest reward:** Ability to use [Charming Wink](Charming_Wink.md)

1. Near the center of Comodo (204, 172) you'll find a lady 'Canell' that you need to talk to. You will need to have a Crystal Mirror with you. She will take the Crystal Mirror.

2. In north Prontera (183, 333), you'll need to find and talk to the NPC Aelle. She will ask you to bring her the following items: 1 Alcohol, 1 China, 1 Apple, 3 Banana, 5 Well Baked Cookies. After giving her those items, she will be drunk, and wants nothing to do with you. She will get angry and send you somewhere in town (seems to be random) every time you try to talk to her.

3. Return to Canell in Comodo, and she will teach you [Charming Wink](Charming_Wink.md).
