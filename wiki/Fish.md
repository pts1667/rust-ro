# Fish

Fish are a race of monster. The majority of them are of the [Water](Water.md) element and reside in Byalan Dungeon. Cards which concern the fish race include:

*  **Armeyer Dinze Card**: Adds a 0.5% chance of the 'Clam Soup' item dropping when killing a Fish race monster
*  **Beetle King Card**: Gain 5 SP each time a Fish monster is killed by Physical Melee Attack. Drain 5 SP as the weapon is unequipped.
*  **Flora Card**: Increase damage on Fish monsters by 20%.
*  **Gullinbursti Card**: Receive 10% more experience points each time a Fish monster is killed. Receive 20% more damage from Fish monsters.
*  **Leaf Cat Card**: Add the chance of gaining 'Crystal Blue' item each time a Fish or Shellfish monster is killed. Add 10% more tolerance against [Water](Water.md) Property Attack.
*  **Rafflesia Card**: Reduce damage from Fish monsters by 30%.
*  **Rotar Zairo Card**: Inflict 10% more damage with Critical Attack. Critical +7 on Fish monsters.
*  **Roween Card**: Increases physical damage versus [Water](Water.md) property monsters by 10%. [Flee](Stats.md) + 5. [Perfect Dodge](Perfect_Dodge.md) + 3. Adds [CRIT](Stats.md) + 15 when fighting Fish race monsters.
*  **Sea-Otter Card**: Add the chance of gaining 'Sushi' or 'Raw Fish' item each time a Fish monster is killed. Sushi and Raw Fish items restore 50% more HP.
*  **Sleeper Card**: Add the chance of gaining 'Box of Drowsiness' item each time a Fish or Shellfish monster is killed.

Equipment concerning the Fish Race includes:
*  **Blue Fish**: Increase damage on Fish monsters by 10%.
*  **Lunakaligo**: If the user's base Str is 77 or higher: Aspd +4%, 15% chance of inflicting [Stun](Status_Effects.md) on the target when doing a melee attack, 5% chance of the 'Bomber Steak' item dropping when killing a [Brute](Brute.md) race monster,5% chance of the 'Green Salad' item dropping when killing a [Plant](Plant.md) race monster, 5% chance of the 'Steamed Ancient Lips' item dropping when killing a Fish race monster.
*  **Sashimi**: 40% chance of the Raw Fish item dropping when killing a Fish race monster. Wind Element One Handed Sword obtainable through the [Sashimi Knife Quest](Sashimi_Knife_Quest.md).
