# Sight Blaster

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Wizard
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 1
- **Quest:** Wizard Skill Quest
- **SP cost:** 40
- **Cast time:** .7 seconds
- **Delay:** None
- **Duration:** 120 seconds
- **Target:** Self
- **Element:** Fire


## Description

For two minutes, you will be surrounded by the fires of [Sight](Sight.md). While Sight Blaster is active, any enemy that steps in a 3x3 area around you will be thrown back and take a 100% [MATK](Stats.md) [Fire](Fire.md) hit. In [WoE](War_of_Emperium.md), there is no knockback. Once the skill has hit a target, Sight Blaster ends. Hits multiple targets if they are all in range of skill when activated.
