# Peco Peco Ride

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Knight
- **Secondary class:** Crusader
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** 1
- **Prerequisites:** ([Swordman](Swordman.md)) [Endure](Endure.md) Lv. 1


## Description

Enables [Knight](Knight.md)s or [Crusader](Crusader.md)s to ride a Peco Peco.

* Knights can buy Peco Pecos in front of the Chivalry building in Northwest [Prontera](Prontera.md).
* Crusaders can buy them in front of Prontera Church in Northeast Prontera. There is no difference between the Knight Peco Peco and Crusader Grand Peco aside from aesthetics.

When riding a PecoPeco:
*  The size modifier for Spear weapons on Medium monsters becomes 100% instead of 75%.
*  [Spear Mastery](Spear_Mastery.md)'s effect gains an additional +1 [ATK](Stats.md) per [Spear Mastery](Spear_Mastery.md) level.
*  The player [moves faster](Movement_Speed.md); approximately equal to the effect of [Increase Agility](Increase_AGI.md).
*  The player gains a [Weight Limit](Weight_Limit.md) bonus of 1000.
*  Attack Speed for all weapons is slowed by 50% ([Cavalier Mastery](Cavalier_Mastery.md) reduces this penalty).

Riding a Peco Peco is also required to perform the [Knight](Knight.md) skill [Brandish Spear](Brandish_Spear.md).
