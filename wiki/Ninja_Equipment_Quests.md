# Ninja Equipment Quests


## Arm Guard [1] Quest

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** 20
- **Class requirement:** Ninja
- **Item requirement:** 1 [Guard \[1\]](Guard.md), 1 Arm Guard \[0\]
- **Zeny requirement:** 10,000 Zeny
- **Item reward:** Arm Guard \[1\]

1. Talk to Boshu (que_ng 28,50) in the Ninja Guild in [Amatsu](Amatsu.md) (147,136). He'll ask you to bring him a [Guard \[1\]](Guard.md).

2. After bringing him the Guard [1], Boshu will examine it (but will not take it from you), then offer to create a slotted version of the Arm Guard if you give him 1 [Guard \[1\]](Guard.md), 1 Arm Guard \[0\], and 10,000 Zeny. (Arm Guard [0]s can be bought from Boonray in the Ninja Guild's supply room.)

3. Give him the required items and pay the fee, and the player will receive a slotted Arm Guard.


## Advanced Arm Guard [0] Quest

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** 50
- **Class requirement:** Ninja
- **Item requirement:** 1 Mirror Shield \[0\], 1 Arm Guard \[0\]
- **Zeny requirement:** 20,000 Zeny
- **Item reward:** Advanced Arm Guard \[0\]

1. Talk to Batshu (que_ng 28,45) in the Ninja Guild in [Amatsu](Amatsu.md) (147,136). He'll ask you to bring him a Mirror Shield \[0\].

2. After bringing him the Mirror Shield [0], Batshu will examine it (but will not take it from you), then offer to create a new version of the Arm Guard if you give him 1 Mirror Shield \[0\], 1 Arm Guard \[0\], and 20,000 Zeny. (Arm Guard [0]s can be bought from Boonray in the Ninja Guild's supply room.)

3. Give him the required items and pay the fee, and the player will receive an Advanced Arm Guard.


## Advanced Arm Guard [1] Quest

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** Unknown
- **Class requirement:** Ninja
- **Item requirement:** 1 Buckler \[1\], 1 Advanced Arm Guard \[0\]
- **Zeny requirement:** 40,000
- **Item reward:** Advanced Arm Guard \[1\] -OR- 1 Bomber Steak

1. Talk to Toshu (que_ng 28,18) in the Ninja Guild in [Amatsu](Amatsu.md) (147,136). He'll ask you for 1 Buckler \[1\], 1 Advanced Arm Guard \[0\], and 40,000 Zeny.

* Note: The NPC calls the Advanced Arm Guard an "Upgraded Arm Guard".

2. When you bring the items to him, he will warn you that there is a chance that the slotting process will fail.

* If he succeeds, you'll receive an Advanced Arm Guard \[1\].
* If he fails, you'll receive 1 Bomber Steak as compensation for the lost items.
