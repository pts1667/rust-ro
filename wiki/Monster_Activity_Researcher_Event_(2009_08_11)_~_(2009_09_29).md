# Monster Activity Researcher Event (2009/08/11) ~ (2009/09/29)

> **Note:** Not implemented on iRO Classic.


## Overview

The following [NPC](NPC.md)s can be temporarily found in some of the game's dungeons. They will give the player a choice of how many monsters they wish to hunt at a time. 50, 100, or 150 monsters can be hunted. [EXP](Experience.md) rewarded per kill is the same for all three amounts (i.e. Killing 100 monsters gives twice as much experience as killing 50), as well as being the same gained when normally killing a monster (effectively making leveling on the monsters equal to 2x EXP). **Valkyrie versions have rewards equal to 1x, while Premium Servers (Chaos/Loki/Iris) have rewards equal to 1.5x.**

* **Note:** *Each quest **is repeatable** so long as the player remains within the level limits.*
* **Note:** *[Mercenary](Mercenary_System.md) kills will **not** increase your kill count!*
