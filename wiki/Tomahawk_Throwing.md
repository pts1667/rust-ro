# Tomahawk Throwing

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 1
- **Prerequisites:** Must have a Tomahawk Equipped
- **SP cost:** 1
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** Animation
- **Target:** Foe
- **Range:** 9 Cells
- **Element:** Wind


## Description

Throw a Tomahawk that will arc like a boomerang and comeback to inflict damage. This skill carries all the stats of a regular attack.
*  This skill is copyable by [Intimidate](Intimidate.md), thus allowing the use of different weapons with it, such as status effect weapons.
