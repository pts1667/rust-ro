# Disarm

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Gunslinger
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** ([Gunslinger](Gunslinger.md)) [Tracking](Tracking.md) Lv. 7
- **SP cost:** 10 + (Skill Level*5)
- **Target:** Foe
- **Range:** 7 cells
- **Status:** Strip
- **Weapon:** Pistol
- **Weapon2:** Rifle


## Description

Aim at an enemy's appendages with a [Pistol](Gun.md) or [Rifle](Gun.md) to render it incapable of attacking. Target cannot be more than 7 cells away, and this skill's level affects its success rate. On monsters, this skill will reduce [ATK](Stats.md) by 25%, although Boss monsters are unaffected.

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chance (%) | 3 | 6 | 9 | 12 | 15 |


## Notes

*  This skill works similar to [Rogue](Rogue.md) and [Stalker](Stalker.md)'s [Divest Weapon](Divest_Weapon.md):
  * [DEX](Stats.md) difference between the caster and the target affects the success chance.
