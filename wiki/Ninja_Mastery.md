# Ninja Mastery

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Ninja
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** 10
- **Prerequisites:** None


## Description

Increase SP Restoration speed by training in the Ninja Arts. The amount of [SP](SP.md) restored by this skill is affected by the character's [Max SP](Max_SP.md).

| Level | Base SP Gain | Bonus (% of Max SP) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 3 | 0.2% |
| 2 | 6 | 0.4% |
| 3 | 9 | 0.6% |
| 4 | 12 | 0.8% |
| 5 | 15 | 1.0% |
| 6 | 18 | 1.2% |
| 7 | 21 | 1.4% |
| 8 | 24 | 1.6% |
| 9 | 27 | 1.8% |
| 10 | 30 | 2.0% |
