# Crusader Skill Quest


## Shrink Skill Quest

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Job level requirement:** None
- **Class requirement:** Crusader
- **Item requirement:** 1 Red Potion, 20 Sticky Mucus, 3 Empty Bottles, 5 Jellopy, 1 Coal, 3 Cyfar, 1 Grape, 1 Unripe Apple
- **Quest reward:** Ability to use [Shrink](Shrink.md)

1. Talk to Ford in [Geffen](Geffen.md) (geffen 110, 117) and you'll be asked to find a Soldier (gef_fild13 297,242).

2. Prepare 1 Red Potion for the soldier and return to Ford for further help.

3. Proceed to the [Prontera](Prontera.md) Church to find Arther Pastor (prt_church 87,127) for assistance to cure the soldier.

4. Collect 20 Sticky Mucus, 3 Empty Bottles, 5 Jellopy, 1 Coal, 1 Grape, 3 Cyfar and 1 Unripe Apple to prepare an antidote.

5. Race the antidote back to the Soldier to cure him. If you fail, return to Arther Pastor for another antidote.

6. Once successful, you'll be taught [Shrink](Shrink.md).
