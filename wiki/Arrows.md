# Arrows


## Introduction

Arrows are a type of ammunition used by bow weapons and by [Arrow Vulcan](Arrow_Vulcan.md) which uses a [Whip](Whip.md) or [Instrument](Instrument.md). However, they are equippable by all [Classes](Classes.md). For example, an assassin could use Stun Arrows and [Grimtooth](Grimtooth.md) mobs, making them [Stun](Status_Effects.md). To equip ammunition, you must double-click the arrow to want to equip.

## Gaining Arrows

*  [Arrow Crafting](Arrow_Crafting.md) is a highly effective method of gaining different kinds of arrows not easily found. This skill can only be obtained by a [quest](Archer_Skill_Quest.md).
*  The [Ore Downgrading](Ore_Downgrading.md) quest can be used to get materials for elemental arrows.
*  Fire Arrows can be bought from the [Alberta](Alberta.md) Island Tool Dealer (alb2trea 87, 65), the [Einbech](Einbech.md) Tool Dealer (inside the building at [Einbech](Einbech.md) 177, 133), and the [Lighthalzen](Lighthalzen.md) Arrow Dealer (inside the building at [Lighthalzen](Lighthalzen.md) 324, 301).
*  Silver Arrows can be bought via the following NPCs.
*  One of the rarest type of arrows, Immaterial Arrow are useless against [Neutral](Neutral.md) monsters, but are lethal to [Ghost](Ghost.md) monsters. The easiest method of obtaining them is by crafting an [Emperium](War_of_Emperium.md), a Piece of Shield, or a Skeletal Armor Piece.
*  The rarest of all arrows is the Holy Arrow. It can be obtained from Holy Arrow Quivers, which are dropped by two monsters at the Rachel Sanctuary, or crafting Golden Ornaments and Valhalla's Flowers, which are dropped by Dame of Sentinel and by Valkyries, respectively. However, it is superior to the Silver Arrow.
*  It is possible to stack 500 arrows in a quiver and halve their [Weight](Weight_Limit.md) using an [NPC](NPC.md) in [Payon](Payon.md), in the weapon shop. It is usually better weight-wise to simply craft them. However, it is possible to open a quiver while being overweight, while [Arrow Crafting](Arrow_Crafting.md) is disabled. Quivers are also good for arrows bought from [NPC](NPC.md)s.
