# Deluge

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Sage
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 5 (Fixed)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Sage](Sage.md)) [Endow Tsunami](Endow_Tsunami.md) Lv. 2
- **SP cost:** (50 - (Skill level*2))
- **Cast time:** 5 seconds
- **Duration:** (Skill level) minutes
- **Target:** Ground
- **Range:** Melee
- **Area:** 7x7
- **Catalyst:** 1 Yellow Gemstone


## Description

Cast an area on the ground that will amplify all [Water](Water.md) element attacks and increase the [Max HP](Max_HP.md) of [Water](Water.md) element characters.

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Water Element Damage (%) | 10 | 14 | 17 | 19 | 20 |
| +Max HP (%) | 5 | 9 | 12 | 14 | 15 |
| SP Cost | 48 | 46 | 44 | 42 | 40 |
| Duration (min) | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |


## Notes

*  Deluge also enables the use of the [Water Ball](Water_Ball.md) and [Aqua Benedicta](Aqua_Benedicta.md) skills.
*  Deluge acts as **shallow water** for skills [Aqua Benedicta](Aqua_Benedicta.md) and [Water Ball](Water_Ball.md). While [Water Ball](Water_Ball.md) consumes Deluge rapidly, [Aqua Benedicta](Aqua_Benedicta.md) can be used endlessly while the spell on the ground lasts.
*  Deluge doubles the duration of [Blinding Mist](Blinding_Mist.md).
*  Deluge cannot be cast on a cell currently affected by any floor skill or ground targeted skill.
*  Initial cast consumes 1 Yellow Gemstone and grants the Elemental Floor buff for the duration. Casting Deluge, [Whirlwind](Whirlwind.md) or [Volcano](Volcano.md) while the buff is active will not incur a gemstone cost.
