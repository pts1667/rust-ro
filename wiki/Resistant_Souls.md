# Resistant Souls

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Crusader
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** ([Crusader](Crusader.md)) [Divine Protection](Divine_Protection.md) Lv. 5, [Demon Bane](Demon_Bane.md) Lv. 5, [Faith](Faith.md) Lv. 5, [Heal](Heal.md) Lv. 5
- **SP cost:** 30
- **Cast time:** 1 second
- **Delay:** 3 seconds
- **Duration:** 180 seconds
- **Target:** Players (Non-[Crusader](Crusader.md)s)


## Description

Temporarily increase resistance against [Demon](Demon.md) race and [Holy](Holy.md) property attacks. [Crusader](Crusader.md)s cannot cast Resistant Souls on themselves, including fellow Crusaders.

| Level | [Demon](Demon.md) and [Holy](Holy.md) Reduction |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 5% |
| 2 | 10% |
| 3 | 15% |
| 4 | 20% |
| 5 | 25% |
