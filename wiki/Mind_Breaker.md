# Mind Breaker

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Scholar
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 5 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Mage](Mage.md)) [Increase SP Recovery](Increase_SP_Recovery.md) Lv. 3, ([Scholar](Scholar.md)) [Soul Siphon](Soul_Siphon.md) Lv. 2
- **SP cost:** (9 + (Skill Level*3))
- **Cast time:** None
- **Duration:** 30 seconds
- **Delay:** (0.7 + (Skill Level*0.1)) seconds
- **Target:** Foe
- **Range:** 9 cells


## Description

Grants a chance to disrupt the enemy's mental balance. When successful, this will decrease the enemy's Soft [MDEF](Stats.md) but will also increase its [MATK](Stats.md). Similar to the [Provoke](Provoke.md) status, the enemy will also target the caster. Does not affect [Boss](Boss_Protocol.md) or [Undead](Undead.md) monsters.

| Level | MATK increase | Soft MDEF decrease | Success chance | Cast delay | SP Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 20 | 12 | 60 | 0.8 | 12 |
| 2 | 40 | 24 | 65 | 0.9 | 15 |
| 3 | 60 | 36 | 70 | 1.0 | 18 |
| 4 | 80 | 48 | 72 | 1.1 | 21 |
| 5 | 100 | 60 | 80 | 1.2 | 24 |


## Tips and Tricks

Because this skill only reduces Soft [MDEF](Stats.md), the damage increase when using Magic against the target is not notable. For this reason, most players use it as a Supportive skill by leaving their [Guild](Guild_System.md)/[Party](Party.md) to cast on [Wizards](Wizard.md), doubling their damage. This is risky however, since the [Scholar](Scholar.md) will be unsupported and can be attacked by their own 'allies'.
