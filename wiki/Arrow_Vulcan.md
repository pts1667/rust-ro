# Arrow Vulcan

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Gypsy
- **Secondary class:** Minstrel
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 10 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Archer](Archer.md)) [Arrow Shower](Arrow_Shower.md) Lv. 5 ([Gypsy](Gypsy.md)) [Slinging Arrow](Slinging_Arrow.md) Lv. 1; ([Minstrel](Minstrel.md)) [Melody Strike](Melody_Strike.md) Lv. 1
- **SP cost:** (10 + (Skill Level*2))
- **Cast time:** (1.8 + (Skill Level*0.2)) seconds
- **Delay:** 0.8 at Levels 1-5; 1 at Levels 6-10
- **Target:** Foe
- **Range:** 10
- **Catalyst:** 1 Arrow
- **Weapon:** Instrument
- **Weapon2:** Whip


## Skill Description

Attacks an enemy nine times using a Whip/Musical weapon. Damage is modified by cards and arrow attribute. The Gypsy/Minstrel is immobile during the activation of this skill and while the damage is being dealt to the enemy over the span of a few seconds.

| Level | Damage (%) | Casting Time | Skill Delay (Seconds) | SP Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 300 | 2.0 | 0.8 | 12 |
| 2 | 400 | 2.2 | 0.8 | 14 |
| 3 | 500 | 2.4 | 0.8 | 16 |
| 4 | 600 | 2.6 | 0.8 | 18 |
| 5 | 700 | 2.8 | 0.8 | 20 |
| 6 | 800 | 3.0 | 1 | 22 |
| 7 | 900 | 3.2 | 1 | 24 |
| 8 | 1000 | 3.4 | 1 | 26 |
| 9 | 1100 | 3.6 | 1 | 28 |
| 10 | 1200 | 3.8 | 1 | 30 |


## Notes

* The skill's damage is calculated and dealt as a single hit, with the animation showing 9 hits for visual effect.
  * %Damage = (200 + (Skill Level*100))
  * Arrow Vulcan Damage = [Base ATK * (Skill Level + 2)] * Cards * Weapon Effects
* Since the skill delay is shorter then the animation, it's possible to cast other skills after the initial 0.8 or 1 second. Targeted skills cannot be cast during this time, only non-targeted skills such as [Improve Concentration](Improve_Concentration.md) or a Song/Dance.
