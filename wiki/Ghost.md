## Cards

Cards which concern the Ghost property include:


## Armor

* Ghostring Card: Enchant an Armor with the Ghost property. Reduce HP Recovery by 25%.


## Weaponry

* Mao Guai Card: Inflict 20% more damage on Ghost Property.


## Garment

* Marionette Card: Increase resistance to Ghost property by 30%. Flee Rate +5
* Whisper Card: Flee Rate +20 Increase damage from Ghost property attacks by 50%.
