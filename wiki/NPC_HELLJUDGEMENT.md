# NPC HELLJUDGEMENT


## Description


## Hell's Judgement

* Area-of-Effect Offensive Skill
* Levels: 10
* Target: Self

An area of effect skill that causes physical damage and [Curse](Status_Effects.md)s all players in an 29x29 area.  When cast, all players struck will see the death animation over their heads.

| Skill Level | [ATK](Stats.md) Modifier |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 100% |
| 2 | 200% |
| 3 | 300% |
| 4 | 400% |
| 5 | 500% |
| 6 | 600% |
| 7 | 700% |
| 8 | 800% |
| 9 | 900% |
| 10 | 1000% |


## Notes

* You can avoid the damage of this skill using [Safety Wall](Safety_Wall.md) when closer than 4 cells to the monster, and [Pneuma](Pneuma.md) when more than 4 cells away from the monster. Having enough [Flee](Stats.md) can  enable the player to "dodge" the hit too.


## Obtainable via

* Ledger of Death - Lv. 5 when attacking (only for [Sage](Sage.md))
