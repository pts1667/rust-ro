# Trigger Happy Shot

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Gunslinger
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 10
- **Prerequisites:** ([Gunslinger](Gunslinger.md)) [Chain Action](Chain_Action.md) Lv. 3
- **SP cost:** (20 + (Skill level*2))
- **Delay:** 0.5 secs
- **Target:** Foe
- **Range:** Ranged
- **Catalyst:** 5 Bullets
- **Weapon:** Pistol


## Description

Rapidly fire 5 bullets from a [Pistol](Gun.md) at one target, consumes 5 bullets for each use.
