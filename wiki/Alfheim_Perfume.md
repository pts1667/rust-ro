# Alfheim Perfume

> **Repeatable** — cooldown: 18 Hour

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Quests required:** [Ring of the Wise King](Ring_of_the_Wise_King.md)
- **Job EXP reward:** 700,000
- **Item reward:** 2 Splendide Coin
- **Window:** yes

1.  In Splendide, speak to High Laphine Grenouille (splendide 228, 162 -> spl_in01 30, 324), she asks you to bring her a seed for a Flower of Alfheim.
1.  Speak to the Middle-ranked Laphine inside the hut at (splendide 284, 228), she will give you a sprout.
1.  Place the Sprout on any of the purifiers around the perimeter of the center of the village.
  * 260, 187
  * 163, 83
  * 169, 117
  * 161, 266
  * 135, 280
  * 161, 365
  * 259, 187
  * 228, 162
1.  Return to Grenouille and give her the flower, she makes a perfume out of it and asks you to take it to the exhausted soldiers.
  * 205, 139
  * 196, 153
  * 181. 135
  * 139, 178
  * 164, 188
  * 177, 257
1.  Return to Grenouille.
  * Receive 2 Splendide Coins and 700,000 [Job Experience](Job_EXP_Chart.md).
