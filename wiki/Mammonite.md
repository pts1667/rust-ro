# Mammonite

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Merchant
- **Secondary class:** Super Novice
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 10 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** None
- **SP cost:** 5
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** Entirely [ASPD](ASPD.md)
- **Target:** Enemy
- **Range:** Melee
- **Catalyst:** [Zeny](Zeny.md)


## Description

Uses a certain amount of [Zeny](Zeny.md) to deal high damage to an opponent. The amount of zeny used can be reduced by 10% if the user has the quest skill [Dubious Salesmanship](Dubious_Salesmanship.md).

| [Zeny](Zeny.md) Cost | 100 | 200 | 300 | 400 | 500 | 600 | 700 | 800 | 900 | 1000 |
