# Spirit Sphere

Floating balls of light summoned by the [Summon Spirit Sphere](Summon_Spirit_Sphere.md) skill. Spirit Spheres grant 3 ATK each that always hit, similar to forged weapons imbued with Star Crumb. A player can have a maximum of 5 spheres on them at one time. Certain skills use spirit spheres. A monk can transfer a sphere to another player using [Spiritual Bestowment](Spiritual_Bestowment.md). If you have an [accessory](Accessories.md) with a Greatest General Card attached, you will have a chance to get a spirit sphere without needing the summon skill.
