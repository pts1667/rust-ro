# NPC AGIUP


## Description

*[image not archived: Npc agiup.gif]*

*  Supportive skill
*  Levels: 5
*  Duration: (5 + (Skill Level*5)) seconds

Increases [Movement Speed](Movement_Speed.md) and [Flee](Stats.md) Rate.  Movement speed increases by 100%, and the boost to flee is based on a percent increase of the caster's agility.

| Skill level | Agility Rate | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 220% | 10 |
| 2 | 240% | 15 |
| 3 | 260% | 20 |
| 4 | 280% | 25 |
| 5 | 300% | 30 |
