# Repeatable EXP Quests


## Overview

**Each of these quests ARE repeatable.**
* iRO Classic Loki server offers a series of repeatable quests that ask the player to collect a certain number (20 / 25 / 50) of a particular item and will reward [EXP](Experience.md) when completed. These items are especially valuable as they can be saved for another character or sold/purchased from other players.


## Item Turn-Ins

* If a player has both a Monster Hunting quest and an Item Turn-In quest that originated from the same [NPC](NPC.md), he will be unable to complete the Item Turn-In quest until he has completed or abandoned the Monster Hunting quest.

## Monster Hunting

The following NPCs will give the player a choice of how many monsters they wish to hunt at a time. 50, 100, or 150 monsters can be hunted. EXP rewarded per kill is the same for all three amounts (i.e. Killing 100 monsters gives twice as much experience as killing 50).

* Kill counts are shared among party members that are within the killer's screen range *(The person who dealt the final blow must be viewable on your screen).*

* If the wrong option is accidentally chosen, players can give up the quest and will lose all current progress, which allows the player to choose a different size of monster hunt.

* Maximum experience gain for each 50-monster turn-in is 1 Base Level and 1 Job Level.
100 monster and 150 monster turn-in allows a maximum gain of 2 and 3 levels respectively.

**The rewards listed are for killing FIFTY monsters. Double or triple this amount to calculate reward values for hunting 100 or 150 monsters.**

* **Note:** *[Homunculus](Homunculus_System.md) and [Mercenary](Mercenary_System.md) kills will **not** increase your kill count!*
* **Note:** It is possible to keep the quest, become a [Transcendent](Transcendent.md) and collect the reward before the minimum level requirement.
