# Super Novice Job Change Guide

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** 45
- **Job level requirement:** 10
- **Class requirement:** Novice
- **Item requirement:** 30 Resin, 30 Sticky Mucus
- **Item reward:** Pantie
- **Quest reward:** Jobchange to [Super Novice](Super_Novice.md)
- **Window:** yes
- **Cat:** no

1. Head to [Al De Baran](Al_De_Baran.md) and go to the Windmill buildind in the southwest region of the map (116, 63). Enter the room to the left and talk to Tzerero.

2. He will ask you to bring the following items:

* Resin x 30
* Sticky Mucus x 30

3. Once you bring the items, and if you fulfill the following requirements, you will be changed into a [Super Novice](Super_Novice.md):

* Job Level 10
* Base Level 45

3. In honor of successfully changing into a Super Novice, you will receive a pair of Panties.
