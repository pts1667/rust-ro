# Shockwave Trap

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Hunter
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 5 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Hunter](Hunter.md)) [Ankle Snare](Ankle_Snare.md) Lv. 1
- **SP cost:** 45
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** [ASPD](ASPD.md) Based
- **Duration:** (240-Skill Level*40) seconds
- **Target:** Ground
- **Range:** Melee
- **Catalyst:** 2 Traps


## Description

This skill consumes 2 Trap items per use. Shockwave Traps drain SP from enemies when stepped on. The level of the skill affects the amount of SP drained.

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SP Drained | 20% | 35% | 50% | 65% | 80% |
| Trap Duration (sec.) | 200 | 160 | 120 | 80 | 40 |
