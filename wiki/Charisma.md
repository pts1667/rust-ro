# Charisma

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Guild
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** Unknown
- **Prerequisites:** Unknown


## Description

The guild master gains charisma through this skill.  Guild members near the guild master gain increased [HIT](Stats.md) and [FLEE](FLEE.md) rate.
