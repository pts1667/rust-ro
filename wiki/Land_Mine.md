# Land Mine

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Hunter
- **Secondary class:** Mercenary
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** None
- **SP cost:** 10
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** [ASPD](ASPD.md) Based
- **Duration:** (240 - (Skill level*40)) seconds
- **Target:** Ground
- **Range:** Melee
- **Element:** Earth
- **Status:** [Stun](Status_Effects.md)
- **Catalyst:** 1 Trap


## Description

A trap that explodes when triggered, inflicting [Earth](Earth.md) property damage with a chance of inflicting the [Stun](Status_Effects.md) status on an enemy. The caster's [DEX](Stats.md), [INT](Stats.md) and skill level affect the amount of damage caused by Land Mine.

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Duration (sec.) | 200 | 160 | 120 | 80 | 40 |


## Information

* Damage Formula: Skill Level * (75 + [DEX](Stats.md)) * {1 + ([INT](Stats.md) / 100)}
