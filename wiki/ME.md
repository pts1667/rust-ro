# ME

**ME** is an acronym, and is known to stand for the following:

* [Magnetic Earth](Magnetic_Earth.md), a [Sage](Sage.md) skill.
* [Magnus Exorcismus](Magnus_Exorcismus.md), a [Priest](Priest.md) skill.
