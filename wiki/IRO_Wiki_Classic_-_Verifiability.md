# Verifiability


## General Information

Information (Text, Images, and other Media) should be verifiable as:

* Coming from iRO (See: IRO Wiki:Use iRO)
* Being current to iRO (i.e. not having been replaced/overwritten by a previous update)

Outdated information should be noted as such.


## Resolving Conflict

In the case of different opinions arising about a certain problem, discussion is to be brought to the talk page of the corresponding article. Cease editing about the problem immediately, to prevent editing wars. If a solution cannot be found, the problem is to be moved to the forums for public opinion. iRO Wiki will then follow the general public opinion on the problem.
