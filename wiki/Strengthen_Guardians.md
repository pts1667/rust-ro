# Strengthen Guardians

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Guild
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** 3
- **Prerequisites:** None


## Description

Enhance the strength of Guardians that are protecting Guild Castles in your Guild's possession. This skill's level affects the guardians' [Attack Power](Stats.md) and [Speed](ASPD.md).

* Note: This skill has no effect on [WoE 2](War_of_Emperium_2.md) guardians as they are considered [Boss](Boss_Protocol.md) property and not actual guardian property.
