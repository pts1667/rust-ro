# Adrenaline Rush

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Blacksmith
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** ([Blacksmith](Blacksmith.md)) [Hammer Fall](Hammer_Fall.md) Lv. 2
- **SP cost:** 17 + (3*Skill Level)
- **Duration:** (30*Skill Level) seconds
- **Target:** Self
- **Weapon:** Axe
- **Weapon2:** Mace


## Description

Adrenaline Rush is the Battle [Blacksmith](Blacksmith.md)s best friend. This skill increases the [ASPD](ASPD.md) of Axe and Mace class weapons across the entire party. It gives a 0.30 delay decrease to [ASPD](ASPD.md) to [Blacksmith](Blacksmith.md)s and a 0.25 delay decrease to all other job classes that have an Axe or Mace class weapon equipped at the time.


## Stats on Adrenaline Rush

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Duration (seconds) | 30 | 60 | 90 | 120 | 150 |
| SP Cost | 20 | 23 | 26 | 29 | 32 |

Keep in mind that [Hilt Binding](Hilt_Binding.md) adds +10% duration for the Blacksmith(s) with the skill.
