# Prepare Potion

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Alchemist
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 10
- **Prerequisites:** ([Alchemist](Alchemist.md)) [Potion Research](Potion_Research.md) Lv. 5
- **SP cost:** 5
- **Target:** Player
- **Catalyst:** 1 Medicine Bowl


## Description

Enables manufacturing of potions and chemicals.
*  The higher the skill level, the higher chance of a successful item creation.
*  Each cast consumes 1 Medicine Bowl.
*  Various ingredients are needed to create potions, refer to the [Alchemist](Alchemist.md) section under Potion Creation for details on whats needed to make potions and chemicals.
