# Official Guild Approval

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Guild
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** 1
- **Prerequisites:** None


## Description

Allows a guild to participate in the [War of Emperium](War_of_Emperium.md) and [War of Emperium 2](War_of_Emperium_2.md). Without this skill, you will not be able to hit the Emperium no matter how much [HIT](Stats.md) your character has.
