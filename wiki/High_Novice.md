## Overview

High Novice is the first step on the road to becoming a Transcendent Class. After [Rebirthing](Rebirth_Walkthrough.md), you'll be sent to the town of the first class you started as. High Novices are the same as their regular Novice counterparts. When becoming a High Novice, you'll learn both novice Quest Skills. High Novices have 25% more [HP](Max_HP.md)/[SP](SP.md) than regular Novices, 1 additional point in each stat through job bonuses, and can be differentiated by their blue (female) or brown (male) clothing. High Novices level the same way regular novices do, and must learn all 9 [Basic Skill](Basic_Skill.md)s again in order to change to their respective [High First Class](High_First_Class.md) job. High Novices must choose the same job path they've had previously, they cannot switch to a different path.
