# Spiritual Thrift

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** High Priest
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** ([Acolyte](Acolyte.md)) [Demon Bane](Demon_Bane.md) Lv. 10, ([Priest](Priest.md)) [Mace Mastery](Mace_Mastery.md) Lv. 10


## Description

Permanently reduces the amount of [SP](SP.md) that is consumed when using skills. This skill is geared towards Battle High Priest builds.

| Level | - Skill SP Cost |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 4% |
| 2 | 8% |
| 3 | 12% |
| 4 | 16% |
| 5 | 20% |
