# Counter Kick Stance

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** TaeKwon Kid
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 1
- **Prerequisites:** ([TaeKwon Kid](TaeKwon_Kid.md)) [Counter Kick](Counter_Kick.md) Lv. 1
- **SP cost:** 1
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** ?
- **Target:** Self


## Definition

Enables a 20% chance of automatically entering the Counter Kick Stance when the [TaeKwon Kid](TaeKwon_Kid.md) successfully hits a target.


## Notes

*  [Soul Linker](Soul_Linker.md)s cannot use this skill.
