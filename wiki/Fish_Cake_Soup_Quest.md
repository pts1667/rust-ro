# Fish Cake Soup Quest

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Item requirement:** 1 Mushroom Spore -OR- 1 [Whip \[1\]](Whip.md) -OR- 1 Bookclip In Memory -OR- 1 China
- **Item reward:** 3 Fish Cake Soup (Repeatable)
- **Window:** yes

1. Go into the house in [Hugel](Hugel.md) (81, 228) and talk to Cellette Lavit inside.

2. Ask her if you can work for her.

3. She will give one Fish Cake Soup to deliver to one of 4 [NPC](NPC.md)s.

*  **Layoma** at [Hugel](Hugel.md) (84, 125).
*  **Neha** at [Hugel](Hugel.md) (76, 134).
*  **Maewan** at [Hugel](Hugel.md) (86, 139)
*  **Erjan** at [Hugel](Hugel.md) (102, 169)

4. You will invariably be late, each of the 4 NPCs will demand an item.

*  Layoma will ask for 1 Mushroom Spore
*  Neha will ask for a [Whip \[1\]](Whip.md) before she accepts the Fish Cake Soup. (she will hit the player twice with it, each for 1/4th the player's total [HP](Max_HP.md))
*  Maewan will ask for a Bookclip In Memory for his collection
*  Erjan will ask for 1 China

5. Return to Cellette Lavit and receive 3 Fish Cake Soup.
