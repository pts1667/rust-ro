# Message Delivery Quest

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** 80
- **Item requirement:** Witherless Rose, 10 Yggdrasil Leaf
- **Item reward:** 10 Yggdrasil Leaf -OR- 10 Yggdrasil Leaf, 3 Yggdrasil Seed, 2 Yggdrasilberry

1. Talk to Eriuous in the [Prontera](Prontera.md) Pub. He asks you deliver a message to his lost love, Elly.

2. Find Billik in [Umbala](Umbala.md) ([Umbala](Umbala.md) 216, 182) and he will tell you about someone in the City of the Dead, aka [Nifflheim](Nifflheim.md).

3. Talk to Feylin (niflheim 152, 215). After talking to him several times he will try to make contact with the one you're searching for, but he'll need 10 Yggdrasil Leaf. When two options appear do not choose Sorry otherwise he will NOT talk to you.

*  Note: You must bring a Witherless Rose to get Feylin to talk to you.

3. Depending on how things turn out, he'll either succeed or fail at summoning Elly.

*  If he fails, you will lose most of your HP.

4. Talk to Erious again. He asks if you met Elly. Choose either answer.

*  If Feylin succeeded, you will be given 10 Yggdrasil Leaf, 3 Yggdrasil Seed, and 2 Yggdrasilberry.

*  If Feylin failed, you will be given 10 Yggdrasil Leaf.
