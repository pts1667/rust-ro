# Kunai


## Kunai

Kunai are equipped like [Arrows](Arrows.md) and are used for the skill [Throw Kunai](Throw_Kunai.md). While [Shuriken](Shuriken.md)s can be purchased directly from the NPC, Kunai must be manufactured. Only [Ninja](Ninja.md)s can make Kunai through an NPC at the Ninja Guild in [Amatsu](Amatsu.md).
They are made in sets of 10 each and weights 2 each kunai.

Fell Poison Kunai are the cheapest to manufacture, making them the preferred Kunai for [Neutral](Neutral.md)-[Element](Element.md) targets.

| High Wind Kunai | 30 | [Wind](Wind.md) | 4 Flash Shuriken + 2 Wind Stone | 38z |

| Fell Poison Kunai | 30 | [Poison](Poison.md) | 20 [Shuriken](Shuriken.md) + 1 Shadow Orb | 28z |
