# Maximum Power-Thrust

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Mastersmith
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** ([Blacksmith](Blacksmith.md)) [Power-Thrust](Power-Thrust.md) Lv. 5
- **SP cost:** 15
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** None
- **Duration:** 180 seconds
- **Target:** Self


## Description

Enhance the damage inflicted by the caster's equipped weapon. Maximum Power-Thrust can only be cast on the caster, and cannot affect other characters. While active, this skill adds a 0.1% chance of breaking the equipped weapon each time it is used to attack, with the exception of Axe or Mace-type weapons since these weapons are naturally immune to battle damage.

This skill does not stack with regular [Power-Thrust](Power-Thrust.md), and will completely override its effects if it is casted.
