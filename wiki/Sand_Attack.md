# Sand Attack

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Thief
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 1
- **Quest:** Thief Skill Quest#Sand Attack Quest
- **SP cost:** 9
- **Cast time:** None
- **Target:** Enemy
- **Element:** Earth


## Description

Attacks a target with an [Earth](Earth.md) property attack that has a 15% chance of inflicting the [Blind](Status_Effects.md) status. Sand Attack inflicts damage equal to 125% of the user's attack power. Sand Attack stacks with [EDP](Enchant_Deadly_Poison.md) and can inflict other status ailments if used with status cards/weapons.
