# Pierce

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Knight
- **Secondary class:** Mercenary
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 10
- **SP cost:** 7
- **Prerequisites:** ([Knight](Knight.md)) [Spear Mastery](Spear_Mastery.md) Lv. 1
- **Delay:** [ASPD](Stats.md) Based
- **Target:** Enemy
- **Range:** 3 cells
- **Weapon:** Spear


## Description

Strikes a single opponent with a spear. Deals multiple strikes depending on the enemy's [Size](Size.md): 1 hit on [Small](Size.md) foes, 2 hits on [Medium](Size.md) foes, 3 hits on [Large](Size.md) foes (players are Medium, except for babies, who are Small). A small accuracy bonus is also added; this is added to the hit rate after it is calculated from the player's [Hit](Stats.md) and the target's [Flee](Stats.md).

| 10 | 200% | 50% |


## Applications

[Knight](Knight.md)s who use [Spear](Spear.md)s use Pierce as the main single-target damage skill. As a result, many Knights prefer to hunt larger-sized opponents to leverage this advantage. Pierce has a larger damage modifier than [Bash](Bash.md) for large targets. However, Bash has the added ability to [Stun](Status_Effects.md), which may be useful for high [Flee](Stats.md) opponents. For small opponents, Bash or [Bowling Bash](Bowling_Bash.md) may be more suitable.


## Notes

*  The accuracy bonus is added after the hit rate is determined. If a player would usually hit a monster for 50% of attacks, using level 10 Pierce raises that rate to 75%.
