# Blaze Shield

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Ninja
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 10
- **Prerequisites:** ([Ninja](Ninja.md)) [Flaming Petals](Flaming_Petals.md) Lv. 5
- **SP cost:** 25
- **Delay:** 1 second
- **Duration:** 20 seconds
- **Target:** Ground
- **Area:** 5x5 cells
- **Element:** Fire
- **Catalyst:** 1 Flame Stone


## Description

Summon a blazing storm in a 5*5 cell area around the caster that will deliver multiple strikes to enemies within its range. The Blaze Shield lasts for 20 seconds, but is automatically canceled after inflicting its maximum number of strikes. Each cast consumes 1 Flame Stone. Consumes 25 SP regardless of the skill's level. Mastered at Skill Level 10.

A ninja can only have a single fire formation active at once. If you recast it, the old one will disappear and the new one will cast successfully. Fire Formation is cancelled by [Magnetic Earth](Magnetic_Earth.md).
