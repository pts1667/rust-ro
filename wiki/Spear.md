# Spear

Spears are used by [Swordman](Swordman.md), [Knight](Knight.md), and [Crusader](Crusader.md) classes. Spears have medium to high attack, and have a longer attack range than [one](One_Handed_Sword.md) and [two](Two_Handed_Sword.md) handed swords or [Dagger](Dagger.md)s. Spears also have a lower attack speed than the aforementioned weapons. There are many varieties of spears, many of them are slotted. Most weapon [NPC](NPC.md)s have spears of some kind for sale.

| Size | Damage |
| --- | --- |
| Small | 75% |
| Medium | 75% |
| Large | 100% |

Note: Forge denotes the Blacksmith ability [Smith Spear](Smith_Spear.md). These versions have 0 slots, but can have elemental or strong modifiers.
