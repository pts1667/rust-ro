# NPC DEFENDER


## Description

*  Supportive skill
*  Levels: 1
*  Duration: ?

All ranged damage dealt to the caster is reduced.

Although it is similar in fuction to the Crusader skill [Defending Aura](Defending_Aura.md), this skill is capable of reducing damage from ranged skills that the latter skill cannot, such as [Acid Bomb](Acid_Bomb.md) and [Blitz Beat](Blitz_Beat.md).
