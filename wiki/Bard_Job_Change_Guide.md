# Bard Job Change Guide

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Job level requirement:** 40
- **Class requirement:** Archer
- **Item requirement:** 1 Type of Flower, 1 Sticky Mucus, 1 Squid Ink, Trunks (Optional)
- **Quest requirements:** [Snowysnow Quest](Snowysnow_Quest.md)
- **Item reward:** Instrument (Varies/Optional)
- **Quest reward:** Jobchange to [Bard](Bard.md)

1. Find the Roaming Bard, Lalo, who is at (226,123) on the [Comodo](Comodo.md) town map. This is a short distance from the bottom right of the Kafra. After a short conversation with the bard he will request that the player brings him a flower. The flower must be one of the following:

*  Hinalle
*  Ment
*  Aloe
*  Illusion Flower
*  Witherless Rose
*  Singing Flower
*  Izidor
*  Frozen Rose

2. Once you have given the bard a flower, he will tell you to go to [Lutie](Lutie.md) to become friends with the Snowman in Lutie:

  *See: [Snowysnow Quest](Snowysnow_Quest.md)*

3. Once you become friends with Mr. Snowman, return to the bard to finish the quest. He will require the player to type exactly what he sings.

4. After successfully copying the bard's lyrics, he will change your job to [Bard](Bard.md).  Upon job change, the bard offers to give you a weapon if you have 60 trunks. Each type of trunk gives the player a different weapon.

*  Trunk : Violin \[3\]
*  Solid Trunk or Barren Trunk : Mandolin \[2\]
*  Fine Trunk : Lute \[2\]
*  Fine Trunk + [Job Level](Levels.md) 50 : Harp \[2\]
