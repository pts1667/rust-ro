# Rod

Rods are equippable by a [Priest](Priest.md), [Acolyte](Acolyte.md), [Mage](Mage.md), [Wizard](Wizard.md), [Super Novice](Super_Novice.md), [Sage](Sage.md), or [Soul Linker](Soul_Linker.md). Rods usually have very low [ATK](Stats.md) but give a bonus to [MATK](Stats.md), along with usually giving a bonus to [INT](Stats.md) or [DEX](Stats.md). Rods are usually slotted, those with more than one variety may have different amounts of slots. Many rods can be purchased from [NPCs](NPC.md). All rods are also naturally unbreakable except in upgrade attempts. Rods, when used by those of the [Acolyte](Acolyte.md) class, are commonly referred to as "Staffs."
