# Falcon Eyes

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Sniper
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 10 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Archer](Archer.md)) [Improve Concentration](Improve_Concentration.md) Lv. 10, [Vulture's Eye](Vulture's_Eye.md) Lv. 10, [Owl's Eye](Owl's_Eye.md) Lv. 10, ([Hunter](Hunter.md)) [Falconry Mastery](Falconry_Mastery.md) Lv. 1
- **SP cost:** Variable
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** [ASPD](ASPD.md) Based
- **Duration:** 30 seconds
- **Target:** Self


## Description

Increases all stats by 5 regardless of skill level. Also increases [ATK](Stats.md), [Hit](Stats.md), and [Crit](Stats.md) for the skill's duration.

| Level | [ATK](Stats.md) | [Hit](Stats.md) | [Crit](Stats.md) | SP Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | +2% | +3 | +1 | 20 |
| 2 | +4% | +6 | +2 | 20 |
| 3 | +6% | +9 | +3 | 25 |
| 4 | +8% | +12 | +4 | 25 |
| 5 | +10% | +15 | +5 | 30 |
| 6 | +12% | +18 | +6 | 30 |
| 7 | +14% | +21 | +7 | 35 |
| 8 | +16% | +24 | +8 | 35 |
| 9 | +18% | +27 | +9 | 40 |
| 10 | +20% | +30 | +10 | 40 |


## Information

* The Hit added is not a percentage but an exact amount.
* Because this skill adds +5 to all stats, it will actually increase Crit by 11~12 overall at level 10 ([LUK](Stats.md) +5).
* This will also give the player an additional +5 Hit (unaccounted for in the chart) regardless of level ([DEX](Stats.md) +5).
* The official International Ragnarok Online site has incorrect information regarding Falcon Eyes. Those flaws are not displayed here.
