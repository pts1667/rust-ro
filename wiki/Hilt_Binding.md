# Hilt Binding

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Blacksmith
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** 1
- **Prerequisites:** None


## Description

[STR](Stats.md) +1, [ATK](Stats.md) +4. Increases the durations of [Adrenaline Rush](Adrenaline_Rush.md), [Power-Thrust](Power-Thrust.md) and [Weapon Perfection](Weapon_Perfection.md) Skills by 10%.
