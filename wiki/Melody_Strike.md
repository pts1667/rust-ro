# Melody Strike

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Bard
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** ([Bard](Bard.md)) [Music Lessons](Music_Lessons.md) Lv. 3
- **SP cost:** 1~9
- **Cast time:** 1.5 seconds
- **Delay:** [ASPD](ASPD.md) Based
- **Target:** Enemy
- **Range:** 9
- **Weapon:** Instrument


## Description

Use an instrument-class weapon to do ranged damage to a single target.

| Skill level | Damage | SP Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1x | 1 |
| 2 | 1.4x | 3 |
| 3 | 1.8x | 5 |
| 4 | 2.2x | 7 |
| 5 | 2.6x | 9 |


## Information

* It has no after-cast delay reducable by [Magic Strings](Magic_Strings.md), but does have an animation delay reducable by [ASPD](ASPD.md).
