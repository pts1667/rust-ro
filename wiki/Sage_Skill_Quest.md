# Sage Skill Quest


## Create Elemental Converter Quest

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Job level requirement:** N/A
- **Class requirement:** Sage
- **Skill requirement:** Level 1 of each Endow
- **Item requirement:** 10 Scorpion Tails, 12 Rainbow Shells, 7 Horns, 10 Snail Shells, 4 Blank Scrolls
- **Quest reward:** Ability to use [Create Elemental Converter](Create_Elemental_Converter.md)

1. In the 1 o'clock direction in [Juno](Juno.md), in the Sage Acadamy, there should be an NPC near the entrance to talk to. He will require some items:

*  10 Scorpion Tails
*  12 Rainbow Shells
*  7 Horns
*  10 Snail Shells
*  4 Blank Scrolls

2. Once you bring the required items, he will teach the skill to you.


## Elemental Change Quest

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Job level requirement:** 40
- **Class requirement:** Sage
- **Skill requirement:** Level 1 of Wanted Endow
- **Item requirement:** 20 Elemental Stones (Varies), 1 Payon Solution, 1 Morroc Solution
- **Quest reward:** Ability to use [Elemental Change](Elemental_Change.md)

1. In the 1 o'clock direction in [Juno](Juno.md), in the Sage Acadamy, there should be an NPC near the entrance to talk to. He will require some items. The items required to learn elemental change depends on which element you elect to learn. Depending on which you chose, you will be required to gather 20 stones of that element.

*  [Earth](Earth.md) [Elemental Change](Elemental_Change.md): 20 Green Lives
*  [Wind](Wind.md) [Elemental Change](Elemental_Change.md): 20 Wind of Verdures
*  [Fire](Fire.md) [Elemental Change](Elemental_Change.md): 20 Red Bloods
*  [Water](Water.md) [Elemental Change](Elemental_Change.md): 20 Crystal Blues

2. In addition to the stones, you will also need one Payon Solution and one Morroc Solution. Once you have the required items, you will be taught the [Elemental Change](Elemental_Change.md) skill corresponding to the element you picked.
