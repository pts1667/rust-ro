# NPC SMOKING


## Description

*[image not archived: Npc smoking.gif]*

*  Supportive skill
*  Levels: 1
*  Duration: 10 seconds

The caster will stand still and continually cause 3 damage on itself every second. During this time, it will ignore players unless it is attacked.

This skill was probably made to discourage players from smoking in real life.
