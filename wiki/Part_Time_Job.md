# Part Time Job

> **Repeatable** — cooldown: 5 hour

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** 70
- **Item requirement:** Varies
- **Quests required:** [Onward to the New World Quest](Onward_to_the_New_World_Quest.md)
- **Base EXP reward:** Varies
- **Job EXP reward:** Varies
- **Window:** yes

1.  After seeing an announcement that Breeder Taab (mid_camp 143, 306) is hiring, offer to help him. Each of the first five volunteers are given a task.
  1. The first volunteer is asked to bring food for his Tatacho, bring him 50 Fresh Fish and you will receive some EXP.
    * Receive 80,000 [Base EXP](Base_EXP_Chart.md) and 30,000 [Job EXP](Job_EXP_Chart.md).
  1. The second volunteer is asked to bring food for his Cornus, bring him 30 Brown Root and 30 Huge Leaf.
    * Receive 90,000 [Base EXP](Base_EXP_Chart.md) and 40,000 [Job EXP](Job_EXP_Chart.md).
  1. The third volunteer is asked to bring food for his Hillthrions, bring him 20 Monster's Feed and 30 Pet Food.
    * Receive 80,000 [Base EXP](Base_EXP_Chart.md) and 30,000 [Job EXP](Job_EXP_Chart.md).
  1. The fourth volunteer is asked to bring some different food for his Hillthrions, bring him 50 Meat and 30 Pet Food.
    * Receive 80,000 [Base EXP](Base_EXP_Chart.md) and 30,000 [Job EXP](Job_EXP_Chart.md).
  1. The fifth volunteer is asked to to bring some bedding for the animals, bring him 30 Fur.
    * Receive 80,000 [Base EXP](Base_EXP_Chart.md) and 30,000 [Job EXP](Job_EXP_Chart.md).
1.  He asks you to go rest. He will not let you volunteer again for 5 hours.
  * Quest is Repeatable.
