# Healing Hands

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Lif
- **Secondary class:** Alchemist
- **Tertiary class:** Homunculus
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 5 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** None
- **Range:** ?
- **Catalyst:** 1 Condensed Red Potion


## Description

The [Lif](Lif.md) may target itself or its master for healing, similar to an [Acolyte](Acolyte.md)'s [Heal](Heal.md). The amount of [HP](Max_HP.md) restored will be based on the [Lif](Lif.md)'s level, Healing Hands' level, and [Lif](Lif.md)'s [INT](Stats.md). Each cast will require a Condensed Red Potion. Cannot be used to damage [Undead](Undead.md).

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SP Required | 13 | 16 | 19 | 22 | 25 |
