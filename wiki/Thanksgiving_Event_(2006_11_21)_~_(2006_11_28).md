## Starting the quest

Make your way to (227,147) in Lighthalzen

![](images/Brandos.jpg)

where you will encounter the Brandos. They're all getting ready for Thanksgivin, and need various items.

First talk to Mr. Brando. He needs 10 meats ![](images/Meat.png) and 2 yams ![](images/Yam.png). Bring him the items for some EXP.

After helping Mr. Brando, turn your attention to Mrs. Brando. She is making Pumpkin Potato Bisque, and needs 5 milk ![](images/519_Milk.png), 5 pumpkins ![](images/Pumpkin.png), 10 carrots ![](images/515_Carrot.png), and 10 potatoes ![](images/Potato.png). Bring her the items for some more EXP.

After her, you need to help Grandma Brando. The holidays must be a bit too much for her, because she wants you to fetch her a vial of 200-proof alcohol ![](images/970_Alcohol.png). She'll reward you with some more EXP.

After Grandma, talk to Kipp. He wants 10 trunks ![](images/1019_Trunk.png) and 3 iron ore ![](images/Iron_Ore.png), though he'll tell you he only wants 3 trunks. You must bring 10 to complete this part of the quest.

Finally, talk to Cherise. She wants 5 Strawberries ![](images/Strawberry.gif) and 3 apples ![](images/512_Apple.png) to make some jam. She'll give you some more EXP.

After you help Cherise, talk to her once more to receive 2 Yggdrasil Berries ![](images/Yggdrasil_Berry.png). Now, whenever you talk to one of the Brandos, they'll heal your HP and SP. You may be healed as often as you like, but you cannot get any more Yggdrasil Berries.
