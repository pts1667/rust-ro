# Solar, Lunar and Stellar Opposition

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** TaeKwon Master
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 3
- **Prerequisites:** None
- **SP cost:** 100
- **Target:** Enemy


## Description

Aligns an enemy as a target of the sun, moon, or stars. **Alignments are virtually permanent** and each can only be chosen once.

Aligned enemies will receive increased damage if the respective Wrath skill is learned, and reward increased [experience points](Experience.md) if the respective Blessings skill is learned.

Alignments on monsters apply to all members of the target's species. There are restrictions on aligning monsters, which are detailed in the below table.

Alignments on players apply to all players who share the target's [class](Classes.md). There are no restrictions on aligning players.

| Level | Effect | Restriction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The target is aligned as a target of the sun. | Can be only used on [Small](Size.md)-sized monsters. |
| 2 | The target is aligned as a target of the moon. | Can be only used on [Medium](Size.md)-sized monsters with a maximum HP of 6,000 or higher. |
| 3 | The target is aligned as a target of the stars. | Can be only used on [Large](Size.md)-sized monsters with a maximum HP of 20,000 or higher. |
