# Footprint Quest

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** N/A
- **Item requirement:** Monster Feed, 1 White Potion
- **Items required:** Holy Threads, Holier Threads,
- **Quests required:** [Ayothaya Dungeon Entrance Quest](Ayothaya_Dungeon_Entrance_Quest.md)
- **Base EXP reward:** Varies
- **Item reward:** 10 Random Potions

1. Find Shuda in [Ayothaya](Ayothaya.md) (ayothaya 233 105), she's sad because her bridegroom is lost, you must help her find the ring that her bridegroom dropped in the sea.

2. Walk right a bit, and you'll see a man who can rent you a fishing tool for finding the ring in the sea for 50z (you need 1 Monster Feed per try when fishing).

3. Try it until you find the Engagement Ring, and then go back to Shuda. She'll ask you to find her bridegroom (Anon).

4. You can find clue about where Anon is from an old man (ayothaya 193 171). He'll tell you that Anon has gone to the Ayothaya Dungeon.

Note: You need to have fully completed the [Ayothaya Dungeon Entrance Quest](Ayothaya_Dungeon_Entrance_Quest.md) to continue (including retrieval of the Old Blue Box).

5. Now you must find 9 of Anon's footprints in Ayothaya Dungeon.

6. There are four footprint spots (when you pass it, a dialog box will appear) in Ayothaya Dungeon Level 1, and five footprints in Ayothaya Dungeon Level 2.

*Level 1 Footprints:*

* (ayo dun01 256,61)
* (ayo dun01 73,142)
* (ayo dun01 243,257)
* (ayo dun01 18,258)

7. When you find the last footprint, you need to stand exactly on tile (ayo_dun02 91,262) and shatter the bush to find Annon, or else you won't find him. Tell him that you're not Shuda and bring a White Potion to treat his wounds. He'll reward you with some EXP.

  * Level 56-60 = 10500 Exp
  * Level 61-65 = 18684 Exp
  * Level 66-70 = 27411 Exp
  * Level 71-75 = 70757 Exp
  * Level 76-80 = 130246 Exp
  * Level 81-85 = 150340 Exp
  * Level 86-90 = 182052 Exp
  * Level 91-99 = 406786 Exp
