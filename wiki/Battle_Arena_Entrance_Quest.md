# Battle Arena Entrance Quest

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Quest reward:** Entrance to Battle Arena

1. Talk to the Iron Man in [Kunlun](Kunlun.md) (180,118). Make sure you're at the least amount of weight possible (0 [Weight](Weight_Limit.md) for the best chance).

2. Accept his challenge, and he'll try and stick as many stones on you as he can.

*  If you fail, you'll either have to take some items off your character, or get some [STR](Stats.md).

*  If you succeed, you'll be left with many stones that you can just stick in storage or drop on the ground.

3. You can now enter the Battle Arena through the Iron Man [NPC](NPC.md).
