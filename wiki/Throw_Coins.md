# Throw Coins

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Ninja
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 10
- **Prerequisites:** ([Ninja](Ninja.md)) [Dagger Throwing Practice](Dagger_Throwing_Practice.md) Lv.10, [Throw Huuma Shuriken](Throw_Huuma_Shuriken.md) Lv.5
- **SP cost:** 50
- **Delay:** 5
- **Target:** Foe
- **Range:** 9 cells
- **Catalyst:** Zeny


## Description

Throw money at a target to inflict an amount of [DEF](DEF.md) piercing damage equal to the amount of zeny spent in the attack. Its damage is reduced against [Boss](Boss_Protocol.md) monsters and in [PvP](PvP~d29d2.md).

| Level | Zeny Spent |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 500~1000 |
| 2 | 1000~2000 |
| 3 | 1500~3000 |
| 4 | 2000~4000 |
| 5 | 2500~5000 |
| 6 | 3000~6000 |
| 7 | 3500~7000 |
| 8 | 4000~8000 |
| 9 | 4500~9000 |
| 10 | 5000~10000 |
