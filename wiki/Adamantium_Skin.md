# Adamantium Skin

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Amistr
- **Secondary class:** Alchemist
- **Tertiary class:** Homunculus
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** ([Amistr](Amistr.md)) [Amistr Bulwark](Amistr_Bulwark.md) Lv. 3


## Description

Increases [Amistr](Amistr.md)'s [maximum HP](Max_HP.md), HP Regeneration and [Defense](DEF.md).

| Level | HP Bonus (%) | HP Regeneration Bonus (%) | Def Bonus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | +2 | +5 | +4 |
| 2 | +4 | +10 | +8 |
| 3 | +6 | +15 | +12 |
| 4 | +8 | +20 | +16 |
| 5 | +10 | +25 | +20 |
