# Axe Mastery

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Alchemist
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** 10
- **Prerequisites:** None
- **Weapon:** Axe


## Description

Increase damage inflicted with Axe class weapons.

| Skill Level | Attack Strength Added by Level |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 3 |
| 2 | 6 |
| 3 | 9 |
| 4 | 12 |
| 5 | 15 |
| 6 | 18 |
| 7 | 21 |
| 8 | 24 |
| 9 | 27 |
| 10 | 30 |
