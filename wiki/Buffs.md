# Buffs

**Buffs** is a term for supportive skills which are designed to temporarily increase a player's abilities. Most often  [Priest](Priest.md)s or  [Acolyte](Acolyte.md)s are asked to "Give buffs." Skills which fall under the category of buffs usually include:

*  [Blessing](Blessing.md)
*  [Increase AGI](Increase_AGI.md)
*  [Magnificat](Magnificat.md) (Party Only)
*  [Impositio Manus](Impositio_Manus.md)
*  [Suffragium](Suffragium.md)
*  [Gloria](Gloria.md)
*  [Aspersio](Aspersio.md)
*  [Kyrie Eleison](Kyrie_Eleison.md)
*  [Assumptio](Assumptio.md) (High Priest)

Other non-Priest Type buff skills include:

*  Sage's Endow Skills
  * [Endow Tsunami](Endow_Tsunami.md)
  * [Endow Tornado](Endow_Tornado.md)
  * [Endow Blaze](Endow_Blaze.md)
  * [Endow Quake](Endow_Quake.md)

*  Blacksmith's Weapon Skills
  * [Power-Thrust](Power-Thrust.md)
  * [Weapon Perfection](Weapon_Perfection.md)
  * [Power Maximize](Power_Maximize.md)
  * [Adrenaline Rush](Adrenaline_Rush.md)

*  Paladin's [Battle Chant](Battle_Chant.md)
*  [Bard](Bard.md) and [Dancer](Dancer.md) Songs
