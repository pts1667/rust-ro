# FS

**FS** is an acronym and can stand for:

* Full Support; a character build with the primary purpose of aiding and supporting other characters versus dealing damage themselves. Common Full Support classes include: [Priest](Priest.md)/[High Priest](High_Priest.md), [Sage](Sage.md)/[Scholar](Scholar.md), [Dancer](Dancer.md)/[Gypsy](Gypsy.md), [Bard](Bard.md)/[Minstrel](Minstrel.md).
* Full Divestment, also referred to as Full Strip often.
