# Cicada Skin Shed

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Ninja
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** ([Ninja](Ninja.md)) [Shadow Leap](Shadow_Leap.md) Lv. 5
- **SP cost:** 9 + (Skill Level * 3)
- **Duration:** Skill Level * 10 secs
- **Target:** Self


## Description

This skill will make all normal attacks and almost all physical offensive skills do zero damage. This skill will also send the ninja back 7 cells if the attack used was meant to hit. In terms of dodging skill based attacks, Cicada Skin Shed will dodge skills whose damage can be modified with % cards. So skills like [Sonic Blow](Sonic_Blow.md), [Guillotine Fist](Guillotine_Fist.md), etc. can be dodged easily with Cicada Skin Shed. The downside is since this only works with skills whose damage can be modified with % cards; magic spells, the magical half of [Soul Destroyer](Soul_Destroyer.md), [High Speed Cart Ram](High_Speed_Cart_Ram.md), [Throw Kunai](Throw_Kunai.md), etc. cannot be dodged with this skill. The skill also cannot dodge elemental attacks from monsters.

## Notes

*  Natural Flee, Perfect Dodge, [Safety Wall](Safety_Wall.md), and [Kyrie Eleison](Kyrie_Eleison.md) take priority over Cicada Skin Shed; if one causes the attack to fail, Cicada does not activate..
*  If [Mirror Image](Mirror_Image.md) is active at the same time as Cicada Skin Skin Shed,  both skills are triggered.
*  In [War of Emperium](War_of_Emperium.md) the dodge effect applies but there is no backslide effect.
*  Any effect given by the attack will still apply, despite no damage being taken. For example, you can be frozen (while simultaneously being thrown back) if you are attacked with a weapon that can freeze.
*  Effects of cards that trigger when the user is being hit (ie. Pest Card) are still applied, with the ninja having no damage thanks to the Cicada Skin Shed protection.
*  This skill cannot be dispelled with [Dispell](Dispell.md).
