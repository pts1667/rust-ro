# Gravitational Field

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** High Wizard
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 5 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Wizard](Wizard.md)) [Quagmire](Quagmire.md) Lv. 1, ([High Wizard](High_Wizard.md)) [Stave Crasher](Stave_Crasher.md) Lv. 1, [Mystical Amplification](Mystical_Amplification.md) Lv. 10
- **Cast time:** 5 seconds
- **Delay:** 2 seconds
- **Duration:** (Skill level + 4) seconds
- **Target:** Ground
- **Range:** Magic
- **Area:** 5x5
- **Element:** Neutral
- **Catalyst:** 1 Blue Gemstone


## Description

Amplify the gravity in an area around a targeted spot that will decrease the ASPD of enemies within range, as well as continually inflict unreducable damage (neither reduction gears nor defense have any effect on the amount of damage dealt by each wave). Although this skill can damage [Boss](Boss_Protocol.md) monsters, it does not affect their [ASPD](ASPD.md). Gravitational Field inflicts a set amount of damage to [WoE](War_of_Emperium.md) Emperiums that is determined by the skill's level. When used on Plants, this skill only inflicts 1 Damage, regardless of its level. While Gravitational Field is active, the caster cannot move or use normal attacks.
