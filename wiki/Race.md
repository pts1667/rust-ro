# Race

Race is an attribute of [Monsters](Monster.md). There are ten races present in the world of Ragnarok Online:

* [Demi-Human](Demi_Human.md)
* [Brute](Brute.md) (Animal)
* [Insect](Insect.md)
* [Fish](Fish.md)
* [Dragon](Dragon.md)
* [Plant](Plant.md)
* [Formless](Formless.md)
* [Angel](Angel.md)
* [Demon](Demon.md) (Devil)
* [Undead](Undead.md)

* Note: Players are always of Demi Human race.
