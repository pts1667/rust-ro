# Demi Human

**Demi-Human** are a race of [Monster](Monster.md). Notably this is the race that **includes players** as well.

Cards which concern the Demi-Human race are:
*  **Am Mut Card**: Receive 10% more [Experience](Experience.md) Points from Demi-Human monsters. Receive 20% more damage from Demi-Human monsters.
*  **Assaulter Card**: Inflict 10% more damage with [Critical](Stats.md) Attack. [Critical](Stats.md) +7 on Demi-Human monsters.
*  **Deviace Card**: Increase damage on Demi-Human, [Brute](Brute.md), [Plant](Plant.md) and [Insect](Insect.md) monsters by 7%.
*  **Dumpling Kid Card**: Add the chance of gaining 'Candy'/'Candy Cane' item each time a Demi-Human monster is killed. Candy and Candy Cane restore 50% more [HP](Max_HP.md).
*  **Hydra Card**: Increase damage on Demi-Human monsters by 20%.
*  **Orc Archer Card**: Add the chance of gaining 'Box of Panting' item each time a Demi-Human monster is killed.
*  **Phendark Card**: Gain 5 [SP](SP.md) each time a Demi-Human monster is killed by Physical Melee Attack. Drain 5 [SP](SP.md) as the weapon is unequipped.
*  **Thara Frog Card**: Reduce damage from Demi-Human monsters by 30%.

Equipment concerning the Demi-Human race are:
*  **Beret**: Headgear. Reduces damage from Demi-Human monsters by 10%.
*  **Combat Knife**: Dagger. Increases damage inflicted on Demi-Human monsters by 10%, but increases damage received by Demon monsters by 10%. Pierces [Defense](DEF.md) of Demi-Human monsters.
*  **Defolty Doll Hat**: Headgear. Reduces damage received from Demi-Human race by 5%. Adds a 2% chance of using [Angelus](Angelus.md) Lv 10 when hit with a physical attack.
*  **Exorciser**: Dagger. Pierces [Defense](DEF.md) of [Demon](Demon.md) monsters. 5% additional tolerance against [Demon](Demon.md) monsters. Increases damage from Demi-Human monsters by 10%.
*  **Feather Beret**: Headgear. Reduces damage from Demi-Human monsters by 10%.
*  **Poo Poo Hat**: Headgear. Reduces damage from Demi-Human monsters by 10%.
