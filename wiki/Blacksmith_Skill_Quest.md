# Blacksmith Skill Quest


## Dubious Salesmanship Quest

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Job level requirement:** 35
- **Class requirement:** Blacksmith
- **Item requirement:** 2 Steel, 8 Coal, 2 Iron Hammer, 1 Detrimindexta
- **Zeny requirement:** 500
- **Quest reward:** Ability to use [Dubious Salesmanship](Dubious_Salesmanship.md)

1. In [Geffen](Geffen.md), near the Blacksmith Guild, talk to the [NPC](NPC.md) Akkie ([geffen](Geffen.md) 173,72). She will ask you to bring the following items: 1 Steel, 5 Coal, 1 Iron Hammer, 1 Detrimindexta.

2. When you return to Akkie with the ingredients she will again ask you for more items. The items are as follows: 1 Steel, 3 Coal, 1 Iron Hammer.

3. After giving her the items, Akkie will teach you [Dubious Salesmanship](Dubious_Salesmanship.md).


## Greed Quest

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Job level requirement:** N/A
- **Class requirement:** Blacksmith
- **Quest reward:** Ability to use [Greed](Greed.md)

1. Talk to the dwarf right outside the old Blacksmith Guild in [Geffen](Geffen.md) ([geffen](Geffen.md), 169,52). All he requires you to do is to make your [Weight](Weight_Limit.md) high to the point where you cannot add 500 weight. The simplest way to do this is by buying Flowers from an NPC until the required amount is achieved.
