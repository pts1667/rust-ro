# Thanksgiving Event (2009/11/24) ~ (2009/12/08)

> **Note:** Not implemented on iRO Classic.

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** Unknown
- **Item requirement:** 10 Meat, 2 Yam, 5 Raw Fish, 20 Potatoes, 5 Milk, 5 Pumpkins, 10 Carrots, 1 Alcohol, 3+ Iron Ore, 15+ Trunk, 2+ Iron Hammer, 1 Nut Shell Hat, 5 Grapes, 3 Apples
- **Base EXP reward:** 627,696
- **Job EXP reward:** 631,380
- **Item reward:** 3 Yggdrasil Berry and 5 Speed Potion
- **External:** <http://iro.ragnarokonline.com/News/Notice/NoticeView.asp?noticeSeqNo=120&Page=1&Condition=&KeyWord=&mNum=6>

1.  In [Payon](Payon.md), speak to Mr. Brando (payon 190, 314) and offer to help him. He asks for 10 Meat, 2 Yams and 5 Raw Fish.
  * Raw Fish can be purchased from the sushi chef in the [Amatsu](Amatsu.md) bar either individually or in sets of 10 pieces.
  * Receive 104,616 [Base EXP](Base_EXP_Chart.md) and 105,230 [Job EXP](Job_EXP_Chart.md).
1.  Speak to Mrs. Brando (186, 300), she needs materials for her mashed potatoes. She asks for 20 Potatoes, 5 Milk, 5 Pumpkins and 10 Carrots.
  * Receive 104,616 [Base EXP](Base_EXP_Chart.md) and 105,230 [Job EXP](Job_EXP_Chart.md).
1.  Speak to Grandma Vira (180, 322) to try and get her to take her medicine, she needs some Alcohol.
  * Receive 104,616 [Base EXP](Base_EXP_Chart.md) and 105,230 [Job EXP](Job_EXP_Chart.md).
1.  Kipp (190, 304) requires 3+ Iron Ore, 15+ Trunk, and 2+ Iron Hammer.
  * Receive 104,616 [Base EXP](Base_EXP_Chart.md) and 105,230 [Job EXP](Job_EXP_Chart.md).
1.  Speak to Cherice (200, 306), she needs 1 Nut Shell Hat, 5 Grapes and 3 Apples.
  * Receive 104,616 [Base EXP](Base_EXP_Chart.md) and 105,230 [Job EXP](Job_EXP_Chart.md).
1.  Return to Mr. Brando, he thanks you for your help.
  * Receive 104,616 [Base EXP](Base_EXP_Chart.md) 105,230 [Job EXP](Job_EXP_Chart.md), 3 Yggdrasil Berry and 5 Speed Potion.
