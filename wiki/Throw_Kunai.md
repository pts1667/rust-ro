# Throw Kunai

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Ninja
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** ([Ninja](Ninja.md)) [Throw Shuriken](Throw_Shuriken.md) Lv.5
- **SP cost:** 35 - 5 * Skill Level
- **Delay:** 1 second
- **Target:** Foe
- **Range:** 9 cells
- **Catalyst:** [Kunai](Kunai.md)


## Description

Throw a [Kunai](Kunai.md) that will strike its target three times in one attack. Each cast consumes 1 Kunai, and the attack's property is affected by the type of Kunai thrown.

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Damage | 300% | 300% | 300% | 300% | 300% |
| SP Cost | 30 | 25 | 20 | 15 | 10 |


## Notes

*  This skill's damage **IS NOT** affected by % damage cards.
*  This skill's damage **IS** affected by +ATK cards.
*  This is a **RANGED** attack even if you are standing 1 cell from the target.
*  This skill **IGNORES** [Cicada Skin Shed](Cicada_Skin_Shed.md) (because it's not affected by % damage cards).
*  Besides the 300% damage of the skill, it will always deal an extra 270 damage. (Displayed as 3 hits of 90 damage.) This 270 damage bypasses [Flee](Stats.md), [Defense](DEF.md), and is [Neutral](Neutral.md)-[Element](Element.md) regardless of the Kunai element.
