# NPC STONESKIN


## Description

*  Supportive skill
*  Levels: 1
*  Duration: 2 Seconds

Reduce all incoming Physical Damage by 20% for 2 seconds, but reduces [MDEF](Stats.md) by 20% for 2 seconds. The skill its auto-casted via Wolfheiden garment.


## Obtainable via

* Ulfhedinn - when being attacked
* Amistr Hat - when being attacked
