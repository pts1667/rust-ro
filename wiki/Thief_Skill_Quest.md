# Thief Skill Quest


## Back Slide Quest

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Job level requirement:** 35
- **Class requirement:** Thief
- **Item requirement:** 20 Grasshopper's Leg
- **Quest reward:** Ability to use [Back Slide](Back_Slide.md)

1. Talk to Alcouskou in the right-hand room of the Thief Guild (moc_prydb1 154,128). Ask to learn Back Slide. He will require you to bring him 20 Grasshopper's Leg, then he will teach you the skill.

## Sand Attack Quest

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Job level requirement:** 25
- **Class requirement:** Thief
- **Item requirement:** 5 Fine Grit, 1 Earthworm Peeling, 1 Cobweb and 1 Cactus Needle
- **Quest reward:** Ability to use [Sand Attack](Sand_Attack.md)

1. Talk to Alcouskou in the right-hand room of the Thief Guild (moc_prydb1, 154,128). Ask to learn Sand Attack. He will require you to bring him 5 Fine Grit, then he will agree to teach you, but first you must bring him a Leather Bag of Infinity from the merchant that sells them in Payon.

2. Talk to Bag Seller in Payon (payon 91,77), and he will tell you to bring him 1 Earthworm Peeling, 1 Cobweb and 1 Cactus Needle.

3. Bring him these and he will give you 1 Leather Bag of Infinity. It's probably not a good idea to drop this special quest item on the ground.

4. You may now return to Alcouskou and ask him about Sand Attack again. As long as you have the Leather Bag of Infinity in your inventory he will now teach you the skill.
