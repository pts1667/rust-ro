# Power-Thrust

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Blacksmith
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 5 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Blacksmith](Blacksmith.md)) [Adrenaline Rush](Adrenaline_Rush.md) Lv. 3
- **SP cost:** (20 - (Skill level*2))
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** None
- **Duration:** (Skill level*20) seconds
- **Target:** Self


## Description

Increases [ATK](Stats.md) of the caster and all onscreen party members. There is a a 0.1% chance to break the weapon each attack. However, Axes and Maces (as well as some other weapon classes) cannot be broken.

Party members will always only receive +5% ATK, regardless of the actual level casted by the user, although the duration remains the same. A lower levelled Power-Thrust can also overwrite the effects of a higher leveled one.

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| +[ATK](Stats.md) | 5% | 10% | 15% | 20% | 25% |
| Duration (seconds) | 20 | 40 | 60 | 80 | 100 |
| SP Cost | 18 | 16 | 14 | 12 | 10 |
