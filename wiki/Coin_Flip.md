# Coin Flip

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Gunslinger
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** None
- **Target:** Self
- **Catalyst:** 1 [Zeny](Zeny.md)


## Description

Flip a coin. If it lands showing heads, the caster will gain 1 golden coin, and if it lands showing tails, the caster will lose 1 coin. The caster can gain a maximum of 10 coins, and the skill's level affects the chance of landing showing heads. Spend 1 [Zeny](Zeny.md) for each use. Gunslinger coins are displayed as glowing orbs around the caster, similar to a [Monk](Monk.md)'s [Spirit Sphere](Spirit_Sphere.md)s.
