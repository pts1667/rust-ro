# 2014 Fourth of July Event

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** 70
- **Item requirement:** 300 Firecrackers, 10 Logs, 10 Candy, Various Skel-Bones, 1 Alcohol
- **Items required:** 20 Bread, 5 Grapes
- **Hunting:** 30 Megalodons
- **Base EXP reward:** 1,000,000
- **Job EXP reward:** 1,000,000
- **Item reward:** 10 Yggdrasil Seeds, 1 Guild Surplus Box
- **Window:** yes


## Ms. Brando

1. Speak to **Ms. Brando** in [Comodo](Comodo.md) (comodo 160, 318) and she will ask that you bring her 10 Logs.
1. Return with the logs and you will receive 1 Yggdrasil Seed.


## Mr Brando

1. Head to [Comodo](Comodo.md) and speak to **Mr. Brando** (comodo 167, 322). He will ask you to bring him 300 Firecrackers (250 first time you talk to him and 50 when you return with the 250).
  * Note: Firecrackers can be purchased inside [Hugel](Hugel.md)'s Party Supplies Shop (hugel 94, 103).
1. Bring the firecrackers back to **Mr. Brando** and he will give you 1 Yggdrasil Seed.


## Auntie Elmirah

1. In [Comodo](Comodo.md) talk to **Auntie Elmirah** (comodo 163, 314) and she will ask you to hunt 30 Megalodons.
1. Return to Auntie Elmirah after killing the megalodons and you will receive a Yggdrasil Seed.


## Kipp

1. Speak to **Kipp** in [Comodo](Comodo.md) (comodo 159, 310) and he will challenge you to a race around the Comodo mountain. Speak to him again and accept the race. You will need to pass by 3 check points and return to Kipp as fast as possible. You do not need to speak to the Check Points.
  1. **Check Point 1** is located at (comodo 228, 273).
  1. **Check Point 2** is located at (comodo 224, 207).
  1. **Check Point 3** is located at (comodo 111, 196).
  1. Return to **Kipp** at (comodo 159, 310).
1. Speak to **Kipp** and if you win the race you will receive 1 Yggdrasil Seed.


## Grandma Vira

1. In [Comodo](Comodo.md) speak to **Grandma Vira** (comodo 173, 315). She will request you bring her 20 Bread and 5 Grapes.
1. Return to **Grandma Vira** with the items she requested and she will give you a Yggdrasil Seed.
  * Note: Currently, upon completion of the quest she will not take the bread and grapes from you.


## Grandpa Winston

1. Talk to **Grandpa Winston** in [Comodo](Comodo.md) (comodo 168, 318) and he will ask that you recover his missing teeth from the Comodo Casino.
1. Enter the Comodo Casino located at (comodo 140, 111) and head upstairs.
1. Look **Underneath The Table** located at (cmd_in02 58, 40) and you will receive Winston's dentures.
1. Return to **Grandpa Winston** and you will receive 1 Yggdrasil Seed.


## Fido

1. Talk to the dog **Fido** located in [Comodo](Comodo.md) (comodo 159, 324) and you will suggest bringing him Skel-Bones. The amount of bones you will need varies.
1. Speak to **Fido** until you receive a Yggdrasil Seed.


## Brando Jr.

1. Speak to **Brando Jr.** in [Comodo](Comodo.md) (comodo 158, 314) and he will ask you to bring him 10 Candy.
1. Return to him with the candies and he will give you 1 Yggdrasil Seed.


## Uncle Fitzgerald

1. Talk to **Uncle Fitzgerald** in [Comodo](Comodo.md) (comodo 152, 315) and he will want you to bring him 1 Alcohol.
1. Speak to him again with the Alcohol and you will receive a Yggdrasil Seed.


## Cherice

1. Speak to **Cherice** in [Comodo](Comodo.md) (comodo 173, 309) and she tell you about a book she is after.
1. Head inside the [Juno](Juno.md) Library located at (yuno 342, 203).
1. Speak to the **Librarian** beside the entrance of the library (yuno_in04 36, 58) and he will direct you to the location of the book.
1. Head past him into the hallway and go into the room to the left. The **The Poetic Edda** is located above one of the bookshelves (yuno_in04 115, 100)
1. Return to **Cherice** with the book and you will receive a Yggdrasil Seed.


## Finale

1. After completing all 10 requests, return to **Ms. Brando** in [Comodo](Comodo.md) (comodo 160, 318) and you will receive 1,000,000 [Base EXP](Base_EXP_Chart.md), 1,000,000 [Job EXP](Job_EXP_Chart.md) and a Guild Surplus Box.
