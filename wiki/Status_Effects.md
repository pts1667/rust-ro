# Status Effects

There are many status debuffs (or status ailments) in Ragnarok Online that work against you in many ways. Both player characters and monsters are capable of inflicting them.


## Bleeding

*For the Monster Exclusive Skill see [NPC BLEEDING](NPC_BLEEDING.md).*

This status ailment causes the following effect:

* Causes "rapid" loss of [HP](Max_HP.md) over time. Contrary to the effect of Poison, this CAN kill the victim - however the damage is slow enough that it rarely will. The red icon disappears but the effect stays even after relogging or map/zone changes.
* Natural HP/SP Regen disabled.

To assist in recovering from this ailment:

* Dying cancels this ailment.
* High [VIT](Stats.md) to prevent occurance and reduce lasting time.
* [Battle Chant](Battle_Chant.md) instantly removes all buffs and status effects from the [Paladin](Paladin.md) who uses it, including Bleeding.
* One of [Battle Chant](Battle_Chant.md)'s random effects removes all abnormal status effects from party members, including Bleeding status.
* One of [Battle Chant](Battle_Chant.md)'s random effects grants 60 seconds of immunity to all status effects to party members, including Bleeding status.
* [Dispell](Dispell.md) removes this ailment. (?)
* [Mercenary's](Mercenary_System.md) skill [Compress](Compress.md) can undo the effect.

Card effects:

* Ungoliant Card grants immunity.
* Wootan Fighter Card increases resistance by 20%.
* Killer Mantis Card gives a 6% chance to inflict bleeding when receiving physical damage.

Equipments:

* Bone Head increases resistance by 5%.

Weapon Effects:

* Hatii Claw Adds a 2% chance of inflicting [Bleeding] on the target when doing a melee attack.


## Blind

*For the Monster Exclusive Skill see [NPC BLINDATTACK](NPC_BLINDATTACK.md).*

This status ailment causes the following effects:

* Reduce your HIT and FLEE rate by 25%.
* Also graphically reduces range of your visibility.

To assist in recovering from this ailment:

* Green Potion, Panacea, Royal Jelly and [Cure](Cure.md) can undo the effect.
* Having high VIT and LUK will reduce amount of time this ailment affects you, as well as chance of being affected.
* INT also reduces the chance and duration.
* Typing "/effect" will disable reduced visibility effect, but HIT and FLEE will still be reduced.
* [Battle Chant](Battle_Chant.md) instantly removes all buffs and status effects from the [Paladin](Paladin.md) who uses it, including Blind.
* One of [Battle Chant](Battle_Chant.md)'s random effects removes all abnormal status effects from party members, including Blind status.
* One of [Battle Chant](Battle_Chant.md)'s random effects grants 60 seconds of immunity to all status effects to party members, including Blind status.
* [Dispell](Dispell.md) removes this ailment.
* [Mercenary's](Mercenary_System.md) skill [Benediction](Benediction.md) can undo the effect.

Card effects:

* Deviruchi Card grants immunity.
* Martin Card increases resistance by 20%.
* Obeaune Card allows use of [Cure](Cure.md), which removes blind.
* Flame Skull Card increases resistance by 30%.

Equipments:

* Blinker and Dark Blinder grants immunity.
* Alarm Mask increases resistance by 50%.
* Geek Glasses increases resistance by 15%.
* Purple Glasses increases resistance by 10%.
* Sunglasses and Slotted Sunglasses increases resistance by 5%.
* Rudra Bow increases resistance by 50%.


## Chaos

Also known as **Confusion**. This status ailment causes the following effects:

* Causes the character to move into unintended directions when trying to navigate.

To assist in recovering from this ailment:

* Getting hit automatically cancels the effect.
* INT reduces chances of occurrence and duration.
* Acolyte's skill - Cure, Panacea, Royal Jelly can undo the effect.
* [Battle Chant](Battle_Chant.md) instantly removes all buffs and status effects from the [Paladin](Paladin.md) who uses it, including Chaos.
* One of [Battle Chant](Battle_Chant.md)'s random effects removes all abnormal status effects from party members, including Chaos status.
* One of [Battle Chant](Battle_Chant.md)'s random effects grants 60 seconds of immunity to all status effects to party members, including Chaos status.
* [Dispell](Dispell.md) removes this ailment.
* [Mercenary's](Mercenary_System.md) skill [Mental Cure](Mental_Cure.md) can undo the effect.

Card effects:

* Evil Snake Lord Card and Giearth Card grant immunity.
* Wootan Shooter Card increases resistance by 20%.

Equipments:

* Rudra Bow increases resistance by 50%.


## Coma

*For the Monster Exclusive Skill see [NPC DARKBLESSING](NPC_DARKBLESSING.md).*

This status ailment causes the following effects:

* Reduces afflicted target's HP to 1.
* Reduces afflicted target's SP to 0.
* When used against monsters, amount of EXP that equals the damage you dealt during this period is not given when monster dies.

To assist in recovering from this ailment:

* [Heal](Heal.md), [Healing Items](Healing_Items.md)
* One of [Battle Chant](Battle_Chant.md)'s random effects grants 60 seconds of immunity to all status effects to party members, including Coma. (*Editor's Note:* Untested. It's hard to classify Coma as a status effect, and I do not know if it is covered by Battle Chant's immunity effect.)

Card effects:

* Golden Thiefbug Card grants immunity.

Other notes:

* You can dodge coma inducing physical attacks if you have high flee.
* Having higher Luk will decrease the chance of Coma when attacked.
* Inversely, having higher Luk will increase the chances for Coma infliction when attacking.


## Critical Wounds

*For the Monster Exclusive Skill see [NPC CRITICALWOUND](NPC_CRITICALWOUND.md).*

This status ailment causes the following effects:

* Lowers the effectiveness of healing skills on you, including [Heal](Heal.md), [Sanctuary](Sanctuary.md), [Aid Potion](Aid_Potion.md) and recovery items. This reduction is 20% times the level of Critical Wounds that inflicted it.
* Lasts 30 seconds.

To assist in recovering from this ailment:

* If a [Crusader](Crusader.md) uses [Sacrifice](Sacrifice.md) on the inflicted person, the Crusader can take the damage and be healed, regardless of the critical wounds on the target.
* The Kafra Shop item, Restoration Potion, increases healing recovery by 20%. This modifier stacks additively, so player using a regeneration potion struck by level 5 would have healing recovery of 20% instead of 0%.

Card effects:

* None.

Other notes:

* You can dodge critical wounds inducing physical attacks if you have high flee, are in [Safety Wall](Safety_Wall.md) (at close range), or in [Pneuma](Pneuma.md) (at long range).
* Relogging cancels the status.


## Curse

*For the Monster Exclusive Skill see [NPC CURSEATTACK](NPC_CURSEATTACK.md) and [NPC WIDECURSE](NPC_WIDECURSE.md).*

This status ailment causes the following effects:

* LUK becomes 0.

To assist in recovering from this ailment:

* LUK reduces chance of being Cursed, VIT reduces duration of being Cursed. *NOTE: VIT may play a part in resisting as well.*
* [Battle Chant](Battle_Chant.md) instantly removes all buffs and status effects from the [Paladin](Paladin.md) who uses it, including Curse.
* One of [Battle Chant](Battle_Chant.md)'s random effects removes all abnormal status effects from party members, including Cursed status.
* One of [Battle Chant](Battle_Chant.md)'s random effects grants 60 seconds of immunity to all status effects to party members, including Cursed status.
* [Dispell](Dispell.md) removes this ailment.
* [Mercenary's](Mercenary_System.md) skill [Benediction](Benediction.md) can undo the effect.

Card effects:

* Evil Snake Lord Card grants immunity.
* Flame Skull Card increases resistance by 30%.

Equipments:
* Colored Drooping Cats increase resistance by 30%.
* Censor Bar increases resistance by 5%.
* Divine Cloth increases resistance by 5%.
* Ear Muffs increases resistance by 10%.
* Headset increases resistance by 10%.
* Rudra Bow increases resistance by 50%.


## Divest

This status ailment causes the following effect:

* The player can not reequip any items into a given equipment slot, depending on which one was targeted.

To prevent from infliction:

* High DEX lowers chance.
* Protection skills of Alchemists grant immunity.

To assist in recovering from this ailment:

* Relogging cancels the status.
* [Alchemist](Alchemist.md) protection skills "cure" divest status.
* [Battle Chant](Battle_Chant.md) instantly removes all buffs and status effects from the [Paladin](Paladin.md) who uses it, including Divest.
* One of [Battle Chant](Battle_Chant.md)'s random effects removes all abnormal status effects from party members, including Divest status.
* One of [Battle Chant](Battle_Chant.md)'s random effects grants 60 seconds of immunity to all status effects to party members, including Divest status.


## Frozen

*For the Monster Exclusive Skill see [NPC WIDEFREEZE](NPC_WIDEFREEZE.md).*

This status ailment causes the following effects:

* All DEF contributed by items reduced by 50%.
* Monsters [Hard-DEF](DEF.md) and [Soft-DEF](DEF.md) is reduced by 50% (round up).
* Any flee will be negated.
* Increases your Hard-MDEF by 25%.
* Ignores Steal, Lex Aeterna, [Wizard](Wizard.md)'s skill - [Storm Gust](Storm_Gust.md), [Ninja](Ninja.md)'s skill - [Snow Flake Draft](Snow_Flake_Draft.md).
* Impossible to move, attack, pick up items, skill usage, sitting, force disconnection, and item usage.
* Armor attribute becomes "Water 1". (175% damage from Wind based attacks, 25% damage from Water, 50% damage from Fire).

To assist in recovering from this ailment:

* Getting hit automatically cancels the effect.
* Priest's skill Status Recovery can undo the effect.
* Swordman's skill Provoke cancels effect.
* Scholar's skill Mind Breaker cancels effect.
* Having high hard MDEF and LUK will reduce amount of time this ailment affects you, as well as chance of being affected.
* One of [Battle Chant](Battle_Chant.md)'s random effects removes all abnormal status effects from party members, including Frozen. However it can't remove the frozen status from the paladin itself since it can't cast any skill as long as he/she is frozen.
* One of [Battle Chant](Battle_Chant.md)'s random effects grants 60 seconds of immunity to all status effects to party members, including Frozen status.
* [Dispell](Dispell.md) removes this ailment.
* [Mercenary's](Mercenary_System.md) skill [Tender](Tender.md) can undo the effect.

Card effects:

* Evil Druid Card grants elemental immunity.
* Marc Card grants immunity.
* Megalodon Card increases resistance by 20%.
* Detardeus Card grants immunity.

Equipments:

* Diabolus Ring increases resistance by 15%.
* Dark Bacilium increases resistance by 20% + 2%*upgrade.
* Winter Hat increases resistance by 10%.
* Note Headphone increases resistance by 10%.
* Shafka increases resistance by 10%.
* Meteo Plate Armor increases resistance by 30%.


## Hallucination

*For the Monster Exclusive Skill see [NPC HALLUCINATION](NPC_HALLUCINATION.md).*

This status ailment causes the following effects:

* The screen goes wavy and you see crazy numbers for all damage that is processed around you, but they are all fake. Even other players see those numbers at you. The wavy screen effect will cause severe local-machine lag on many newer computers (less than 1 fps), making it difficult to move or use skills or items.

To assist in recovering from this ailment:

* Panacea, Royal Jelly.
* Typing "/effect" will disable the "wavy" rendering effect, but damage will still be displayed as random numbers.
* [Battle Chant](Battle_Chant.md) instantly removes all buffs and status effects from the [Paladin](Paladin.md) who uses it, including Hallucination.
* One of [Battle Chant](Battle_Chant.md)'s random effects removes all abnormal status effects from party members, including Hallucination status.
* One of [Battle Chant](Battle_Chant.md)'s random effects grants 60 seconds of immunity to all status effects to party members, including Hallucination status.
* [Dispell](Dispell.md) removes this ailment.
* [Mercenary's](Mercenary_System.md) skill [Mental Cure](Mental_Cure.md) can undo the effect.

Card effects:

* None


## Hell's Power

*This status ailment is inflicted only by Satan Morroc.*

This status ailment causes the following effect:

* The effected player cannot be resurrected.
* A purple skull appears on the players screen to indicate the status effect is active and lasts approximately 5 minutes.

To prevent affliction:

* Unknown

To assist in recovering from this ailment:

* Relogging does NOT cancel the effect
* Unknown if ailment can be countered by [Soul Linker](Soul_Linker.md)'s [Kaizel](Kaizel.md) which normally gives automatic resurrection.


## Poison

*For the Monster Exclusive Skill see [NPC POISON](NPC_POISON.md).*

This status ailment causes the following effects:

* Status-oriented [DEF](DEF.md) is reduced by 25%.
* If HP is over 25%, you will lose 1.5% + 2 HP of your max HP every second.
* SP Regeneration is disabled.

To assist in recovering from this ailment:

* Green Herb, Green Potion, Panacea, Royal Jelly, [Thief](Thief.md)'s skill - [Detoxify](Detoxify.md) can undo the effect.
* Having high [VIT](Stats.md) and [LUK](Stats.md) will reduce amount of time this ailment affects you, as well as chance of being affected.
* [Priest](Priest.md)'s [Slow Poison](Slow_Poison.md) prevents damage taken from [Poison](Poison.md) but does not cure the reduced [DEF](DEF.md) effect. It is a very rarely used counter.
* [Battle Chant](Battle_Chant.md) initially removes all buffs and status effects from the [Paladin](Paladin.md) who uses it, including [Poison](Poison.md).
* One of [Battle Chant](Battle_Chant.md)'s random effects removes all abnormal status effects from party members, including [Poison](Poison.md) status.
* One of [Battle Chant](Battle_Chant.md)'s random effects grants 60 seconds of immunity to all status effects to party members, including [Poison](Poison.md) status.
* [Dispell](Dispell.md) removes this ailment.
* [Mercenary's](Mercenary_System.md) skill [Recuperate](Recuperate.md) can undo the effect.

Card effects:

* Argiope Card grants [Poison](Poison.md) element, and immunity to [Poison](Poison.md) attacks, but not the [Poison](Poison.md) status itself.
* Ghoul Card increases resistance to [Poison](Poison.md) by 20%.
* Poporing Card allows use of [Detoxify](Detoxify.md) (which cancels [Poison](Poison.md)).
* Venomous Card has a 30% chance of inflicting poison to self and a 30% chance of inflicting poison on the attacker when receiving physical damage.

Equipments:

* Eye of Dullahan grants immunity.
* Gas Mask increases resistance by 30%.
* Oxygen Mask increases resistance by 20%.
* Rudra Bow increases resistance by 50%.

## Stone

*For the Monster Exclusive Skill see [NPC PETRIFYATTACK](NPC_PETRIFYATTACK.md) and [NPC WIDESTONE](NPC_WIDESTONE.md).*

This status ailment causes the following effects:

* All DEF contributed by items is reduced by 50%.
* Monsters Hard-DEF and Soft-DEF is reduced by 50% (round up).
* If HP is over 25%, you will lose 1% of your HP every 5 seconds.
* Increases your MDEF by 25%.
* Changes your elemental status as "Earth LV 1"
* Ignores Steal and Lex Aeterna.
* Impossible to move, attack, pick up items, skill usage, sitting, force disconnection, and item usage.

To assist in recovering from this ailment:

* Getting hit by any damaging attack or skill (physical or magical) automatically cancels the effect.
* [Status Recovery](Status_Recovery.md) and [Blessing](Blessing.md) can undo the effect.
* Having high hard MDEF and LUK will reduce amount of time this ailment affects you, as well as chance of being affected.
* One of [Battle Chant](Battle_Chant.md)'s random effects removes all abnormal status effects from party members, including Stone status, however it can't remove the Stone status from the paladin itself since it can't cast any skill as long as he/she is in stone status.
* One of [Battle Chant](Battle_Chant.md)'s random effects grants 60 seconds of immunity to all status effects to party members, including Stone status.
* [Dispell](Dispell.md) removes this ailment.
* [Mercenary's](Mercenary_System.md) skill [Tender](Tender.md) can undo the effect.

Card effects:

* Medusa Card grants immunity.
* Evil Druid Card grants elemental immunity.
* Munak Card increases resistance by 15%.
* Argos Card increases resistance by 20%.
* Flame Skull Card increases resistance by 30%.
* Seeker Card increases resistance by 30%.
* Gemini-S58 Card increases resistance by 50% if the user has 80 or more base VIT.

Equipments:

* Dark Bacilium increases resistance by 20% + 2%*upgrade.
* Diabolus Armor increases resistance by 5%.
* Divine Cloth increases resistance by 5%.

**Note**: The Stone status effect is a two-stage effect. When a character is first inflicted with the Stone status, they are still able to move, and being attacked does not cancel the effect - note that during this time they can be effected by Lex Aeterna, and cannot attack or use skills but still able to use items. Once the Stone "hardens," the character is immobilized, and being attacked will break the Stone curse.

## Undead

*This status ailment is caused solely by the Monster Exclusive Skills [NPC CHANGEUNDEAD](NPC_CHANGEUNDEAD.md) and [NPC DRAGONFEAR](NPC_DRAGONFEAR.md).*

This status ailment causes the following effect:

* The player become [Undead](Undead.md) property.
* Support buffs such as [Blessing](Blessing.md) and [Increase AGI](Increase_AGI.md) are removed.
* Support buffs such as [Blessing](Blessing.md) and [Increase AGI](Increase_AGI.md) deal damage to the player and the buffs are not applied, even in PVM. They will do 1 damage, but cannot be copied by the [Rogue](Rogue.md) class using [Intimidate](Intimidate.md)
* [Heal](Heal.md) and [Sanctuary](Sanctuary.md) have no effect on the player in [PvM](PvM~a1fb2.md)
* [Aspersio](Aspersio.md) will deal damage to the player and can be copied by Rogue's Intimidate (verify?)
* The ailment causes the player's sprite to glow green.

To prevent from infliction:

* Bathory Card causes the skill inflicting this status to miss.
* Evil Druid Card causes the skill inflicting this status to miss.

To assist in recovering from this ailment:

* Relogging cancels the status.
* [Battle Chant](Battle_Chant.md) instantly removes all buffs and status effects from the Paladin who uses it, including Undead.
* [Dispell](Dispell.md) removes this ailment.
