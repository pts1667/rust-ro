# Beast Charge

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Hunter
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 1
- **Prerequisites:** ([Archer](Archer.md)) [Double Strafe](Double_Strafe.md) Lv. 10 ([Hunter](Hunter.md)) [Beast Bane](Beast_Bane.md) Lv. 10
- **SP cost:** 12
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** [ASPD](ASPD.md) Based
- **Target:** [Brute](Brute.md) Monster, [Insect](Insect.md) Monster
- **Weapon:** Bow


## Description

Endowed to Hunters who are under the effects of the [Hunter Spirit](Hunter_Spirit.md). An advanced form of [Double Strafing](Double_Strafe.md) modified to do additional damage against brute and insect type monsters. The skill can only be used to target monsters which are [Brute](Brute.md) or [Insect](Insect.md) type. The damage of the skill is greatly boosted by the [STR](Stats.md) of the [Hunter](Hunter.md).  The Hunter must have learned Double Strafe Lv. 10 and Beast Bane Lv. 10 in order for this skill to appear.


## Note

*  Damage formula : (50% + 8% * STR) * ATK * 2 Hits
