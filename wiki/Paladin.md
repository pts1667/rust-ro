## Overview

*[image not archived: PALA.PNG‎]*

Do you believe in the gods? Witness the Paladin's might and see the power of the divine for yourself! Non-believers are sure to convert and sinners will repent! The Paladin is a shining symbol of piety and devotion who uses his fighting ability in the service of the gods and his fellow man. His skills can punish the wicked or inspire the faithful.

Paladins are the Transcendent variant of the [Crusader](Crusader.md) class. Aside from the [HP](Max_HP.md)/[SP](SP.md) boost, the Paladin's defensive capabilities have not seen significant changes. They do receive many new offensive abilities which inspiring many INT-built Crusaders to become battle-built Paladins. For those Paladins who wish to continue as an INT-type build, the [Battle Chant](Battle_Chant.md) skill allows them to play a supportive role in large party settings, and the 20 additional skills points allow them to learn a much wider variety of skills.

The ultimate tank of the game. This Paladin pulls off a combination of the protective shield skills along with high [VIT](Stats.md) to guarantee survival. [Martyr's Reckoning](Martyr's_Reckoning.md) can be used to annihilate any monster or player without reduction gears. [Rapid Smiting](Rapid_Smiting.md) can be spammed on those who do carry them. This build can pull off the dangerous combo by hitting with [Martyr's Reckoning](Martyr's_Reckoning.md) while it is in the [Rapid Smiting](Rapid_Smiting.md) cool down. [Defending Aura](Defending_Aura.md) eliminates the threat of ranged attackers, while [Shield Reflect](Shield_Reflect.md) makes sure that any [Assassin](Assassin.md) or [Knight](Knight.md) who tries to sneak up on you will not get away without heavily damaging themselves as well. Outside of [WoE](War_of_Emperium.md), [Shrink](Shrink.md) bounces back any foolish player or monster who's planning to get a quick kill on you. In case you confront a [Wizard](Wizard.md), [Shield Boomerang](Shield_Boomerang.md) will take care of them momentarily. The one major undeniable weakness of this type is that they are vulnerable to [Full Divestment](Full_Divestment.md) and [Divest Shield](Divest_Shield.md). Considering the high [DEX](Stats.md) of the build though, most [Rogues](Rogue.md) and [Stalkers](Stalker.md) will find it hard to strip you.

## Tactics


## WoE

During [WoE](War_of_Emperium.md), a Paladin's role depends heavily on the user's build. Objectively, most players expect Paladins to protect their teammates. A fast cast [Sacrifice](Sacrifice.md) is vital to the survival of your teammates. High [VIT](Stats.md) allows you to move around and keep up your HP without worrying about [Stun](Status_Effects.md) & recovers more HP with healing items.

[Battle Chant](Battle_Chant.md) is very useful in buffing your party, and inflicting status effects on an enemy. Here are some special uses for [Battle Chant](Battle_Chant.md)
*  Allows [Wizard](Wizard.md) and [Sage](Sage.md) classes to have instant cast spells
*  Allows [Monks](Monk.md) and [Champions](Champion.md) to have additional ATK and doubles their max SP for a very powerful [Guillotine Fists](Guillotine_Fist.md)
*  Buffs [Lord Knights](Lord_Knight.md) and doubles their max HP to allow them to achieve 60k HP [frenzies](Frenzy.md)
*  Immunity to [Status Effects](Status_Effects.md) for one minute allows team mates to not freeze/stone curse/stun even without the appropriate gear/stats.

In short, [Battle Chant](Battle_Chant.md) can be used both offensively to buff your allies before breaking a pre-cast. It can also be used defensively to increase the damage done by your allies, or to slow down invading armies by inflicting them with status effects. Another application of [Battle Chant](Battle_Chant.md) is that its negative buffs and damage can affect even cloaked characters and [stalkers](Stalker.md) under [Stealth](Stealth.md).

Another special effect of [Battle Chant](Battle_Chant.md) is that when activated, it removes all status effects both good([Priest](Priest.md) buffs, ASPD potions) and bad(negative status effects including strip).

[Gloria Domini](Gloria_Domini.md) is a skill that cannot be missed. Depending on the [Gloria Domini](Gloria_Domini.md) skill level, opponents will lose 20~40% of their current sp. The skill is best used with high [DEX](Stats.md) and within [Magic Strings](Magic_Strings.md). Some uses of the skill:

*  Bypasses opponent's Paladin's [Sacrifice](Sacrifice.md), good against Wizards with low HP.
*  Reduces a [Champion](Champion.md)'s SP, [Guillotine Fist](Guillotine_Fist.md)'s damage will be reduced tremendously.
*  Reduces a [Assassin Cross](Assassin_Cross.md) or [Stalker](Stalker.md) SP, lowering their amount of time for [Cloaking](Cloaking.md) or [Stealth](Stealth.md).

[Martyr's Reckoning](Martyr's_Reckoning.md) is a situational skill. While it achieves high damage-per-second with high [VIT](Stats.md) and high [AGI](Stats.md), it drastically reduces your HP to half depending on the [ASPD](ASPD.md). Some uses of the skill are as follows:

*  Assists in killing a [Classical Pluck](Classical_Pluck.md) team
*  Kills anyone without a Ghostring Armor
*  Very good against Gyspy/Dancer spamming [Dazzler](Dazzler.md)

Builds that are focused with solely Martyr's Reckoning will find that it is harder for [Assassin Cross](Assassin_Cross.md)es to kill you and other melee classes to hit you.
