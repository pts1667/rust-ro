# Blast Mine

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Hunter
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 5 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Hunter](Hunter.md)) [Freezing Trap](Freezing_Trap.md) Lv. 1, [Land Mine](Land_Mine.md) Lv. 1, [Sandman](Sandman.md) Lv. 1
- **SP cost:** 10
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** [ASPD](ASPD.md) Based
- **Duration:** (30 - (Skill level*5))
- **Target:** Ground
- **Range:** Melee
- **Area:** 3x3
- **Element:** Wind
- **Catalyst:** 1 Trap


## Description

Blast Mine is a trap that will cause [Wind](Wind.md) property damage its surrounding 3x3 cell area. Once the trap is set, the caster can move the trap by attacking before it activates and explodes.

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Trap Duration (sec.) | 25 | 20 | 15 | 10 | 5 |


## Information

* Damage Formula: Skill Level * (50 + [DEX / 2]) * {1 + (INT / 100)} * Number of stacked monster
* This trap ignores [DEF](DEF.md)
