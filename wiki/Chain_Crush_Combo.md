# Chain Crush Combo

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Champion
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 10 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Champion](Champion.md)) [Glacier Fist](Glacier_Fist.md) Lv. 2
- **SP cost:** (2 + 2*Skill Level)
- **Delay:** (1~5) 0.8 seconds (6~10) 1.0 second
- **Target:** Enemy ([via Combo](Monk.md))
- **Range:** Melee


## Description

Continue with more attacks and combos. The number of hits and the damage dealt is affected by the skill level. The skill only works during the delay of [Glacier Fist](Glacier_Fist.md) or [Raging Thrust](Raging_Thrust.md). It has a delay time that is affected by [AGI](Stats.md) and [DEX](Stats.md). [Guillotine Fist](Guillotine_Fist.md) can still be used after this skill as long as you have [Fury](Fury.md).

| Level | +[ATK](Stats.md) (%) | Hits | Base Delay Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 500% | 1 | 0.8 second |
| 2 | 600% | 1 | 0.8 second |
| 3 | 700% | 2 | 0.8 second |
| 4 | 800% | 2 | 0.8 second |
| 5 | 900% | 3 | 0.8 second |
| 6 | 1000% | 3 | 1.0 second |
| 7 | 1100% | 4 | 1.0 second |
| 8 | 1200% | 4 | 1.0 second |
| 9 | 1300% | 5 | 1.0 second |
| 10 | 1400% | 5 | 1.0 second |
