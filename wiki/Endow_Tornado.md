# Endow Tornado

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Sage
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 5 (Fixed)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Mage](Mage.md)) [Lightning Bolt](Lightening_Bolt.md) Lv. 1, ([Sage](Sage.md)) [Study](Study.md) Lv. 5
- **SP cost:** 40
- **Cast time:** 3 seconds
- **Duration:** 20/30 minutes
- **Target:** Self, Party Member
- **Catalyst:** 1 Wind of Verdure


## Description

Temporarily endow a Party Member's weapon with the [Wind](Wind.md) property for this skill's duration. This skill's level affects the property endowment success rate. If the skill fails, there is a chance to break the equipped weapon and no elemental attribute will be applied. Each cast consumes 40 SP and 1 Wind of Verdure regardless of skill level or success.

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Success (%) | 70 | 80 | 90 | 100 | 100 |
| Duration (min) | 20 | 20 | 20 | 20 | 30 |
