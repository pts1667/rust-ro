# Ranger Job Change Guide

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** 99
- **Job level requirement:** 50 (Normal 2nd) / 70 (Transcended)
- **Class requirement:** Hunter, Sniper
- **Item requirement:** None
- **Quests required:** Access to Turtle Island
- **Item reward:** Sniper Goggles, Green Apple Ring, Wolf Flute
- **Quest reward:** Job Change to Ranger
- **Cat:** no

A. Talk to the Test Supervisor located near the boat back to Alberta. He will give you further briefing about the job change and check whether you have attained base level 99.

B. After being briefed about the job change, he will then instruct you remove all of your equipment and make your weight 0 (zenny is not included). You will be warped back to Alberta.

C. Return to the Test Supervisor. He will give you a survival test of reaching the next Test Supervisor located at the North-West part of the island. He will provide you with 1 Bow, 50 Arrows and 10 Fly Wings.

D. When the next supervisor is located, talk and choose "I came here for the test". He will then give you a mission of killing 1 Dragon Tail and return with 1 Wing of Dragon Fly.

<small>Notes:
*  When you talk to the to the representative, make sure you drop the arrows and fly wings left on your inventory or else he will warp you back to the starting point (which is beside the 1st test representative)
*  It is much easier if you have your falcon with you to kill the required monster if you have no more arrows left or a Spring Rabbit just looted the arrow you dropped.

E. After passing the initial tests, he will then warp you to the test waiting area. When your turn is in, you will be warped to the main test area.

**Mission 1 : The Test of Accuracy**

1.1 - In this mission, you will need to kill a poring-class family (consists of Poring, Marin, Poporing and Drops) around you. This is just somewhat like to Hunter Job Test, the only difference is the monsters are poring-class. Instructions will be broadcasted.

1.2 - You need to kill a monster with a name "Poring". The monsters will be summoned in different locations, types, and names every 5 seconds. So it's good if you can find the the "Poring" very quick. Passing score is 15 out of 20.

**Mission 2 : Trapping Ability Test**

In this mission, they aim to test your trapping skills by killing plant monsters that block your way.

2.1 - After you finish the 1st test, you will be warped to the 2nd test area, instructions will be broadcasted. Just stay where you are and wait for the time to start. NPC and monsters that will appear on the test area indicates the time has started. You will be given 3 minutes to finish the test.

2.2 - Talk to the representative near you. He will give you 10 Bombrings, 100 Novice Potions and 2k+ bulky report files which will make your weight go 90%.

2.3 - Using your bombrings, you need to eliminate the plant monsters which blocks your way through the next representative. The plant monsters are Flora, Geographer, Nephentes, Drosera, Muscipular, Mandragora, Parasite.

<small>Note: Bombrings follows you and attacks you for 1 second and explodes in 3 seconds right after you place it. The explosion is 5x5 cell area so better don't get caught in the explosion or you will die and have to repeat mission 1. You need to consume all the bombrings to proceed.

2.4 - Reach for the Test Supervisor Kowsyun at the end of the path, you need not to talk to her, just walk near her and the npc will activate. After successuly killing some of the monsters and giving the report files, she will warp you to the 3rd test area.

**Mission 3 : The Usage of a Warg**

3.1 - Talk to the Supervisor and he will give you the instructions for the test.

3.2 - He will provide you with 1 Job Change Flute to summon a mercenary Baby Desert Wolf.

3.3 - Summon the mercenary and talk to the supervisor to begin the test.

3.4 - You will be under the Stone Curse effect which prevents you to move. You will need to control the mercenary to detonate the 20 Egg Bombs that will appear randomly one by one on the map. Passing score is 10 out of 20.

3.5 - After passing the test, you will moved to the Manager to change your job. You will be awarded with 1 Sniper Goggles, 1 Green Apple Ring, and 1 Wolf Flute.
