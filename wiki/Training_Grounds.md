# Training Grounds

The Novice Training Grounds is a facility of which all new characters are sent to as a [Novice](Novice.md). It contains many tutorials, free exp and items to new players.

The training grounds is the place most people stay at until job level 10, when they can turn into first classes/expanded classes.

The training grounds has 4 original maps, and 6 maps that are identical except for the monsters they hold. The training grounds has no [Kafra](Kafra.md) storage or [Mail](Mail_System.md) service, and thus no way to recive items from outside the grounds. The training grounds has many areas that are normally inacessable, but are accessable via Fly Wings. Without [GM](Game_Master.md) help/exploits, it is impossible to go back to the training grounds.
