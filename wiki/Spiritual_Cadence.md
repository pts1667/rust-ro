# Spiritual Cadence

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Monk
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** 5 (Fixed)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Monk](Monk.md)) [Root](Root.md) Lv. 2


## Description

Spiritual Cadence is a hybrid version of [Increase HP Recovery](Increase_HP_Recovery.md) and [Increase SP Recovery](Increase_SP_Recovery.md). Spiritual Cadence grants the user an additional HP/SP recovery boost, as well as the ability to regenerate HP/SP when overweight and/or in the [Fury](Fury.md) status. When below 50% weight, HP/SP is recovered at intervals of 10 seconds. When overweight, HP/SP is recovered once every 20 seconds. This skill will not work when at 90% weight or more.

The formula for the amount of HP/SP recovered is as follows:

| Skill Level | HP regen | SP regen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [(MaxHP/500) + 4 HP] | [(MaxSP/500) + 2 SP] |
| 2 | [(MaxHP/250) + 8 HP] | [(MaxSP/250) + 4 SP] |
| 3 | [(MaxHP/166) + 12 HP] | [(MaxSP/166) + 6 SP] |
| 4 | [(MaxHP/125) + 16 HP] | [(MaxSP/125) + 8 SP] |
| 5 | [(MaxHP/100) + 20 HP] | [(MaxSP/100) + 10 SP] |
