# Prontera Culverts Entrance Quest

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** None
- **Item reward:** 3 Red Potions, 1 Milk, 1 Orange Potion
- **Quest reward:** Access to the Prontera Culverts

1. Go to the [Knight](Knight.md) Guild in [Prontera](Prontera.md) (prontera 45, 346).

2. Talk to the Recruiter (prt_in 88, 105), choose "Volunteer." He'll warp you to outside the Culverts, and give you 3 Red Potions, 1 Milk, 1 Orange Potion. The Culvert Guardian will now let you enter the Prontera Culverts.
