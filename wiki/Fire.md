# Fire

Elemental property. Fire is the opposite of [Earth](Earth.md), and does the most damage against it. [Water](Water.md) does the most damage to Fire property monsters/armor. Weapons can be forged with the Fire property using a Flame Heart.

## Cards

Cards which concern the Fire Property include:


## Head Gear

* Leib Olmai Card: Add the chance of gaining 'Red Blood' item each time a Brute monster is killed. Add 10% more tolerance against Fire Property Attack.


## Armor

* Pasana Card: Enchant an Armor with the Fire property. DEF +1


## Garment

* Jakk Card: Increase resistance to the Fire property by 30%. Flee Rate +5


## Weaponry

* Vadon Card: Increase damage inflicted upon Fire property monsters by 20%.
