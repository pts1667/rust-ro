# Skill Equipment

Skill equipment, also known as **Skill Gear**, allows players to obtain new skills. These may be due to [Cards](Card_System.md) compounded in a piece of equipment, or the equipment itself. Skills obtained this way will appear in the skill window and can be placed on the hotkey bar. See [Autocast](Autocast.md) for similar equipment and cards which allow skills to trigger automatically.
