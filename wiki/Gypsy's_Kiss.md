# Gypsy's Kiss

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Dancer
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 10 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Dancer](Dancer.md)) [Hip Shaker](Hip_Shaker.md) Lv. 3
- **SP cost:** Cast: 35 + 5*LVL Use: 1 SP/5 seconds
- **Cast time:** None
- **Duration:** 180 seconds
- **Target:** Self
- **Area:** 7x7
- **Weapon:** Whip
- **Weapon2:** Instrument


## Description

Increase max SP and SP regeneration while reducing the SP consumption of all players within the area of effect. SP consumption reduction is affected by the [Dancer](Dancer.md)'s [INT](Stats.md) and level of [Dance Lessons](Dance_Lessons.md).

| 4 | +19% | -32% | 55 |
| 5 | +20% | -35% | 60 |
| 6 | +21% | -38% | 65 |
| 7 | +22% | -41% | 70 |
| 8 | +23% | -44% | 75 |
| 9 | +24% | -47% | 80 |
| 10 | +25% | -50% | 85 |

## Soul Link

The [Bard and Dancer Spirits](Bard_and_Dancer_Spirits.md) enables use of Gypsy's Kiss 10 when used on a [Bard](Bard.md) who has learned level 10 [Song of Lutie](Song_of_Lutie.md).
