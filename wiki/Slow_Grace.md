# Slow Grace

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Dancer
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 10
- **Prerequisites:** ([Dancer](Dancer.md)) [Hip Shaker](Hip_Shaker.md) Lv. 3
- **SP cost:** Cast: (25 + (Skill Level*3)); Use: 1 SP / 10 seconds
- **Cast time:** None
- **Duration:** 180 seconds
- **Target:** Self (Ground)
- **Range:** 7x7
- **Weapon:** Whip
- **Weapon:** Instrument


## Description

Decrease attack and movement speed of all enemies within the area of effect, canceling the effects of any skill which provides a bonus to said stats. This skill's [ASPD](ASPD.md) reduction is affected by the caster`s [DEX](Stats.md) and [Dance Lessons](Dance_Lessons.md) skill level, and the Movement Speed reduction is affected by the caster`s [AGI](Stats.md) and Dance Lessons skill level.

## Soul Link

The [Bard and Dancer Spirits](Bard_and_Dancer_Spirits.md) enables use of Slow Grace 10 when used on a [Bard](Bard.md) who has learned level 10 [Impressive Riff](Impressive_Riff.md).
