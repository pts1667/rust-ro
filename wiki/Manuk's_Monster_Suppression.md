# Manuk's Monster Suppression

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** 70
- **Quests required:** [Two Tribes](Two_Tribes.md)
- **Base EXP reward:** 500,000
- **Job EXP reward:** 300,000
- **Item reward:** 10 Manuk Coin
- **Window:** yes

1.  In [Manuk](Manuk.md) speak to Manuk Gardener Strom (manuk 252, 116), and challenge him.
1.  He tells you to kill 1 Rata and 1 Duneyrr.
1.  After killing those, return to Strom for your reward.
  * Receive 10 Manuk Coin, 500,000 [Base Experience](Base_EXP_Chart.md), and 300,000 [Job Experience](Job_EXP_Chart.md).
