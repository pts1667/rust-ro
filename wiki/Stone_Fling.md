# Stone Fling

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Thief
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 1
- **Quest:** Thief Skill Quest#Stone Fling Quest
- **SP cost:** 2
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** [ASPD](ASPD.md) Based
- **Target:** Enemy
- **Range:** 7 cells
- **Status:** [Stun](Status_Effects.md), [Blind](Status_Effects.md)
- **Catalyst:** 1 Stone


## Description

Fling a stone at a target to inflict [Neutral](Neutral.md) property damage with the 5% chance of causing the [Stun](Status_Effects.md) or [Blind](Status_Effects.md) status. Stone Fling has 50 [Attack Strength](Stats.md) which pierces the target`s [Defense](DEF.md).
* Note: When a monster uses Stone Fling, it only has 30 attack strength.


## Obtainable Via

* Stapo Card: Allows usage of the skills [Find Stone](Find_Stone.md) and Stone Fling.
