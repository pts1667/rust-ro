# Leap

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** TaeKwon Kid
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 5 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** None
- **SP cost:** 50


## Description

Leap forward to overcome obstacles. If the target cell is dead or inaccessible, then the Leap skill will fail. Leap cannot be used in areas where Fly Wings are disabled, excluding [PvP](PvP~d29d2.md) and [WoE](War_of_Emperium.md) maps.

| Skill Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cast time (sec) | 5 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 |
| Leap Range (cells) | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 10 |


## Notes

*  Leap is disabled in [WoE 2](War_of_Emperium_2.md) castles.
*  In pvp, using leap will make the taekwon perform the animation, but when the character lands, they will have advanced 0 cells.
*  Spell breaking Leap causes the Leap-user to be visually invisible for 3~5 seconds.  However, they still give and receive damage normally.
*  If a guild leader is a Taekwon class, once they put skill points into leap, that leader's [Urgent Call](Urgent_Call.md) cast time is doubled (from 5 to 10 seconds).
