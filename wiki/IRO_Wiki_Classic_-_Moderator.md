# Moderator

Moderators of the wiki are to abide by all wiki rules and policies.  If a moderator fails to uphold such rules and policies, their moderator status may be revoked, along with possible removal from the wiki.  Moderators are chosen at the discretion of Resplendent.  If a moderator fails to contribute for a period of time sufficient for them to appear no longer active on the wiki, their moderator status may also be revoked.

See [Moderators](Moderators.md) for a list of current wiki moderators.
