# Hammer Fall

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Blacksmith
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 5 (Fixed)
- **Prerequisites:** None
- **SP cost:** 10
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** [ASPD](ASPD.md) Based
- **Target:** Ground
- **Range:** Melee
- **Area:** 5x5


## Description

Causes enemies within a 5x5 area (including the targetted cell in the middle) to be [Stun](Status_Effects.md)ned by chance.

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stun Chance (%) | 30 | 40 | 50 | 60 | 70 |
