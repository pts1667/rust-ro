# NPC ICEBREATH


## Ice Breath

* Area-of-Effect Physical Attack
* Levels: 10
* Range: 9 Cells
* Target: Single Opponent

A physical [Water](Water.md) elemental attack that deals damage to all targets in a path towards in an area of 9x14 cells.  Has a 70% chance to [Freeze](Status_Effects.md) all targets hit.

| Skill Level | Damage |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 100% |
| 2 | 200% |
| 3 | 300% |
| 4 | 400% |
| 5 | 500% |
| 6 | 600% |
| 7 | 700% |
| 8 | 800% |
| 9 | 900% |
| 10 | 1000% |


## Notes

* You can avoid the damage of this skill using [Safety Wall](Safety_Wall.md) when 4 cells or closer to the monsters and [Pneuma](Pneuma.md) when farther than 4 cells from the monster. Having a high enough amount of [Flee](Stats.md) can enable the player to "dodge" the hit, too. A water elemental armor may negate the damage.
