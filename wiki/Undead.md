# Undead

**For the status effect, see [Status Effects#Undead](Status_Effects.md).**

**Undead** refers to an [Element](Element.md) and a monster [Race](Race.md), most monsters of the latter being of the former and vice-versa. Nonetheless, the two are, in fact, separate and should not be confused.


## Element

Undead elemental players and monsters can be damaged using the [Heal](Heal.md)/[Turn Undead](Turn_Undead.md)/[Sanctuary](Sanctuary.md)/[Magnus Exorcismus](Magnus_Exorcismus.md) skills. Undead elemental players and monsters are also not affected by the knockback from the [Fire Wall](Fire_Wall.md) skill and are immune to [Frozen](Status_Effects.md), [Stone](Status_Effects.md) status and the [Provoke](Provoke.md) skill.

## Cards

Cards concerning the Undead *Element* include:


## Head Gear

* Permeter Card: Add 15% more tolerance against [Shadow](Shadow.md) Property Attack. Add 15% more tolerance against Undead Property Attack.


## Armor

* Evil Druid Card: Enchant an Armor with the Undead property. [INT](Stats.md) +1, [DEF](DEF.md) +1.


## Garment

* Orc Zombie Card: Increase resistance to Undead property attacks by 30%. Flee Rate +5.


## Equipment

Equipment concerning the Undead *Element* include:

* Asprika:Garment. Reduces damage from melee attacks of all elements by 30%. Enables the use of [Teleport](Teleport.md) Lv 1.
* Valkyrja's Shield: Add a 20% resistance against [Water](Water.md), [Fire](Fire.md), [Shadow](Shadow.md) and Undead Property; [MDEF](Stats.md) +5

## Cards

Cards which concern Undead *Race* include:


## Armor

* Geographer Card: Add the chance of auto casting level 2 [Blessing](Blessing.md) on the user when receiving Physical Damage. If the user is equipped with items compounded to the Rideword, Enchanted Peach Tree, Cookie and Fur Seal cards at the same time, it reduces spell casting time by 10%. [VIT](Stats.md) + 10. If [Acolyte](Acolyte.md) Class characters are equipped with items compounded to the set of cards listed above, they receive 30% less damage and receive 5% more Experience Points from [Demon](Demon.md) and Undead monsters.


## Weaponry

* Fur Seal Card: Accuracy +10, [Flee](Stats.md)Rate +3. If worn by [Acolyte](Acolyte.md) Class characters, it adds Critial +9 on Devil and Undead monsters.
* Goblin Archer Card: Inflict 10% more damage with Critical Attack. Critical +7 on Undead monsters.
* Scorpion King Card: Increase damage on Undead monsters by 20%. *(not implemented)*
* Zombie Master Card: Gain 5 [SP](SP.md) each time an Undead monster is killed by Physical Melee Attack. Drain 5 [SP](SP.md) as the weapon is unequipped.


## Shield

* Teddy Bear Card: Reduce damage from Undead monsters by 30%.


## Foot Gear

* Zombie Prisoner Card: Receive 10% more Experience Points from Undead monsters. Receive 20% more damage from Undead monsters.


## Accessory

* Wraith Card: Add the chance of gaining 'Giggling Box' item each time an Undead monster is killed.


## Equipment

Equipment which concern Undead *Race* include:

* Divine Cross: Two Handed Staff. Reduces damage received from [Demon](Demon.md) and Undead races by 15%. Set bonus with Spiritual Ring: [Dex](Stats.md) +2, [MAtk](Stats.md) +10%, Reduces damage received from [Demon](Demon.md) and Undead races by an additional 10%.
* Golden Mace: Mace. Increases damage inflicted on Undead monster by 10%. Indestructible.
* Munak Hat: Headgear. Reduces damage received from Undead race by 10%.
* Sabbath: Axe. Increases the user's [Crit](Stats.md) rate on Undead race targets by 50. Adds a 0.5% chance of inflicting [Coma](Status_Effects.md) on Undead race targets when doing a physical attack.
