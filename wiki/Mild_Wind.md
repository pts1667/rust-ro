# Mild Wind

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** TaeKwon Kid
- **Type:** Active
- **Levels:** 7 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** [Kihop](Kihop.md) Lv. 5, [Peaceful Break](Peaceful_Break.md) Lv. 5, [Happy Break](Happy_Break.md) Lv. 5
- **SP cost:** 20/50
- **Duration:** 10 min
- **Target:** Self


## Skill Description

Endows players weapon (or fists) with an element based on the level of Mild Wind used. Also effects the element of [Soul Linker](Soul_Linker.md)'s [Estun](Estun.md), [Estin](Estin.md), and [Esma](Esma.md).

| Level | SP Cost | Element |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 20 | Earth |
| 2 | 20 | Wind |
| 3 | 20 | Water |
| 4 | 20 | Fire |
| 5 | 50 | Ghost |
| 6 | 50 | Dark |
| 7 | 50 | Holy |


## Notes

* This skill activates like Endure, where using the skill automatically endows the level you used (as opposed to enchant poison, which forces you to choose a target).  Thus, to endow different elements, you must hotkey the different levels of Mild Wind.
* Overrides [Enchant Poison](Enchant_Poison.md)'s element change, but keeps its chance of inflicting poison status. However, if enchant poison expires before mild wind does, your element returns to neutral.
