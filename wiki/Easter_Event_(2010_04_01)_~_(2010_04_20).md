# Easter Event (2010/04/01) ~ (2010/04/20)

> **Note:** Not implemented on iRO Classic.

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** 40
- **Hunting:** 50 Deviruchi, Wraith Dead, Dullahan, or Nightmare Terror
- **Base EXP reward:** Varies
- **Job EXP reward:** Varies
- **Item reward:** 1 Easter Egg Shell
- **Quest reward:** Ability to transform eggs
- **Window:** yes


## Lifting the curse

1. Talk with Lina (prontera 202,297) and offer to help free her from her curse.

2. Kill 50 Deviruchi, Wraith Dead, Dullahan, or Nightmare Terror.

3. Talk with Lina and listen to the music she plays. Search the city which corresponds to the music for the curse's manifestation — which can be seen as a mark on the ground — and click it.

The curse is manifested at the following locations:

* [Geffen](Geffen.md) (207, 114)
* [Juno](Juno.md) (164, 46)
* [Rachel](Rachel.md) (36, 132) (near the west gate)
* [Lutie](Lutie.md) (144, 51)

4. Talk with Lina, who rewards you with 1 Easter Egg Shell and some [Base EXP](Base_EXP_Chart.md) and [Job EXP](Job_EXP_Chart.md).


## Egg transformation

The creature in the egg next to Lina exchanges 1  Egg, 1  Star Dust, 20  Jellopies, and 300,000 zeny for one of the following sets of items, chosen randomly:

* 1 Easter Egg Shell
* 1 Yggdrasil Berry & 1 Star Dust
* 2 Yggdrasil Seed & 5 Eggs
* 2 Special Toast
* 5 Holy Egg
* 1 pet egg (possible pets include Deviruchi, Peco Peco, Picky, Poporing, and Savage Babe)

Eggs can be purchased from the creature itself in sets of 5 for 2,000 zeny.


## Notes

* The Easter Egg Shell has the following properties: [def](Stats.md): -7, [mdef](Stats.md): -7, recovers 1% of your [HP](Max_HP.md) every 10 seconds, [Flee](Stats.md) + 2 if [refined](Refinement_System.md) to +7 or higher, recovers 1% of your [SP](SP.md) every 10 seconds if refined to +9 or higher.
* Holy Eggs enable a single use of level 2 [Resurrection](Resurrection.md).
* After you have completed the quest, Lina will buff you if you talk to her.
