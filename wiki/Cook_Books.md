## Fried Grasshopper Legs

Don't worry-- grasshopper legs are actually quite tasty, and won't hop around once they're deep fried.
* Ingredients:
  * 5 Grasshopper Legs
  * 1 Old Frying Pan
  * 1 Cooking Oil
* Directions:
1. Heat the old frying pan over medium heat. (New frying pans tend to absorb oil, and take longer to heat. If you don't have a used frying pan, then use Savage's fat to pre-treat a new frying pan.)
1. Add 2 TS of Cooking Oil to the pan, and lightly fry 3 Grasshopper Legs. Once the legs turn golden brown, add the the other 2 legs and 1 TS of Cooking Oil, and then fry them until they're crispy.

## Smooth Noodle

Only expert chopstick users can enjoy this noodle without frustration.
* Ingredients:
  * 1 Bag of Grain
  * 1 Savory Sauce
  * 5 Jack o' Pumpkin
  * 3 Carrots
* Directions:
Remember that the noodles are the essense of this recipe.
1. Grind the grain to create high protein flour.
1. Mix the flour with cold water, and then knead.
1. Stretch the dough lengthwise, and fold it into multiple layers until it is flexible yet firm.
1. Thinly slice the dough to make noodles.
1. Refrigerate the noodles for one night so they taste smooth and chewy.
1. Cut a Jack o' Pumkin in half, and scoop out the pulp. The pumpkin's rind will be used as a noodle bowl.
1. Steam 4 Jack o' Pumpkins. Gently tap and then squeeze the pumpkins until the pulp fibers are threadlike.
1. Slice the Carrots, and slightly fry the slices.
1. Boil the noodles at high heat. If the water boils out, pour a cup of cold water and continue boiling.
1. When the noodles are cooked, strain out the water, and then rinse the noodles in cold water for a chewier texture.
1. Place the boiled noodles and threadlike pumpkin pulp into the pumpkin bowl, and pour anchovy broth until the noodles and the pulp covered.
1. Sprinkle the fried carrot slices on top, and serve.

## Mixed Juice

Try the unique taste of mixed fruit juices!
* Ingredients:
  * 3 Apple Juice
  * 2 Carrot Juice
  * 1 Grape Juice
  * 2 Orange Juice
* Directions:
This juice's flavor depends on the ratios of each ingredient, and the order in which they are mixed.
Carrot Juice is the main ingredient, and the taste varies according to the ratios of the juices that are mixed with it. If you prefer your juice to be sweet and sour, we recommend adding Apple Juice, Orange Juice, and then Grape Juice in that order. For a juice that is sweeter, mix Grape Juice, Apple Juice, and then Orange Juice in that order. Try different mixes of these four different juices for new, unique juice flavors. The right mix can introduce you to a new world of flavor!

If you don't have enough herbs, you can substitute a portion of them with more Yellow Spice.

## Bearfoot Special

* Ingredients:
  * 20 Bear's Footskin
  * 10 Carrots
  * 10 Jack o' Pumpkins
  * 2 Aloe Leaflets
  * 1 Hinalle Leaflet
  * 1 Savory Sauce
* Directions:

Note: Bear's Footskin has distinctive smell. Make sure to remove all the hair and dirt before cooking, frequently change water while soaking the Footskin, and steam using the highest heat setting.
