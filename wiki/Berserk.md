# Berserk

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Swordman
- **Secondary class:** Mercenary
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** 1
- **Quest:** Swordman Skill Quest#Berserk Quest
- **SP cost:** 1
- **Target:** Self

  *This article is about the [Swordman](Swordman.md) skill which causes [Provoke](Provoke.md)-like status when the character is at low health. For information about the [Lord Knight](Lord_Knight.md) skill that greatly increases attack speed, see [Frenzy](Frenzy.md).*


## Description

Empowered by rage, the player enters a condition that is equivalent to Level 10 [Provoke](Provoke.md) status when their HP is reduced to less than 25% of [Max HP](Max_HP.md). The Provoked status lasts until the player's HP is restored to more than 25% of Max HP or if the Provoke effect is canceled by certain skills or situations. It can be turned on and off.


## Notes

* The skill doesn't consume SP when cast, but cannot be cast with 0 SP.
* When HP is less than 25%, the player's HP bar turns red. This indicates that the skill is activated.
