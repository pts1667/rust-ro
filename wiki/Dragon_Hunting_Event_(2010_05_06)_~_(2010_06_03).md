# Dragon Hunting Event (2010/05/06) ~ (2010/06/03)

> **Note:** Not implemented on iRO Classic.


## Overview

The following [NPC](NPC.md)s can be temporarily found near maps with Dragons. Players can choose to kill 50 or 100, 100 or 200 of each set at a time. The needed kills available for the quests, depends on the type of Dragon you're starting the quest for.

* **Note:** *Each quest **is repeatable** so long as the player remains within the level limits.*
* **Note:** *[Mercenary](Mercenary_System.md) kills will **not** increase your kill count!*
* **Note**: Users wearing the Baby Dragon Hat will gain 21.4% more EXP for each turn-in (on either server).
* **Note**: Users wearing the Baby Dragon Hat are able to take the [Base EXP](Base_EXP_Chart.md) and turn it to [Job EXP](Job_EXP_Chart.md) and vice versa when finishing the quest (on either server).
* **Note**: The Exp Reward have been increased to 1.5 times their original value during the 2010/09/09 ~ 2010/09/23 event.
