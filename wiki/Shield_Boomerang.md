# Shield Boomerang

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Crusader
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 5
- **Prerequisites:** ([Crusader](Crusader.md)) [Smite](Smite.md) Lv. 3
- **SP cost:** 12
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** 0.7 seconds
- **Target:** Enemy
- **Range:** (1+Skill Level*2) cells
- **Element:** Neutral
- **Formula name:** Equipment
- **Formula value:** Shield


## Description

Allows a [Crusader](Crusader.md) to seemingly attack an enemy with their shield, dealing damage based on their [ATK](Stats.md) and properties associated with the shield.

| Level | [ATK](Stats.md) | Range |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 130% | 3 |
| 2 | 160% | 5 |
| 3 | 190% | 7 |
| 4 | 220% | 9 |
| 5 | 250% | 11 |


## Information

* Requires user to have a shield equipped.
* Shield Boomerang carries status effects, whether from cards or special weapons like Sword Breakers and Mail Breakers.
* The damage dealt from Shield Boomerang is always [Neutral](Neutral.md).
* Anything that adds or effects weapon-type ATK does not add damage; base weapon ATK, weapon upgrades, [Mastery Skills](Weapon_Mastery.md), [Spirit Sphere](Spirit_Sphere.md)s, [Provoke](Provoke.md), the [Magnum Break](Magnum_Break.md) bonus, [Impositio Manus](Impositio_Manus.md), etc.
* What does add damage:
  * Attack from [STR](Stats.md), [DEX](Stats.md), and [LUK](Stats.md).
  * Shield upgrades.
  * Shield weight (Cross Shield, Strong Shield and Bradium Shield is the best choice).
  * Damage modifier cards, which all of the above are multiplied by.
  * [Crusader Spirit](Crusader_Spirit.md) (via Soul Linker).


## Strategies

* Some players prefer using this skill to inflict statuses over [Smite](Smite.md), as it has a high range at level 5. Smite has aspects other than inflicting stun than Shield Boomerang does not have, and vice-versa, however.

* The Tamuran Card is also a good choice for Shield Build Crusader's.
