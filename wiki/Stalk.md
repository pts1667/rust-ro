# Stalk

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Rogue
- **Type:** Passive
- **Levels:** 5 (Fixed)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Thief](Thief.md)) [Hiding](Hiding.md) Lv. 1


## Description

Allows the player to walk while hiding. [Movement Speed](Movement_Speed.md) depends on the stalk level.

Normal Walking Speed = 150 (100%)

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Movement Speed (Low = Fast) | 321 | 312 | 303 | 294 | 285 |
| Movement Speed (%) | 46.7% | 48% | 49.5% | 51% | 52.6% |
