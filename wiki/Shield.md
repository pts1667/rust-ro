# Shield

Shields can be equipped by all [Classes](Classes.md). They take up the Right Hand slot in the equipment window. As such, when a character is weilding a two-handed weapon, they cannot wear a shield.

Sheilds give extra [DEF](DEF.md) and can usually be upgraded. The most basic non-slotted shields can be purchased from armor [NPC](NPC.md)s.
