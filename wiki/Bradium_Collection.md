# Bradium Collection

> **Repeatable** — cooldown: 24 Hour

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** 70
- **Item requirement:** 20 Bradium Fragments
- **Quests required:** [Ring of the Wise King](Ring_of_the_Wise_King.md)
- **Base EXP reward:** 40,000
- **Job EXP reward:** 40,000
- **Item reward:** 3 Manuk Coin
- **Window:** yes

1.  In [Manuk](Manuk.md), go into the last room in the horseshoe-shaped building (manuk 309, 322) and talk to the Manuk Engineer. They ask you to collect 20 Bradium Fragments (Dropped by Bradium Golems).
1.  Return with 20 Refined Bradium, and receive 3 Manuk Coin, 40,000 [Base Experience](Base_EXP_Chart.md) and 40,000 [Job Experience](Job_EXP_Chart.md).
