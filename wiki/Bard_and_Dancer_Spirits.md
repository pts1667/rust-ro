# Bard and Dancer Spirits

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Soul Linker
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 5 (Fixed)
- **Prerequisites:** None
- **SP cost:** (560 - (Skill level*100))
- **Cast time:** 1 second
- **Delay:** N/A
- **Duration:** (100 + (Skill level*50)) seconds
- **Target:** [Bard](Bard.md)s and [Dancer](Dancer.md)s


## Description

Imbues any [Bard](Bard.md) or [Dancer](Dancer.md) with the Spirit of the Bards and Dancers, giving them special powerful buffs that last until the Spirit buff runs out.


## Information

* All [Performance Skills](categories/Performance_Skills.md) affect the Bard/Dancer him/herself.
* While singing/dancing, Movement Speed is greatly increased.
* Their skills become linked, meaning that if the Bard/Dancer has level 10 of the given skill, they can use the same level of the linked counterpart of the opposite gender as well (example if you have Song of Lutie level 10 you will be able to use Gypsy's Kiss level 10.) They are not affected by [Music Lessons](Music_Lessons.md) and [Dance Lessons](Dance_Lessons.md) respectively.
* The following are linked:
  * [Perfect Tablature](Perfect_Tablature.md) and [Focus Ballet](Focus_Ballet.md)
  * [Impressive Riff](Impressive_Riff.md) and [Slow Grace](Slow_Grace.md)
  * [Magic Strings](Magic_Strings.md) and [Lady Luck](Lady_Luck.md)
  * [Song of Lutie](Song_of_Lutie.md) and [Gypsy's Kiss](Gypsy's_Kiss.md)
