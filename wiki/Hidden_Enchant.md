# Hidden Enchant


## Overview

The Hidden Enchantment [NPC](NPC.md) can add special [Stat](Stats.md) bonuses to different armors. Unlike the regular [Socket Enchant](Socket_Enchant.md) NPC, the gear is **randomly given** +1 ~ +3 of one of the 6 [Stats](Stats.md). The NPC is located near the south gate of [Prontera](Prontera.md) (prontera 165, 60). When enchanting an armor, all previous enchantments, upgrades, and cards are lost. There is a chance of failure, in which case the item is destroyed. Each attempt costs 400,000 [Zeny](Zeny.md); no other items (other than the actual armor) are required. **An already-enchanted armor *can* be enchanted again, but will lose any bonuses, upgrades, cards, etc.**

If you have an unupgraded, uncarded, unslotted, unenchanted armor and want a slotted-enchanted-upgraded version, (if it's slottable/upgradeable) it is **necessary** to first slot it with the [Socket Enchant](Socket_Enchant.md) NPC, *then* use the Hidden Enchant NPC to add stat bonuses, then [Upgrade](Refinement_System.md).  Once successful, a [Card](Card_System.md) may then be added to it. This ensures no upgrades/cards/slots/stat bonuses are lost along the way (assuming each step is successful).


## Success rates

The chance of success varies depending on the class of armor.

| Armor Class | Chance of Success | Chance of Success | Chance of Success | Chance of Success |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Armor Class | +1 | +2 | +3 | Cumulative |
| Unslotted | 40% | 26.67% | 13.33% | 80% |
| Slotted | 36% | 24% | 12% | 72% |
| High Grade | 32.73% | 21.82% | 10.91% | 65.46% |
