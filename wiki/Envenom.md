# Envenom

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Thief
- **Secondary class:** Super Novice
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 10 (Fixed)
- **Prerequisites:** None
- **SP cost:** 12
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** [ASPD](ASPD.md) Based
- **Target:** Enemy
- **Range:** Melee
- **Status:** [Poison](Poison.md)


## Description

Attack an enemy with the chance of inflicting the [Poison](Poison.md) status. The target`s [VIT](Stats.md) affect its chance of being afflicted with Poison. Envenom consumes 12 SP regardless of skill level, doesn`t work on [Boss](Boss_Protocol.md) monsters, and Stacks with [Enchant Deadly Poison](Enchant_Deadly_Poison.md).

| Skill Level | [Poison](Poison.md) Chance | [ATK](Stats.md) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 14% | +15 |
| 2 | 18% | +30 |
| 3 | 22% | +45 |
| 4 | 26% | +60 |
| 5 | 30% | +75 |
| 6 | 34% | +90 |
| 7 | 38% | +105 |
| 8 | 42% | +120 |
| 9 | 46% | +135 |
| 10 | 50% | +150 |

* Granted By Poison Spore Card
