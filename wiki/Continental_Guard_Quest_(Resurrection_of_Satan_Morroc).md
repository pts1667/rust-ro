# Continental Guard Quest (Resurrection of Satan Morroc)

> **Note:** Not implemented on iRO Classic.

<big>**Note: On iRO Classic to access Dimensional Gorge and fight Satan Morocc, the character must complete the [Dandelion's Request](Dandelion's_Request.md) quest, not Continental Guard.**</big>

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** 80
- **Party requirement:** 2 People
- **Item requirement:** 30 Live Coal and 50 Glacial Heart
- **Base EXP reward:** 2,000,000
- **Item reward:** Seal of Continental Guard \[1\] **-OR-** Death Loop \[1\] **-OR-** Rune Spellstone \[1\]
- **Quest reward:** Access to fight Injured Satan Morroc
- **Window:** yes

1. Approach any Continental Messenger and he will ask for your help. He can be found at any of the following locations:

* [Prontera](Prontera.md) (164, 304)
* [Geffen](Geffen.md) (128, 90)
* [Payon](Payon.md) (200, 113)
* [Alberta](Alberta.md) (128, 64)
* [Al De Baran](Al_De_Baran.md) (136, 138)

2. In [Morroc](Morroc.md), Talk to the Continental Official (176, 103), and tell him that you want to volunteer.

3. Talk to Chief Balrog in [Morroc](Morroc.md) (159, 113) and tell him that you want to join the Continental Guard.  He'll ask you to bring him 50 Glacial Heart and 30 Live Coal.

4. Once you bring him the items, he'll give you a Continental Guard Paper.

5. Exit [Morroc](Morroc.md) to the East. Talk to the Continental Guard there (moc fild20 38, 174), and tell him that you have to enter the field to investigate. **Being in a [Party](Party.md) of at least two people is required to enter the field.**

6. Look for the Group of Evil in the Dimensional Gorge (moc fild21 171, 227).

7. Once you defeat Satan Morroc, the Group of Evil will change. Each party member must click on it to receive Piece of Morocc Skin. Any player who was on the map can receive the skin regardless of whether or not they were in the party that killed Satan Morroc.

* Satan Morroc will announce his defeat with "I'll let live just a little longer. You'll never find me through this time-space gap!"

8. The Piece of Morocc Skin can be given to Chief Balrog, in exchange for one of the following accessories of choice:
*  Seal of Continental Guard \[1\] (+50 [HP](Max_HP.md), +3% [ASPD](ASPD.md))
*  Death Loop \[1\] (+5 [Crit](Stats.md))
*  Rune Spellstone \[1\] (+50 [SP](SP.md), -1% skill delay)


## Satan Morroc

The best strategy is to have one or two tanks for Satan Morroc, with [Biochemist](Biochemist.md)s and others attcking him.  Avoiding the mob is crucial to success, as by hitting them, their AI is activated, and they will then run loose and use their skills, which can easily wipe a [Party](Party.md).

Satan Morroc functions by using combos. At specific times he will make an announcement, which is the start of a skill combo. He always speaks when casting the first skill of the combo, and the combos are all the same order of skills cast. These combos are as follows:

**80% HP - Magic Combo**
  Recharge Time: 20 seconds
  Announcement: "Impressive! I wonder how far your recklessness will take you."
  Skills: [Stone Curse](Stone_Curse.md) 10, [Firebolt](Fire_Bolt.md) 10, [Frost Diver](Frost_Diver.md) 10, [Jupitel Thunder](Jupitel_Thunder.md) 10

**60% HP - Hell Combo**
  Recharge Time: 35 seconds
  Announcement: "I almost pity how outmatched you are against me."
  Skills: [Area Sleep](NPC_WIDESLEEP.md) 5, [Hell's Power](NPC_HELLPOWER.md) 1, [Hell's Judgement](NPC_HELLJUDGEMENT.md) 5

**50% HP - Vampire Combo**
  Recharge Time: 50 seconds
  Announcement: "It's time to finish the game!"
  Skills: [Vampire's Gift](NPC_VAMPIRE_GIFT.md) 9, [Area Soul Drain](NPC_WIDESOULDRAIN.md) 10, [Evil Land](NPC_EVILLAND.md) 10

**40% HP - Mixed Combo**
  Recharge Time: 1 Minute
  Announcement: "This is how you attack? Watch and learn, weaklings!"
  Skills: [Pulse Strike](NPC_PULSESTRIKE.md) 5, [Area Stone Curse](NPC_WIDESTONE.md) 5, [Meteor Storm](Meteor_Storm.md) 5

**30% HP - Earthquake Combo**
  Recharge Time: 3 Minutes
  Announcement: "You worthless humans. Your so-called holy powers have no effect on me!"
  Skills: [Area Hell's Power](NPC_WIDEHELLDIGNITY.md) 1, [Area Stun](NPC_WIDESTUN.md) 5, [Earthquake](NPC_EARTHQUAKE.md) 10

**10% HP**
  Announcement: "Oh, you're stronger than I thought!"
  Skills: None

**5% HP**
  Announcement: "No, this can't be happening! I'm Satan Morroc, Demon King of Destruction!"
  Skills: [Dragon Fear](NPC_DRAGONFEAR.md) 10
