# Rin's Request

<!-- Quest info -->
- **Level requirement:** 70
- **Quests required:** Started [Two Tribes](Two_Tribes.md)
- **Item reward:** 1 Treasure Box
- **Window:** yes

1.  Read the sign near the center of the Midgard Camp (mid camp 192, 239) and accept the request.
1.  On level 2 of the Yggdrasil Dungeon, find the stranger (several locations) and walk near him. He will disappear.
1.  Search the map for the Escaped Dandelion and kill him.
1.  Talk to Pinedel (mid camp 146, 306) to receive 1 Treasure Box.
