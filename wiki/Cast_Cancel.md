# Cast Cancel

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Sage
- **Type:** Supportive
- **Levels:** 5 (Fixed)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Sage](Sage.md)) [Study](Study.md) Lv. 2
- **SP cost:** 2
- **Cast time:** None
- **Delay:** None
- **Target:** Self


## Description

Cast Cancel enables [Sages](Sage.md) to cancel their own spells while they are casting. This skill can only be used while a spell is being cast. When a spell is cancelled, a fraction of the [SP](SP.md) used to cast the spell is recovered. Increasing the skill level of Cast Cancel will increase the amount of SP recovered from cancelled spells.

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SP Retained (%) | 10 | 30 | 50 | 70 | 90 |


## Information

*  It is also possible to cancel casting by creating a chat just before the cast finishes, although this consumes the full SP and retains the skill's cooldown delay. Area of Effect skills will still animate, but they will not do damage while in chat.


## Obtainable via

*  Beholder Card - Lv. 1
