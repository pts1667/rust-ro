# Venom Splasher

<!-- Skill info -->
- **Class:** Assassin
- **Type:** Offensive
- **Levels:** 10 (Selectable)
- **Prerequisites:** ([Assassin](Assassin.md)) [Poison React](Poison_React.md) Lv. 5, [Venom Dust](Venom_Dust.md) Lv. 5
- **SP cost:** (10 + (Skill level*2))
- **Cast time:** 1 second
- **Delay:** [ASPD](Stats.md)
- **Target:** Enemy
- **Range:** Melee
- **Element:** Poison
- **Catalyst:** 1 Red Gemstone


## Description

Consume a Red Gemstone to turn a target into a "poison bomb". The target will then explode after a few seconds, dealing splash damage around it and poisoning enemies by chance.

| 1 | 550% | 5 | 8 | 12 |

| 7 | 850% | 8 | 11 | 24 |
| 8 | 900% | 8.5 | 11.5 | 26 |

## Prerequisite conditions

* Enemy HP must be less than 3/4.
* Cannot be used against [Boss](Boss_Protocol.md) monsters.
* Cannot successfully 'bomb' another target before the re-use delay ends.
* The enemy does *not* need to be [Poison](Poison.md)ed.


## Interaction with other skills

* [Poison React](Poison_React.md) adds extra (30% * Skill level)% [ATK](Stats.md) passive bonus.
* Enchant Poison will cause the damage to ignore Immune Garments.
* Autocasts work (Falchion and Fireblend).
* Does not stack with [Enchant Deadly Poison](Enchant_Deadly_Poison.md).
* [Righthand Mastery](Righthand_Mastery.md) and [Lefthand Mastery](Lefthand_Mastery.md) do not affect penalties.


## Information

* This skill always hits.
* The explosion will hit every target in a 5x5 area, for (base damage / # of targets in a 3x3 area) damage.
* Damage is reduced by [DEF](DEF.md).
* [Element](Element.md) is taken from the Right Hand.
* Left Hand weapon ATK is not factored in.
* Factors [ATK](Stats.md) Cards in left hand weapon.
* Not affected by +% cards, only affected by ATK cards.
* If the target uses the hide skill during the explosion, all damage will be cancelled but the chance to poison the target remains.
