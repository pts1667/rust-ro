# NPC WIDESOULDRAIN

> **Npc skill** — name: Area Soul Drain; type: Multiple Target; levels: 10; target: Self

Drains an amount of [SP](SP.md) from players within range based on the amount of damage they would take from a physical hit. Higher levels of the skill drain more SP. The SP drained from players is then recovered by the monster as [HP](Max_HP.md). This skill deals no damage.

| Skill Level | Area of Effect | SP Drain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 11x11 | 20% |
| 2 | 15x15 | 40% |
| 3 | 19x19 | 60% |
| 4 | 23x23 | 80% |
| 5 | 27x27 | 100% |
| 6 | 27x27 | 20% |
| 7 | 27x27 | 40% |
| 8 | 27x27 | 60% |
| 9 | 27x27 | 80% |
| 10 | 27x27 | 100% |


## Obtainable via

* Eraser Lv. 1 when doing a magical attack (Lv. 3 is used if the weapon upgrade is +10)
